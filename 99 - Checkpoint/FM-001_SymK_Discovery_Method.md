# FM-001 --- The SymK Discovery Method

## Purpose

Define the methodology for discovering and validating foundational
concepts.

## Principles

-   Engineering before speculation.
-   Contradictions are evidence.
-   Move one abstraction level up.
-   Categories before primitives.
-   Representation ≠ Ontology.
-   Engineering validates ontology.

## Process

1.  Observe engineering problem.
2.  Identify contradiction.
3.  Formulate hypotheses.
4.  Validate across domains.
5.  Freeze baseline.
6.  Return to engineering.

## Governance

No foundational abstraction shall be introduced without engineering
evidence.
