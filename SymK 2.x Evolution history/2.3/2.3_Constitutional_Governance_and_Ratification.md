# 2.3 — Constitutional Governance and Ratification Stage Frame

**Status:** Scaffold only — not effective  
**Stage:** Closed  
**Effect:** Defines the intended governance-analysis architecture only.

## Purpose

Create durable constitutional governance, activate it through transitional authority, and only then submit the exact 2.2 Candidate to the authority that the effective procedure constitutes.

## Required separation

```text
project-steward transitional authority
              |
              v
       2.3A governance design
              |
      Stage-Accept and activate
              |
              v
 constituted Ratifying authority
              |
              v
  2.3R review of exact 2.2 Candidate
```

The Candidate cannot authorize its own Ratifier. 2.3A Stage-Acceptance and 2.3R Ratification are different acts, authorities, objects and records.

## Governance objects to define

Formal 2.3 must define:

- constitutional statuses and transitions;
- competent actors, jurisdiction and records for each transition;
- Ratifier composition or office, quorum, decision rule, conflicts, recusals and continuity;
- access, notice, evidence, participation, response, review, appeal and dissent;
- exceptions, waivers, experiments, expiry and compatibility effects;
- urgent operational routing to competent operational or external authority;
- amendment, supersession, retirement, versioning, migration and re-ratification;
- precedence and collisions with external/legal/professional/community authority; and
- the transition from project-steward governance to enduring constitutional governance.

## Standing and Evidence boundary

Affected-subject standing/challenge is an unresolved locus, not a settled twelfth Authority kind. Procedure must neither make it depend on Intelligence, expertise, ownership or capability nor invent universal veto, remedy, legal standing or political representation.

Evidence may challenge claims, premises, applicability, consequences or decisions made under authority. Change to an authority or governed artifact follows the competent procedure defined for that object.

## Urgent-action boundary

Credible risk may trigger urgent consideration and routing by competent operational or external authority. The constitutional procedure must not manufacture a general containment duty, suspension power or emergency authority. Any temporary action requires a real source, owner, scope, record, review and expiry.

## Required proof obligations

The formal stage must demonstrate anti-circularity, jurisdictional competence, usable challenge paths, conflict management, continuity, source-governance consistency, bounded exceptions, reversible experimentation, version migration and operation under severe-harm, legal-collision, parent/child, dissent and Ratifier-conflict cases.

## Stop conditions

Stop if governance presumes the Constitution is already Ratified, if 2.3R precedes effective 2.3A, if authority is created by implication, if standing collapses into Authority, if evidence automatically rewrites an artifact, or if urgent risk language creates unsupported power.

## Successful gates

- **2.3A:** governance mechanism Stage-Accepted and effective under a valid transition clause.
- **2.3R:** exact 2.2 Candidate Ratified, amended or returned by the authority constituted through 2.3A.

No scaffold text satisfies either gate.
