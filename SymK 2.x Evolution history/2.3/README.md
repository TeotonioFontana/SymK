## Entry and dependency gates

A separate instruction must open 2.3. General governance design may use accepted predecessor evidence, but candidate-specific testing requires the exact 2.2 Candidate. Ratification is impossible until 2.3A is effective, the constituted authority has jurisdiction, and conflicts, recusals, standing, dissent, records, amendment and appeal paths are operational.

## Fixed invariants

Governance must preserve jurisdiction; separate typed Authority from affected-subject standing/challenge; direct Evidence toward claims, premises, applicability, consequences and decisions under authority; route artifact or authority change through competent procedure; trigger urgent consideration rather than invent emergency containment power; preserve external authority; and prevent the Candidate from creating the authority required to Ratify itself.
