# 2.2 — Constitutional Axiom Set Stage Frame

**Status:** Scaffold only — not effective  
**Stage:** Closed  
**Effect:** Defines the intended analytical architecture only.

## Purpose

Derive and test a minimal constitutional Candidate from the Stage-Accepted 2.1 conceptual baseline without allowing Proposed source wording, file location, publication quality or repetition to create authority.

## Constitutional artifact classes to distinguish

Formal 2.2 must distinguish at least: constitutional purpose clause; axiom; jurisdiction article; governance rule; principle; method; definition; policy; interpretation; and nonconstitutional explanatory material. Classification is a conclusion to be justified, not inherited from a filename.

## Admission tests

Every retained proposition must pass:

1. necessity;
2. irreducibility;
3. universality or explicit jurisdictional scope;
4. semantic compatibility with accepted concepts;
5. independence and non-duplication;
6. enforceable or interpretable consequence;
7. counterexample survival;
8. Three-View adequacy;
9. tension and deferred-question preservation; and
10. migration and derived-project compatibility.

Failure may lead to revision, decomposition, reclassification, deferral or exclusion. It must not be hidden.

## Required proposition record

For each candidate proposition record exact wording, artifact class, purpose, source lineage, accepted-concept dependency, jurisdiction, implications, prohibitions, non-entailments, counterexamples, conflicts, dissent, uncertainty, reopening conditions and affected artifacts.

## Cluster architecture

- 2.2C examines A0/A1/A4.
- 2.2D examines A2/A3.
- 2.2E examines A6/J1.
- 2.2F tests the resulting alternatives as a system.
- 2.2G may consolidate only what prior streams support.
- 2.2H may Stage-Accept, revise or return the exact candidate; it cannot Ratify.

## Mandatory crosswalks

The formal stage must maintain proposition-to-source, proposition-to-DR, proposition-to-TN/DQ, proposition-to-View, proposition-to-counterexample, proposition-to-affected-artifact and 2.2-to-2.3 boundary crosswalks.

## Stop conditions

Stop and route the issue if analysis would reopen an accepted decision without competent authority, erase a tension pole or DQ remainder, manufacture authority/standing/right/duty/remedy, depend on 2.3 procedure not yet effective, or treat the emerging Candidate as Ratified.

## Stage gate

An exact minimal set may be presented to 2.2H only after all admission tests, negative controls, dissent records and impacts are complete. The only successful 2.2 status is **Stage-Accepted Constitutional Candidate — not Ratified**.
