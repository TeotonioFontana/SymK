### 3.3 2.3 governance findings

1. The two-axis, jurisdiction-qualified layer model and nearest-adequate-owner direction have Stage-Accepted 2.0 program authority through DR-005, but not constitutional Ratification.
2. `03_CONFLICT_AND_CHALLENGE_PROTOCOL.md` is intentionally directional. It does not define bodies, access, notice sufficiency, evidence thresholds, deadlines, appeals, remedies, exception approval, compatibility statuses or migration mechanics.
3. Its “immediate containment duties” and commands to contain/freeze/notify exceed the accepted semantics. Formal 2.3 must instead define when credible risk triggers urgent consideration by competent operational or external authority and which existing authority can restrict, suspend or act.
4. Evidence may challenge claims, premises, applicability, consequences or decisions under authority; it does not automatically amend an authority or governed artifact.
5. Affected-subject standing/challenge remains an unresolved locus distinct from the eleven typed Authority kinds. Formal procedure must not make standing depend on Intelligence, expertise, capability or ownership while also avoiding an invented universal veto, remedy or political model.
6. The Ratifier, composition, decision rule, quorum, conflicts of interest, record requirements and review authority are absent.
7. Transitional authority must acknowledge project-steward Stage-Acceptance while preventing the candidate Constitution from circularly creating the authority that Ratifies it.
8. Proposal, review, contest, Stage-Acceptance, Ratification, amendment, supersession, retirement, exception, experimental fork, expiration, appeal and re-ratification states require one explicit lifecycle.
9. A6/J1 force, compatibility exceptions and conflict procedure are joint 2.2/2.3 boundary questions; neither stage can settle the other stage's authority silently.
10. 2.3R can occur only after 2.3A is effective and has tested itself against the exact 2.2 candidate.

## 4. Prepared 2.2 work architecture

Formal 2.2 should use separately authorized streams after accepted 2.1. This preparation proposes, but does not open, the following sequence:

1. **2.2A — Opening, source freeze and admission method:** freeze accepted 2.1/H/2.1.11 inputs; finalize the axiom/admission standard and Candidate meaning.
2. **2.2B — Constitutional purpose, scope and class system:** reconstruct the umbrella purpose/scope; distinguish axiom, jurisdiction article, governance rule, principle, method, definition and policy.
3. **2.2C — A0/A1/A4 epistemic-purpose cluster:** perform independent necessity, non-duplication and harm-exclusion tests.
4. **2.2D — A2/A3 role, standing, Responsibility and Formation cluster:** decompose overcompressed commitments and test non-universality.
5. **2.2E — A6/J1 inheritance and jurisdiction cluster:** decide artifact classes while leaving procedure and Ratification to 2.3.
6. **2.2F — Tension, DQ, Three-View and cross-domain stress tests:** apply all TN-001–031, DQ routes, mandatory cases, negative controls and derived-project probes.
7. **2.2G — Minimal-set consolidation and Proposed constitutional candidate:** produce the exact non-Ratified proposal with dissent, reopening conditions and affected-artifact map.
8. **2.2H — Project-steward checkpoint:** Stage-Accept, revise or return the candidate; never Ratify it in 2.2.

No stream label or ordering acquires authority from this preparatory plan.

## 5. Prepared 2.3 work architecture

Evidence development may overlap with 2.2 drafting, but formal 2.3 must use the exact 2.2 candidate as a live test:

1. **2.3A1 — Opening and authority freeze:** identify transitional authority, external constraints and the anti-circularity condition.
2. **2.3A2 — Constitutional status lifecycle:** define proposal through retirement, authority effects and records.
3. **2.3A3 — Ratifier constitution:** define body/person, membership or office, quorum/decision rule, conflicts, recusals and continuity.
4. **2.3A4 — Challenge, standing and conflict procedure:** define access, notice, evidence, participation, routing, review, appeal and preserved dissent without collapsing standing into Authority.
5. **2.3A5 — Exceptions, waivers, experiments and urgent operational action:** define competent owners, scope, expiration, compatibility qualification, review and prohibition on emergency-power invention.
6. **2.3A6 — Amendment, supersession, versioning and re-ratification:** define material-change tests, migration, dependent impact and reopening.
7. **2.3A7 — Candidate live test and governance stress test:** run the procedure against A0–A4/A6/J1 disputes, severe harm, legal collision, parent/child conflict, dissent and Ratifier-conflict cases.
8. **2.3A checkpoint:** project steward Stage-Accepts, revises or returns the governance mechanism under transitional authority.
9. **2.3R checkpoint:** only the authority constituted by effective 2.3A may Ratify, amend or return the 2.2 candidate.

## 8. Reopening and stop conditions

Stop preparation and return to the proper owner if:

- Human review or 2.1.11 changes a material source proposition;
- a constitutional defect proves to originate in DR-009–018 rather than a Proposed artifact;
- any TN pole or DQ remainder loses an owner;
- a proposed procedure manufactures standing, right, duty, remedy, emergency power or authority without competent source;
- 2.3 governance depends on the Constitution's authority before 2.3A exists;
- 2.2 candidate selection relies on Ratification that 2.3 has not supplied; or
- a preparatory artifact is mistaken for stage opening, Candidate promotion or Ratification.
