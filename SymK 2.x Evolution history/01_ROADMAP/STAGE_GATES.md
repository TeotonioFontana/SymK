# SymK 2.x Stage Gates

Every stage must demonstrate:

1. defined scope and explicit exclusions;
2. complete work-package disposition;
3. decision and evidence traceability;
4. counterexample and compatibility review;
5. preserved dissent and deferred questions;
6. affected-artifact analysis;
7. derived-project impact analysis where applicable;
8. canonical consistency review;
9. a completed stage summary;
10. explicit acceptance authority and date.

A gate may pass with open questions only when each question is explicitly deferred, does not destroy the coherence required by the stage, and has a destination in the roadmap.
