# SymK 2.x Stage Gates

Every stage must demonstrate:

1. defined scope and explicit exclusions;
2. complete work-package disposition;
3. decision and evidence traceability;
4. counterexample and compatibility review;
5. preserved dissent and deferred questions;
6. affected-artifact analysis;
7. derived-project impact analysis where applicable;
8. canonical consistency review;
9. a completed stage summary;
10. explicit acceptance authority and date.
11. identity and project-name consistency where the stage changes, constitutionalizes, packages, migrates, implements, or releases the governed identity.

A gate may pass with open questions only when each question is explicitly deferred, does not destroy the coherence required by the stage, and has a destination in the roadmap.

## Identity and name gate

Whenever the current project name or historical expansion may misstate the accepted identity, engineered object, participant topology or jurisdiction, the responsible stage must compare explicit alternatives and either decide the issue or defer it with a stable placeholder, owner, downstream consequences and a rule preventing misleading use.

The project may defer the substantive name challenge only through an exact convention, owner and downstream gate. Under the current route, “SymK” remains a controlled historical proper name through 3.0, “Symbiotic Knowledge” remains historical lineage rather than presumed current semantics, 3.0E does not endorse the name permanently, and the challenge belongs to separately opened 3.1.

Physical path, repository, package, namespace, link and publication migration remains separately governed and follows only a competent semantic decision.
