# 2.0.3 — Jurisdiction Analysis

**Status:** Questioned  
**Primary artifact:** A5 — Philosophical Jurisdiction

## Central question

> **Which philosophical and engineering commitments must SymK make, and which must it refuse to pretend it can settle?**

## Working jurisdiction matrix

| Field | Proposed relationship to SymK | Reason | Boundary still to test |
|---|---|---|---|
| Epistemology | Primary jurisdiction | Knowledge is the shared medium; inquiry, warrant, reliance, challenge, and revision are central | SymK does not attempt complete Epistemology |
| Intelligence | Minimal necessary commitment | Cooperation requires identifiable bearers and attributable capacities | No complete theory of mind, consciousness, or general intelligence |
| Ontology | Minimal constitutional commitment | SymK inevitably refers to participants, claims, representations, relations, and contexts | Domain ontology belongs principally to derived projects |
| Logic | Minimal consistency and inheritance commitment | Constitutional relationships and compatibility require inference and noncontradiction | Domain reasoning belongs principally to projects |
| Ethics | Imported and partially internal constraint | “Responsible” cooperation requires affected parties, consequences, accountability, and repair | Whether ethics is constitutional jurisdiction remains open |
| Education | Necessary relational commitment | Stable cooperation requires formation and habituation | Complete pedagogy belongs outside 2.0 |
| Social and political philosophy | Imported constraint | Cooperation, authority, institutions, power, and affected parties are social | SymK must not become a complete political theory |
| Philosophy of mind | Neighboring inquiry | Intelligence questions may touch mind, agency, and consciousness | Enter only where participant coherence requires it |
| Cognitive science | Evidence source | Capacity and performance claims require empirical support | Does not define constitutional meaning alone |
| Knowledge Engineering | Central engineering discipline | Constructs epistemic conditions and artifacts | Does not manufacture Knowledge or truth |
| Software engineering | Realization discipline | Builds dependable computational components | Does not define SymK concepts through implementation |
| Domain engineering | Derived-project jurisdiction | Converts inherited constraints into domain systems | Cannot silently rewrite SymK commitments |

## Boundary correction

The intended division is not:

```text
SymK = Epistemology only
Projects = Ontology and Logic only
```

It is:

```text
SymK
├── Epistemology: primary jurisdiction
├── Intelligence: minimal participant account
├── Ontology: minimal constitutional commitments
└── Logic: minimal consistency and inheritance requirements

Derived project
├── inherited SymK constraints
├── domain ontology
├── domain logic and reasoning
├── project axioms
└── Engineering Charter
```

## Governing method

> **Include what SymK must assume to remain coherent; defer what it does not need to settle; expose every boundary as challengeable.**

## Expected output

A reviewed jurisdiction matrix, a proposed A5 statement or reclassification, and explicit boundaries for philosophy, engineering, and derived-project responsibility.

## Open classification question

Determine whether A5 is a constitutional axiom, a constitutional scope rule, or part of the Constitution’s jurisdiction article.
