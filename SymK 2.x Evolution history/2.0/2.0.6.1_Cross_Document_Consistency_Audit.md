# 2.0.6.1 — Cross-Document Consistency Audit

**Status:** Completed
**Date:** 18 August 2026
**Release candidate:** `2.0-rc.1`
**Authority:** Controlled migration step 8 under `SYMK-2X-DR-007`
**Result:** Pass with explicit governed deferrals; eligible for final 2.0 stage review

## 1. Audit question

> Do the canonical SymK artifacts affected by migration express consistent identity, jurisdiction, artifact classification, authority, status, lineage, and references without hiding questions intentionally deferred to later 2.x stages?

Consistency does not require every historical source, Proposed candidate, or legacy artifact to repeat the accepted 2.0 position. It requires that current authority is unambiguous, differences are correctly classified, conflicts are visible, and no narrower or older artifact silently controls the new baseline.

## 2. Scope

The audit examined:

- 71 Markdown artifacts under `00_GOVERNANCE`;
- all 18 Markdown artifacts in the constitutional working package;
- all 22 pre-audit Markdown artifacts in the SymK 2.0 evolution package;
- Product Vision and Foundation directory status;
- candidate Constitution, axioms, J1, layer model, and conflict protocol;
- constitutional source map, canonical indexes, registries, and package baselines;
- legacy axiom and principle status surfaces;
- DR-001–DR-007, deferred questions, evidence, changes, and stage summary;
- local Markdown links and declared canonical source paths;
- and repository diff integrity.

The audit did not substantively revise Foundation Papers, decide the final axiom set, dispose legacy artifacts, or implement procedures assigned to later stages.

## 6. Governed exceptions that do not fail 2.0 consistency

### 6.1 Proposed A0

A0 still calls cooperation the objective. This conflicts substantively with DR-002, but it is not a silent or authoritative contradiction because:

- A0 is Proposed and explicitly not Ratified;
- A0 v0.3 contains its own consistency notice;
- the candidate Constitution and registries identify the conflict;
- the accepted Product Vision and constitutional orientation state the controlling 2.0 position;
- and reconstruction, necessity, and minimality are assigned to SymK 2.2 through DQ-006, DQ-015, DQ-017, DQ-019, DQ-020, and DQ-021.

The 2.0 stage may close with this governed candidate conflict because 2.0 establishes orientation and jurisdiction, while 2.2 decides the axiom set. Treating the Proposed A0 as current purpose would fail consistency.

### 6.2 Foundation Papers v0.1

The four papers preserve the historical premise “cooperation is the objective.” They remain valid S0 inquiry sources, not current program or constitutional norms. Their directory README now records the version boundary and prohibits silent rewriting. Later paper revision must preserve lineage and regenerate PDFs.

### 6.3 Legacy artifacts and principles

The Meta-PLC axiom, Symbiotic Cooperation Roles, Pool Exclusivity, SymK Philosophy, and early principles preserve earlier identities and assumptions. Their canonical registration is historical and pending disposition, not current 2.0 orientation. Final disposition belongs to SymK 2.4 through DQ-008.

### 6.4 A6 and J1 procedure

A6 remains Proposed and its necessity or artifact class remains assigned to 2.2. J1's classification is settled for migration, but its exact procedural force relative to Ratified axioms belongs to 2.3. Neither uncertainty is concealed.

### 6.5 Mature concepts and procedures

Knowledge, Intelligence, cooperation, Amplified Intelligence, responsibility, Education, Training, answer quality, formal projections, inheritance mechanics, compatibility statuses, and lowering remain assigned to DQ-001–DQ-026. The 2.0 corpus uses bounded meanings sufficient for identity and jurisdiction without claiming those later decisions.

## 7. Prohibited conclusions from this audit

This audit does not establish that:

- A0–A4 or A6 are necessary, independent, or ready for Ratification;
- J1 is Ratified;
- Foundation Paper claims are true or constitutionally selected wholesale;
- legacy artifacts are superseded;
- LexBrain, IaaC, sshConnectivity, Composer, or LibScan has passed project compatibility validation;
- detailed conflict or inheritance procedures are complete;
- the formal semantic or implementation architecture is decided;
- or SymK 2.0 has already received final acceptance.

## 8. Gate finding

> **The affected canonical corpus passes the SymK 2.0 cross-document consistency gate with explicit governed deferrals. No unresolved migration defect requires another canonical content migration before final stage review. The remaining substantive conflicts are visible, non-Ratified, bounded, and assigned to later 2.x stages.**

Step 8 is therefore complete. Step 9 must update the stage summary and request an explicit final SymK 2.0 decision. Until that decision, the stage remains `2.0-rc.1` and not complete.
