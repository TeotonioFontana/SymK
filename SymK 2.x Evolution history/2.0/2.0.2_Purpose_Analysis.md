# 2.0.2 — Purpose Analysis

**Status:** Questioned  
**Primary artifact:** A0 — Cooperative Purpose

## Central question

> **What constitutional purpose must every SymK-compatible system preserve?**

## Current statement under examination

> **SymK exists to enable sustained, responsible, and evolvable cooperation among Intelligences. Knowledge is their shared medium; cooperation is their objective.**

## Term-by-term examination

| Term | Question | Failure if absent | Current uncertainty |
|---|---|---|---|
| SymK exists to enable | Does SymK enable, govern, engineer conditions for, or constitute cooperation? | Identity may overclaim causation or understate purpose | Wording open |
| sustained | Must constitutional cooperation persist over time? | Brief coordination could claim full compatibility | Applicability to short interactions open |
| responsible | Is this irreducible or derived? Responsible to whom and for what? | Efficient exploitation could qualify as cooperation | Ethical jurisdiction unresolved |
| evolvable | Is revision part of purpose or engineering consequence? | Stable systems could preserve error and hidden harm | Degree and limits open |
| cooperation | How does it differ from coordination, compliance, collusion, or mutual benefit? | Any coordinated action could qualify | Definition belongs mainly to 2.1 |
| among Intelligences | Is every participant necessarily an Intelligence? What about affected parties? | Participant boundary becomes incoherent | Minimal account required, full definition deferred |
| Knowledge | What must be true of the medium for this statement to hold? | Representations may be mistaken for Knowledge | Full definition deferred to 2.1 |
| shared medium | Does sharing mean access, common reference, interoperability, or relation? | Forced consensus or unrestricted disclosure may follow | Authoritative interpretation needed |
| objective | Is cooperation the final objective or a means to responsible outcomes? | Cooperation itself could be treated as inherently good | Relation to consequences open |

## Required tests

- Remove each term and identify the newly compatible failure cases.
- Test asymmetric benefit and burden.
- Test cooperation that harms outsiders.
- Test coerced cooperation and formal consent without meaningful agency.
- Test short-lived interactions.
- Test nonhuman, collective, and hybrid participants.
- Test disagreement without consensus.
- Test efficient collusion.

## Expected output

A proposed A0 statement and exposition whose terms are necessary, interpreted, scoped, and related coherently to Product Vision and A5.

## Explicit deferrals

The complete definitions of Knowledge, Intelligence, Education, Training, responsibility, and cooperation belong principally to 2.1. Version 2.0 must establish only the minimum meaning required to use them honestly in the purpose statement.
