# 2.0.5 — Governing Tension Register

**Status:** Initial population; no tension resolved by registration alone

| ID | Tension | Why both sides matter | 2.0 obligation |
|---|---|---|---|
| SYMK-2X-TN-001 | Durable identity ↔ continuous evolution | Identity enables continuity; evolution enables correction | Define change without silent replacement |
| SYMK-2X-TN-002 | Shared Knowledge ↔ plural representations and access constraints | Cooperation needs relation; truth and responsibility do not require identical access or models | Interpret “shared medium” without forced consensus |
| SYMK-2X-TN-003 | Non-privilege ↔ real differences in capability, law, and responsibility | Origin must not confer privilege; differences remain consequential | Preserve evidence-bearing differentiation |
| SYMK-2X-TN-004 | Stable cooperation ↔ dissent and constitutional challenge | Stability supports cumulative work; dissent prevents conformity and hidden error | Make challenge part of stability |
| SYMK-2X-TN-005 | Reusable universal concepts ↔ domain specificity | SymK needs reuse; projects need faithful domain meaning | Establish jurisdiction and inheritance boundaries |
| SYMK-2X-TN-006 | Formal rigor ↔ dimensions suppressed by formalization | Engineering needs precise models; every model is selective | Govern projection purposes and exclusions |
| SYMK-2X-TN-007 | Philosophical rigor ↔ engineering usability | Meaning must be coherent; artifacts must work | Separate conceptual commitment from realization while tracing both |
| SYMK-2X-TN-008 | Responsibility ↔ uncertain agency | Consequences require answerability; not every Intelligence has legal or moral agency | Avoid transferring responsibility through vague Intelligence attribution |
| SYMK-2X-TN-009 | Useful governance ↔ bureaucracy | Lineage and control are necessary; paperwork can replace thought | Require records only when they preserve decision value |
| SYMK-2X-TN-010 | Cooperation as objective ↔ harmful or exclusionary cooperation | Cooperation enables shared achievement; coordinated conduct may harm outsiders | Include affected parties and consequence tests |

## Register rule

A tension is not automatically a contradiction. It remains visible until a decision shows how the system preserves both legitimate concerns, chooses one with reasons, or explicitly accepts a residual cost.
