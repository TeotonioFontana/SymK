**Status:** Stage-Accepted
**Decision:** `SYMK-2X-DR-006 — Stage-Accepted`
**Accepted by/date:** Project steward, 2026-08-18

## 1. Purpose

Preserve the material tensions created or exposed by the Stage-Accepted SymK 2.0 identity, purpose, jurisdiction, and layer-boundary decisions.

A tension is not automatically a contradiction or defect. It identifies two concerns that cannot safely be collapsed into a slogan or eliminated without analysis.

## 2. Governing scope rule

> **SymK must govern the consequences its designs foreseeably create, but it must not convert every consequential social question into universal SymK doctrine.**

Accordingly, 2.0.5 will register all material tensions together while examining complex tensions separately and proportionately. Registration does not imply that SymK has jurisdiction to resolve the complete philosophical, legal, economic, ethical, organizational, or social controversy surrounding a tension.

## 4. Register rule

A tension remains visible until governed work:

- preserves both legitimate concerns through an explicit relationship;
- gives one concern precedence within a stated scope and explains the cost;
- imports a legitimate external authority;
- assigns the matter to a derived project or organization;
- or preserves the disagreement and defines what evidence is required for maturation.

No tension is resolved merely because it has been named, placed in a table, or assigned to a later stage.

## 5. Explicit exclusions

This work package will not:

- design universal labor, education, or social policy;
- select which individuals or social groups deserve investment;
- settle complete theories of justice, agency, responsibility, Knowledge, or Intelligence;
- prescribe professional regulation across domains;
- quantify displacement or economic concentration without evidence;
- or treat organizational strategy as universal SymK doctrine.

## 6. Expected output

A consolidated tension register that prevents the 2.0 baseline from appearing more harmonious or final than the accepted evidence supports, while routing each tension to the authority and later stage capable of maturing it. This output is Stage-Accepted through `SYMK-2X-DR-006`.
