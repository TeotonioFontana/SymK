## 1. Purpose

Consolidate the inventory, jurisdiction classification, minimum dispositions, and complex dossiers into the governing SymK 2.0 tension baseline.

The register does not claim that the tensions are solved. It establishes what must remain visible, who must govern the next decision, what SymK may and may not require, and how later evidence can mature or challenge the baseline.

## 4. Governing principles extracted from the register

### 4.1 Tension visibility

A material tension must remain visible until an authorized disposition explains its scope, evidence, residual cost, and conditions for revision.

### 4.2 Jurisdiction-proportionate response

SymK acts universally only where its coherence or compatibility requires it. Elsewhere it uses minimal distinctions, project declarations, consequence review, imported authority, empirical validation, or explicit non-universalization.

### 4.3 Consequence without conceptual imperialism

Foreseeable consequence creates a duty to examine, evidence, expose affected parties, and permit challenge. It does not grant SymK ownership of complete social, legal, educational, economic, ethical, or organizational policy.

### 4.4 External authority without epistemic surrender

Law, professional rules, institutions, and legitimate external authorities govern action within their jurisdiction. Their authority does not manufacture truth or prevent SymK from recording epistemic and consequential limitations.

### 4.5 Operational maturation

Daily development and operation provide evidence that may refine, qualify, or challenge the register. Repeated tension is not administrative noise; it may reveal defective implementation, project design, jurisdiction, or SymK assumptions.

## 5. Prohibited global simplifications

The register rejects the following shortcuts:

- every tension must be resolved by selecting one pole;
- every foreseeable consequence belongs to universal SymK jurisdiction;
- consequences outside SymK's substantive jurisdiction may be ignored;
- compliance proves truth, responsibility, or compatibility;
- cooperation is necessarily beneficial;
- amplification excludes displacement;
- automation removes the need for formation;
- exceptional performance confers legitimate authority;
- shared Knowledge requires identical access or representation;
- formalization preserves complete meaning automatically;
- parent authority is infallible;
- operational success ratifies concepts;
- disclosure alone legitimizes incompatibility;
- and Stage-Accepted means permanently settled.

## 7. Reopening conditions

A tension disposition should be reopened when evidence shows:

- recurrent conflict across projects or domains;
- systematic harm or exclusion concealed by the present balance;
- an external-authority change that alters applicable action;
- a new representation or implementation that exposes suppressed meaning;
- project obligations that cannot be operationalized proportionately;
- governance burden exceeding preserved decision value;
- formation, dependency, or displacement effects materially different from assumptions;
- or a tension currently classified as local proving necessary to universal SymK coherence.

## 10. Accepted decision

### SYMK-2X-DR-006 — Governing disposition of material tensions

**Status:** Stage-Accepted

**Question:** What minimum disposition must SymK adopt toward the tensions created by its 2.0 identity, purpose, jurisdiction, and layer model?

**Proposed position:**

> **SymK shall preserve every material tension through a governed disposition proportionate to its jurisdiction. It shall establish universal guardrails where its coherence or compatibility would otherwise fail; preserve minimal distinctions where concepts must not collapse; require derived projects to declare domain balances; require review of foreseeable consequences and affected parties; trace and respect legitimate external authority; keep empirical claims provisional until supported by evidence; explicitly refuse to universalize social, organizational, legal, educational, economic, or ethical policy outside its jurisdiction; and preserve residual tension, dissent, and reopening conditions until evidence and legitimate authority support a more mature balance. Registration, disclosure, implementation, popularity, or Stage acceptance alone shall not be treated as resolution.**

**Dissent and uncertainty:**

- whether each minimum obligation belongs constitutionally, conceptually, procedurally, or in project governance;
- whether the register contains all material tensions;
- whether some social consequences require stronger universal duties;
- whether some current guardrails exceed SymK jurisdiction;
- and how operational evidence should change disposition status.

**Authority:** Stage-Accepted for continuation of SymK 2.0; not constitutional ratification or adoption of substantive external policy.

## 11. Accepted 2.0.5.5 conclusion

The complete register provides a controlled tension baseline without falsely harmonizing the SymK 2.0 decisions or expanding SymK into a universal social philosophy.

`SYMK-2X-DR-006` completes 2.0.5 and permits 2.0.6 to consolidate a baseline that preserves both its commitments and its unresolved tensions.
