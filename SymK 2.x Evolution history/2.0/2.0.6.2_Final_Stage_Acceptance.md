## 3. SYMK-2X-DR-008 — Final SymK 2.0 Identity and Jurisdiction baseline

**Status:** Stage-Accepted

**Decision:**

> **SymK 2.0 is accepted as the Identity and Jurisdiction baseline for the SymK 2.x program. SymK is the engineering discipline for creating Domain Intelligence Amplifiers: knowledge systems in which professionals, AI, and other Intelligences cooperate through governed domain Knowledge toward an Amplified Intelligence capable of producing better and faster answers to complex questions at lower total cost, under epistemic accountability, responsibility, and learning. SymK governs the universal epistemic and Knowledge Engineering conditions necessary for this discipline. Derived projects govern their domain purposes, Ontology, Logic, substantive reasoning, standards, and answer thresholds; organizations and deployments govern local authorization and operation; legitimate external authorities retain authority within their jurisdictions. The two-axis layer model, primary ownership, explicit interlayer relationships, nearest-adequate-owner conflict routing, upward evidence, lateral authority, visible tensions, and consequence return form the accepted 2.0 governance orientation.**

## 4. Effect of acceptance

This decision:

- promotes `2.0-rc.1` to the Stage-Accepted SymK `2.0` baseline;
- completes the Identity and Jurisdiction stage;
- makes DR-001–DR-008 the governed decision lineage for later 2.x work;
- establishes the canonical Product Vision as the controlling current identity and purpose orientation;
- accepts J1's non-axiomatic classification direction while leaving it Proposed;
- accepts the layer and conflict models as program baselines while leaving their constitutional artifacts Proposed;
- preserves every recorded dissent, tension, counterexample, and deferred question;
- authorizes progression to SymK 2.1;
- and permits later evidence and governed challenge to reopen any accepted conclusion.

## 5. What acceptance does not do

This decision does not:

- constitutionally Ratify A0–A4, A6, J1, the candidate Constitution, layer model, conflict protocol, or another artifact;
- decide which candidate axioms are necessary, independent, or fundamental;
- resolve A0's conflict or A6's classification;
- finalize Knowledge, Intelligence, cooperation, Amplified Intelligence, responsibility, Education, or Training;
- Ratify Foundation Papers or select all their conclusions;
- dispose or supersede legacy axioms and principles;
- finalize constitutional governance, project inheritance, formal representations, or lowering architecture;
- establish compatibility of LexBrain, IaaC, sshConnectivity, Composer, LibScan, or another project;
- mandate a labor, education, selection, privacy, economic, legal, ethical, or social policy;
- or declare SymK philosophically or operationally complete.

## 7. Reopening rule

Stage acceptance supplies a governed baseline, not immunity from evidence. A later challenge may reopen a 2.0 conclusion when it identifies the affected decision and version, preserves evidence and consequences, routes the challenge to the owning authority, and makes compatibility effects visible.

## 8. Successor
