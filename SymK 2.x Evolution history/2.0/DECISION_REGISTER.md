# SymK 2.0 Decision Register

`SYMK-2X-DR-001` through `SYMK-2X-DR-008` form the Stage-Accepted SymK 2.0 Identity and Jurisdiction decision lineage. DR-008 completes the stage and authorizes progression to 2.1. None of these program decisions constitutionally Ratifies a candidate artifact.

| SYMK-2X-DR-001 | 2.0.1 | What is SymK’s primary identity? | Stage-Accepted | Engineering discipline for creating Domain Intelligence Amplifiers; Knowledge is medium, cooperation is process, Amplified Intelligence is target, domain reasoning is capability, faster/better/lower-cost answers are outcome | Terminology maturity, bearer attribution, professional scope, breadth of “answers,” discipline maturity, ethical reach, labor effects | Product Vision, Constitution, Charter, legacy philosophy and axioms; canonical migration deferred |
| SYMK-2X-DR-002 | 2.0.2 | What is SymK’s irreducible purpose? | Stage-Accepted | Make engineering of Domain Intelligence Amplifiers systematic, governable, reusable, and evolvable; their value is better answers faster and at lower total cost under accountability, responsibility, and learning constraints | Constitutional compression, place of cost, professional-domain scope, AI necessity, breadth of answers, responsibility boundary | Product Vision, A0, Constitution scope and interpretations; canonical migration deferred to 2.0.6 |
