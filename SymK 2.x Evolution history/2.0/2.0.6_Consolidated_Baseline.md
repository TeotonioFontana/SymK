## 3. Scope and exclusions

### 3.1 What 2.0 consolidates

Version 2.0 establishes:

- SymK's primary identity;
- why the discipline exists;
- the complete value chain it seeks to enable;
- the universal philosophical and engineering questions SymK must govern;
- the questions that derived projects, organizations, deployments, and external authorities must govern;
- layer authority, ownership, interlayer relationships, conflict, and feedback direction;
- material tensions and their minimum governing dispositions;
- and the questions explicitly deferred to later 2.x stages.

### 3.2 What 2.0 does not finalize

Version 2.0 does not finalize:

- mature definitions of Knowledge, Intelligence, cooperation, Amplified Intelligence, Education, Training, responsibility, representation, or epistemic conditions;
- the final constitutional axiom set;
- constitutional ratification and amendment mechanics;
- complete legacy-artifact disposition;
- final foundational concept and principle specifications;
- Project Constitution and Engineering Charter protocols;
- formal-language selection or dual-lane Concept Package design;
- project compatibility results;
- corpus-wide migration;
- or implementation and ontology-lowering architecture.

These exclusions are governed deferrals, not omissions concealed by the release candidate.

## 4. Consolidated SymK identity

### 4.1 Primary identity

> **SymK is the engineering discipline for creating Domain Intelligence Amplifiers—knowledge systems in which professionals, AI, and other Intelligences cooperate through governed domain Knowledge to produce an Amplified Intelligence capable of delivering faster, better, and lower-cost answers to complex questions.**

This identity names:

- **engineering discipline** as SymK's durable category;
- **Domain Intelligence Amplifier** as the class of systems the discipline enables;
- **governed domain Knowledge** as the cooperative medium;
- **cooperation among Intelligences** as the operating process;
- **Amplified Intelligence** as the target capability;
- **domain reasoning** as the exercised capability;
- **answers to complex questions** as the output;
- **better, faster, and lower total cost** as the principal value dimensions;
- **professional decision and action** as the immediate use;
- and **domain purpose and consequence** as project-governed ends and feedback.

### 4.2 Plain-language projection

> SymK provides the discipline for building systems that help skilled professionals and AI reason together over governed domain Knowledge so they can answer difficult questions better, faster, and at lower total cost—without losing sources, context, responsibility, or what is learned through use.

This is an explanatory projection, not a second definition.

## 5. Consolidated purpose

> **SymK exists to make the engineering of Domain Intelligence Amplifiers systematic, governable, reusable, and evolvable across working domains. These systems organize cooperation among Intelligences through governed domain Knowledge to increase the capacity to produce better answers to complex questions, faster and at lower total cost, while preserving epistemic accountability, responsibility, and learning.**

The purpose is the discipline's contribution. It is not:

- the ultimate purpose of law, medicine, infrastructure, science, or another domain;
- a command to maximize cooperation;
- a command to minimize cost independently;
- a promise to eliminate uncertainty;
- or a universal mandate to replace or preserve professional roles.

- and observed consequence may challenge any governing layer.

## 7. Consolidated jurisdiction

### 7.1 SymK jurisdiction

SymK has constitutive jurisdiction over the universal epistemic and Knowledge Engineering requirements necessary to create Domain Intelligence Amplifiers.

It has bounded foundational jurisdiction over:

- Knowledge and epistemic status;
- Intelligence and capability attribution;
- Amplified Intelligence and cooperative organization;
- cooperation, challenge, memory, and feedback;
- representation, projection, provenance, context, and semantic lineage;
- responsibility distinctions necessary to prevent disappearance of accountability;
- Education, Training, habit formation, and continuing challenge capacity where persistent systems shape participants;
- and the governance of evolution, inheritance, and consequence return.

SymK does not claim complete Epistemology, philosophy of mind, ethics, pedagogy, Ontology, Logic, law, economics, or social policy.

### 7.2 Derived-project jurisdiction

Derived projects govern:

- domain purpose and boundaries;
- domain Ontology and Logic;
- substantive domain reasoning;
- professional standards and answer thresholds;
- domain concepts and methods;
- project axioms and obligations;
- product and subproject specialization;
- and project-specific consequence and compatibility decisions.

### 7.3 Organization and deployment jurisdiction

Organizations and deployments govern:

- local authorization and policy;
- staffing, investment, and operating choices;
- risk acceptance;
- configuration and access;
- adoption, suspension, and use;
- and action under applicable law and professional authority.

### 7.4 External authority

Law, regulation, professional institutions, ethics, science, technical standards, and other legitimate fields retain authority within their jurisdiction. SymK traces and respects those constraints without claiming to own them or treating authority as truth.

## 8. Consolidated layer model

SymK governance operates across two axes:

1. **Jurisdictional reach:** universal SymK → derived domain → product/system → organization → deployment.
2. **Realization depth:** constitutional → conceptual/semantic → engineering governance → specification/architecture → implementation → operation.

The principal layers are:

| ID | Layer | Primary authority |
|---|---|---|
| S0 | Epistemic sources and inquiry | Evidence, criticism, alternatives, and candidate conclusions |
| S1 | SymK Constitution | Universal constitutional compatibility commitments |
| S2 | SymK governed conceptual and engineering corpus | Universal semantic and methodological authority according to status |
| P1 | Derived-project Constitution | Domain constitutional identity and commitments |
| P2 | Derived-project governed conceptual corpus | Domain Ontology, Logic, concepts, and methods |
| E1 | Engineering Charter and binding project standards | Durable project engineering obligations |
| E2 | Specification and architecture | Contextual design, contracts, projections, and decisions |
| I1 | Implementation | Concrete technical realization |
| O1 | Organization and deployment | Local authorization, policy, configuration, and operation |

Every governed artifact has one primary owner. Ownership means accountability for status, jurisdiction, change, lineage, and challenge—not exclusive authorship or possession of every authority affecting the artifact.

## 9. Interlayer movement and conflict

SymK distinguishes:

- inheritance;
- specialization;
- instantiation;
- composition;
- role adoption;
- projection;
- implementation;
- local adoption;
- and challenge.

Constraint travels downward within jurisdiction. Narrower layers enrich meaning and design. Evidence travels upward. External authority enters laterally. Each layer changes only the artifacts it owns.

Conflicts are routed to the nearest adequate owner. They move upward when evidence challenges a parent commitment or exceeds the narrower layer's jurisdiction. Material unresolved conflicts remain visible in affected compatibility claims.

The model matures through:

```text
governed baseline
    → daily use
    → observed consequence
    → authority challenge
    → governed review
    → revised or reaffirmed baseline
```

## 10. Governing tensions and safeguards

The Stage-Accepted register contains thirty tensions. Their minimum dispositions use:

- universal guardrails;
- minimal distinctions;
- project declarations;
- consequence review;
- external-authority trace;
- empirical validation;
- explicit non-universalization;
- and preserved tension.

Five complex dossiers preserve the causal relationships among:

1. professional amplification, displacement, formation, selection, dependency, and deskilling;
2. actionable answers, uncertainty, speed, cost, validation, and harm prevention;
3. truth, expertise, authority, collective capability, responsibility, legitimacy, and challenge;
4. semantic alignment, plural representation, unequal access, memory, privacy, forgetting, and tacit Knowledge;
5. inheritance, project autonomy, external authority, unresolved incompatibility, ownership, and implementation evidence.

SymK requires consequence visibility without converting these tensions into universal labor, education, selection, privacy, legal, economic, ethical, or legitimacy policy.

## 11. Success and failure conditions

### 11.1 SymK 2.0 succeeds when

- its primary identity survives translation across LexBrain, IaaC, sshConnectivity, and future domains;
- purpose, value chain, and jurisdiction do not collapse into one slogan;
- Knowledge, cooperation, Amplified Intelligence, reasoning, answers, value, and domain outcomes retain distinct roles;
- every governed artifact has declared authority and scope;
- projects can identify what they inherit, specialize, add, project, implement, adopt, or challenge;
- external authority and local authorization remain visible without becoming universal SymK doctrine;
- uncertainty, dissent, tension, and consequence remain challengeable;
- implementation evidence can mature higher commitments;
- and the framework guides engineering without becoming ceremonial bureaucracy.

### 11.2 SymK 2.0 fails when

- SymK is presented merely as cooperation, a repository, Meta-PLC, specification platform, or constitutional framework;
- cooperation is treated as intrinsically good or as the final objective;
- faster and cheaper outputs are treated as sufficient evidence of better answers;
- a formal model, schema, implementation, authority, or accepted document silently defines truth or universal meaning;
- domain ontology leaks into SymK or SymK dictates domain truth;
- capability, authority, responsibility, and legitimacy are collapsed;
- professional and social consequences are either ignored or converted into unsupported universal doctrine;
- incompatibility is hidden by terminology or disclosure alone;
- or operational evidence cannot challenge governing authority.

## 12. Counterexample and compatibility review

| Case | Result under consolidated baseline |
|---|---|
| Fluent chatbot without governed domain Knowledge, evidence, scope, or responsibility | Not a mature Domain Intelligence Amplifier |
| Repository preserving artifacts without governed domain reasoning | Useful component, not the complete SymK outcome |
| Strong legal system with no transferable governance or conceptual lineage | Valuable product, not sufficient evidence of SymK disciplinary fulfillment |
| Different architectures preserving the same governed commitments | Compatible in principle; SymK does not mandate one technology |
| Efficient cooperation that manipulates affected parties | Incompatible with responsible purpose |
| High-stakes answer requiring greater time and cost for validation | Potentially compatible; cost is jointly constrained, not independently minimized |
| Justified refusal under insufficient evidence | May be the better answer |
| Automation that displaces intermediate tasks | Foreseeable consequence requiring evidence and review; neither automatically compatible nor incompatible |
| Organization prohibiting a technically compatible deployment | Legitimate local or external authority may control use |
| Implementation exposing a defect in a SymK concept | Evidence must reach the concept owner; code does not amend it silently |
| Project explicitly challenging an inherited commitment | Governed challenge; compatibility must reflect unresolved conflict |
| System without an artificial participant | Remains provisionally possible under plural Intelligence identity; mature test deferred |

The baseline survives these cases without importing their domain-specific answers into universal SymK.

## 15. Controlled migration plan

### 15.1 Migration principle

Canonical migration must expose changed conclusions and preserve prior wording, status, and lineage. It must not use 2.0 to ratify the final concepts or axiom set assigned to later stages.

### 15.2 Proposed migration order

1. **Product Vision:** produce a 2.0-aligned version with accepted identity, purpose, value chain, jurisdiction, success/failure conditions, and tension summary.
2. **Constitutional orientation:** align the candidate Constitution's purpose and scope while keeping every substantive axiom Proposed.
3. **Jurisdiction:** reclassify A5 as a Proposed Constitutional Jurisdiction Article and update indexes and references.
4. **Normative hierarchy:** replace the simple ladder with the two-axis, jurisdiction-qualified layer model.
5. **Conflict orientation:** incorporate nearest-adequate-owner routing, upward evidence, lateral authority, and visible unresolved incompatibility without prematurely finalizing procedures.
6. **Source map:** trace DR-001–DR-006 and correct Foundation Paper status/location metadata.
7. **Registries:** register the 2.0 baseline, its Stage-Accepted authority, unresolved conflicts, and later-stage destinations.
8. **Cross-document verification:** test terminology, statuses, links, lineage, and absence of accidental Ratification.
9. **Stage summary:** complete the gate report and request explicit SymK 2.0 acceptance.

**Migration progress:** Steps 1–3 completed on 18 August 2026 through canonical Product Vision v0.2, candidate Constitution orientation, and reclassification of A5 as J1. Steps 4–9 remain pending and require sequential review.

### 15.3 Migration exclusions

The migration must not:

- Ratify A0–A6;
- settle A6's artifact class;
- finalize Knowledge or Intelligence definitions;
- select FOL, OWL, SHACL, or another formalism;
- create Project Constitution or Engineering Charter protocols;
- change LexBrain, sshConnectivity, Composer, or LibScan canonical artifacts;
- perform legacy corpus disposition beyond recording impact;
- or claim SymK 2.0 as final philosophical closure.

## 18. Accepted migration decision

### SYMK-2X-DR-007 — Consolidated SymK 2.0 baseline and migration readiness

**Status:** Stage-Accepted for migration on 18 August 2026

**Question:** What is the consolidated SymK 2.0 baseline, and is it ready for canonical migration and final stage review?

**Proposed position:**

> **The Stage-Accepted identity, purpose, jurisdiction, layer, and tension decisions form a coherent SymK 2.0 baseline. SymK is the engineering discipline for creating Domain Intelligence Amplifiers through governed cooperation among Intelligences over domain Knowledge, directed toward Amplified Intelligence and better, faster, lower-total-cost answers to complex questions under epistemic accountability, responsibility, and learning. SymK governs the universal epistemic and engineering conditions necessary for that discipline; projects, organizations, deployments, and external authorities govern their declared domains and legitimate jurisdictions. The baseline is ready for controlled canonical migration but shall not pass the SymK 2.0 stage gate until canonical conflicts are migrated, cross-document consistency is verified, and explicit final stage acceptance is granted.**

**Authority granted:** The project steward accepted the `2.0-rc.1` migration baseline and authorized execution of the bounded migration plan in section 15, step-by-step for review. This is not constitutional Ratification and not final SymK 2.0 stage acceptance.

## 19. Preserved dissent

The release candidate preserves all dissent recorded in DR-001 through DR-006, including:

- maturity of Domain Intelligence Amplifier and Amplified Intelligence;
- scope of professional domains and the breadth of “answers”;
- constitutional place of faster, better, and lower-cost value;
- minimum ethical and Education commitments;
- bearer attribution and collective Intelligence;
- axiom minimality and A6 classification;
- layer granularity and formal projection authority;
- compatibility during challenge and exception;
- professional displacement, selection, formation, dependency, and opportunity;
- privacy, access, retention, forgetting, and affected-party standing;
- and the possibility that operational evidence will reopen any accepted balance.

## 20. Proposed conclusion

The accepted 2.0 decisions are coherent enough to migrate but not yet reflected in the canonical corpus. The honest next act is bounded migration followed by consistency review—not declaring the stage complete from the evolution workspace alone.
