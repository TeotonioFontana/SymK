# 2.0.5.1 — Tension Inventory

**Status:** Substep-Accepted  
**Parent work package:** `2.0.5 — Governing Tension Register`  
**Source decisions:** `SYMK-2X-DR-001` through `SYMK-2X-DR-005`
**Accepted by/date:** Project steward, 2026-08-18

## 1. Method

This inventory names material tensions without classifying final jurisdiction or proposing resolution. The group headings aid review but do not prejudge the formal classification required by 2.0.5.2.

Each tension must satisfy three entry conditions:

1. both poles express a legitimate concern or observable force;
2. overemphasis on either pole can damage SymK's purpose, coherence, or consequences;
3. the tension is created, intensified, or made relevant by an accepted SymK 2.0 decision.

## 7. Inventory completeness tests

The inventory is provisionally complete for 2.0.5.1 only if it includes tensions concerning:

- identity and evolution;
- universal and domain meaning;
- natural and formal representation;
- truth, authority, and action;
- cooperation, dissent, roles, and responsibility;
- answer quality, speed, and cost;
- amplification, displacement, selection, and formation;
- governance burden and authority challenge;
- external authority and local operation;
- and learning through consequences.

All categories are represented. Completeness here means adequate coverage of accepted 2.0 decisions, not exhaustive coverage of society or philosophy.
