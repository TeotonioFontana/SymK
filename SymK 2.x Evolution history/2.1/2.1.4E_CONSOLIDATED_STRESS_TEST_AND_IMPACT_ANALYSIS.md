## 1. Gate question and result

> Does the combined A–D model preserve accepted SymK authority, survive mandatory counterexamples, respect jurisdiction and deferral boundaries, and support coherent DR-012?

The gate passes after the project-steward lane/view correction. The unreconciled exhaustive two-lane formulation does not pass and is superseded before acceptance. The three-view system, recursive lifecycle, typed epistemic distinctions, artifact boundary, and Ontology/Logic minimum pass as a coherent candidate.

## 2. Candidate system tested

1. Knowledge Engineering is foundational and concerned with epistemic conditions rather than direct manufacture of truth or Knowledge.
2. Epistemic Condition is a governed supporting distinction.
3. The lifecycle is recursive: framing, inquiry/acquisition, representation/claim, grounding/evaluation, integration, reliance/application, consequence, challenge/revision, and preservation/supersession may iterate.
4. Knowledge, claim, Ground, Evidence, Provenance, assessment, epistemic status, governance status, uncertainty, disagreement, reliance authorization, asset, record, Document, and platform Projection remain distinguishable.
5. `KnowledgeObject` is not foundational; a narrowly qualified engineering umbrella remains deferred.
6. Representation and Projection retain DR-010 dispositions.
7. Human, Scientific, and Engineering are coordinated semantic views.
8. Human readability, formal expressibility, and machine inspectability are cross-view capabilities.
9. Semantic/constitutional, corpus-integrity, and grounding/verification lanes are program-work lanes on another axis.
10. SymK governs a minimum semantic and inferential boundary without selecting formal language or package anatomy.

## 3. Compatibility and counterexamples

The candidate passes Product Vision v0.3 and DR-009 through DR-011. It preserves Knowledge as medium without stored-substance reification; applies explicit dispositions, counterexamples, alignment, and declared-loss rules; retains DR-010 structure; and does not redefine DR-011 Knowledge through artifact, status, record, or Intelligence.

It also passes true-lucky-claim; false-but-accepted-claim; valid-inference/false-premise; skilled non-propositional practice; copied corpus; provenance-rich misinformation; consensus with suppressed dissent; useful inaccurate model; machine confidence score; authorized risky use; Document-as-Evidence; runtime drift; readable-but-uninspectable claim; inspectable schema with hidden assumptions; reproducible but inaccessible study; and derived-project counterevidence cases.

## 4. Jurisdiction, ownership, and three-view correction

SymK owns the minimum distinctions needed to preserve its identity and prevent semantic drift. Domains and projects own specialized Ontologies, Logics, methods, evidence standards, competence criteria, assessment schemes, risk thresholds, technical representations, and operational conditions under SymK constraints.

The project-steward challenge identified a real defect: two representational capabilities had been mistaken for a complete set of semantic views. The corrected model distinguishes:

- three semantic views: Human, Scientific, Engineering;
- three cross-view capabilities: human readability, formal expressibility, machine inspectability; and
- multiple program-work lanes, including semantic/constitutional, corpus-integrity, and grounding/verification.

The counts need not match. Future work may refine one classification only through a decision that preserves the others.

## 5. Downstream impact

Foundation Papers v0.2 must explain epistemic conditions and lifecycle; distinguish Knowledge from claims, Grounds, Evidence, status, reliance, assets, records, Documents, and Projections; avoid container language; disclose uncertainty, disagreement, authority, and consequences; and explain three-view alignment. No paper is revised here.

2.5 must decide package distribution and preserve views/capabilities without hard-coding a runtime design. 2.9 must select or validate formalisms, profiles, lowering, conformance, and implementation readiness. No language, schema, identifier, architecture, or derived-project change is accepted.

## 6. Dissent and reopening

The proposal must preserve view naming and sufficiency; possible future formal view; condition taxonomy and ownership; evidence, defeat, and assessment comparability; practical, experiential, collective, and distributed Knowledge cases; `KnowledgeObject` risk/use; package split; machine-validation and semantic-loss limits; challenge rights; reliance and responsibility boundaries; and grounding counterevidence.

Reopen if later work shows an accepted Knowledge mode cannot be represented without contradiction; a necessary view is excluded; package design must reify Knowledge; alignment suppresses dissent or uncertainty; or derived-project evidence reveals systematic failure.

**Pass to revised Proposed DR-012 preparation.** This result accepts no concept or decision. The first draft’s exhaustive two-lane formulation remains superseded; only the reconciled three-view proposal may be presented for acceptance.
