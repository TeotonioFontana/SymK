# SymK 2.1.9 — Integrated Conceptual Baseline Candidate

**Status:** Planned  
**Depends on:** reviewed outputs from 2.1.1–2.1.8

## Question

> Do the proposed concepts form one coherent system capable of interpreting the SymK 2.0 identity and supporting axiom analysis without hidden circularity, jurisdictional leakage, or representational preemption?

## Required integration tests

1. dependency and reciprocal-dependency consistency;
2. scope, bearer, Context, and attribution consistency;
3. prohibited-conflation review across every package;
4. complete 2.0 value-chain interpretation;
5. candidate A0–A4 and A6 interpretability without selecting them;
6. Foundation Paper cross-impact completeness;
7. derived-project translation tests that do not import domain Ontology or Logic into SymK;
8. Human/Scientific/Engineering alignment, cross-view capabilities, and DR-009 human/machine minimum adequacy without formal-language selection;
9. tension, dissent, uncertainty, and deferred-question survival;
10. affected-artifact and migration analysis.

## Deliverables

- `2.1-rc.1` conceptual baseline candidate if the tests pass;
- integrated concept and dependency map;
- consolidated proposed decision lineage;
- axiom-readiness report for 2.2;
- complete Foundation Paper v0.2 revision plan;
- explicit blockers, dissent, and reopening conditions;
- authorization question for 2.1.10 publication work.

## Constraint

This package does not itself rewrite Foundation Papers or reconstruct final `FC-*` packages. It must make those later changes traceable and bounded.
