# SymK 2.1.10 — Foundation Papers v0.2 and Human Communication

**Status:** Planned  
**Depends on:** accepted 2.1.9 publication authorization

## Question

> Can the integrated conceptual understanding be communicated to humans through coherent, accessible, traceable Foundation Papers without turning explanation into constitutional authority or erasing the inquiry preserved in v0.1?

## Publication set

1. Knowledge Foundation Paper v0.2;
2. Intelligence Foundation Paper v0.2;
3. Education and Training Foundation Paper v0.2;
4. Knowledge Engineering Foundation Paper v0.2.

The review begins from four paired, historically preserved publication units. The Markdown file is the governed source and the PDF file is its human-facing publication projection:

1. `Knowledge/SymK_Foundation_Paper_Knowledge_v0.1.md` and `Knowledge/SymK_Foundation_Paper_Knowledge_v0.1.pdf`;

All paths are relative to `00_GOVERNANCE/01_Product_Vision/01_FOUNDATION/`.

## Controlled method

1. freeze and identify the v0.1 Markdown and PDF sources;
2. convert revision-ledger entries into section-level revision plans;
3. revise the governed Markdown sources as new v0.2 editions;
4. preserve material historical arguments, alternatives, dissent, sources, and open questions;
5. align terminology and cross-references among all four papers;
6. state their relationship to the 2.0 identity and provisional 2.1 baseline;
7. state clearly that Foundation Papers are epistemic sources, not Ratified norms or final `FC-*` packages;
8. generate new v0.2 PDFs from Markdown;
9. render and inspect every PDF page for layout, legibility, correspondence, and publication defects;
10. record publication evidence and affected-artifact lineage.

## Communication tests

Each paper must be understandable by an informed newcomer without requiring the complete evolution history. Together they must explain why the concepts matter to the engineering of Domain Intelligence Amplifiers, while preserving the limits of SymK’s philosophical jurisdiction.

## Human review checkpoint

Human-targeted publication requires human review. Automated source comparison, text extraction, terminology analysis, and page rendering may support the review, but cannot satisfy it alone.

Each complete paper must be read by the project steward or a designated human reviewer. The resulting review record must identify:

1. intended and actual reader assumptions;
2. comprehension failures or ambiguous explanatory transitions;
3. terminology that conflicts with the integrated 2.1 baseline or another paper;
4. passages that may falsely appear constitutional or finally authoritative;
5. missing context, rationale, dissent, limitations, or cross-references;
6. citation, typography, diagram, page-flow, legibility, and accessibility defects; and
7. the disposition of every material finding.

For each paper, the allowed review outcomes are: revise into v0.2; retain an identified v0.1 passage with justification; issue a governed erratum or addendum; or defer a named issue to 2.1.11. After revision, the complete v0.2 PDF must receive a second human-readability and publication review.

**Checkpoint 2.1.10H:** the project steward accepts the four review reports and the human-facing v0.2 publication set, or explicitly routes unresolved issues to 2.1.11. Acceptance at this checkpoint does not give the papers constitutional authority.

## Deliverables

- four versioned Markdown v0.2 sources;
- four visually verified PDF v0.2 publications;
- completed revision ledger;
- cross-paper terminology report;
- publication and lineage record;
- issues routed to 2.1.11 rather than silently corrected outside review.

## Out of scope

Constitutional Ratification, final concept packages, formal ontologies, implementation specifications, marketing simplification that distorts the conceptual baseline, or deletion/overwrite of v0.1 history.
