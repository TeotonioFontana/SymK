## Communication tests

Each paper must be understandable by an informed newcomer without requiring the complete evolution history. Together they must explain why the concepts matter to the engineering of Domain Intelligence Amplifiers, while preserving the limits of SymK’s philosophical jurisdiction.

## Human review checkpoint

Human-targeted publication requires human review. Automated source comparison, text extraction, terminology analysis, and page rendering may support the review, but cannot satisfy it alone.

Each complete paper must be read by the project steward or a designated human reviewer. The resulting review record must identify:

1. intended and actual reader assumptions;
2. comprehension failures or ambiguous explanatory transitions;
3. terminology that conflicts with the integrated 2.1 baseline or another paper;
4. passages that may falsely appear constitutional or finally authoritative;
5. missing context, rationale, dissent, limitations, or cross-references;
6. citation, typography, diagram, page-flow, legibility, and accessibility defects; and
7. the disposition of every material finding.

For each paper, the allowed review outcomes are: revise into v0.2; retain an identified v0.1 passage with justification; issue a governed erratum or addendum; or defer a named issue to 2.1.11. After revision, the complete v0.2 PDF must receive a second human-readability and publication review.

**Checkpoint 2.1.10H:** the project steward accepts the four review reports and the human-facing v0.2 publication set, or explicitly routes unresolved issues to 2.1.11. Acceptance at this checkpoint does not give the papers constitutional authority.
