## 1. Finding and disposition

The project steward found that a prospective Human-lane collaborator could understand what SymK is yet still lack a concrete answer to the question: what is SymK intended to produce in practice? The requested correction was to name the domain-centred, knowledge-intensive systems that SymK enables and to connect the explanation to LexBrain.

`SYMK-2X-HF-007` is dispositioned `REVISE_V0.2`. The implementation state is `IMPLEMENTED_PENDING_HUMAN_REREAD`; it is not acceptance or closure.

## 2. Implemented outcome paragraph

> The principal practical result SymK is intended to enable is a class of governed, domain-centred, knowledge-intensive systems - principally Domain Intelligence Amplifiers - in which qualified people, AI and other participants cooperate through governed domain Knowledge to answer complex questions. LexBrain is one concrete derived-project example: a legal-domain knowledge system shaped by SymK, not SymK itself.

The paragraph appears immediately after the definition and mindset call in the governed Reader Orientation Markdown and as a visually distinct second call on the PDF cover.

The wording deliberately distinguishes:

- SymK as the framework and engineering discipline;
- Domain Intelligence Amplifiers as its principal, non-exhaustive realization orientation;
- domain-centred, knowledge-intensive systems as intended practical outcomes; and
- LexBrain as a concrete legal-domain derived-project example rather than the universal framework, an accepted validation result or a completed operational system.

This preserves the accepted layered identity and the Product Vision's framework-to-derived-project boundary while giving a prospective Human-lane collaborator a concrete reason to continue into the full paper set.
