## 1. Decision question

> What does SymK mean by Amplification; to which identified Bearer may Amplified Intelligence be attributed; what minimum conditions constitute a Domain Intelligence Amplifier; and how must capability, system class, integration, dependency, lifecycle, Responsibility, professional transformation, Formation, value, and Engineering Representation remain distinct without an artificial-Intelligence mandate, architecture essentialism, property transfer, or implementation-owned semantics?

## 2. Proposed decision

> **SymK shall govern Amplification as a Foundational relationship. Amplification is a comparative, scoped, temporal, and evidenced relation in which an identified intervention or organized arrangement materially contributes to an increase in an identified organized Bearer's relevant effective capability, relative to a materially comparable baseline, across declared relevant variation. A material Amplification claim preserves the capability object and predicate version; Bearer, level, boundary, Identity, Domain, Scope, Context, and time; baseline, comparator, intervention, and counterfactual; capability dimensions, specializations, target profile, gains, losses, unchanged dimensions, and unknowns; integration, resources, assistance, dependencies, providers, alternatives, failures, causal contribution, Evidence, counterevidence, uncertainty, distribution, lifecycle, assessment authority, challenge, status, and Projection loss.**
>
> **Amplification is not improvement in every respect, a substance, an absolute property, a scalar score, an output status, or a synonym for assistance, enablement, augmentation, access, acceleration, scaling, optimization, automation, substitution, replacement, complementation, composition, integration, Learning, productivity, benefit, or value. More Knowledge content, retrieval, one better answer, higher benchmark performance, faster response, greater volume, fewer Human steps, lower labor, artificial-Intelligence presence, market success, Authorization, compliance, certification, or approval does not establish Amplification without the required Bearer-level capability evidence.**
>
> **SymK shall govern Amplified Intelligence as a compound attribution or qualification rather than an independently foundational substance. Intelligence of an identified organized Bearer is amplified only when a valid Amplification relation is established for that same Bearer and its relevant multidimensional capability. The claim shall select the lowest adequate organized level and may coexist with separately evidenced claims at other levels. Capability, gain, loss, Intelligence, agency, Authority, Responsibility, value, benefit, and status do not transfer automatically among a participant, component, relation, team, institution, configured System, provider, profession, predecessor, successor, user, or beneficiary.**
>
> **SymK shall govern Domain Intelligence Amplifier as a Foundational specific System class. A DIA is constituted by seven conditions: (1) an identified organized System; (2) a declared Domain purpose and complex-question orientation; (3) plural distinguishable qualified Intelligence participants; (4) actual Cooperation among them; (5) governed Domain Knowledge as epistemic medium; (6) an explicit amplified-Bearer and capability target; and (7) evidenced system capability to support repeatable Amplification across relevant variation. Each condition, its Evidence, the actual System boundary, and the applicable lifecycle state shall remain challengeable.**
>
> **The DIA class is substrate- and architecture-neutral. Artificial Intelligence may be a participant, component, resource, tool, Amplifier, Bearer, provider, or assessor; it is neither sufficient nor universally necessary. Human participation is common but not universally necessary. A repository, Knowledge system, model, multi-agent assembly, workflow, tool, platform, project, organization, product, Human–AI pair, architecture, system card, certification, or marketing label is not a DIA merely by type or designation. Fully Human, institutional, artificial, distributed, or hybrid configurations remain eligible only when all seven conditions are evidenced.**
>
> **A DIA, an Amplifier, an amplified Bearer, an Intelligence participant, an Intelligence-bearing System, a resource, a provider, a user, a beneficiary, an affected subject, an authority, and a responsible subject are non-substitutable roles. The DIA System need not itself be the amplified Bearer or an Intelligence Bearer. Cooperation among capable participants does not automatically establish whole-level Intelligence; connection, aggregation, coordination, communication, orchestration, workflow completion, or shared output does not establish integration or system-level capability.**
>
> **DIA class concept, candidate System, actual membership, operational episode, Amplification occurrence, class claim, assessment, governance status, Authorization, responsible-operation qualification, value judgment, and record remain distinct. A System can satisfy the descriptive DIA class while operating irresponsibly or producing harmful consequences. A responsible, authorized, compliant, valuable, or popular System can fail the DIA class or lack Amplification Evidence. Successful operation once does not prove persistent membership or repeatable support.**
>
> **No universal mechanism stack is constitutive. Coordination, mediation, Representation, retrieval, reasoning support, orchestration, feedback, Learning, supervision, memory, automation, and other mechanisms are admitted only as actual, level-qualified, effect-tested relations. Functional integration requires Evidence that material relations enable the claimed organized Bearer or System capability; the presence of named components or interfaces is insufficient.**
>
> **Material dependencies shall remain typed and visible. Constitutive, enabling, operational, epistemic, governance, economic, formative, infrastructural, provider, maintenance, supervision, and affected-consequence dependencies do not automatically establish System membership, control, lock-in, fragility, irresponsibility, or disqualification. Indispensable external resources may remain outside the membership boundary, but they may not disappear from capability, counterfactual, alternative, Power, lifecycle, cost, or Responsibility analysis.**
>
> **Every material DIA or Amplification claim is versioned and lifecycle-bound. Purpose, design, sourcing, procurement, integration, validation, Authorization, deployment, operation, monitoring, challenge, response, repair, update, degradation, outage, suspension, retirement, replacement, and succession may alter Identity, membership, capability, dependency, Responsibility, or qualification. Material change requires proportionate reassessment rather than automatic inheritance from a product name, account, contract, legal entity, model version, certificate, or prior record.**
>
> **Descriptive Amplification and DIA membership shall remain independent from responsible amplification and possible SymK-compatible-DIA qualification. Valid capability gain does not establish Authority, Authorization, Responsibility, safety, fairness, legality, ethical adequacy, benefit, answer quality, favorable outcome, lower total cost, professional legitimacy, or SymK compatibility. Applicable Power, control, Authority, Responsibility, affected-subject, consent-integrity, challenge, accountable-response, correction, repair, remedy, and unresolved-gap relations remain governed under DR-014 and later constitutional, Domain, project, professional, and external authority.**
>
> **Professional amplification uses the same Bearer-level Amplification relation and receives no productivity, employment, credential, output, or adoption shortcut. Professional transformation is a multi-level, non-scalar profile of changes in capability, tasks, roles, Authority, Responsibility, participation, employment, service, dependencies, Power, affected consequences, and Formation or succession. Practitioner, role, task, team, institution, configured System, service organization, profession or community, output, beneficiary, affected subject, and future entrant remain distinct.**
>
> **Automation, substitution, replacement, and task, role, employment, capability, formation, or geographic-institutional displacement remain distinct. Retained capability is a scoped same-Bearer qualification under declared support withdrawal, degradation, delay, substitution, resources, relevant variation, time, and recovery horizon; it is not universally required for Amplification and is not unsupported self-sufficiency. Execution, framing, evaluation, challenge, intervention, fallback, recovery, Learning, and formation capabilities remain separately evidenced. Deskilling is a Bearer-level capability reduction or nondevelopment claim, not an inference from task removal, lower frequency, new specialization, reduced head count, or changed workflow alone.**
>
> **Where responsible or evolvable persistent operation materially depends on capability vulnerable to decay, displacement, concentration, or provider loss, the arrangement shall expose a credible plural capability-continuity path—or an explicit unresolved gap—covering maintenance, practice, renewal, replenishment, substitution, challenge, recovery, or succession as relevant. Education may contribute but is neither universally necessary nor sufficient. This condition does not require preservation of every traditional task, unaided capability, local expert, occupation, institution, or curriculum and does not establish labor, professional, credential, education, or employment policy.**
>
> **Human, Scientific, and Engineering Views shall preserve materially aligned meaning. Human intelligibility, scientific construct and evidence validity, and Engineering specification and inspectability shall not substitute for one another. Material disagreement over Bearer, boundary, baseline, capability, loss, dependency, provider, lifecycle, professional consequence, Responsibility, value, or status is Evidence and shall remain explicit until reconciled, qualified, restricted, or preserved as dissent. Every profile, graph, score, schema, system card, dashboard, audit, certificate, workflow, ontology, formal model, or runtime record remains a Representation or Projection and does not create the represented condition.**
>
> **SymK owns the universal semantic minimum for Amplification, Amplified Intelligence, and the DIA class. Constitutional work owns normative force and compatibility obligations. Domains, projects, sciences, professions, organizations, legal systems, educational authorities, providers, assessors, and affected authorities own applicable purposes, question classes, capability dimensions, target profiles, thresholds, methods, architectures, duties, allocations, policies, statuses, and remedies within jurisdiction. Engineering artifacts may represent or project the system but do not own its meaning.**

No clause has authority until the project steward explicitly Stage-Accepts this proposal.

## 4. Product Vision and predecessor compatibility

The proposed decision makes the accepted Product Vision chain interpretable:

    identified organized DIA
        + Domain purpose and complex-question orientation
        + plural qualified Intelligences in actual Cooperation
        + governed Domain Knowledge as epistemic medium
        + explicit amplified-Bearer/capability target
        + evidenced repeatable support
            -> valid Amplification claim about identified Bearer(s)
            -> possible Amplified Intelligence attribution
            -> later reasoning / answer / action / value / consequence / feedback

Dependencies, Power, Authority, Responsibility, affected consequences, professional transformation, and capability continuity accompany the chain without becoming its descriptive capability predicate.

### DR-009

Amplification and DIA each pass all six admission tests. Every other candidate is individually routed; foundationality, authority, and artifact form remain separate.

### DR-010

Identity, Context, Scope, Domain, Representation, Projection, Bearer, and System remain prior typed structures. No product, account, organization, profession, role, certificate, or implementation field selects the actual subject or predicate.

### DR-011

Knowledge and Intelligence remain distinct Foundational concepts. Intelligence is actual substrate-realized multidimensional capacity of an identified organized Bearer. Dimension, specialization, configuration, attributed profile, performance, score, and Projection remain separate. Amplification qualifies change without redefining Intelligence.

### DR-012

Governed Domain Knowledge is an epistemic medium, not stored substance. Claims, Grounds, Evidence, Provenance, status, uncertainty, disagreement, challenge, reliance, consequence, and revision remain active. Human, Scientific, and Engineering Views preserve material alignment and declared loss.

### DR-013

Actual Cooperation is constitutive of DIA but does not establish whole-level Intelligence. Formation, Learning, Training, Education, memory, feedback, persistence, reciprocal formation, and system Learning remain distinct. Education remains non-universal.

### DR-014

Capability, Agency, Action, Causal Contribution, control, Power, Authority, Authorization, Responsibility, affected standing, consent, accountability, response, repair, remedy, and status remain distinct. Responsibility allocation remains sourced, object-first, typed, multi-level, temporal, non-additive, stable, and gap-aware.

No predecessor reopening is required.

## 5. Eight analytical planes and thirty-part claim envelope

Every material claim shall preserve, proportionately:

1. **Structural plane:** Bearer, System, boundary, Domain, Scope, Context, Identity, version, and time.
2. **Capability plane:** Intelligence dimensions, specializations, configurations, target profile, and relevant variation.
3. **Comparative plane:** baseline, comparator, intervention, counterfactual, persistence, and causal contribution.
4. **Cooperative-system plane:** participants, Cooperation, organization, integration, and whole/member relations.
5. **Epistemic plane:** Domain Knowledge, claims, Grounds, Evidence, Provenance, uncertainty, disagreement, challenge, and revision.
6. **Mechanism/dependency plane:** mechanisms, resources, providers, supports, alternatives, fragility, outage, and lifecycle.
7. **Governance/consequence plane:** Power, control, Authority, Authorization, Responsibility, affected subjects, response, value, and cost.
8. **Professional/formation plane:** tasks, roles, capability, retention, deskilling, displacement, concentration, formation, and succession.

11. governed Domain Knowledge medium;
12. target capability dimensions and specializations;

14. actual capability configuration and attributed profile;

16. new, lost, or disputed integration;

20. mechanisms and material effects;
21. causal contribution and alternative explanations;
22. Evidence, counterevidence, Provenance, assessment, and assessor authority;
23. uncertainty, disagreement, challenge, intended use, and expiry;

30. actual condition, attribution claim, assessment, governance status, and record as separate states.

Material omissions qualify, restrict, contest, or defeat the claim. A status label does not repair them.

## 6. Amplification and Bearer rules

1. Identify the capability predicate before measuring change.
2. Identify one organized Bearer and one level per claim.
3. Prefer the same Bearer across baseline and target when Identity permits.
4. Use explicit new-System counterfactuals when no same-Bearer baseline exists.
5. Require material comparability of resources, assistance, time, Knowledge, selection, and variation.
6. Preserve every material gain, loss, unchanged dimension, and unknown.
7. Declare target-profile and trade-off authority.
8. Do not infer durable capability from one episode or output.
9. Do not transfer capability among participants, components, relations, wholes, providers, professions, predecessors, or successors.
10. Distinguish unaided retained, support-conditioned, externally produced, developmental, and configured-System capability.
11. Declare material dependencies, providers, alternatives, and removal conditions.
12. Preserve causal alternatives, uncertainty, assessment authority, and defeat conditions.
13. Revalidate after material change.
14. Permit multiple claims at different levels only with separate Evidence.
15. Keep capability and its Representation, assessment, status, and record distinct.

## 7. DIA class rules

1. All seven constitutive conditions are required.
2. Exact Domain purposes, complex-question criteria, participant qualifications, capability profiles, thresholds, and methods remain specialized.
3. Fix the DIA boundary before favorable or unfavorable attribution.
4. External dependency may be material without deciding membership.
5. Artificial Intelligence and Human participation are neither universal requirements nor sufficient conditions.
6. Evidence actual Cooperation rather than infer it from connection or orchestration.
7. Evidence governed Knowledge across the relevant lifecycle rather than infer it from repository existence.
8. Make the amplified-Bearer target explicit; it may differ from the DIA System.
9. Require relevant-variation and failure Evidence for repeatable support.
10. Keep descriptive membership separate from active, degraded, authorized, responsible, beneficial, or compatible status.
11. Require independent integration and capability Evidence for whole-level Intelligence.
12. Revalidate after material change, outage, replacement, retirement, or succession.

## 8. Integration, dependency, lifecycle, and Responsibility rules

1. No mechanism family is universal by name.
2. Separate condition, mechanism, operation, effect, and Evidence.
3. Qualify integration by level, capability, boundary, time, and effect.
4. Record dependency type, object, provider, availability, substitutability, control, alternatives, failure, and consequence.
5. Do not include dependencies only after success or omit them after failure.
6. Preserve provider, deployer, user, Participant, Bearer, DIA, beneficiary, affected-subject, authority, and responsible-subject roles.
7. Apply DR-014 object-first, sourced, multi-level Responsibility without a residual sink.
8. Preserve affected-subject non-erasure, challenge, accountable response, correction, repair, remedy, and visible gaps.
9. Distinguish descriptive predicates from responsible and compatible qualifications.
10. Version claims across lifecycle phases and revalidate material changes.
11. Keep outage, degradation, fallback, recovery, membership, capability, Authorization, Responsibility, and value statuses independent.
12. Preserve predecessor/successor Identity, history, retained relations, challenge, response, and gaps.

## 9. Professional transformation and Formation rules

1. Apply ordinary Amplification to identified professional-practice Bearers.
2. Do not treat roles, tasks, outputs, occupational labels, credentials, or professions as automatic Bearers.
3. Separate practitioner, team, institution, configured System, service organization, profession/community, beneficiary, affected subject, and future entrant.
4. Represent professional transformation across capability, work, Authority, employment, service, dependency, Power, consequence, and Formation without one scalar.
5. Separate automation, substitution, replacement, and each displacement object.
6. Separate service output, service performance, service capacity, professional capability, access, and value.
7. Qualify retained capability by Bearer, capability, support change, resources, variation, time, and recovery horizon.
8. Preserve framing, evaluation, challenge, intervention, fallback, recovery, Learning, and formation capability separately from execution.
9. Establish deskilling through Bearer-level loss or nondevelopment Evidence.
10. Test the actual formative function of removed work rather than preserve tradition by default.
11. Expose capability, provider, evidence, Authority, formation, and response concentration.
12. Require a credible plural continuity path or explicit gap only where responsible/evolvable operation materially depends on vulnerable capability.
13. Keep Education non-universal and exact policy with competent authority.

## 10. Deferred-question answers proposed

### DQ-011 — Mature DIA concept and evidentiary test

> A DIA is the seven-condition specific organized-System class established in this decision. Assessment links structural Identity and boundary; constitutive-condition actuality; participant-Intelligence and Cooperation; governed Domain Knowledge; target-Bearer capability and Amplification across relevant variation; and lifecycle, dependency, qualification, and counterevidence. Class membership, successful episode, persistent status, responsible operation, value, and record remain independent. Operational methods and thresholds remain for 2.7.

### DQ-012 — Proper Bearer of Amplified Intelligence

> Attribute Amplified Intelligence to the lowest adequate identified organized Bearer whose relevant capability gain is evidenced. Permit multiple separately evidenced claims at different levels; prohibit transfer among participants, components, relations, teams, institutions, configured Systems, providers, professions, predecessors, and successors; state support, dependency, retention, boundary, and time qualifications.

### DQ-013 — Professional replacement and future experts

> Govern professional transformation through stable level-specific capability and work claims; distinguish automation, substitution, replacement, task/role/employment/capability/formation displacement, and service change; expose retained, evaluative, fallback, recovery, tacit/practical, dependency, concentration, Power, affected-distribution, and Responsibility conditions; and require a credible capability-continuity path or explicit gap when responsible/evolvable operation materially depends on vulnerable expert capability. Exact labor, education, credential, professional, economic, legal, and project policies remain with their authorities.

### DQ-016 — Artificial Intelligence in every DIA

**Proposed universal answer: No.** Artificial Intelligence is neither sufficient nor universally necessary. Fully Human or institutional configurations remain eligible when all conditions are evidenced.

### DQ-019 — Education in every persistent DIA

The Stage-Accepted universal answer remains **No**. The proposed narrower answer is:

> Where responsible or evolvable persistent operation materially depends on vulnerable capability, the arrangement requires a credible plural capability-continuity path—or an explicit unresolved gap—covering renewal, replenishment, substitution, challenge, recovery, or succession as relevant. Education can contribute but is neither universally necessary nor sufficient.

If this decision is Stage-Accepted, these universal conceptual components gain provisional program authority. Operational, constitutional, professional, legal, and policy remainders remain open.

## 15. Alternatives considered

### A. Amplification as any improvement

Rejected because output, access, speed, automation, value, luck, and capability become indistinguishable.

### B. Amplification as unaided retained capability only

Rejected because genuine support-conditioned and configured-System capability would be erased.

### C. Amplification as net scalar gain

Rejected because no universal aggregation authority preserves material losses and distribution.

### D. Amplified Intelligence as a new substance

Rejected because it reifies a comparative attribution and encourages property transfer.

### E. DIA as any useful tool or Knowledge System

Rejected because plural Intelligence, Cooperation, target, and repeatable support disappear.

### F. DIA as necessarily Human–AI

Rejected as unsupported substrate privilege that fails fully Human and institutional cases.

### G. DIA as any multi-agent artificial System

Rejected because architecture and agent count do not establish Intelligence, Cooperation, Knowledge governance, or Amplification.

### H. One universal mechanism stack

Rejected because mechanisms vary and can be present but ineffective.

### I. Every indispensable provider belongs to the DIA

Rejected because dependency and membership remain distinct, although neither may be hidden.

### J. Whole-System Bearer whenever components cooperate

Rejected because Cooperation does not establish integrated whole-level Intelligence.

### K. Responsible amplification built into the descriptive predicate

Rejected because harmful or unauthorized capability gain must remain representable and governable.

### L. Professional amplification as productivity

Rejected because productivity combines output, labor, resources, work allocation, and cost without identifying capability.

### M. Preserve every Human task to prevent deskilling

Rejected because tasks can be obsolete, harmful, or non-formative; capability and continuity are the relevant objects.

### N. Education required for every persistent DIA

Rejected under DR-013 because plural continuity paths exist and materiality is conditional.

### O. Pure project-local definition

Rejected because it permits incompatible SymK identities and marketing-defined membership.

### P. Comparative relational Amplification plus seven-condition substrate-neutral DIA

Adopted as the proposed direction because it passes every analytical gate while preserving specialization and challenge.
