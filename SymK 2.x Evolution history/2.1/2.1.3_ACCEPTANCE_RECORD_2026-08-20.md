## 3. Stage-Accepted dispositions

| Candidate | Stage-Accepted disposition for continuation of 2.1 |
|---|---|
| Knowledge | Foundational SymK concept with the scoped plural epistemic-achievement minimum stated in revised DR-011 |

These are provisional program semantics. Final identifiers, package anatomy, formal expressions, specialization procedures, operational criteria, and runtime structures remain assigned to later governed work.

## 5. Preserved dissent and reopening conditions

All twenty questions in revised DR-011 Section 12 remain active. Acceptance does not convert uncertainty into closure. In particular, later work must preserve and test:

- practical Knowledge and Intelligence overlap;
- the anti-luck and bearer conditions for Knowledge;
- Intelligence thresholds, integration, variation, and novelty criteria;
- universal versus Domain-owned capability dimensions;
- specialization admission, authority, overlap, inheritance, and comparison;
- general-factor and scalar-Projection interpretation;
- literal artificial, collective, institutional, distributed, and hybrid bearing;
- understanding and multi-level attribution;
- the Knowledge-as-medium metaphor; and
- multidimensional amplification gains, losses, dependencies, and trade-offs.

The reopening conditions in revised DR-011 Section 13 remain binding throughout 2.1 and the assigned later stages.

## 6. Non-effects

This acceptance does not:

- complete or finally Stage-Accept SymK 2.1;
- constitutionally Ratify Knowledge, Intelligence, or another norm;
- admit emotional, social, logical-mathematical, or another named Intelligence specialization;
- establish a universal dimension inventory, hierarchy, weighting, threshold, score, or comparison method;
- decide that every artificial, collective, institutional, distributed, or hybrid candidate literally bears Knowledge or Intelligence;
- complete Knowledge Engineering, Cooperation, Education, responsibility, Amplified Intelligence, answer value, or feedback;
- revise or overwrite a canonical Foundation Paper v0.1;
- promote the G/H early review drafts to the governed v0.2 publication set;
- create, rename, or migrate final `FC-*`, property, relationship, principle, or semantic-package artifacts;
- accept the `02_CORE_MODEL` candidate scaffold;
- select JSON, YAML, OWL, FOL, SHACL, a database, an API, Python, or another implementation form;
- modify any derived project or implementation;
- accept the complete Proposed migration plan; or
