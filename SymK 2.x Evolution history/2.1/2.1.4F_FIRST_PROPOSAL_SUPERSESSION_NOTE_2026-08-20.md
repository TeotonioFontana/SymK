# SymK 2.1.4F — First DR-012 Proposal Supersession Note

**Status:** Historical lineage record — first draft superseded before repository registration  
**Date:** 20 August 2026  
**Authority:** Project-steward review challenge and `SYMK-2X-EV-103`  
**Decision effect:** None

## 1. Purpose

The first DR-012 proposal was formulated in the active review conversation after Streams A–E. It correctly consolidated the Knowledge Engineering, epistemic-condition, lifecycle, artifact, evidence, status, reliance, Representation, Projection, and Ontology/Logic results, but described the representational architecture through two lanes in a way that could be read as exhaustive.

Before the proposal was registered or accepted, the project steward challenged that formulation:

> We are still talking about two lanes, but as far as I remember we met the conclusion that another lanes were necessary.

The challenge was accepted as correct. The first proposal was not adopted and is not reconstructed here as if exact proposal-time wording had been preserved in a file. This note preserves the material lineage and reason for supersession without inventing a false immutable artifact.

## 2. Defect and correction

The first formulation collapsed:

1. the DR-009 human/machine minimum;
2. the mature semantic views needed by 2.1.4; and
3. program-work lanes for semantic governance, corpus integrity, and grounding/verification.

That collapse made scientific inquiry appear reducible to either readable prose or machine-semantic expression and obscured claims, methods, evidence, models, uncertainty, replication, defeat, and revision.

2.1.4D corrects this with Human, Scientific, and Engineering semantic views; readability, formal expressibility, and machine inspectability as cross-view capabilities; triangular alignment; and separate program-work lanes. DR-009 remains a minimum human/machine obligation but is superseded as an exactly-two-view interpretation.

Only `2.1.4G_REVISED_PROPOSED_DR-012_KNOWLEDGE_ENGINEERING_EPISTEMIC_CONDITIONS_AND_THREE_VIEW_REPRESENTATION.md` is eligible for acceptance.
