# SYMK-2X-DR-012 — Knowledge Engineering, Epistemic Conditions, and Responsible Representation

**Status:** Proposed — awaiting project-steward review  
**Date proposed:** 20 August 2026  
**Work package:** 2.1.4  
**Source baseline:** `2.1-dev.19`  
**Normative effect:** None unless explicitly Stage-Accepted by the project steward  
**Depends on:** Stage-Accepted DR-009 through DR-011  
**Recorded evidence:** `SYMK-2X-EV-098`–`SYMK-2X-EV-100`  
**Analytical basis:** 2.1.4 opening control and Streams A–E  
**Decision lineage:** No prior DR-012 proposal; this is the first proposal for review

## 1. Decision question

> What does SymK engineer when it cannot engineer truth or Knowledge directly; which epistemic conditions, artifacts, relationships, lifecycle distinctions, and representation obligations must it govern; and what minimum Ontology, Logic, and two-lane capabilities are required without allowing formalism, storage, authority, or implementation to redefine Knowledge?

## 2. Proposed decision

> **SymK shall govern Knowledge Engineering as a foundational concept. Knowledge Engineering is the disciplined design, construction, governance, evaluation, and evolution of epistemic conditions: arrangements of participants, processes, roles, resources, representations, controls, environments, and feedback that materially affect how Knowledge is pursued, achieved, attributed, expressed, preserved, communicated, assessed, challenged, applied, and revised within declared Contexts and Scopes.**
>
> **Knowledge Engineering may enable, constrain, preserve, expose, or impair epistemic achievement, but it does not manufacture truth, create actual bearing by declaration, or transform a claim, Document, model, graph, database, semantic package, assessment, approval, or operational record into Knowledge merely through storage, structure, inference, authority, adoption, or naming. Epistemic Condition shall be governed as a supporting distinction naming the discipline’s direct engineered object, not as a container, universal Entity parent, or guarantee of epistemic success.**
>
> **SymK shall govern the epistemic lifecycle as a recursive model rather than a linear promotion pipeline. Purpose and question formation, participant and boundary identification, inquiry, encounter, acquisition, formation, expression, preservation, transformation, assessment, challenge, reliance authorization, application, consequence observation, revision, supersession, withdrawal, restriction, retirement, Education, and institutional memory may interact through typed, scoped, temporal, and challengeable relationships. A lifecycle transition changes an activity, Representation, assessment, authorization, or operational condition; it does not by itself create truth or actual Knowledge.**
>
> **The lifecycle shall preserve propositional, practical, and familiarity Knowledge without forcing one mode’s representation or validation process onto the others. Propositional Knowledge requires truth and an appropriate non-accidental epistemic connection; practical Knowledge requires competence across a relevant task class; familiarity requires direct or accumulated epistemically significant encounter. Claims, procedure Documents, demonstrations, performance records, and encounter histories may support attribution without containing the achievement attributed to the Bearer.**
>
> **SymK shall distinguish Knowledge, Knowledge semantics, Knowledge attribution, Knowledge claim, Ground, Evidence, Provenance, Epistemic Assessment, uncertainty, disagreement, governance status, Reliance Authorization, Knowledge asset, Epistemic record, Document, semantic package, runtime instance, and platform Projection. `KnowledgeObject` shall not be admitted as a foundational concept or subtype of Knowledge. It may be reconsidered only as a qualified engineering-level umbrella whose use creates no epistemic entailment and whose constituent roles remain explicit.**
>
> **Ground shall identify a reason, process, competence, experience, encounter, or relation materially relevant to a claim or epistemic achievement. Evidence shall identify the contextual role something plays in supporting, challenging, or assessing a specified object under declared criteria. Provenance shall preserve origin, participation, custody, derivation, transformation, and responsibility. Ground, Evidence, and Provenance may contribute to epistemic support, but none alone entails truth, Knowledge, warrant, applicability, or authorization for reliance.**
>
> **Epistemic Assessment, governance status, Reliance Authorization, applicability, lifecycle status, challenge state, representation integrity, and normative or legal authority shall remain separate. Truth shall not be treated as an authority-assigned workflow status. Reliance may be authorized proportionately under uncertainty without claiming Knowledge, and a well-supported claim may remain unauthorized for a particular use because of Scope, consequence, privacy, professional responsibility, or external authority.**
>
> **Representation shall retain the DR-010 foundational meaning: a situated form through which selected aspects of a subject are made available for interpretation, communication, reasoning, storage, or action. Projection shall retain its governed supporting meaning: the relationship through which selected dimensions of a source are expressed in another Representation with transformations, additions, omissions, strengthening, weakening, and known semantic loss declared. A reduced operational Projection may refer to a fuller governed record; it may not present itself as complete when material meaning has been lost.**
>
> **Semantic authority belongs to the governed concept record and decision lineage. The human-semantic lane shall preserve purpose, meaning, scope, distinctions, examples, counterexamples, interpretation, uncertainty, dissent, and known limits. The machine-semantic lane may govern explicitly incorporated formal claims within its declared expressive scope and shall preserve selected identities, versions, typed relationships, qualification, constraints, dependencies, lineage, and declared loss. Neither lane may silently rewrite the other.**
>
> **SymK shall require only the minimum Ontology and Logic capabilities necessary to preserve concept and term, subject and Representation, Identity and identifier, actual bearing and attribution, process and artifact, Context, Scope, Domain, Applicability, typed relationships, provenance, assessment, authority, time, contradiction, absence, uncertainty, revision, and non-entailments. SymK 2.1 selects no universal upper ontology, formal Logic, serialization, schema, inference regime, or lowering architecture.**
>
> **Derived projects and relevant external authorities shall own Domain-specific concepts, epistemic criteria, evidence standards, professional roles, status vocabularies, uncertainty measures, reliance thresholds, Ontologies, Logics, and operational Projections while preserving the universal SymK minimum. Runtime and repository structures instantiate or project governed semantics; their operational centrality does not grant them semantic authority.**

## 4. Required distinctions

If accepted, subsequent SymK work shall preserve at least:

- Knowledge Engineering from Epistemology, Education, data engineering, information management, software engineering, AI engineering, knowledge management, and Domain practice;
- actual Knowledge from Knowledge semantics, attribution, claim, asset, Representation, status, reliance, and record;
- epistemic achievement from engineered condition;
- acquisition from extraction;
- inquiry or encounter from its resulting record;
- Ground from Evidence role;
- Evidence from source, provenance, confidence, assessment, and authority;
- Provenance from truth, warrant, applicability, and authorization;
- claim content from assertion act, source commitment, and Knowledge attribution;
- propositional Knowledge from practical Knowledge and familiarity;
- assessment from assessed object and actual bearing;
- epistemic assessment from governance status;
- governance status from legal or normative authority;
- reliance authorization from epistemic sufficiency;
- uncertainty from disagreement;
- confidence measure from the uncertainty or assessment it represents;
- contradiction from difference in Scope, Context, vocabulary, method, model, authority, or time;
- active state from current truth;
- supersession, withdrawal, restriction, retirement, invalidation, and deletion;
- Document from claim, evidence, asset, and Knowledge;
- semantic package from runtime instance;
- source subject from Representation;
- Representation from Projection;
- syntax, schema conformance, logical validity, semantic fidelity, epistemic sufficiency, empirical adequacy, applicability, governance validity, normative validity, reliance authorization, usefulness, and consequential acceptability;
- universal SymK meaning from Domain specialization;
- and formal or operational projection from semantic authority.

## 5. Epistemic-condition admission rules

A candidate Epistemic Condition must identify:

1. the epistemic subject, activity, or relationship affected;
2. the relevant Bearer or proposed Bearer;
3. the typed enabling, constraining, evidentiary, preservational, impairing, or exposing effect;
4. participants, processes, roles, resources, environments, or controls involved;
5. Domain, Context, Scope, Applicability, and time;
6. evidence and uncertainty supporting the attribution;
7. failure, challenge, and revision conditions;
8. relevant alternatives and dependencies;
9. material consequences and affected parties where applicable;
10. and the authority governing any required response.

Not every cause of belief, system feature, or environmental fact is thereby a governed Epistemic Condition.

## 6. Lifecycle rules

1. **Recursive rather than linear:** challenge, consequence, and new inquiry may enter at any phase.
2. **Multiple entry points:** Knowledge may precede expression; practical formation may precede explicit claims; familiarity may arise without a discrete proposition.
3. **No automatic promotion:** no sequence of workflow states entails truth or Knowledge.
4. **Mode plurality:** propositional, practical, and familiarity paths retain distinct evidence and validation requirements.
5. **Transformation lineage:** translation, summarization, formalization, aggregation, inference, and generation create traceable derivations.
6. **Qualified assessment:** every assessment identifies its object, criteria, assessor, method, evidence, Scope, Context, time, uncertainty, and challenge path.
7. **Scoped reliance:** every material reliance decision identifies authorized use, authority, safeguards, consequence, review, and expiry conditions.
8. **Application inside the lifecycle:** use, non-use, bypass, misuse, and operational deviation are epistemically relevant.
9. **Consequential feedback:** observed effects become new contributions requiring interpretation and may challenge any earlier layer.
10. **Historical preservation:** revision, supersession, withdrawal, restriction, and retirement do not rewrite earlier states or uses.
11. **Formation:** interfaces, incentives, defaults, practices, and institutions may educate or train participants whether or not they declare that purpose.
12. **Institutional memory:** artifact retention does not guarantee continued capacity to interpret, challenge, or apply what was preserved.

## 7. Grounds, evidence, assessment, and status rules

1. Something is Evidence only relative to an inquiry, claim, attribution, assessment, or challenge.
2. The same item may support one claim, challenge another, and remain irrelevant to a third.
3. Grounds may include non-artifact relations such as competence, practice, experience, or encounter.
4. Provenance establishes lineage, not truth.
5. Formal validity establishes consequence from premises under a formalism, not premise truth or responsible applicability.
6. Consensus and confidence are evidence requiring interpretation, not epistemic conclusions by themselves.
7. Disagreement is preserved and typed before resolution.
8. Absence does not universally entail false, unknown, uncollected, unavailable, inapplicable, rejected, or prohibited.
9. A material Epistemic Assessment remains qualified by criteria, assessor, Scope, Context, authority, and time.
10. Truth shall not share one field or identifier with governance status.
11. Reliance under uncertainty must preserve risk, consequence, reversibility, safeguards, alternatives, and review conditions.
12. Earlier authority, reliance, and consequence remain historical facts after an assertion is defeated or retired.

## 8. Representation and Projection rules

Every material Projection shall eventually declare:

1. source and target identities and versions;
2. governing concept or package version;
3. purpose, audience, and owning layer;
4. source claims or dimensions it intends to express;
5. preserved identities, distinctions, relationships, and constraints;
6. translation, abstraction, aggregation, decomposition, and other transformations;
7. additions, enrichment, or inferred content;
8. strengthened or weakened constraints;
9. omissions and inexpressible dimensions;
10. known semantic loss;
11. uncertainty about the mapping;
12. affected reasoning, applicability, or reliance;
13. alignment or verification evidence;
14. route to the fuller source;
15. and authority status.

A reduced Projection need not duplicate the complete governed record. It must not conceal that it is reduced.

## 9. Minimum Ontology and Logic rules

Future machine-semantic profiles must be capable of preserving or explicitly addressing:

- stable identity and version;
- concept, term, instance, subject, and Representation;
- typed relations rather than generic connectivity;
- Context, Scope, Domain, Applicability, authority, and time;
- actual bearing, attribution, assessment, authorization, and record;
- claim, Ground, Evidence, Provenance, uncertainty, disagreement, and defeaters;
- explicit non-entailments;
- open-world, closed-world, explicit-negation, default-negation, or local-closure assumptions;
- contradiction without forced consensus or uncontrolled inference;
- temporal validity, supersession, and retirement;
- defeasible and non-monotonic epistemic revision where applicable;
- asserted, assessed, permitted, required, authoritative, and relied-upon modalities;
- inferential premises, rules, methods, and lineage;
- and declared expressive limits.

No single profile must express every SymK dimension. Its omissions and authority boundary must be explicit.

## 10. Alternatives considered

### A — Treat Knowledge itself as the engineered product

**Rejected:** governance and implementation would appear capable of manufacturing truth and actual bearing.

### B — Use the conventional artifact-centered Knowledge Engineering definition

**Rejected as insufficient:** acquisition, representation, reasoning, and validation remain essential but do not adequately govern authority, consequence, Education, disagreement, practical Knowledge, familiarity, or epistemic non-substitution.

### C — Make `KnowledgeObject` the universal epistemic parent

**Rejected:** it conflates achievement, claim, asset, record, Document, semantic declaration, and operational resource.

### D — Treat a repository or knowledge graph as the primary semantic authority

**Rejected:** storage centrality and structural richness do not confer truth, warrant, completeness, or conceptual ownership.

### E — Use one status or confidence field

**Rejected:** epistemic assessment, governance, applicability, reliance, lifecycle, authority, integrity, and uncertainty answer different questions.

### F — Select a formal Ontology or Logic before completing the semantic obligations

**Rejected:** the formalism would preselect expressible meaning and could silently define Knowledge through representational convenience.

### G — Govern epistemic conditions, lifecycle, typed artifacts, status separation, and declared Projection loss

**Proposed:** preserves the accepted Knowledge boundary, remains actionable for engineering, supports both semantic lanes, and leaves Domain and implementation decisions with their proper owners.

## 11. Consolidated stress-test result

The proposal passes all fourteen mandatory 2.1.4 counterexamples:

1. true claim accepted for the wrong reason;
2. false claim with impeccable provenance and schema validity;
3. richly structured `KnowledgeObject`;
4. practical competence not recoverable from a procedure;
5. incompatible claims in one governed asset;
6. authoritative assertion later defeated;
7. correct generated answer without adequate grounding;
8. valid inference from false or inapplicable premises;
9. emergency reliance on uncertain content;
10. one claim represented in Markdown, JSON, a database, and SharePoint;
11. platform Projection dropping provenance, uncertainty, or supersession;
12. legal and medical document types sharing a universal relation but not a value vocabulary;
13. one Document playing several epistemic roles over time;
14. and an asset superseded or retired without historical rewriting.

It also passes DR-009, DR-010, DR-011, Product Vision, candidate A1/A4, human/machine lane, package-deferral, and derived-project-safety tests.

These are analytical compatibility results. They do not constitute operational validation under 2.7.

## 12. Consequences if accepted

### Product Vision

The governed Knowledge medium becomes operationally intelligible without becoming a stored substance. The value chain retains separate questions of Knowledge, cooperation, capacity, reasoning, answer, evaluation, action, consequence, and learning.

### Candidate Constitution

- A1 should preserve truth, Knowledge, Ground, Evidence, assessment, authority, status, and reliance as distinct.
- A4 should use the complete Epistemic Condition boundary and avoid implying guaranteed achievement.
- Authority and responsibility consequences must be coordinated with 2.1.6.

No axiom is Ratified or revised by DR-012.

### Foundation Papers

The future Knowledge and Knowledge Engineering v0.2 papers must incorporate:

- the artifact and status boundaries;
- the recursive lifecycle;
- plural Knowledge modes;
- Ground/Evidence/Provenance distinctions;
- typed uncertainty and disagreement;
- reliance separation;
- two-lane alignment;
- and declared semantic loss.

The Education and Intelligence papers require related terminology reconciliation. Publication remains assigned to 2.1.10.

### Later 2.1 work

- 2.1.5 owns Cooperation, Education, Training, and formation.
- 2.1.6 owns detailed agency, authority, authorization, responsibility, and accountability.
- 2.1.7 owns Amplified Intelligence and DIA integration.
- 2.1.8 owns reasoning, answer quality, value, cost, and feedback.
- 2.1.9 integrates the complete conceptual baseline.

### 2.5 and 2.9

2.5 shall decide final semantic-package anatomy, identifiers, dependencies, alignment contracts, and foundational artifacts.

2.9 shall select formal profiles, schemas, runtime records, conformance mechanisms, and lowering architecture.

Neither stage may silently reverse the semantic distinctions accepted here.

### Derived projects

Projects inherit the universal distinctions and own Domain-specific evidence rules, standards, Ontologies, Logics, professional authorities, thresholds, vocabularies, and operational Projections.

No current project, package, database, SharePoint structure, AWS representation, API, or code type is accepted or modified through DR-012.

## 13. Non-effects of acceptance

Stage-Acceptance would not:

- Ratify a constitutional norm;
- complete SymK 2.1;
- establish a complete philosophical theory of Knowledge, truth, warrant, evidence, or belief;
- decide every possible Knowledge Bearer;
- settle practical Knowledge/Intelligence overlap;
- make Knowledge storable or transferable as an artifact;
- accept `KnowledgeObject`;
- accept a final status or uncertainty vocabulary;
- establish universal evidence thresholds;
- authorize reliance in any particular Domain or deployment;
- establish detailed agency, authority, or responsibility rules;
- create or migrate a Knowledge semantic package;
- decide whether Knowledge and epistemic-artifact semantics use one package or several;
- accept the current property or Document package scaffolds;
- revise any Foundation Paper or candidate axiom;
- select an Ontology, Logic, schema, serialization, database, API, programming language, or platform;
- modify LexBrain, LibScan, Composer, Osteolab, IaaC, sshConnectivity, SharePoint, AWS, or another derived system;
- grant production records semantic authority;
- or reopen DR-009 through DR-011.

## 14. Preserved dissent and open questions

1. `EpistemicCondition` may be too broad to deserve an independently named governed distinction.
2. Knowledge Engineering may be a foundational discipline or method rather than a foundational concept.
3. The term “Knowledge Engineering” may remain misleading despite the conditions interpretation.
4. `Ground` and `Evidence` may ultimately be roles within one support family.
5. Warrant may remain too theory-dependent for a universal formal representation.
6. Knowledge claim may require separate treatment of content, assertion act, commitment, and attributed proposition.
7. Knowledge asset may be better modeled as a role, graph, collection, or lifecycle boundary than an object class.
8. A qualified engineering-level `KnowledgeObject` may retain interoperability value.
9. Status and uncertainty vocabularies may require stronger Domain ownership.
10. Practical and familiarity Knowledge may require additional primitives.
11. Some artificial, institutional, collective, or hybrid systems may literally bear Knowledge.
12. Semantic packages may themselves act as Knowledge assets without containing Knowledge.
13. The boundary between epistemic assessment and Reliance Authorization remains partly dependent on 2.1.6.
14. Minimum machine-semantic obligations may need consequence-proportional profiles.
15. No formalism has yet demonstrated complete preservation of every required distinction.

## 15. Confidence and reopening conditions

**Confidence:** High in the Knowledge/artifact/status/reliance separation; High in epistemic conditions as the direct engineered object; High in lifecycle recursion; High in status-plane separation; High in Representation/Projection alignment and loss obligations; High in rejecting `KnowledgeObject` as foundational; Medium–High in Knowledge Engineering as a foundational concept; Medium in `EpistemicCondition` as a named distinction; Medium in exact Ground/Evidence/Warrant boundaries; Low–Medium in any surviving use of `KnowledgeObject`.

Reopen or revise if:

- practical or familiarity Knowledge cannot be modeled without artifact reification;
- a valid case requires actual Knowledge to be literally stored or transferred;
- claim, asset, record, and Representation cannot retain coherent identities;
- derived-project testing demonstrates Domain leakage or unusable universal requirements;
- status separation cannot support authoritative action under uncertainty;
- formal profiles cannot preserve the required non-entailments;
- projection-loss controls fail to protect materially affected use;
- 2.1.6 contradicts the authority and reliance distinctions;
- minimum obligations produce disproportionate bureaucracy or concealment;
- or operational consequences reveal systematic false confidence or epistemic harm.
