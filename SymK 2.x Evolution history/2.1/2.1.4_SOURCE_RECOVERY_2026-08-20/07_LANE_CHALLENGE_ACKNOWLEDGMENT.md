Yes—you remember correctly. I overlooked an important earlier conclusion.

The earlier SymK review established that the original two views:

- Human
- Engineering

had evolved in `SYMK-ARCH-001` into three views:

- **Human View**
- **Engineering View**
- **Scientific View**

They were described as different representations of the same governed Knowledge, not three independent bodies of Knowledge.

There is also a separate three-lane program model in the migration plan:

- semantic and constitutional;
- corpus integrity;
- grounding and verification.

These are workstreams, not semantic representations.

The current records therefore contain three different structures that must not be conflated:

| Structure | Elements | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Semantic views | Human, Engineering, Scientific | Different expressions and examinations of governed meaning |
| Current DR-009 semantic lanes | Human-semantic, machine-semantic | Alignment between interpretive meaning and computable formal claims |
| Program lanes | Semantic/constitutional, corpus-integrity, grounding/verification | How the overall evolution program operates |

My Stream D and Proposed DR-012 incorrectly treated the DR-009 two-lane formulation as if it exhausted the representational architecture. It does not. At minimum, the earlier Scientific View has not been explicitly reconciled.

The likely distinction is:

- **Human-semantic view:** meaning, purpose, explanation, distinctions, examples, dissent, interpretation.
- **Scientific view:** hypotheses, observations, methods, evidence, models, uncertainty, reproducibility, counterevidence, empirical adequacy, and conditions of defeat.
- **Engineering/machine view:** identities, types, relationships, constraints, formal semantics, interfaces, computable projections, validation rules, and runtime lowering.

This third view matters enormously in 2.1.4. Without it, “machine-semantic” risks collapsing two different things:

1. a scientific model or evidence-bearing formalization intended to investigate reality;
2. an engineering representation intended to make a system computable or operational.

A statistically fitted model, scientific ontology, measurement instrument, software schema, and production database are not epistemically equivalent simply because machines can process all of them.

Therefore:

> **Proposed DR-012 should not be Stage-Accepted in its present form. It requires a lane/view reconciliation first.**

The corrected next step is to reopen the relevant part of Stream D and determine:

1. whether Human, Scientific, and Engineering should be three governed semantic views;
2. whether “machine-semantic” is a property available to both Scientific and Engineering views rather than a view itself;
3. how evidence moves among the views without transferring authority;
4. how their projections and mismatches are recorded;
5. and how these views relate—without conflation—to the three program lanes.

My present recommendation is:

> **Adopt three governed epistemic-semantic views—Human, Scientific, and Engineering—while treating machine inspectability as a representational capability, not as the complete identity of one opposing lane.**

So your recollection catches a genuine defect in the draft. DR-012 needs revision before approval.
