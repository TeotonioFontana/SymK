## 1. Question and analytical result

> What exactly has Stage 2.1 accepted across DR-009 through DR-017, how can its names and dispositions be normalized without rewriting those decisions, and which collisions, missing objects, undefined dependencies, or contradictions must be carried into the integrated dependency analysis?

Stream A supports the following working answer:

> **The accepted Stage 2.1 system contains one governing method, seventeen Foundational concepts or Foundational relationship/class anchors, a large but consistently routable set of governed supporting distinctions, imported support, derived specializations, Engineering Representations or Projections, excluded interpretations, and deferred matters. DR-017 adds a Stage-Accepted layered identity and organizing-center direction but does not admit an eighteenth Foundational concept.**

> **The accepted system is normalizable without changing a decision. Its stable semantic spine is: structural identity and boundaries; Knowledge and Intelligence attribution; engineering of epistemic conditions; Cooperation and Formation; Responsibility and governance; Amplification and DIA classification; Question, Reasoning, Answer, and Value; and the broader layered SymK identity. The exact accepted decision objects remain authoritative over every compact inventory and working label in this record.**

> **No direct contradiction is established at the normalization level. Twelve candidate integration blockers remain: the unclassified governed-knowledge-undertaking label; an ungoverned cross-package Capability family; repeated claim/assessment/status/record patterns without a common typed interface; incomplete relation-kind vocabulary; generic subject/object and level references without a universal Entity parent; ambiguous organization and lowest-adequate-level criteria; temporal qualification without a shared Time concept; several cross-package homonyms; unresolved dependencies among Foundational anchors; the accepted 2.0 Product Vision wording versus DR-017's broader layered identity; the relationship between responsible Knowledge Engineering and descriptive predicates; and the absence of final package identifiers and formal semantics. These are inputs to Streams B–F, not decisions or proof that the accepted baseline fails.**

Stream A therefore passes its normalization gate provisionally and supplies a controlled inventory to Stream B. It creates no dependency graph, no new or changed disposition, no DR-018 content, and no `2.1-rc.1` baseline.

## 2. Authority and preservation rule

The authority order for this normalization is:

1. the exact Stage-Accepted decision object and its acceptance record;
2. the applicable earlier Stage-Accepted decision where a later decision explicitly retains it;
3. the decision register and concept register as compact control surfaces;
4. accepted analytical lineage needed to interpret boundaries, dissent, confidence, and reopening conditions;
5. this Stream A inventory as a non-authoritative analytical Representation.

Normalization means controlled correspondence, not editorial replacement. In particular:

- a normalized label does not supersede wording in DR-009–017;
- grouping terms into a family does not merge their identities or dispositions;
- capitalization clarifies governed use but does not make ordinary-language occurrences invalid;
- an alias is accepted only as an interpretive pointer, never as a new concept;
- a homonym must retain relation kind, object, level, Scope, Context, Domain, and source;
- an omitted item remains governed by its exact accepted decision and is not silently discarded;
- a later decision may specialize, retain, or constrain an earlier meaning only where its accepted text says so; and
- every possible material conflict must cite both accepted clauses and their reopening conditions before being treated as a decision contradiction.

### 4.2 Foundational non-substitution spine

The seventeen anchors remain pairwise non-identical even when materially related:

- Identity is not identifier, name, version, continuity record, or legal identity.
- Context is not Scope; Scope is not Domain; Domain is not project taxonomy.
- Representation is not its subject, Knowledge, Evidence, status, or implementation.
- Knowledge is not Intelligence, claim, document, data, information, asset, record, acceptance, or Reliance Authorization.
- Intelligence is not performance, score, output, Agency, autonomy, consciousness, moral standing, Authority, or Responsibility.
- Knowledge Engineering is not creation of truth, Knowledge possession, knowledge management, information technology, or one Ontology.
- Cooperation is not coordination, communication, aggregation, compliance, consensus, output, success, consent, legitimacy, or responsible operation.
- Formation is not Learning, Training, Education, adaptation, model update, persistence, improvement, or architectural change.
- Responsibility is not causal Contribution, Agency, control, role, Authority, Authorization, accountability, blame, liability, remedy, or a record.
- Amplification is not assistance, access, augmentation, automation, substitution, replacement, performance, productivity, benefit, Value, or one better Answer.
- DIA is not AI, a tool, product, workflow, organization, Human–AI pair, multi-agent assembly, Knowledge system, certificate, or marketing label.
- Question is not Domain problem, frame, formulation, prompt, request, task, query, test, decision problem, workflow, or record.
- Reasoning is not inference, proof, computation, retrieval, generation, deliberation, judgment, explanation, trace, or output.
- Answer is not response, output, recommendation, decision, action, outcome, consequence, status, or record.
- Value is not quality, utility, usefulness, benefit, preference, objective, price, market success, outcome, score, or optimization result.

## 6. Lexical and capitalization normalization rules

The following conventions are analytical controls for 2.1.9. They do not revise the accepted texts.

1. Use the exact capitalized name for a governed concept where ambiguity is material: Knowledge, Intelligence, Knowledge Engineering, Cooperation, Formation, Responsibility, Amplification, Question, Reasoning, Answer, Value, Identity, Context, Scope, Domain, Representation, Authority, Authorization, Evidence, Feedback, Uptake, Correction, Learning, Power, System, and View.
2. Use **Domain Intelligence Amplifier** on first reference and **DIA** thereafter. Do not use “intelligence amplifier,” “amplifier,” or “AI system” as aliases for DIA.
3. Use **Amplified Intelligence** only for the DR-015 compound attribution; use **Amplification** for the comparative relationship.
4. Use **Complex Question**, not “complex problem,” as the accepted supporting qualification of Question. A Domain problem may be complex in ordinary language without acquiring that governed status.
5. Use **Epistemic Condition** for the DR-012 supporting distinction; “condition” alone must identify its object and owning package.
6. Use **Reliance Authorization** for the DR-012 scoped decision relation; do not shorten it to reliance, acceptance, approval, validity, or status when the authorization relation is intended.
7. Use **Human View**, **Scientific View**, and **Engineering View** for semantic orientations. Use human readability, formal expression, and machine inspectability for capabilities. Use program lane for the three independent program workstreams.
8. Use **Bearer** only as a predicate-relative attribution role. Prefer “Knowledge Bearer,” “Intelligence Bearer,” “amplified Bearer,” “Responsibility bearer,” or another qualified form where confusion is possible.
9. Use **Participant**, **Contributor**, **Agent**, **role occupant**, **authority**, **responsible subject**, **beneficiary**, **affected subject**, **provider**, **user**, **resource**, and **source** as distinct roles; never collapse them into actor.
10. Use **causal Contribution** for the DR-014 causal relation and **Contribution** for the DR-013 cooperative role/relation. If both are possible, qualify the term explicitly.
11. Use **Integration** with a qualifier: formative integration, functional/system integration, view alignment, package integration, or analytical integration. The bare word does not carry one universal predicate.
12. Use **status** only with a type: epistemic status, governance status, membership status, lifecycle status, Authorization status, assessment status, or other declared regime.
13. Use **profile** only with its object: attributed capability profile, Answer-Quality profile, responsible-answer profile, temporal profile, total-cost profile, constrained-value profile, professional-transformation profile, or another declared projection.
14. Use **lifecycle** only with its governed object: epistemic, cooperative relation, Formation, Responsibility, DIA, Amplification claim, Question, Answer, artifact, project, or implementation lifecycle.
15. Use **record** only as a Representation of identified claims, assessments, statuses, decisions, relations, or events. A record is never a generic truth-bearing substitute.
