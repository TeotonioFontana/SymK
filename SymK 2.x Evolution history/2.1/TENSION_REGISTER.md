# SymK 2.1 Tension Register

The thirty Stage-Accepted 2.0 tensions (`SYMK-2X-TN-001` through `SYMK-2X-TN-030`) remain active by reference. 2.1 does not renumber or duplicate them.

## 2.1 tension lenses

| Lens | Primary packages | Inherited concern |
|---|---|---|
| Knowledge and reliance | 2.1.3 / 2.1.4 | Truth, justification, practical competence, operational reliance, uncertainty, and challenge must not collapse into one status |
| Intelligence attribution | 2.1.3 / 2.1.7 | Capability, performance, bearer, emergence, authority, and moral standing must remain distinguishable |
| Representation | 2.1.2 / 2.1.4 | Computability and interoperability may simplify, strengthen, or lose meaning |
| Formation and power | 2.1.5 | Cooperation requires habits, yet formation may become assimilation, coercion, or hidden curriculum |
| Distributed responsibility | 2.1.6 | Agency, causation, authority, authorization, accountability, and liability may diverge among participants |
| Amplification and professional transformation | 2.1.7 | Amplification may include automation, displacement, dependency, deskilling, concentration, and changed formation pathways |
| Responsible value | 2.1.8 | Quality, speed, cost, uncertainty, prevention of harm, and affected-party consequences may conflict |

The next available identifier for a genuinely new tension is `SYMK-2X-TN-031`. A new identifier requires a distinct tension, not a restatement of an inherited one.

## 2.1.2 tension disposition

The Stage-Accepted structural baseline creates no new tension identifier and resolves none of the inherited tensions. Its principal risks remain governed by TN-005 (universal concepts/domain specificity), TN-006 (formal rigor/suppressed dimensions), TN-007 (philosophical rigor/engineering usability), TN-020 (standardization/experimentation), TN-027 (primary owner/overlapping authority), and TN-028 (practice/implementation-defined meaning). The dissent preserved under DR-010 remains capable of reopening an individual disposition through the DR-009 method.
