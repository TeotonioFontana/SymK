## 5. Publication corrections

Two defects were detected and corrected entirely in the projection layer:

### `G-PUB-001` — wide table overflow

The initial typesetting attempt projected dense multi-column tables beyond A4 margins. The final toolchain converts each source row into one stacked labelled record. The first source column becomes the record entry and every remaining source header labels its corresponding cell. Eleven tables are projected this way: three each in Knowledge, Intelligence, and Education and Training; two in Knowledge Engineering.

This transformation preserves every source header and cell value, improves page flow and reading order, and changes no governed Markdown or accepted meaning.

### `G-PUB-002` — incompatible experimental table tagging

The initial experimental LaTeX tagging path conflicted with long-table structures and was rejected. Those PDFs are not controlled outputs. The final Writer export supplies structural tags, language, bookmarks, PDF/UA-1 declaration, embedded fonts, selectable text, and stable A4 rendering without the long-table defect.

No other publication correction was required.
