## 4. Governing semantic effect

Acceptance establishes for continuation of 2.1 that:

1. a prompt, request, task, query, workflow record, or implementation artifact may represent or operationalize a Question but does not automatically establish its Identity, scope, completeness, or meaning;
2. framing, formulation, decomposition, translation, narrowing, and changed answer form require declared Question lineage and may create a new version or Question;
3. complexity is evidenced relative to material interaction, uncertainty, contest, distribution, change, cross-level or cross-jurisdictional dependence, consequence, reflexivity, or non-decomposability, not length, cost, duration, AI use, status, or provider label;
4. Reasoning remains distinct from inference, proof, computation, retrieval, simulation, generation, explanation, trace, fluent presentation, Human review, and output;
5. actual Reasoning, its operations and products, its Representation or trace, the claim or assessment, and governance status remain separate;
6. protected inspectability and proportionate process Evidence are required without a universal demand for verbatim internal chains;
7. Answer remains distinct from response, output, interpretation, recommendation, decision support, approval, decision, Action, outcome, and consequence;
8. partial, plural, conditional, negative, abstaining, or qualified no-answer forms may satisfy a governed answer obligation;
9. Answer Quality preserves dimension-level Evidence, uncertainty, disagreement, non-applicability, failure, distribution, and non-compensable conditions without universal scalar substitution;
10. faster, better, lower total cost, greater Value, favorable outcome, responsible use, Authorization, and compliance remain independently evidenced predicates;
11. total-cost claims expose material cost objects, families, Bearers, boundaries, counterfactuals, horizons, transfers, externalities, distributions, residuals, uncertainty, and exclusions;
12. Value remains plural, claimant- and affected-subject-aware, authority-bounded, temporally and counterfactually scoped, and capable of positive, negative, mixed, conditional, disputed, incomparable, unknown, or inapplicable states;
13. benefit, harm, avoided harm, objective, expected and actual outcome, consequence, side effect, residual effect, causal Contribution, attribution, and Responsibility remain distinct;
14. feedback occurrence, transmission, receipt, interpretation, Uptake, response, Correction, change, Learning, Formation, and improvement do not manufacture one another;
15. Correction preserves prior state, use, consequence, reason, authority, challenge, and lineage instead of overwriting history;
16. Learning continues to require the DR-013 stable-Learner, durable-change, relevant-variation, Evidence, competing-explanation, and lineage boundaries;
17. consequences may return recursively to validation, Responsibility, policy, concept, and constitutional review without automatic promotion or authority;
18. the Human, Scientific, and Engineering Views preserve aligned meaning and declared loss without substituting lived legitimacy, scientific validity, or technical inspectability for one another; and
19. SymK owns the universal semantic minimum while Domain, scientific, professional, organizational, legal, ethical, political, economic, and other competent authorities retain their jurisdictions.
