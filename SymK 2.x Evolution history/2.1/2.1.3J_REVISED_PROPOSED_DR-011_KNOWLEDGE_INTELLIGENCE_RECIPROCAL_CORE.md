## 1. Decision question

> What minimum concepts of Knowledge and Intelligence must SymK govern, what can bear them, how must multidimensional Intelligence and its specializations be distinguished from profiles and Projections, and how should Knowledge and Intelligence be reciprocally related without making either a hidden definition, Representation, performance result, authority status, or automatic implication of the other?

## 2. Revised proposed decision

> **SymK shall govern Knowledge and Intelligence as distinct foundational concepts. Knowledge is a scoped epistemic achievement attributable to a Bearer, in which success concerning what is the case, how to act, or a subject of familiarity is appropriately connected to truth, competence, or encounter rather than to mere luck, assertion, acceptance, or Representation. Intelligence is a substrate-realized, multidimensional capacity of an organized system to integrate relevant resources in producing context-sensitive interpretations or effective responses across materially varying situations, including cases not individually specified in advance.**
>
> **The universal Intelligence concept does not require a particular Bearer to be named. Every actual Intelligence is borne by an organized system. That system occupies the governed Bearer role for the predicate; an Intelligence attribution subsequently identifies it as the proposed Bearer and fallibly claims that the actual bearing relation obtains. Actual bearing, attribution claim, assessment, authority for a use, and attribution record remain distinct.**
>
> **SymK shall distinguish Intelligence, capability dimension, Intelligence specialization, actual capability configuration, attributed capability profile, and score or classification. A specialization constrains the universal Intelligence concept to a declared Domain or governed family of situations and inherits its universal nucleus while declaring the dimensions, relevant variation, evidence, limitations, assessment conditions, semantic authority, and lineage appropriate to that specialization. Specializations may overlap, nest, correlate, and share mechanisms; their names do not create mutually exclusive types or separate foundational concepts. A Bearer's actual configuration is not its attributed profile, and neither is identical to a score, ranking, class, general factor, schema, or other purpose-specific Projection.**
>
> **Knowledge and Intelligence do not definitionally presuppose one another, and an attribution of either does not automatically establish the other. Their reciprocal connections shall be governed as directional, typed, scoped, contingent, and challengeable relationships. Intelligence may enable the pursuit, achievement, interpretation, testing, connection, application, expression, and revision of Knowledge-related work. Knowledge may enable, constrain, scaffold, develop, specialize, and evidence Intelligence through realized Bearers, practices, and Representations. No single symmetric `reciprocal_with` or generic `related_to` relation preserves this meaning.**
>
> **Bearer remains the governed supporting attribution role established by DR-010. Attribution is governed as a supporting relationship family. Knowledge Attribution and Intelligence Attribution use predicate-specific criteria; their records and attributed profiles are Representations rather than the relations, achievements, capacities, or actual configurations themselves.**
>
> **Propositional Knowledge requires truth and an appropriate non-accidental epistemic connection attributable to the Bearer. Practical Knowledge requires competence across a relevant task class. Familiarity Knowledge requires a direct or accumulated epistemically significant encounter. These modes may combine without being reduced to one test. Intelligence is a capacity rather than a performance event. A material attribution must identify an organized proposed Bearer and preserve its stable boundary, actual-bearing claim, capability profile, relevant variation, Domain, Scope, Context, resources, dependencies, failures, alternatives, and temporal conditions. Online learning, agency, autonomy, rationality, consciousness, moral standing, authority, and responsibility are not universal entailments.**
>
> **Knowledge remains the governed epistemic medium in the SymK value chain, while operation and sharing occur through claims, grounds, methods, capabilities, histories, and other Representations. Cooperation may compose different capability configurations but does not automatically create a collective Bearer. Later Amplified Intelligence analysis must evaluate change in an integrated multidimensional capability configuration, including gains, losses, new integration, dependencies, uncertainty, and consequences; additional content, access, output, one better performance, or one higher score does not by itself establish amplification.**
>
> **Every material Knowledge or Intelligence attribution shall preserve, at an appropriate level for its consequence, predicate identity and version; Bearer identity and stable boundary; Domain, Scope, Context, time, and criteria; evidence, failures, alternatives, resources, assistance, and dependencies; source, assessor, authority, intended use, status, uncertainty, challenge, and lineage. Intelligence attributions shall additionally preserve specialization identity and inheritance where applicable, claimed dimensions and their relations, the attributed profile, omitted material dimensions, and the identity and declared loss of any scalar or categorical Projection. Derived projects and relevant semantic authorities own Domain-specific propositions, type schemes, competence standards, specialization content, operational dimensions, profiles, thresholds, tests, role allocations, and technical Projections while preserving this universal minimum.**

## 4. Required distinctions

If accepted, later SymK work must preserve at least:

- Knowledge from knowledge claim, truth alone, grounds alone, asset, Representation, data, information, status, consensus, reliance, and authority;
- propositional Knowledge, practical Knowledge, and familiarity without forcing one mode's criteria onto every other mode;
- Intelligence from substrate, organized System, capability generally, competence, skill, performance, score, participant type, and attribution;
- universal Intelligence from capability dimension, specialization, actual capability configuration, attributed profile, and Projection;
- actual multidimensionality from a universal closed dimension list or an assessment instrument's structure;
- Intelligence specialization from mutually exclusive type, trait, skill, role, status, competence, behavior, or outcome;
- actual configuration from attributed profile, and both from score, class, ranking, benchmark result, or general factor;
- flexible response from persistent adaptation, learning, development, design, and evolution;
- Intelligence from agency, autonomy, rationality, understanding, consciousness, wisdom, moral standing, authority, authorization, and responsibility;
- actual bearing from attribution claim, assessment, adoption for use, and stored record;
- Bearer from source, evidence producer, assessor, authority, actor, represented subject, owner, responsible party, and affected party;
- actual Scope from declared Scope, current Context, Domain, Applicability, and authority;
- epistemic enabling from constitutive dependency, evidence, development, operation, Representation, and authorization;
- cooperation among participant Bearers from a new system-level cooperative Bearer; and
- universal SymK meaning from Domain specialization and engineering Projection.

## 5. Bearer, attribution, and boundary rules

1. **Actual-bearing requirement:** every actual Knowledge or Intelligence instance has a Bearer; recognition and attribution do not create the bearing relation.
2. **Proposed-Bearer rule:** an attribution explicitly identifies the organized system proposed to occupy the Bearer role and may be mistaken.
3. **Explanatory level:** attribute at the lowest organized level that explains the predicate without omitting constitutive integration.
4. **Stable boundary:** keep the success boundary visible when analyzing failure, dependencies, cost, authority, responsibility, and consequence.
5. **Resource/component distinction:** disclose every material dependency; include it in the Bearer only when coupling is sufficiently stable, integrated, and identity-relevant.
6. **Temporal validity:** state which changes in participants, models, tools, data, procedures, or environment preserve, restrict, supersede, or replace the attribution.
7. **Multiple levels:** permit separately evidenced individual, component, collective, institutional, distributed, and hybrid attributions without copying one predicate automatically across levels.
8. **No origin privilege:** biological, artificial, social, or hybrid origin proves or disproves neither predicate by itself.
9. **No role transfer:** capability or Knowledge does not automatically grant authority, permission, responsibility, legal personality, or moral standing.
10. **Profile fallibility:** an attributed profile may omit, misidentify, or misweight dimensions and remains distinct from the actual configuration.

## 6. Multidimensionality and specialization rules

1. **Constitutive multidimensionality:** actual Intelligence has distinguishable, interacting aspects; no single observation, task, or score exhausts the capacity.
2. **No fixed universal inventory:** Stage-Acceptance would not establish one closed list, weighting, hierarchy, or independence relation for dimensions.
3. **Specialization inheritance:** every admitted Intelligence specialization inherits the universal nucleus and explicitly states its constraints.
4. **Authority and Domain:** the appropriate semantic authority owns specialization content; popular or commercial labels supply evidence, not admission.
5. **Non-exclusivity:** specializations may overlap, nest, correlate, and share dimensions or mechanisms.
6. **Bearer-specific configuration:** a particular organized Bearer may realize several specializations and an uneven configuration within and across them.
7. **Profile separation:** a profile is a governed Representation of a claimed actual configuration, not the configuration itself.
8. **Projection separation:** every score, class, ranking, benchmark, and general factor declares its purpose, source dimensions, transformation, omissions, and semantic loss.
9. **No example admission:** emotional, social, logical-mathematical, and other named constructs remain candidates until their authority, construct boundaries, evidence, and inheritance are reviewed.

## 7. Reciprocal relation rules

### Intelligence toward Knowledge

Permitted universal relation types include `pursues`, `contributes_to_achievement_of`, `interprets`, `tests`, `challenges`, `connects`, `applies`, `expresses_or_projects`, and `revises_contribution_or_representation`, each qualified by capability profile, role, Domain, Scope, Context, evidence, and authority where relevant.

### Knowledge toward Intelligence

Permitted universal relation types include `enables`, `scaffolds`, `constrains`, `develops`, `specializes`, `evidences`, and `persists_as_reusable_support`, each mediated by an actual Bearer, process, resource, practice, or Representation.

### Prohibited universal implications

```text
Knowledge attribution       -/-> Intelligence attribution
Intelligence attribution    -/-> Knowledge attribution
capability dimension        -/-> complete Intelligence
specialization label        -/-> specialization instance
attributed profile          -/-> actual configuration
score or classification     -/-> Intelligence
stored representation       -/-> Knowledge or Intelligence
successful performance      -/-> Knowledge or Intelligence
Knowledge or Intelligence   -/-> authority, authorization,
                                 responsibility, consciousness,
                                 legal personality, or moral standing
participant cooperation     -/-> collective Intelligence Bearer
additional content or score -/-> Amplified Intelligence
```

The relation between practical Knowledge and Intelligence remains a preserved overlap question. Some cases may satisfy both predicates through competence across variation; no universal identity or implication is accepted by this proposal.

## 8. Alternatives considered

### A — Retain the first DR-011 proposal unchanged

**Rejected:** it correctly uses profiles and rejects scalar identity but leaves actual multidimensionality, specialization anatomy, actual configuration, and profile/Projection levels insufficiently governed.

### B — Treat multidimensionality as assessment structure only

**Rejected:** this permits the actual capacity to remain an unexplained scalar while all complexity is assigned to instruments, hiding uneven strengths, trade-offs, integration, and failure.

### C — Adopt a universal list or taxonomy of Intelligence types

**Rejected:** no reviewed evidence supports one substrate- and Domain-independent closed inventory; mutually exclusive types would suppress overlap, hierarchy, covariance, and specialization.

### D — Admit emotional, social, and logical-mathematical Intelligence directly

**Rejected:** the examples expose the need for a specialization mechanism but do not by themselves establish construct validity, ownership, or universal admission.

### E — Define Intelligence through one general factor, score, benchmark, or formal model

**Rejected:** a purpose-specific Projection would acquire semantic authority, population and instrument assumptions would become universal, and material dimensions and failure could disappear.

### F — Treat every capability dimension as a separate Intelligence

**Rejected:** this fragments the organized capacity, confuses aspect with specialization, and makes naming sufficient for foundational proliferation.

### G — Reconciled multidimensional nucleus plus governed specialization and Projection levels

**Proposed:** preserves the independent anchor, survives the old and new counterexamples, strengthens cooperation and amplification tests, and remains expressible in both semantic lanes without choosing a universal dimension list or implementation.

## 10. Consequences if accepted

### Product Vision

The value chain remains intact. `Intelligences` refers to organized systems for which qualified capability attributions are supportable. Cooperation may compose different capability configurations but does not automatically create another Bearer. Amplified Intelligence must later be evaluated as change in an integrated multidimensional capacity rather than as more content, access, output, speed, or one higher score.

### Candidate Constitution

- A1 must distinguish Knowledge from attribution, asset, status, and reliance and avoid defining warrant solely through authorized reliance.
- A2 must distinguish actual capability and configuration from attribution, profile, score, role, authority, authorization, responsibility, and standing.
- A4 must qualify epistemic activities by capability profile and role and explain that expression, preservation, sharing, and revision operate through Representations and governed processes.

No axiom is revised or Ratified by DR-011.

### Foundation Papers

The future v0.2 Intelligence paper must use the revised multidimensional anchor, Bearer separation, specialization rule, configuration/profile/Projection distinctions, typed reciprocity, and preserved dissent. The H review draft remains early evidence rather than the publication source. Knowledge Engineering must preserve the same representation levels. Education and cooperation work must examine formation and complementary profiles. Publication remains 2.1.10 after 2.1.4–2.1.9 reconciliation.

### Derived projects and engineering

Derived projects inherit the universal meanings and own authorized Domain specializations, operational dimensions, thresholds, evidence standards, assessment methods, and Projections. A legal `DocumentType` and a medical `DocumentType` may specialize one qualified classification relationship without sharing a value scheme. Neutral metadata stores preserve full assertions; SharePoint, AWS, filesystems, search indexes, MySQL JSON, APIs, code types, Intelligence scores, and type labels are declared Projections. No current semantic package, schema, or runtime assertion is promoted by acceptance.

## 12. Preserved dissent and open questions

1. Knowledge may be better understood as a standing state or relation than an `achievement` in some modes.
2. The anti-luck phrase `appropriately connected` remains underdetermined until mode- and Domain-specific criteria are supplied.
3. Belief, epistemic commitment, uptake, or another condition may be required for propositional Knowledge depending on Bearer class.
4. Every actual Intelligence may necessarily possess at least practical Knowledge even though cross-attribution is not licensed from current evidence.
5. Every actual Knowledge Bearer may require a minimal Intelligence even though Knowledge can be independently defined.
6. The threshold separating capability, competence, practical Knowledge, narrow Intelligence, and general Intelligence remains contestable.
7. `Integration`, `materially varying`, and `not individually specified in advance` require Domain-operational criteria.
8. Institutions, collectives, distributed systems, and artificial systems may literally bear the predicates or may warrant only explanatory shorthand.
9. Understanding may be a Knowledge mode, Intelligence dimension, both, or a distinct concept.
10. `Knowledge as medium` may retain a misleading container implication unless v0.2 explanatory text is precise.
11. Actual Scope can exceed or differ from declared Scope and may remain partly unknowable.
12. The lowest explanatory Bearer rule may require revision for multi-level emergence and distributed causation.
13. `Multidimensional` may name a universal ontological condition or the minimum unavoidable structure of a useful capacity account; the distinction remains open.
14. Which dimensions, if any, belong in a universal semantic inventory remains unresolved.
15. Dimension identity may vary across substrates, Domains, cultures, and assessment traditions.
16. A higher-order or general factor may reflect real organization, a bounded statistical regularity, an assessment artifact, or some combination.
17. The admission threshold and semantic authority for an Intelligence specialization remain to be operationalized.
18. Specialization overlap, nesting, covariance, inheritance, and conflict require later package mechanics.
19. Cross-profile comparison can silently import one Domain's priorities or one population's instrument.
20. Amplification may involve gains and losses across dimensions; 2.1.7 must decide which changes qualify under governed values and consequences.

## 13. Confidence and reopening conditions

**Confidence:** High in the need for two distinct foundational concepts; High in the separation of bearing, claim, assessment, authority, record, actual configuration, attributed profile, and Projection; High in performance/Representation/status/origin non-substitution; High in directional typed reciprocity; Medium-High in the revised Intelligence wording and multidimensional nucleus; Medium in specialization admission, exact dimension identity, practical-Knowledge overlap, thresholds, and collective/artificial literal bearing.

Reopen or revise if:

- 2.1.4 cannot preserve Knowledge, attribution, asset, status, and reliance without contradiction;
- practical Knowledge and Intelligence prove inseparable across the relevant counterexamples;
- multidimensionality cannot be distinguished from assessment structure or adds no challengeable semantic content;
- every viable Intelligence requires a fixed universal dimension inventory that this proposal excludes;
- specialization inheritance cannot prevent Domain leakage or semantic duplication;
- cross-substrate or cross-Domain evidence makes dimension identity incoherent;
- simple adaptive systems satisfy the Intelligence wording without the intended organized capacity;
- fixed capable systems are wrongly excluded by the variation condition;
- multi-level attribution cannot be expressed without contradictory Bearers;
- 2.1.6 requires agency or normative responsiveness for every SymK Intelligence participant;
- 2.1.7 cannot distinguish amplification from accumulated content, access, one score, or performance, or cannot govern dimensional trade-offs;
- 2.5 cannot preserve the required levels and relationships without semantic duplication or loss; or
- cross-domain evidence shows the universal minimum to be unusable or harmful.
