## SYMK-2X-DR-009 — Foundational concept governance method

- **Stage/status:** SymK 2.1.1 / Stage-Accepted for continuation of 2.1.
- **Question:** By what method will 2.1 decide that a concept or distinction belongs to SymK, and how will meaning remain stable across human explanation, machine-semantic expression, project specialization, and implementation?
- **Historical formulations:** ontology-first Primitive Evaluation; current `FC-*` catalog and PRR attacks; three-layer concept specification; implementation-driven knowledge primitives; accepted 2.0 two-axis ownership and projection model.
- **Sources and evidence:** `SYMK-2X-EV-002`–`005`, `SYMK-2X-EV-033`, `SYMK-2X-EV-067`–`074`; DR-001–DR-008.
- **Alternatives:** continue primitive-first evaluation; natural-language glossary; formal-language-first foundation; project-local definitions; jurisdiction-first integrated method.
- **Adopted position:** The complete statement in `2.1.1_Stage_Frame_and_Concept_Governance.md`, Section 17.
- **Reasoning:** SymK needs concepts because of its accepted identity and constitutional work, not because every concept is metaphysically primitive. Authority, jurisdiction, counterexamples, dependencies, representations, and project evidence must be governed together without allowing a format or historical status to preempt meaning.
- **Rejected or deferred alternatives:** Alternatives A–D are rejected as sole methods; their useful elements remain subordinate techniques. Final package anatomy, formal languages, version pinning, and lowering remain deferred.
- **Counterexamples and tests:** Section 14 of the 2.1.1 analysis.
- **Canonical consequences:** None; acceptance authorizes the method and later analysis, not canonical migration.
- **Foundation Paper consequences:** Record impacts throughout 2.1; distinguish wider inquiry from accepted semantic baseline; preserve v0.1; publication remains 2.1.10.
- **Derived-project consequences:** Projects later receive stable parent meanings and explicit specialization/projection duties while retaining domain Ontology and Logic authority.
- **Dissent and confidence:** Six preserved concerns in Section 19; confidence Medium-High for use as a review method, lower for final package and formal-alignment mechanics.
- **Authority/date:** Project steward, 18 August 2026.
- **Lineage:** Depends on DR-001–DR-008; governs 2.1.2–2.1.9 and supplies method constraints to 2.2 and 2.5.

## SYMK-2X-DR-010 — Minimum structural semantic kernel

- **Stage/status:** SymK 2.1.2 / Stage-Accepted for continuation of 2.1.
- **Question:** Which concepts and distinctions are required by the accepted SymK identity, and which apparent primitives belong instead to project Ontology, formal representation, or implementation?
- **Historical formulations:** Entity-first primitive sequence; Relationship as first-class Entity; Identity as characteristics enabling unique recognition; Context and Applicability placeholders; mandatory human/semantic/reference engineering models.
- **Sources and evidence:** `SYMK-2X-EV-002`–`005`, `SYMK-2X-EV-033`, `SYMK-2X-EV-069`–`080`; DR-001–DR-009.
- **Alternatives:** retain Entity/Relationship as universal primitives; admit every cross-cutting term; defer all structure; adopt a selective minimum semantic kernel.
- **Adopted position:** The complete statement in `2.1.2_Concept_Inventory_and_Structural_Boundaries.md`, Section 14.
- **Reasoning:** Identity, Context, Scope, Domain, and Representation are independently necessary to the 2.0 identity and later concepts. The other candidates are more accurately governed as relationship types, attribution roles, imported vocabulary, or engineering meta-model choices without losing their importance.
- **Rejected or deferred alternatives:** Alternatives A–C are rejected as the governing structure. Final package assignment, formal semantics, versioning, and domain specializations remain deferred.
- **Counterexamples and tests:** Sections 11–12 of the 2.1.2 analysis.
- **Canonical consequences:** Acceptance establishes a program semantic baseline but does not migrate, rename, or promote current `FC-*` artifacts.
- **Foundation Paper consequences:** Align Context, Scope, Domain, Identity, Representation, Projection, Bearer, and System language across all v0.2 papers; remove Entity-first assumptions from philosophical definitions.
- **Derived-project consequences:** Projects inherit the minimum meanings while owning domain Context dimensions, Scope rules, Domain Ontology and Logic, identifiers, projections, representations, and concrete system boundaries.
- **Dissent and confidence:** Eight preserved concerns in Section 16; confidence High for the distinctions, Medium for standalone foundational-package classification.
- **Authority/date:** Project steward, 18 August 2026; approval evidence `SYMK-2X-EV-081`, closure instruction `SYMK-2X-EV-082`.
- **Lineage:** Depends on Stage-Accepted DR-009; governs structural usage in 2.1.3–2.1.9 and informs 2.5 reconstruction.

## SYMK-2X-DR-011 — Knowledge, Intelligence, and their reciprocal core

- **Stage/status:** SymK 2.1.3 / Stage-Accepted for continuation of 2.1; not constitutionally Ratified.
- **Question:** What minimum concepts of Knowledge and Intelligence must SymK govern, what can bear them, how must multidimensional Intelligence be distinguished from specialization, profile, and Projection, and how should Knowledge and Intelligence be reciprocally related without making either a hidden definition, Representation, performance result, authority status, or automatic implication of the other?
- **Historical formulations:** Knowledge as epistemic achievement borne by an Intelligence and suitable for responsible reliance; Intelligence as substrate-realized organized capacity integrating Knowledge with adaptation; Knowledge as shared medium; participant-type and artifact-centered engineering usages.
- **Sources and evidence:** `SYMK-2X-EV-002`–`005`, `SYMK-2X-EV-073`, `SYMK-2X-EV-078`, and `SYMK-2X-EV-086`–`097`; DR-001–DR-010. The G/H Foundation Paper review artifacts supply challenge evidence only; their conclusions enter authority solely through accepted DR-011.
- **Alternatives:** retain both v0.1 definitions; define Knowledge through Intelligence; define Intelligence through Knowledge/performance; delegate both to projects; select a scalar or formal model; retain the first proposal unchanged; treat multidimensionality as assessment structure only; adopt a closed type taxonomy; admit named specializations directly; fragment every dimension into a separate Intelligence; or use the reconciled multidimensional nucleus with governed specialization and Projection levels.
- **Adopted position:** The complete statement in `2.1.3J_REVISED_PROPOSED_DR-011_KNOWLEDGE_INTELLIGENCE_RECIPROCAL_CORE.md`, Section 2, accepted subject to every stated non-effect, dissent item, later dependency, and reopening condition. `2.1.3F` remains preserved as first-proposal lineage.
- **Reasoning:** The independent anchors eliminate hidden circularity; the common attribution model prevents claims and records from creating predicates; the reconciliation distinguishes the universal concept, actual multidimensional capacity, specialization, actual configuration, attributed profile, and purpose-specific Projection; typed reciprocal relations preserve the Product Vision value chain; the combined system passes all original mandatory counterexamples, ten additional multidimensional/specialization cases, cross-domain translation, candidate-axiom, predecessor, jurisdiction, and two-lane tests.
- **Rejected or deferred alternatives:** Alternatives A–F in the revised proposal are rejected as governing models. Practical-Knowledge overlap, understanding, literal collective/artificial bearing, thresholds, universal dimension inventory, specialization admission, general-factor interpretation, cross-profile comparison, responsibility, amplification trade-offs, package form, and operational evidence remain governed remainders.
- **Counterexamples and tests:** `2.1.3E_CONSOLIDATED_STRESS_TEST_AND_IMPACT_ANALYSIS.md`, `2.1.3I_MULTIDIMENSIONAL_INTELLIGENCE_RECONCILIATION_ANALYSIS.md`, and revised proposal Section 9.
- **Canonical consequences:** Acceptance establishes provisional program semantics for continuation of 2.1 only. It does not migrate a canonical concept package or Foundation Paper; final package reconstruction remains 2.5 and publication remains 2.1.10.
- **Foundation Paper consequences:** Replace cross-definition with independent anchors and typed reciprocity; use the multidimensional definition and Bearer separation; distinguish dimension, specialization, actual configuration, attributed profile, and Projection; preserve v0.1 and the G/H review lineage; revise and visually verify governed v0.2 only in 2.1.10.
- **Derived-project consequences:** Projects inherit the minimum and own authorized Domain propositions, schemes, competence standards, specialization content, operational dimensions, profiles, thresholds, tests, roles, and technical Projections. Existing packages and runtime structures receive no authority automatically.
- **Dissent and confidence:** Twenty preserved questions in revised proposal Section 12; confidence High in distinct concepts, attribution/configuration/profile/Projection separation, non-substitution, and typed reciprocity; lower in exact dimension identity, specialization admission, thresholds, overlap, comparison, amplification trade-offs, and literal non-individual bearing.
- **Authority/date:** Project steward, 20 August 2026; acceptance evidence `SYMK-2X-EV-097`; acceptance record `2.1.3_ACCEPTANCE_RECORD_2026-08-20.md`.
- **Lineage:** Depends on Stage-Accepted DR-009 and DR-010 and accepted M0; governs 2.1.4–2.1.9 and informs 2.2, 2.5, 2.7, and 2.9 while preserving all declared reopening conditions.

## SYMK-2X-DR-012 — Knowledge Engineering, Epistemic Conditions, and Three-View Representation

- **Stage/status:** SymK 2.1.4 / Stage-Accepted for continuation of 2.1; not constitutionally Ratified.
- **Question:** What does SymK engineer when it cannot engineer truth or Knowledge directly, which epistemic distinctions and lifecycle obligations must it govern, and what must Human, Scientific, and Engineering views preserve across Representation and Projection?
- **Historical formulations:** Knowledge Engineering as extraction, storage, information management, or direct Knowledge production; `KnowledgeObject` as a universal container; DR-009 human/machine minimum interpreted as exhaustive lanes; proposed property/document packages and platform runtime forms.
- **Sources and evidence:** `SYMK-2X-EV-002`–`005`, `073`, `078`, and `098`–`106`; DR-001–DR-011. `2.1.4_SOURCE_RECOVERY_2026-08-20/` preserves the exact selected Streams B–E, first proposal, project-steward challenge, reconciliation, complete revised proposal response, and acceptance. 2.1.4F and 2.1.4G remain the earlier compressed repository reconstruction.
- **Alternatives:** engineer Knowledge/truth directly; reduce the field to data/information/software engineering; admit foundational `KnowledgeObject`; exactly two exhaustive lanes; formal-language-first authority; delegate all epistemic semantics to projects; or adopt the reconciled lifecycle system with Human, Scientific, and Engineering views.
- **Adopted position:** The complete statement in `2.1.4H_RECOVERED_FULL_REVISED_PROPOSED_DR-012_KNOWLEDGE_ENGINEERING_EPISTEMIC_CONDITIONS_AND_THREE_VIEW_REPRESENTATION.md`, accepted subject to every non-effect, dissent item, dependency, confidence qualification, and reopening condition. `2.1.4_DR-012_SOURCE_RECOVERY_AND_CORRECTIVE_ACCEPTANCE_RECORD_2026-08-20.md` corrects the locator without changing the decision. The first proposal remains superseded; 2.1.4G is retained as a condensed reconstruction.
- **Reasoning:** Engineering acts on conditions and artifacts without manufacturing epistemic achievement; the recursive lifecycle prevents pipeline and status substitution; typed distinctions prevent claims, Evidence, Provenance, acceptance, or authorization from becoming truth or Knowledge; the three-view reconciliation preserves scientific inquiry omitted by an exhaustive two-lane reading; triangular alignment and declared loss prevent silent drift.
- **Rejected or deferred alternatives:** Alternatives A–F in the revised proposal are rejected as governing models. Exact condition taxonomy, evidence/assessment schemes, view extension, qualified `KnowledgeObject`, package distribution, formal languages, runtime structures, challenge rights, responsibility, and operational grounding remain governed remainders.
- **Counterexamples and tests:** Recovered original Stream E, `2.1.4E_CONSOLIDATED_STRESS_TEST_AND_IMPACT_ANALYSIS.md`, and complete revised proposal Section 16.
- **Canonical consequences:** Provisional program semantics only. No package, identifier, Foundation Paper, axiom, schema, formalism, implementation, or derived-project artifact is created or migrated.
- **Foundation Paper consequences:** Explain epistemic conditions and lifecycle; distinguish Knowledge from claims, artifacts, statuses, and reliance; replace container language; preserve uncertainty and dissent; explain three-view alignment. Publication remains 2.1.10.
- **Derived-project consequences:** Projects inherit the minimum and own authorized Domain methods, evidence standards, Ontologies, Logics, assessment schemes, thresholds, artifacts, and operational conditions. Grounding may return counterevidence.
- **Dissent and confidence:** Twenty questions in complete revised proposal Section 19; confidence High in anti-reification, status separation, lifecycle, and three-view coordination; lower in view naming/sufficiency, package distribution, formal validation coverage, and qualified `KnowledgeObject` usefulness.
- **Authority/date:** Project steward, 20 August 2026; acceptance evidence `SYMK-2X-EV-105`; source-recovery correction evidence `SYMK-2X-EV-106`; current locator record `2.1.4_DR-012_SOURCE_RECOVERY_AND_CORRECTIVE_ACCEPTANCE_RECORD_2026-08-20.md`.
- **Lineage:** Depends on Stage-Accepted DR-009 through DR-011; governs 2.1.5–2.1.9 and informs 2.2, 2.5, 2.7, and 2.9 while preserving all reopening conditions.
