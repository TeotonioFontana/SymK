## Publication model

The Markdown edition is the governed source. The PDF is a publication projection regenerated from that source. Existing v0.1 Markdown and PDF editions remain preserved; substantive revision creates v0.2 rather than silently overwriting history.

Foundation Papers remain epistemic sources and human communication. They do not become constitutional norms or final `FC-*` specifications through publication.

## 2.1.10 publication gates

Each v0.2 paper must pass:

1. conceptual consistency with the provisional 2.1 baseline;
2. consistency with the Stage-Accepted 2.0 Product Vision;
3. cross-paper terminology review;
4. preservation of v0.1 lineage and material dissent;
5. source and quotation integrity review;
6. human-readability review for a newcomer audience;
7. explicit authority and status review;
8. Markdown-to-PDF correspondence and visual inspection.
