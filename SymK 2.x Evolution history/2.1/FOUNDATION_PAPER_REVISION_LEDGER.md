## Publication model

The Markdown edition is the governed source. The PDF is a publication projection regenerated from that source. Existing v0.1 Markdown and PDF editions remain preserved; substantive revision creates v0.2 rather than silently overwriting history.

Foundation Papers remain epistemic sources and human communication. They do not become constitutional norms or final `FC-*` specifications through publication.

## Authorized early review artifact

On 20 August 2026, after reviewing the 2.1.3 Intelligence wording recommendations, the project steward accepted those recommendations and requested an updated paper. The resulting `2.1.3G_INTELLIGENCE_FOUNDATION_PAPER_V0.2_REVIEW_DRAFT.md` is an early, non-canonical synthesis maintained in the 2.1 evolution history.

This exception permits review and maturation before the planned publication step; it does not revise or replace the governed v0.1 Markdown/PDF pair, accept Proposed DR-011, establish the final v0.2 wording, open 2.1.10, or waive reconciliation through 2.1.4–2.1.9, cross-paper review, human review, PDF verification, and publication acceptance.

After preserving 2.1.3G, the project steward challenged the treatment of multidimensional Intelligence and requested a new paper version. The resulting `2.1.3H_INTELLIGENCE_FOUNDATION_PAPER_V0.2_REVIEW_DRAFT_R2.md` makes multidimensionality constitutive, distinguishes dimension, specialization, actual capability configuration, attributed profile, and Projection, treats emotional, social, and logical-mathematical Intelligence as candidate overlapping specializations, and reframes cooperation and amplification through complementary and changing profiles. Its visually verified 39-page A4 PDF review projection is maintained beside the Markdown source. Both artifacts have the same early-review boundaries and do not supersede v0.1, accept the candidates they discuss, or constitute the 2.1.10 publication.
