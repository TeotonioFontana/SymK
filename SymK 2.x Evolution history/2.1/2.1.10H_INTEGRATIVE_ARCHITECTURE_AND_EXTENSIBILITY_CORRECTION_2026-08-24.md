## 1. Purpose

This corrective closure records a coordinated explanatory clarification across the four Foundation Papers and the Reader Orientation and Shared Glossary. It answers how the papers relate without converting that relation into constitutional rank, a closed taxonomy or a claim that the current paper set exhausts SymK.

## 2. Corrected architecture

The active Human-review set now states consistently that:

- **Knowledge Engineering is the integrative center.** It is the discipline through which SymK designs, constructs, governs, evaluates and evolves epistemic conditions.
- **Knowledge is constitutive.** It names the scoped epistemic achievement and shared medium that the engineering work must preserve without reducing it to information, storage, consensus or approval.
- **Intelligence is constitutive.** It names the capacities of qualified Bearers that participate in the work without receiving Authority, rank or moral status merely from capability.
- **Formation is constitutive.** It names comparatively durable change in people, systems and organizations that can matter to the undertaking without being presumed good, educational or successful.
- **Education and Training are conditional formative practices.** They matter when deliberately shaping participants or capabilities, but neither is a universal prerequisite of every SymK undertaking.

This is a functional architecture of purpose. It does not create a hierarchy of constitutional authority among the concepts and does not change their accepted dispositions.

## 3. Governed extensibility

The architecture remains deliberately open. A future supporting concept may be admitted when evidence-controlled governance shows that it is needed to preserve a material distinction, relation, boundary or failure mode. Admission does not make a concept Foundational by convenience, repetition or implementation reuse. The concept must retain bounded meaning, lineage, jurisdiction and non-substitution controls.

This clarification therefore leaves room for future supporting concepts while preventing an informal expansion of the accepted Foundational inventory.
