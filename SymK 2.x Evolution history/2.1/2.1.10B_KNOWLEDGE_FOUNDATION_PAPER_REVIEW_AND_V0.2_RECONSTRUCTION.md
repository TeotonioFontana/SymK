## 2. Result

**Yes, provisionally.** The Stream A manifest and the complete Knowledge review queue verify. All 119 `K-CLM-*` review units were dispositioned before the v0.2 draft was constructed. Eighteen bibliography entries, twelve inline citation groups and all sixteen source links were checked. Seventeen bibliography entries verify without a material bibliographic correction. The Quillian entry requires correction from “Semantic Networks” to the source title “Semantic Memory”; that correction is implemented in v0.2. No externally attributed verbatim quotation occurs in the paper. The displayed JTB formula is a standard analytical formulation and is identified as such rather than presented as a quotation.

The v0.2 draft retains the v0.1 paper's strongest contributions: epistemic humility; the plural propositional, practical and familiarity modes; the distinction between epistemology and knowledge representation; the comparative formalism survey; the claim/Ground/Evidence/status/artifact separations; challengeability; lineage; typed uncertainty; negative results; and anti-universalization.

It reconstructs the material conflicts and ambiguities identified below. In particular, it:

- replaces “Cooperation is the objective” with the accepted branching, defeasible and recursive value chain;
- uses the exact Stage-Accepted Bearer-relative Knowledge minimum rather than defining Knowledge through an Intelligence or cooperative system;
- separates Knowledge from responsible reliance and Reliance Authorization;
- replaces the monolithic `KnowledgeObject` tree with a non-container review envelope whose constituent roles remain explicit;
- explains Human, Scientific and Engineering Views as coordinated views rather than fixed human/semantic/machine projection classes;
- replaces the linear status ladder with the accepted recursive lifecycle;
- converts K-01–K-12 from proposed constitutional commitments into explanatory obligations traceable to DR-009–018, without axiom status;
- separates Evidence challenge from competent artifact or authority-source amendment procedure;
- treats affected-subject standing/challenge as an unresolved locus rather than a settled authority kind or automatic veto/remedy;
- preserves the eleven typed authority kinds without allocating actual authority;
- maps the eight v0.1 open questions to accepted answers, preserved dissent and downstream remainders; and
- replaces the Knowledge-asset admission gate with a non-certifying review envelope aligned to the seventeen anchors, Three Views, typed authority and consequence return.

The v0.1 Markdown and PDF remain unchanged. No v0.2 PDF is created. No Human review, cross-paper integration, accessibility pass, publication acceptance, Stream C, 2.1.11 opening, constitutional selection, Ratification or commit follows.
