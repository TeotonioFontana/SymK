2. **Dependency test:** every constitutive, necessary, enabling, constraining, evidentiary, governance, lifecycle, and realization dependency is typed and directionally coherent.
3. **Reciprocity test:** reciprocal relations avoid circular definition, self-validation, and hidden priority.
4. **Bearer/object test:** capability, Knowledge, Intelligence, Agency, Authority, Responsibility, Value, cost, benefit, harm, outcome, consequence, feedback, and Learning claims identify the adequate Bearer, object, subject, or level.
5. **Scope/Context/Domain test:** no concept or relation silently expands across jurisdictions, purposes, time, or Domain boundaries.
6. **Non-substitution test:** all accepted prohibited entailments and concept/status/Representation/implementation boundaries survive cross-cluster combination.
7. **Value-chain test:** the complete Product Vision chain remains branching, defeasible, revisable, and non-automatic.
8. **Identity-direction test:** historical protocol, DIA orientation, broader Knowledge Engineering framework, governed-undertaking center, and bounded jurisdiction coexist without contradiction or mission erasure.
9. **Three-View test:** Human meaning and standing, Scientific construct/evidence validity, and Engineering inspectability/realizability remain aligned without substitution.
10. **Authority/Responsibility test:** no semantic, epistemic, technical, organizational, legal, or implementation relation manufactures Authority, Authorization, Responsibility, standing, consent, or remedy.
11. **Tension and dissent test:** no integration edge silently resolves a preserved tension, dissent question, uncertainty, or reopening condition.
12. **Deferred-question test:** every accepted universal component and every downstream remainder remain correctly separated and routed.
13. **Constitutional-readiness test:** A0–A4, A6, J1, and the conflict/challenge protocol can be evaluated without treating them as accepted or forcing unresolved semantics.
14. **Foundation-Paper impact test:** every accepted concept and identity consequence has a complete cross-paper revision route without revising the papers.
15. **Derived-project translation test:** LexBrain, sshConnectivity, IaaC, and materially different cases can map to the universal system without project terms becoming universal law.
16. **Artifact test:** the candidate remains usable across explanatory, semantic, and future formal artifacts without allowing one Representation to own meaning.
17. **Jurisdiction test:** SymK's universal semantic minimum remains bounded against Domain, scientific, professional, organizational, legal, ethical, economic, educational, political, and implementation authority.
18. **Migration test:** every affected canonical, historical, review, package, publication, project, and implementation artifact receives a later action or explicit non-effect.

Failure of a test must identify whether the problem is an integration defect, a representational defect, a genuine accepted-decision contradiction, missing evidence, unresolved jurisdiction, or downstream implementation detail.

## 7. Principal analytical hazards

- **Graph-as-ontology reification:** treating the working dependency graph as the final universal Ontology or package architecture.
- **Circularity concealment:** renaming a cycle, reciprocal relation, or mutual enabling condition as a definition without an external anchor.
- **Universal-node inflation:** promoting a useful supporting relation, profile, role, undertaking label, or workflow element into a Foundational concept because it appears often.
- **Edge ambiguity:** recording “depends on,” “uses,” “supports,” “governs,” or “contributes to” without relation kind, direction, Scope, Context, or evidence.
- **Bearer disappearance:** attributing Knowledge, Intelligence, Reasoning, Agency, Authority, Responsibility, Value, cost, Learning, or consequence to “the system” without an adequate level.
- **Property transfer:** moving capability, status, Knowledge, value, Authority, Responsibility, or evidence among participants, components, wholes, providers, records, predecessors, or successors.
- **Value-chain linearization:** converting the branching, recursive, defeasible chain into one mandatory workflow or guaranteed production function.
- **DIA exhaustiveness:** treating every legitimate SymK-governed undertaking as a DIA or requiring DIA membership before SymK concepts apply.
- **Framework expansion:** using DR-017 to turn SymK into all of Knowledge Engineering, Intelligence theory, epistemology, ethics, governance, law, organizations, education, or social order.
- **Actor re-centering:** replacing Human centrality with AI, model, collective, institution, System, graph, or workflow sovereignty.
- **Human erasure:** interpreting non-anthropocentrism as equal capability, status, Authority, Responsibility, rights, or moral standing.
- **View collapse:** treating human intelligibility, scientific validity, or engineering precision as sufficient for the other Views.
- **Formal preemption:** allowing graph, schema, logic, identifier, or code convenience to decide semantic distinctions assigned to 2.5 or 2.9.
- **Metric/proxy capture:** using availability of measurements to alter the accepted Question, quality, cost, Value, outcome, or Learning concepts.
- **Authority laundering:** treating canonical location, integration completeness, expert agreement, or project adoption as constitutional, legal, professional, or operational authority.
- **Tension burial:** omitting dissent, reopening conditions, negative cases, unknowns, or deferred remainders to make the baseline appear complete.
- **Paper pre-revision:** drafting v0.2 language before the integrated baseline and revision plan pass their gates.
- **Premature release-candidate labeling:** creating `2.1-rc.1` before all integration tests and the complete DR-018 proposal checkpoint pass.

## 10. Mandatory cross-package counterexample set

At minimum, integration must explain:

1. a Human and an artificial component each possess relevant Intelligence, but their aggregation lacks whole-level Intelligence, Cooperation, or Reasoning;
2. a configured System validly bears amplified capability while Authority and Responsibility remain with different external subjects;
3. governed Knowledge exists without a current Answer, and an Answer exists without becoming Knowledge;
4. a participant contributes Evidence without becoming a Knowledge Bearer, reasoner, decision authority, or responsible subject for the whole undertaking;
5. valid Reasoning produces a poor Answer because the Question version or answer obligation was wrong;
6. a high-quality Answer creates low or negative Value for a materially affected subject;
7. a favorable outcome occurs despite poor Reasoning, and an adverse outcome occurs despite responsible Reasoning and Answer use;
8. feedback reaches a System but is not valid Evidence, is not taken up, and produces no Correction or Learning;
9. member Learning occurs while whole-System capability declines, and System behavior improves through replacement without System Learning;
10. Cooperation occurs under unequal Power without valid consent, legitimate Authority, fair benefit, or complete Responsibility allocation;
11. an undertaking is SymK-governed but does not satisfy all seven DIA conditions;
12. a DIA satisfies its descriptive class while operating irresponsibly or producing transferred costs and harms;
13. the same concept maps coherently across Human and Scientific Views but loses a material affected-subject relation in the Engineering View;
14. a precise schema or graph is internally consistent but represents the wrong Scope, Domain, Bearer, or Question version;
15. a project-specific Ontology or metric is highly successful yet conflicts with the universal SymK semantic minimum;
16. A0 can be read as making Cooperation the final objective, conflicting with DR-017's broader purpose and DR-016's value-chain distinctions;
17. the governed-undertaking label improves integration but proves unnecessary as a separate Foundational concept; and
18. integration reveals a genuine conflict between two Stage-Accepted clauses that cannot be repaired by representation, Scope, Context, Domain, or relation typing alone.
