## 6. Duplication and paper ownership

Untraced duplication was controlled by three rules:

1. an exact accepted definition is fully stated in its home paper and only repeated elsewhere when the other paper's argument materially depends on its complete nucleus;
2. a repeated definition must use the exact accepted minimum before adding paper-specific explanation; and
3. paper-local review envelopes, examples, literature, and obligations retain distinct voice and are not forced into one canonical chapter template.

| Home paper | Principal explanatory ownership retained | Cross-paper use |
|---|---|---|
| Knowledge | Knowledge modes; claims/Grounds/Evidence/Provenance/status/reliance; representation traditions | other papers reference without redefining Knowledge |
| Intelligence | capacity, Bearer, configuration/profile/performance, specialization, Amplification and DIA | other papers preserve non-entailment and DIA orientation |
| Education and Training | Formation, Learning, Training, Education, formative Power/effect/achievement | other papers preserve no-universal-Education and system-Learning boundaries |
| Knowledge Engineering | epistemic conditions, acquisition, object network, recursive lifecycle, adjacent jurisdictions | other papers reference the condition model without importing one workflow |

Shared boilerplate was limited to status, lineage, exact semantic minima where repeated, and the accepted Authority/Evidence/urgency correction. Literature sections, examples, counterexamples, review envelopes, section order, and explanatory voice remain paper-specific.
