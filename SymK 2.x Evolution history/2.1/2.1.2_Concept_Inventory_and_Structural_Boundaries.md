# SymK 2.1.2 — Concept Inventory and Structural Boundaries

**Status:** Planned  
**Depends on:** 2.1.1

## Question

> Which concepts and distinctions are required by the accepted SymK identity, and which apparent primitives belong instead to project Ontology, formal representation, or implementation?

## Scope

This package will inventory the complete 2.0 value-chain vocabulary and examine structural candidates including Entity, Relationship, Identity, Context, Scope, Applicability, Domain, System, Bearer, Representation, and Projection.

## Required analyses

1. trace each candidate to the 2.0 identity, Foundation Papers, candidate Constitution, existing `FC-*` files, and project uses;
2. distinguish conceptual necessity from modeling convenience;
3. identify project-owned or implementation-owned meanings;
4. test whether Context and Scope are concepts, relationships, properties, or applicability conditions;
5. distinguish Identity from identifier and Representation from represented subject;
6. determine the minimum Ontology and Logic commitments required for the remaining 2.1 inquiry;
7. produce an initial dependency map without assuming a purely linear primitive order.

## Deliverables

- complete candidate inventory;
- provisional disposition for every structural candidate;
- dependency and reciprocal-dependency map;
- prohibited boundary conflations;
- affected-artifact and Foundation Paper ledger entries;
- proposed decision or explicit preservation of unresolved alternatives.

## Out of scope

No final universal ontology, formal vocabulary, schema, graph model, JSON/YAML contract, or `FC-*` package reconstruction.
