# SymK 2.1.6 — Responsibility, Agency, and Authority

**Status:** Planned  
**Depends on:** 2.1.1–2.1.5

## Question

> How must SymK distinguish responsibility when capability, agency, causation, role, authority, authorization, accountability, liability, and legal personality belong to different participants?

## Scope

Examine causal contribution, agency, capability, role, authority, authorization, decision, accountability, responsibility, liability, legal personality, moral standing, affected parties, and institutional allocation.

## Required tests

- human approval does not automatically validate Knowledge or absorb all responsibility;
- artificial contribution does not automatically imply authority, autonomy, liability, or moral standing;
- causal participation does not by itself settle accountability;
- collective or hybrid capability must not make responsibility disappear;
- authority must be scoped, sourced, and challengeable;
- affected parties remain within the responsibility horizon even when they do not operate the system;
- the minimum ethical boundary remains bounded and does not become a complete moral theory.

## Deliverables

- responsibility distinction map;
- minimum responsibility concept required by SymK;
- conceptual treatment of DQ-005 and DQ-017;
- consequences for Knowledge, cooperation, DIA, candidate A0/A2, and project declarations;
- Foundation Paper revision-ledger entries;
- explicit remainder for 2.2 and 2.6.

## Out of scope

Universal ethics, legal liability rules, professional regulation, final governance procedures, or constitutional ratification.
