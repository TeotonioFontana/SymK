## 3. Stage-Accepted identity direction

For continuation of SymK 2.1, the following identity direction is Stage-Accepted:

> **SymK is the engineering discipline and general framework for responsible Knowledge Engineering through governed cooperation among heterogeneous forms and Bearers of Intelligence. It engineers accountable and evolvable epistemic and cooperative conditions—not Knowledge or truth directly—through which complex questions can be framed, Evidence assessed, Reasoning conducted, Answers produced, Value evaluated, decisions and actions governed, consequences observed, and Learning and Correction sustained.**

This is provisional program authority. Exact final Product Vision wording remains subject to 2.1.9 integration, controlled canonical revision, cross-document review, and the final Stage 2.1 gate.

Acceptance does not turn SymK into the complete field of Knowledge Engineering or a universal theory of Intelligence, Knowledge, inquiry, cooperation, organizations, ethics, law, economics, pedagogy, Ontology, Logic, or social order.

## 4. Stage-Accepted center-of-gravity rule

For continuation of SymK 2.1:

> **The primary unit of SymK governance is not an Intelligence in isolation but the governed knowledge undertaking in which one or more Intelligence Bearers, supporting participants, representations, institutions, and engineered systems contribute under declared relations, scopes, roles, authorities, responsibilities, Evidence, consequences, and challenge conditions.**

The phrase “governed knowledge undertaking” remains a working analytical label. Acceptance does not admit a new Foundational concept, assign a final identifier, require one artifact, or select final package anatomy.

The rule means that no Human, artificial system, model, institution, collective, component, configured System, DIA, document, representation, workflow, metric, or implementation becomes the sovereign semantic center merely by participating, operating, deciding, or producing outputs. Actual Authority and Responsibility remain separately sourced and allocated.

## 5. Stage-Accepted Copernican corrections

### 5.1 Attributional correction

Human resemblance, origin, substrate, and historical role labels are not universal reference standards for Intelligence. Intelligence remains attributed to an organized Bearer only for a scoped capability under relevant Context, variation, and Evidence, as governed by DR-011.

The “Copernican correction” language already preserved in the 2.1.3 Intelligence Foundation Paper review drafts remains historical explanatory lineage. Acceptance does not give those review drafts independent publication or normative authority.

### 5.2 Knowledge Engineering correction

No Human, AI model, institution, collective, component, or configured System is automatically the sovereign center of the knowledge process. The governed undertaking supplies the reference structure within which Questions, epistemic conditions, contributions, roles, Authority, Responsibility, Reasoning, Answers, Value, consequences, Correction, and Learning become intelligible and challengeable.

The second correction does not erase subjects or treat every Intelligence Bearer as equivalent. It requires their different capabilities, contributions, dependencies, Power, standing, Authority, Responsibility, and affected consequences to remain visible.

## 6. Stage-Accepted layered identity and DIA relation

The following layered relation governs continuation:

| Layer | Stage-Accepted treatment |
|---|---|
| Historical origin | The Human–AI Symbiotic Cooperation Protocol is preserved as lineage and a possible derived specialization; its fixed role topology is not universal SymK law |
| SymK 2.0 orientation | The engineering discipline for creating Domain Intelligence Amplifiers remains accepted and historically authoritative |
| Stage 2.1 general identity | SymK is also the general responsible Knowledge Engineering framework stated in Section 3 of this record |
| Principal realization | Domain Intelligence Amplifiers remain a central Product Vision objective and the Foundational specific System class governed by DR-015 |
| Project realization | Derived projects specialize Domain purpose, Ontology, Logic, methods, thresholds, policies, architectures, and implementations while preserving applicable inherited constraints |

DIA is therefore Stage-Accepted as a principal but non-exhaustive realization of the broader SymK framework. A SymK concept or governed distinction may apply outside an established DIA claim; such applicability does not manufacture DIA membership or complete SymK compatibility.

AI, multiple agents, software, a knowledge base, a platform, a workflow, a product label, or several participants remain neither sufficient nor universally necessary for the broader framework or for DIA status. Actual DIA membership continues to require all seven DR-015 conditions.

## 8. Human standing, Authority, and Responsibility

Acceptance is non-anthropocentric in Intelligence attribution and non-agent-centric in knowledge-system architecture. It is not anti-Human and creates no equivalence of capability, status, Authority, Responsibility, moral standing, legal personality, or rights.

- Human dignity, rights, moral status, legal standing, professional duties, and democratic legitimacy remain governed by competent authorities where applicable.
- Artificial or collective capability does not create legal or moral personality, legitimate Authority, consent, standing, rights, duties, blame, liability, or remedy.
- Human review or decision does not automatically create truth, epistemic warrant, Answer Quality, or complete Responsibility.
- System-level capability does not erase provider, deployer, institution, professional, operator, participant, beneficiary, affected-subject, predecessor, or successor relations.
- Capability and contribution use the lowest adequate organized level; Authority and Responsibility remain independently governed under DR-014.
