## 3. Stage-Accepted dispositions

### Foundational anchors

| Candidate | Stage-Accepted disposition for continuation of 2.1 |
|---|---|
| Amplification | Foundational comparative, scoped, temporal, and evidenced relationship concerning material contribution to increased relevant effective capability of an identified organized Bearer relative to a materially comparable baseline across declared relevant variation |
| Domain Intelligence Amplifier | Foundational substrate- and architecture-neutral specific System class constituted by the seven accepted conditions below |

Both pass the six DR-009 eligibility tests. Final identifiers and package anatomy remain for 2.5.

### Amplified Intelligence and attribution

- Amplified Intelligence is a governed supporting compound attribution or qualification, not an independently foundational substance.
- The amplified Bearer is the lowest adequate identified organized level whose relevant capability gain is evidenced.
- Claims at participant, component, relation, team, institution, configured-System, distributed, hybrid, professional, predecessor, or successor levels require separate Evidence.
- Capability, gain, loss, Intelligence, Agency, Authority, Responsibility, value, benefit, and status do not transfer automatically among roles or levels.
- Actual capability configuration remains distinct from an attributed profile, Projection, score, category, dashboard, certificate, assessment, status, or record.

### Comparison, intervention, and capability change

Baseline, comparator, counterfactual, same-Bearer comparison, and new-System alternatives are governed supporting comparison distinctions. Assistance, enablement, access, augmentation, acceleration, scaling, optimization, automation, substitution, replacement, complementation, composition, and integration remain non-equivalent intervention, performance, work-allocation, lifecycle, or relation families.

Capability gain, loss, unchanged dimensions, and unknowns remain separately represented without a universal net scalar. Support-conditioned, retained, developmental, and configured-System capability are distinct qualifications. Content, retrieval, output, benchmark, speed, scale, labor reduction, productivity, adoption, market success, AI presence, Authorization, compliance, certification, benefit, and value do not establish Amplification by themselves.

### Seven constitutive DIA conditions

Every actual DIA membership claim requires:

1. an identified organized System;
2. a declared Domain purpose and complex-question orientation;
3. plural distinguishable qualified Intelligence participants;
4. actual Cooperation among them;
5. governed Domain Knowledge as epistemic medium;
6. an explicit amplified-Bearer and capability target; and
7. evidenced System capability to support repeatable Amplification across relevant variation.

Exact Domain purposes, complexity criteria, participant qualifications, capability profiles, thresholds, and validation methods remain specialized. Artificial Intelligence and Human participation are neither universal requirements nor sufficient conditions. Fully Human, institutional, artificial, distributed, and hybrid configurations remain eligible only when all seven conditions are evidenced.

The DIA System, Amplifier, amplified Bearer, Intelligence participant, Intelligence-bearing System, resource, environment, provider, user, beneficiary, affected subject, authority, and responsible subject are non-substitutable roles. Concept, candidate, membership, operational episode, Amplification occurrence, claim, assessment, governance status, Authorization, qualification, and record remain distinct.

### Integration, dependency, lifecycle, and Responsibility

- Integration is a governed level-, capability-, boundary-, mechanism-, effect-, dependency-, time-, and Evidence-qualified functional relationship or qualification.
- No universal mechanism stack is constitutive; condition, mechanism, operation, effect, and Evidence remain separate.
- Dependencies remain typed across constitutive, enabling, operational, epistemic, governance, economic, formative, infrastructural, provider, maintenance, and supervisory relations.
- Indispensability does not decide System membership, but material dependencies and providers may not disappear from capability, counterfactual, alternative, Power, lifecycle, cost, or Responsibility analysis.
- Fragility, concentration, lock-in, switching cost, outage, degradation, fallback, recovery, replacement, retirement, and succession remain governed qualifications or lifecycle states without automatic class or capability effects.
- Material change requires proportionate reassessment; capability, DIA membership, Identity, Authorization, Responsibility, value, and qualification do not inherit automatically.
- Descriptive Amplification and DIA membership remain independent of responsible amplification, SymK-compatible-DIA qualification, benefit, value, safety, fairness, legality, Authorization, and complete ethical adequacy.
- DR-014 continues to govern Power, control, Authority, Responsibility, affected-subject, consent-integrity, challenge, accountable-response, correction, repair, remedy, and gap relations without an actual allocation through this decision.

### Professional transformation and capability continuity

Professional amplification is ordinary Foundational Amplification applied to an identified professional-practice Bearer. Practitioner, role, task, team, institution, configured System, service organization, profession or community, output, beneficiary, affected subject, and future entrant remain distinct.

Professional transformation is a governed multi-level non-scalar change-profile family. Automation, substitution, replacement, service change, and task, role, employment, capability, formation, or geographic-institutional displacement remain distinct. Retained capability is a scoped temporal and support-condition qualification, not a universal Amplification criterion or unsupported self-sufficiency. Execution, framing, evaluation, challenge, intervention, fallback, recovery, Learning, and formation capabilities require separate Evidence. Deskilling requires Bearer-level capability reduction or nondevelopment Evidence.

Where responsible or evolvable persistent operation materially depends on capability vulnerable to decay, displacement, concentration, or provider loss, a credible plural capability-continuity path—or an explicit unresolved gap—must cover relevant maintenance, practice, renewal, replenishment, substitution, challenge, recovery, or succession. Education may contribute but is neither universally necessary nor sufficient. No labor, employment, credential, professional, education, curriculum, or workforce policy is created.

### External, Domain, Engineering, excluded, and deferred material

- Domain purposes, question classes, capabilities, profiles, thresholds, causal and validation methods remain Domain, scientific, professional, or derived-project specializations.
- Professions, tasks, curricula, credentials, duties, allocations, consent validity, liability, remedies, labor, economic, education, and public policy remain with their constitutional, Domain, professional, organizational, legal, educational, political, or external authorities.

- Exact package anatomy, identifiers, statuses, metrics, thresholds, observability, validation, formalization, and lowering remain assigned to 2.5 and 2.7–2.9.

## 4. Governing semantic effect

Acceptance establishes for continuation of 2.1 that:

1. Amplification is a relationship, not a substance, intrinsic property, scalar, output status, or value judgment.
2. Every material Amplification claim identifies the capability predicate, organized Bearer and level, boundary and Identity, Domain, Scope, Context, time, baseline, comparator, intervention, relevant variation, Evidence, counterevidence, uncertainty, dependencies, gains, losses, unknowns, distribution, lifecycle, authority, status, and Projection loss proportionately to consequence.
3. The same-Bearer baseline is preferred where Identity permits; genuine new Systems use explicit counterfactual alternatives and uncertainty.
4. Multiple amplification claims may coexist only with separate Evidence at every level.
5. Amplified Intelligence is Intelligence of the same identified Bearer qualified by a valid Amplification relation.
6. A DIA is the seven-condition specific organized-System class, not a product, tool, architecture, workflow, repository, organization, project, label, or certificate.
7. AI is neither sufficient nor universally necessary for DIA membership.
8. Cooperation, connection, aggregation, coordination, orchestration, workflow completion, and shared output do not establish integration or whole-level Intelligence automatically.
9. One successful episode does not establish repeatable support, persistent membership, durable capability, or favorable lifecycle status.
10. Capability, class membership, Agency, Authority, Authorization, Responsibility, legality, responsible operation, compatibility, value, benefit, and record remain independent predicates.
11. A DIA may satisfy the descriptive class while acting irresponsibly or causing harm; a responsible or authorized System may fail DIA or Amplification Evidence.
12. Dependency, provider, boundary, lifecycle, professional, Formation, Power, affected-subject, and Responsibility evidence remains visible and challengeable.
13. Human, Scientific, and Engineering Views preserve aligned meaning without substituting intelligibility, construct validity, or inspectability for one another.
14. Engineering artifacts may represent or project predicates but cannot create them or own their universal meaning.
15. SymK owns the universal semantic minimum; competent constitutional, Domain, project, scientific, professional, organizational, legal, educational, provider, assessor, and affected authorities retain their jurisdictions.
