## 5. Required review questions

Record a finding whenever the answer is not an unqualified pass.

### 5.1 Reader and comprehension

- Can an informed newcomer understand the paper without reading the complete evolution history?
- Are the actual reader assumptions the same as the intended assumptions?
- Does any explanatory transition require missing background or an unstated inference?
- Do diagrams, tables, callouts and examples clarify rather than create additional ambiguity?

### 5.2 Conceptual and cross-paper coherence

- Does terminology conform to the integrated 2.1 baseline and to the other three papers?
- Are paper-specific explanatory centers preserved without creating competing definitions?
- Are Cooperation, Representation/Projection, Bearer, Authority, affected-subject standing/challenge, Evidence/procedure, Formation, Amplification, DIA, Value and the branching lifecycle kept distinct where required?
- Does any wording recreate a prohibited entailment or flatten a governed distinction?

### 5.3 Authority, status and jurisdiction

- Could any passage falsely appear constitutional, Ratified, finally authoritative, canonical beyond its status or sufficient to amend an authority/artifact automatically?
- Is affected-subject standing/challenge kept distinct from the eleven typed Authority kinds?
- Does Evidence challenge claims, premises, applicability, consequences or decisions under authority while competent procedure governs authority/artifact change?
- Does urgent concern trigger consideration by competent operational or external authority without inventing emergency duty or containment power?
- Are philosophical, Engineering, Domain, legal, ethical, organizational and operational jurisdictions kept distinct?

### 5.4 Rationale, dissent and limits

- Is necessary historical rationale preserved from v0.1?
- Are material alternatives, dissent, uncertainty, limitations and reopening conditions visible enough for a human reader?
- Are cross-references sufficient to prevent a qualified claim from appearing universal?

### 5.5 Publication and accessibility

- Are citations, typography, diagrams, page flow, tables, links and references understandable in actual reading?
- Is every page legible at the reviewer’s ordinary reading settings?
- Does the tagged reading order work in any assistive technology actually used by the reviewer?
- Is any accessibility conclusion limited to the tools and Human experience actually tested?
