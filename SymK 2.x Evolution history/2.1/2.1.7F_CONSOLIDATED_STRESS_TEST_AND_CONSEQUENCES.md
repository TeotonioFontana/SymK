## 1. Consolidated question and result

> Do the Stream A–E accounts of Amplification, amplified-Bearer attribution, the Domain Intelligence Amplifier class, integration and dependency, lifecycle and Responsibility, and professional transformation form one coherent, challengeable, jurisdictionally bounded conceptual system that survives the complete 2.1.7 evidence set and is sufficient to prepare Proposed DR-015?

**Stream F result: Pass for separately instructed proposal preparation.**

The evidence supports the following consolidated working spine:

> **Amplification is a comparative, scoped, temporal, and evidenced relation in which an identified intervention or organized arrangement materially contributes to an increase in an identified organized Bearer's relevant effective capability, relative to a materially comparable baseline, across declared relevant variation. The claim must preserve capability dimensions and specializations, gains, losses, unchanged and unknown dimensions, integration, dependencies, alternatives, resources, uncertainty, distribution, lifecycle, and assessment authority.**

> **Amplified Intelligence is a governed compound attribution: Intelligence of an identified Bearer is qualified as amplified only through a valid Amplification claim. It is not a new substance, a transferable property of a tool, an output label, or automatic proof of a whole-level Intelligence Bearer.**

> **A Domain Intelligence Amplifier is a specific System class constituted by seven candidate conditions: (1) an identified organized System; (2) declared Domain purpose and complex-question orientation; (3) plural distinguishable qualified Intelligence participants; (4) actual Cooperation among them; (5) governed Domain Knowledge as epistemic medium; (6) an explicit amplified-Bearer and capability target; and (7) evidenced system capability to support repeatable Amplification across relevant variation. No architecture, product category, artificial substrate, mechanism stack, output, certification, or marketing label substitutes for them.**

> **A valid descriptive Amplification or DIA claim does not itself establish responsible operation, Authority, Authorization, benefit, value, safety, fairness, legality, ethical compatibility, lower total cost, professional legitimacy, or SymK compatibility. Those qualifications require their own evidence and authority. Material dependencies, Power, affected consequences, lifecycle changes, professional capability loss, and capability-continuity gaps remain visible rather than being netted into the capability predicate.**

This result passes the Product Vision, DR-009 through DR-014, all twenty-four mandatory cases, twenty additional negative controls, stable-boundary and non-entailment audits, Human/Scientific/Engineering View alignment, jurisdiction, candidate-artifact, Foundation Paper, deferred-question, derived-project, and downstream-dependency gates.

## 6. Cross-stream interface audit

### 6.1 Stream A to Stream B

Stream A supplies the comparative relation and claim anatomy. Stream B prevents “the subject improved” from remaining ambiguous by selecting the lowest adequate organized level and allowing multiple separately evidenced claims. The interface passes because:

- the same Bearer is preferred across baseline and target where possible;
- new-system counterfactuals remain explicit where no predecessor exists;
- gain cannot transfer from component to participant, relation, team, institution, configured system, profession, or successor;
- unaided retained, support-conditioned, externally produced, developmental, and configured-system capability remain distinguishable; and
- actual configuration, attribution, profile, Projection, assessment, status, and record remain separate.

### 6.2 Stream B to Stream C

Stream B does not require the amplified Bearer to be the DIA System. Stream C preserves that separation. The interface passes because:

- a DIA can support Amplification of a participant without itself bearing Intelligence;
- a configured system can be the amplified Bearer only with independent whole-level integration and capability Evidence;
- DIA, Amplifier, participant, resource, beneficiary, provider, and Intelligence-bearing System are non-substitutable roles;
- class membership and successful amplification are separately evidenced; and
- a valid episode neither proves persistent membership nor copies capability to all members.

### 6.3 Stream C to Stream D

Stream C states conditions, not a universal architecture. Stream D supplies open mechanism, integration, dependency, and lifecycle tests. The interface passes because:

- no named mechanism is constitutive by itself;
- functional integration is level- and capability-specific;
- external providers can be materially necessary without automatically becoming members;
- change, outage, replacement, retirement, and succession can alter membership or claim validity;
- descriptive DIA status remains independent from responsible or SymK-compatible qualification; and
- Power and Responsibility cannot be hidden inside the system boundary.

### 6.4 Stream D to Stream E

Stream D exposes support, dependency, Power, and lifecycle. Stream E tests professional consequences without redefining Amplification. The interface passes because:

- professional amplification uses the ordinary Amplification relation;
- tasks, roles, outputs, employment, practitioners, teams, institutions, configured systems, professions, and future entrants remain separate;
- retained, evaluative, challenge, intervention, fallback, recovery, Learning, and formation capabilities are separately qualified;
- deskilling is a capability reduction or nondevelopment claim, not a synonym for task removal;
- capability continuity is a responsible/evolvable qualification under material dependency, not a descriptive DIA condition; and
- Education remains one possible continuity path, never a universal condition.

### 6.5 Combined interface result

No circularity is fatal. Amplification depends on prior Intelligence meaning and a Bearer; DIA depends on Amplification as its target relation; a DIA instance can then be evidence within an Amplification claim without defining the relation by self-reference. The class is identified by constitutive organization and capacity to support repeatable amplification, while each actual amplification remains separately evidenced.

## 8. Stable-boundary audit

The model survives these boundary operations:

1. **Component expansion:** adding a model, database, expert, platform, or workflow does not copy its capability to the original Bearer or whole.
2. **Post-success expansion:** indispensable resources may not be included only after success to inflate system capability.
3. **Post-failure contraction:** providers, data, maintenance, reviewers, and affected consequences may not disappear from the boundary after failure.
4. **Participant turnover:** institutional or system capability may persist only through identified organization, memory, relations, and Evidence, not predicate copying.
5. **Provider exit:** external support loss triggers dependency, fallback, Identity, and revalidation analysis.
6. **Version change:** model, policy, data, mechanism, role, or authority change can alter the claim without automatically creating a new System or preserving the old one.
7. **Organizational restructuring:** legal continuity does not prove capability continuity; operational continuity does not prove legal or responsibility continuity.
8. **Support withdrawal:** removal tests distinguish support-conditioned from retained capability without making retention universally necessary.
9. **Task removal:** automation changes work allocation but not automatically capability, role, employment, service, or formation.
10. **Success/failure symmetry:** the same material boundary applies when attributing benefit, error, harm, cost, and Responsibility.
11. **Level change:** participant, relation, team, institution, configured system, profession, and successor require new claims, not renamed old claims.
12. **Time change:** persistence, drift, decay, deskilling, recovery, replacement, and succession qualify claims rather than being hidden by an evergreen status.

**Result:** Pass. Boundary sensitivity is represented; boundary shopping is prohibited.

## 22. Alternatives considered

### A. Amplification as any improvement

Rejected. It cannot distinguish output, access, speed, automation, value, luck, or capability.

### B. Amplification as unaided retained capability only

Rejected. It wrongly excludes genuine support-conditioned and configured-system capability while hiding the need to qualify retention.

### C. Amplification as net scalar gain

Rejected. No universal aggregation authority or commensurability exists; material losses and distribution disappear.

### D. Amplified Intelligence as a new substance

Rejected. It reifies a governed comparative attribution and invites transfer among participants, tools, and systems.

### E. DIA as any useful tool or Knowledge system

Rejected. It omits plural Intelligence, actual Cooperation, explicit target, and repeatable Amplification capacity.

### F. DIA as necessarily Human–AI

Rejected. It adds substrate privilege unsupported by the Product Vision or accepted concepts and fails fully Human/institutional cases.

### G. DIA as any multi-agent AI system

Rejected. Agent count and architecture do not establish participant Intelligence, Cooperation, governed Knowledge, or amplification.

### H. One universal mechanism stack

Rejected. Relevant mechanisms vary by Domain, System, capability, and lifecycle; named features can be present but ineffective.

### I. Every indispensable provider belongs to the DIA

Rejected. Dependency and membership are distinct, though both must be visible.

### J. Whole-system boundary whenever components cooperate

Rejected. Cooperation does not establish integrated whole-level Intelligence or the proper amplified Bearer.

### K. Responsible amplification built into the descriptive predicate

Rejected. Harmful, unauthorized, or irresponsible capability gain must remain representable so it can be governed.

### L. Professional amplification as productivity

Rejected. Productivity combines output, labor, resources, work allocation, and cost without identifying capability.

### M. Preserve every Human task to prevent deskilling

Rejected. Tasks can be obsolete, harmful, or non-formative; capability and continuity are the relevant objects.

### N. Education required for every persistent DIA

Rejected under DR-013. Continuity can be sustained through plural means and is conditionally material only for responsible/evolvable qualification.

### O. Pure project-local definition

Rejected. It permits incompatible SymK identities and marketing-defined membership.

### P. Comparative relational Amplification plus a seven-condition, substrate-neutral DIA class

Retained as the recommended proposal direction because it passes all gates while preserving Domain specialization and challenge.
