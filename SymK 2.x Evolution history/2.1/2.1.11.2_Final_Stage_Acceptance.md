# SymK 2.1.11.2 — Final Stage Acceptance

**Status:** Planned  
**Prerequisite:** successful 2.1.11.1 audit and explicit project-steward review

## Decision question

> Has SymK 2.1 established a coherent, bounded, traceable, communicable, and sufficiently mature conceptual system to support constitutional axiom analysis in 2.2 without pretending philosophical finality or prematurely selecting formal representations?

## Acceptance must record

- final gate evidence;
- accepted decision lineage;
- conceptual baseline version;
- Foundation Paper publication versions and verification state;
- preserved dissent and open questions;
- downstream transfers and blockers;
- canonical and derived-project consequences;
- explicit acceptance authority and date;
- the exact distinction between Stage-Acceptance and constitutional Ratification;
- successor authorization for SymK 2.2.

No acceptance language may imply that v0.2 Foundation Papers, historical `FC-*` files, candidate axioms, or machine-semantic projections became Ratified merely by completing this stage.
