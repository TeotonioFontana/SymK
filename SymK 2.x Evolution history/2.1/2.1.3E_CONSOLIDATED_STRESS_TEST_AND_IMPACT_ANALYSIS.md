## 1. Gate question

> Do the independent Knowledge and Intelligence anchors, common Bearer/attribution model, and typed reciprocal relationship account jointly survive the mandatory counterexamples, Product Vision, candidate-axiom, cross-domain, two-lane, jurisdiction, and dependency tests required before DR-011 may be proposed?

## 2. Candidate system under test

The tested system contains five linked recommendations:

1. **Knowledge:** a scoped, plural epistemic achievement attributable to a Bearer, with mode-specific truth, competence, or encounter conditions and a non-accidental connection.
2. **Intelligence:** a scoped, substrate-realized, organized capacity to integrate available resources in interpretation or effective response across relevant variation.
3. **Bearer:** retain the Stage-Accepted DR-010 governed supporting role rather than reopen it as a foundational kind.
4. **Attribution:** govern a relationship family separating actual bearing, fallible claim, assessment, authority for use, and stored record.
5. **Reciprocity:** govern directional, typed, contingent enabling, constraining, developmental, evidentiary, operational, and representational relationships without automatic cross-attribution.

## 5. Cross-domain translation

### 5.1 Shared universal property

`document type` can be governed universally as a qualified classification relationship applying to a Document. The universal minimum identifies the subject, property definition, Domain, scheme, type concept, assertion source, status, provenance, material Scope and Context, and lineage.

### 5.2 Legal specialization

A legal Domain may define values such as initial pleading, appeal, judgment, or evidentiary filing under a controlled legal classification scheme. Legal authority, procedure, jurisdiction, and status remain Domain-owned.

### 5.3 Medical specialization

A clinical Domain may define prescription, examination request, report, referral, or clinical note under a controlled medical classification scheme. Clinical authority, confidentiality, professional rules, and safety thresholds remain Domain-owned.

### 5.4 Same form, different semantic authority

The same property identifier or storage shape does not make the legal and medical values interchangeable. A value is interpreted through its Domain, scheme, version, Scope, and authority. A foreign-Domain document in a declared homogeneous corpus is a boundary violation to report or quarantine, not a reason to universalize one Domain's types.

### 5.5 Discoverer and storage

- the provider's Domain declaration is an input claim and processing constraint, not self-validating truth;
- the discoverer may bear a scoped classification capability;
- its result is an attribution claim about the Document;
- acceptance is a governed assessment under Domain authority;
- neutral storage preserves the qualified assertion and history;
- SharePoint, AWS, filesystem, search-index, and database fields are purpose-specific Projections;
- and no storage environment becomes a Knowledge or Intelligence Bearer merely by retaining the assertion.

The existing `02_CORE_MODEL` semantic-package scaffold is compatible as a candidate projection only while its `Proposed` and `normative_effect: none` boundaries remain effective. It supplies implementation-oriented test material; it does not decide DR-011.

## 7. Candidate Constitution stress test

### A1 — Epistemic Humility

**Compatibility:** Strong. The candidate system supplies the missing distinction among actual Knowledge, attribution, claim, assessment, asset, Representation, status, and reliance.

**Required 2.2 review:** A1 currently defines epistemic warrant as grounds sufficient for responsible reliance. The Knowledge anchor separates epistemic achievement from later reliance authorization; constitutional wording must avoid making operational reliance the definition of Knowledge or warrant universally. A1 should also distinguish actual Scope from declared Scope.

### A2 — Governed Role Allocation

**Compatibility:** Strong. The model directly supports the separation of capability, Intelligence, Knowledge, role, authority, authorization, responsibility, standing, and origin.

**Required 2.2 review:** “Capability: scoped capacity supported by evidence” mixes the actual capacity with its evidentiary attribution. A2 should distinguish the bearing relation from the claim and assessment. Stable system boundaries and separate responsibility allocation must remain explicit.

### A4 — Engineering Epistemic Conditions

**Compatibility:** Strong. The reciprocal model explains how engineering can affect Knowledge and Intelligence causally without manufacturing either directly.

**Required 2.2 review:** A4's list of activities must be role-qualified; not every Intelligence can pursue, achieve, express, preserve, share, challenge, apply, and revise. Expression, preservation, and sharing occur through Representations; revision changes claims, models, records, assessments, or capability, not truth by decree.

These findings guide later axiom review. A1, A2, and A4 remain Proposed and unchanged.

## 9. Two-lane stress test

### Human-semantic lane

The A–D analyses preserve definitions, reasons, examples, counterexamples, dissent, confidence, and reopening conditions. A newcomer can distinguish the concepts without reading a schema.

### Machine-semantic lane

Later projections can preserve concept identity/version, Bearer, actual relation, attribution claim, assessment, record, Knowledge mode, Intelligence profile, Domain, Scope, Context, evidence, alternatives, resources, authority, uncertainty, time, challenge, lineage, and typed reciprocal relation.

### Mismatch test

A boolean `is_knowledge`, `is_intelligent`, a generic `related_to`, a type label, a score, or a repository column loses governed meaning. Such forms may exist only as declared reductions backed by richer authoritative records and explicit semantic loss.

**Result:** Pass without selecting JSON, YAML, ontology, logic, class hierarchy, database, or API design.

## 11. DR-011 proposal requirements

The Proposed decision must:

1. state the Knowledge and Intelligence working definitions independently;
2. classify both as candidate foundational concepts for Stage-Acceptance review;
3. retain Bearer under DR-010 and propose Attribution and reciprocal relations as governed supporting relationship families;
4. preserve mode-specific Knowledge and profile-based Intelligence criteria;
5. prohibit representation, performance, status, origin, authority, and stored-content substitutions;
6. state attribution and stable-boundary requirements;
7. state reciprocal relationships as directional, typed, scoped, contingent, and challengeable;
8. record Product Vision, candidate-axiom, Foundation Paper, derived-project, and later-stage consequences;
9. preserve dissent, confidence, and reopening conditions;
10. state that acceptance would grant provisional program authority only and would not create final concept packages, revise Foundation Papers, Ratify axioms, or select implementation forms.

## 12. Gate conclusion

The candidate conceptual system survives the mandatory and additional tests without reopening DR-009 or DR-010. Its principal uncertainty—the overlap between practical Knowledge and Intelligence—can be governed as explicit dissent without making either concept a hidden definition of the other.

Work package 2.1.3 has therefore completed analytical Streams A–E. The next authorized artifact is a consolidated **Proposed `SYMK-2X-DR-011`** for project-steward review. No acceptance is implied by this gate result.
