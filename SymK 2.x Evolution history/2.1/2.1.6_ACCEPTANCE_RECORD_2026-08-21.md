## 4. Governing semantic effect

Acceptance establishes for continuation of 2.1 that:

1. Responsibility is a sourced, scoped, phased, and challengeable relationship family connecting an identified subject at a declared level to an identified object under a governing source, frame, jurisdiction, and standard.
2. Responsibility is not a source-free property, trait, conserved substance, percentage, title, approval state, legal status, moral judgment, workflow field, or record.
3. Factual, governance, Responsibility, allocation/level, ethical/affected-subject, and representation/status planes remain analytically non-substitutable.
4. The twenty-four-dimension material claim envelope governs proportionately to consequence without becoming a mandatory universal schema.
5. Causal Contribution, agency, control, role, Authority, Authorization, answerability, liability, remedy, affected status, and record may belong to different subjects and levels without requiring one all-bearing subject.
6. Divergence is represented through object-specific typed relations rather than repaired by predicate copying or a universal residual bearer.
7. Prospective Responsibility, retrospective Responsibility, duty, obligation, answerability, accountability, blame, liability, correction, repair, remedy, and record remain distinct.
8. Allocation is object-first, typed, multi-subject, multi-level, temporal, non-additive, stable across outcome, and explicit about transfer, retention, succession, and gaps.
9. Human, artificial, collective, institutional, configured-system, hybrid, legal, component, member, whole, predecessor, and successor predicates require their own sources and Evidence.
10. A gap is object-specific and cannot be inferred from complexity, distribution, opacity, automation, missing blame, or absence of one all-controlling subject.
11. SymK-compatible governance preserves the seven commitments without selecting a complete moral, political, or legal theory.
12. Affected subjects remain within the consequence and response horizon without becoming Participants, responsible parties, decision authorities, or veto holders automatically.
13. Consent claims preserve object, notice, understanding, voluntariness, practical refusal, Scope, duration, change, withdrawal, representation, and record without one-field substitution.
14. Formal challenge permission is distinct from practical protected access, capable reception, assessment, reasoned response, correction, review, appeal, repair, and remedy routes.
15. Human, Scientific, and Engineering Views remain aligned by object, subject level, source, Scope, phase, time, relation kind, Evidence, transformation, omission, disagreement, and declared loss.
16. Engineering artifacts represent or project the system and may never define its universal meaning silently.

The complete dispositions, rules, eighty prohibited entailments, twenty-four mandatory cases, sixteen negative controls, and three-view obligations in accepted DR-014 govern over this summary.
