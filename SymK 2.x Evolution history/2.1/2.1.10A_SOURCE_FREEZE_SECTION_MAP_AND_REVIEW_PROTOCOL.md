## 10. Finding and disposition protocol

### 10.1 Finding identifiers

Use `2.1.10-{PAPER}-{STREAM}-{NNN}`, where `PAPER` is `K`, `I`, `E`, or `KE`, `STREAM` is `B`, `C`, `D`, `E`, `F`, `G`, or `H`, and `NNN` is a zero-padded sequence. A finding cites:

- exact v0.1 Markdown lines and PDF pages;
- affected claim IDs from the material-claim inventory;
- exact DR/Product Vision/source authority;
- finding class;
- consequence if unchanged;
- proposed disposition;
- cross-paper dependencies; and
- verification evidence.

### 10.2 Finding classes

1. conceptual conflict;
2. cross-paper inconsistency;
3. communication defect;
4. source, citation, quotation, or historical defect;
5. authority, status, or jurisdiction ambiguity;
6. publication or accessibility defect;
7. missing Human-review evidence; or
8. downstream 2.1.11 issue.

### 10.3 Allowed dispositions

Every material finding receives exactly one:

1. `REVISE_V0.2` — revise into the new v0.2 source with exact lineage;
2. `RETAIN_V0.1_JUSTIFIED` — retain the identified passage with explicit justification;
3. `ERRATUM_OR_ADDENDUM` — preserve the source and issue a governed correction or addition; or
4. `DEFER_2.1.11` — route a named issue to 2.1.11 with owner and consequence.

`DELETE`, `HARMONIZE`, `POLISH`, `FIXED`, or an empty status is not a valid disposition. Deletion may occur only as the textual operation implementing an approved disposition whose historical source and rationale remain preserved.

## 11. Human-review evidence protocol

Automated source comparison, semantic analysis, citation extraction, rendering and visual inspection are aids. They do not satisfy Human review.

### 11.1 Human roles

- **Human reviewer:** the project steward or an explicitly designated Human who reads the complete governed object.
- **Review recorder:** captures coverage, findings, disagreement and dispositions without manufacturing review evidence.
- **Semantic editor:** implements accepted v0.2 changes without altering v0.1.
- **Publication verifier:** checks governed Markdown/PDF correspondence and every rendered page.

One person may occupy multiple roles only when the record discloses that fact. Tool or AI participation is recorded separately and is never named as the Human reviewer.

### 11.2 Required Human-review record

For each paper and the coordinated set, record:

1. reviewer identity and role;
2. review date and uninterrupted/segmented reading intervals;
3. exact Markdown/PDF hashes and version;
4. intended audience and reviewer’s relevant prior knowledge;
5. complete-object coverage statement, including any skipped material;
6. comprehension failures, ambiguity and explanatory-sequence findings;
7. terminology and cross-paper findings;
