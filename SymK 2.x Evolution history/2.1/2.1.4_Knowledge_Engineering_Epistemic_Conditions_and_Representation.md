## Scope

- acquisition versus extraction;
- subject versus representation;
- formal validity versus epistemic sufficiency;
- preservation, strengthening, simplification, and loss in projection;
- governance status versus truth status;
- validation of structure, inference, evidence, practice, and consequence.
