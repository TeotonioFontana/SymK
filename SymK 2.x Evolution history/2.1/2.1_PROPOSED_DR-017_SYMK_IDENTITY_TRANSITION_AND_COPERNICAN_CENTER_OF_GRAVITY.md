## 1. Trigger and reason for a separate decision

After the complete 2.1.8 lineage and Proposed DR-016 were preserved in commit `9b636713921504e8459ddb84f1dc7aea95b80171` and pushed to `master`, the project steward paused the usual acceptance sequence and identified a higher-order consequence of the migration:

> When we started the migration to SymK version 2, our target was to close some gaps and improve the structure and the organization of the project. But, in fact we achieved much more than that. We crossed the chasm in several ways. We moved from a symbiotic cooperation protocol to the engineering of knowledge through a general framework of cooperation between several forms of intelligence. And even more than this, we proposed a Copernican change in the center of gravity of intelligence.

The instruction “please work on this” authorizes analysis and durable registration of that consequence. It does not accept DR-016, accept this proposal, reopen an accepted predecessor automatically, revise the Product Vision, complete 2.1.8, or open 2.1.9.

The observation cannot be hidden inside DR-016. DR-016 governs Questions, Reasoning, Answers, quality, time, total cost, Value, outcomes, consequences, feedback, correction, and Learning. The observation instead asks what SymK has become through the combined effect of 2.0 and 2.1, how its historical protocol, accepted DIA identity, and broader Knowledge Engineering framework relate, and where its organizing center now lies.

## 2. Decision question

> **What higher-order identity consequence follows from SymK 2.0 and Stage 2.1, and should SymK understand its center of gravity as an isolated Human, artificial system, Intelligence Bearer, Human–AI pair, or the governed knowledge undertaking through which heterogeneous Intelligences cooperate under explicit epistemic, authority, responsibility, value, consequence, and learning conditions?**

## 3. Proposed decision

Stage 2.1 is proposed to recognize that SymK has undergone a substantive identity transition rather than only a structural migration.

### 3.1 Proposed general identity

> **SymK is the engineering discipline and general framework for responsible Knowledge Engineering through governed cooperation among heterogeneous forms and Bearers of Intelligence. It engineers accountable and evolvable epistemic and cooperative conditions—not Knowledge or truth directly—through which complex questions can be framed, Evidence assessed, Reasoning conducted, Answers produced, Value evaluated, decisions and actions governed, consequences observed, and learning and correction sustained.**

This statement is a proposed Stage 2.1 identity direction. It has no normative authority until explicit project-steward acceptance and does not replace the canonical Product Vision by appearing here.

### 3.2 Proposed center-of-gravity rule

> **The primary unit of SymK governance is not an Intelligence in isolation but the governed knowledge undertaking in which one or more Intelligence Bearers, supporting participants, representations, institutions, and engineered systems contribute under declared relations, scopes, roles, authorities, responsibilities, Evidence, consequences, and challenge conditions.**

“Governed knowledge undertaking” is a working analytical label in this proposal, not a newly admitted Foundational concept or final package name. It denotes the question-centered, consequence-sensitive cooperative whole that must be governed without reifying any participant, artifact, workflow, or implementation as its sovereign center.

### 3.3 Proposed relation to Domain Intelligence Amplifiers

The Stage-Accepted DIA identity remains a decisive and durable realization of SymK, but it is proposed not to exhaust SymK's general identity:

- a **Domain Intelligence Amplifier** remains the Stage-Accepted seven-condition Foundational specific System class governed by DR-015;
- creating responsible and evolvable DIAs remains a central Product Vision objective and principal application of the discipline;
- the **general SymK framework** governs the prior and wider epistemic, cooperative, responsibility, representation, value, and learning conditions through which a DIA can be conceived, assessed, engineered, challenged, and evolved;
- not every use of a SymK distinction is thereby a DIA, and the presence of AI, software, a knowledge base, or multiple participants does not establish a DIA;
- the general framework must remain bounded by SymK's Knowledge Engineering jurisdiction and must not become a claim to govern all Intelligence, Knowledge, inquiry, science, organizations, ethics, or social cooperation.

### 3.4 Proposed non-anthropocentric and non-agent-centric interpretation

SymK neither replaces Human centrality with artificial-system centrality nor treats all forms of Intelligence as equivalent. It proposes two related corrections:

1. **Attributional Copernican correction:** Human resemblance, origin, substrate, and historical role labels are not universal reference standards for Intelligence. Intelligence is attributed to an organized Bearer only for a scoped capacity under relevant Context, variation, and Evidence, as governed by DR-011.
2. **Knowledge-engineering Copernican correction:** no Human, AI model, institution, collective, component, or configured System is automatically the sovereign center of the knowledge process. The governed undertaking—its Question, epistemic conditions, contributions, relations, responsibilities, Answer, Value, consequences, correction, and lineage—provides the reference structure within which participant capabilities and roles become intelligible.

The first use is already preserved explicitly as “The Copernican correction” in `2.1.3G_INTELLIGENCE_FOUNDATION_PAPER_V0.2_REVIEW_DRAFT.md` and `2.1.3H_INTELLIGENCE_FOUNDATION_PAPER_V0.2_REVIEW_DRAFT_R2.md`. DR-017 does not appropriate that phrase for a different claim or treat those review drafts as accepted norms. It preserves their attributional reference-point change and proposes a second, related architectural and Knowledge Engineering consequence for explicit review.

The second correction does not erase subjects. It makes their differences, contributions, dependencies, Power, Authority, Responsibility, and affected consequences more visible.

## 4. The chasms crossed

### 4.1 From repair to refoundation

The migration began as controlled consolidation: close gaps, resolve conflicting authority, improve structure, classify documents, preserve lineage, and prevent implementations or representations from owning meaning. The result now includes a coherent conceptual and governance architecture able to state what SymK is, what it engineers, what it must not claim, and how evidence can challenge it.

The work therefore crossed from repository and conceptual repair into disciplined refoundation. This does not make the migration plan retrospectively mistaken; it records that successful inquiry changed the scale of the result.

### 4.2 From a Human–AI cooperation protocol to plural governed cooperation

The historical Symbiotic Cooperation Protocol made a valuable initial move: different capacities could cooperate rather than be reduced to substitution. Its fixed Human/AI role structure was nevertheless too narrow for the evidence later examined.

The current framework can distinguish and govern contributions by Human individuals, artificial systems, teams, collectives, institutions, professions, configured systems, distributed systems, hybrid formations, services, and other organized Bearers without granting any of them capability, standing, Agency, Authority, or Responsibility solely by type or origin.

The protocol survives as historical lineage and may remain a legitimate derived specialization. It no longer defines the universal participant topology or role allocation of SymK.

### 4.3 From cooperation protocol to Knowledge Engineering discipline and framework

A protocol primarily governs interaction. The Stage 2.1 architecture governs a larger epistemic and engineering system:

- Knowledge and Intelligence attribution;
- Knowledge Engineering objects, lifecycle, epistemic conditions, Evidence, provenance, status, reliance, and three Views;
- Cooperation, Participation, Contribution, Formation, Power, challenge, Feedback, Uptake, Correction, and Learning;
- Agency, causal Contribution, roles, Authority, Authorization, Responsibility, accountability arrangements, affected subjects, and repair;
- Amplification, amplified-Bearer attribution, DIA conditions, Integration, dependency, provider relations, lifecycle, professional transformation, and capability continuity;
- Questions, framing, inquiry, Reasoning, Answers, quality, time, total cost, Value, outcome, consequence, feedback return, and correction lineage.

Cooperation remains constitutive, but it is now one governed process within a complete Knowledge Engineering framework rather than the complete identity or final objective.

### 4.4 From a dyad to heterogeneous Intelligence ecology without flattening difference

Plurality is not a count of two or more nominal actors. It requires separate attribution and governance of each claimed Intelligence Bearer and of any claimed higher-level formation. Cooperation among intelligent participants does not automatically create collective or hybrid Intelligence. Component Intelligence does not automatically transfer to a System, and System capability does not transfer to every member.

The framework therefore supports an ecology of heterogeneous capabilities while rejecting three shortcuts:

- **origin privilege:** Human or artificial origin does not settle capability or role;
- **equivalence:** different Bearers need not possess the same dimensions, status, authority, or moral standing;
- **aggregation:** interaction or composition does not automatically create a new Intelligence Bearer.

### 4.5 From output production to a recursive governed epistemic lifecycle

The early protocol could be read as organizing contributions toward an output. The current framework separates Question, Reasoning, Answer, quality, Authority, decision, action, outcome, consequence, Feedback, Correction, Learning, and improvement. No fluent output, accepted recommendation, authorized decision, favorable outcome, measured signal, update, or score creates the next relation automatically.

Consequences can return through Evidence and challenge to validation, Responsibility, policy, concepts, and constitutional commitments. The resulting architecture is recursive and self-corrective without treating every return signal as Evidence or every change as Learning.

## 6. What becomes central—and what does not

### 6.1 Central organizing relations

The proposed center includes at least:

1. an identified Domain purpose under competent Domain or project authority;
2. a governed Question or question family with framing and affected horizon;
3. declared Context, Scope, assumptions, uncertainty, and answer obligations;
4. relevant Intelligence Bearers and other participants with separately evidenced roles and contributions;
5. governed Knowledge claims, Grounds, Evidence, provenance, epistemic status, and reliance conditions;
6. Cooperation and Integration relations with Power, challenge, dependency, and formation conditions;
7. explicit Agency, Authority, Authorization, Responsibility, and affected-subject boundaries;
8. Reasoning and Answer relations with adequate process and conclusion Evidence;
9. multidimensional quality, time, total-cost, and plural-Value profiles;
10. decisions, actions, outcomes, consequences, and causal claims kept distinct;
11. Feedback, Uptake, Correction, Learning, and improvement kept distinct; and
12. recursive return to validation, responsibility, policy, concept, and constitutional governance.

This is an analytical orientation, not a mandatory universal workflow or final package schema.

### 6.2 Things that do not become the center automatically

None of the following is the sovereign or exhaustive center merely by existing:

- the Human user, expert, author, operator, reviewer, or decision-maker;
- an AI model, agent, assistant, provider, training process, or output;
- an institution, collective, profession, platform, or project;
- a DIA, configured System, architecture, knowledge graph, database, corpus, or interface;
- a document, Foundation Paper, axiom, schema, ontology, metric, benchmark, workflow, or runtime record;
- one Answer, decision, outcome, value function, stakeholder preference, or optimization objective.

Some of these may hold decisive authority or responsibility for a particular matter. That is a governed relation, not universal centrality.

## 7. Human standing, authority, and responsibility are not erased

The proposed reframing is non-anthropocentric in Intelligence attribution and non-agent-centric in knowledge-system architecture. It is not anti-Human and does not claim moral equivalence among Bearers.

- Human dignity, rights, moral status, legal standing, professional duties, and democratic legitimacy remain governed by competent external and Domain authorities where applicable.
- Artificial or collective capability does not itself create legal or moral personality, legitimate Authority, consent, standing, rights, duties, blame, liability, or remedy.
- Human decision or review does not automatically create epistemic quality, truth, legitimate reliance, or adequate responsibility.
- System-level contribution does not erase provider, deployer, institution, professional, operator, participant, beneficiary, or affected-subject relations.
- The lowest adequate organized level must be used for capability and contribution claims, while Responsibility and Authority are allocated independently under DR-014.

The purpose of de-centering is to prevent privilege and disappearance, not to remove persons from the moral and governance horizon.

## 8. Layered identity model proposed

| Layer | Proposed status and role |
|---|---|
| Historical origin | Symbiotic cooperation protocol between Human and artificial Intelligence; preserved as lineage and possible specialized protocol, not current universal role law |
| Accepted 2.0 orientation | Engineering discipline for creating Domain Intelligence Amplifiers; remains authoritative unless and until explicitly revised |
| Proposed Stage 2.1 general identity | Engineering discipline and general framework for responsible Knowledge Engineering through governed cooperation among heterogeneous Intelligence Bearers |
| Principal realization | Domain Intelligence Amplifiers as a Foundational specific System class and central Product Vision objective |
| Project realization | Derived projects specialize Domain purpose, Ontology, Logic, methods, thresholds, policies, architectures, and implementations while preserving inherited constraints |

The layered model avoids both historical erasure and identity compression. The broader framework explains why DIAs require the accepted conceptual system; the DIA target prevents the general framework from becoming directionless philosophical expansion.

## 9. Alternatives considered

### A — Preserve the DIA-centered identity as exhaustive

This protects continuity and operational focus but leaves Stage 2.1's general epistemic, responsibility, representation, and cooperative architecture appearing merely instrumental to one System class. It does not adequately explain why the framework governs conditions that precede, assess, challenge, and may exist outside a completed DIA.

### B — Return to the Human–AI symbiotic protocol as the primary identity

This preserves historical simplicity but reinstates fixed participant types and encourages origin-based role allocation. It cannot govern collective, institutional, distributed, hybrid, non-AI, or changing formations adequately.

### C — Define SymK as generic Knowledge Engineering

This captures the enlarged scope but loses SymK's distinctive commitments to plural Intelligence, governed Cooperation, DIA realization, responsibility, value, consequence return, and bounded jurisdiction. It risks claiming an entire established field.

### D — Center an ecology or network of Intelligences

This improves on the dyad but still makes participants rather than the governed epistemic undertaking the organizing reference. It may romanticize plurality, hide Power and Responsibility, or infer beneficial emergence from interaction.

### E — Replace Human centrality with AI or system centrality

This is rejected. It reproduces the same category error with a different privileged subject and encourages model, platform, or implementation authority over meaning.

### F — Adopt the layered identity and governed-undertaking center

This is the proposed direction. It preserves history, the accepted DIA target, and operational focus while recognizing the broader Knowledge Engineering framework and its two Copernican corrections.

## 10. Counterexample and failure tests

The proposed identity must survive at least these cases:

1. A Human-only institutional team uses governed Knowledge, plural expertise, challenge, correction, and lineage. The framework may apply even when AI is absent; DIA status still requires the DR-015 conditions.
2. An AI system produces fluent answers without governed Questions, Evidence, responsibility, or consequence return. AI capability alone does not establish SymK compatibility or DIA status.
3. A Human expert has legitimate decision Authority but supplies a weakly grounded Answer. Authority does not manufacture Knowledge or Answer Quality.
4. An artificial system contributes material Reasoning while holding no legitimate decision Authority. Contribution can be real without Authority transfer.
5. A configured hybrid System exhibits an evidenced amplified capability while no component alone bears it. System-level capability may be attributed without transferring every property or responsibility to the whole.
6. A group interacts frequently but does not cooperate under DR-013. Plurality and communication do not manufacture Cooperation.
7. A knowledge graph preserves structure but erases dissent, uncertainty, or provenance. Representation does not own Knowledge or satisfy the three Views automatically.
8. A process improves latency and direct price while externalizing review, correction, environmental, professional, or affected-party costs. Optimization does not establish responsible Value.
9. A favorable outcome follows a poor Answer through luck or external intervention. Outcome does not validate every upstream relation.
10. A feedback dashboard records signals no competent recipient interprets or uses. Collection does not establish Feedback, Evidence, Correction, or Learning automatically.
11. A legal or professional authority requires a Human decision for a specific Domain. Non-anthropocentric Intelligence attribution does not override that authority.
12. A project uses SymK concepts for a bounded knowledge undertaking but never claims to be a DIA. Conceptual compatibility must not be converted into DIA status by label.

Failure against these cases reopens the affected part of the proposal.

## 12. Consequences if Stage-Accepted

Stage-Acceptance would establish a provisional program-level identity direction for integration, not immediate textual migration of every affected artifact.

### 12.1 Product Vision

The canonical Product Vision would require a controlled later revision that:

- states the broader Knowledge Engineering and governed-cooperation identity;
- preserves DIA creation as the principal realization and value-bearing objective rather than the exhaustive category;
- replaces any residual actor-, dyad-, pipeline-, or output-centered reading;
- preserves Knowledge as governed medium and epistemic achievement rather than a directly engineered product;
- integrates the recursive consequence, correction, and learning return;
- retains bounded jurisdiction, affected parties, external authority, and failure conditions.

This proposal does not perform that revision.

### 12.2 Integrated conceptual baseline

If separately opened, 2.1.9 must integrate DR-017 with DR-009–016 and test:

- whether the broader identity is supported by the complete dependency graph;
- whether “governed knowledge undertaking” is only an orientation or needs a disposition under DR-009;
- whether DIA remains central without exhausting the discipline;
- whether the center-of-gravity rule creates circularity, jurisdictional expansion, or hidden workflow assumptions;
- whether the layered identity remains intelligible across Human, Scientific, and Engineering Views;
- whether every affected 2.0 statement is preserved, revised, narrowed, or superseded explicitly.

### 12.3 Constitution and axiom review

2.2 must reconsider A0 and the Constitution's purpose language. Cooperation must not again become the final objective, but its constitutive role must remain protected. Candidate constitutional language must distinguish:

- purpose from process;
- participant capability from legitimate role and Authority;
- the general Knowledge Engineering framework from its DIA realization;
- semantic centrality from moral, legal, professional, or organizational authority;
- non-anthropocentric attribution from claims of equal standing or responsibility.

No axiom or constitutional article is selected or Ratified here.

### 12.4 Foundation Papers

The coordinated 2.1.10 review must express the transition across all four papers:

- **Intelligence:** the attributional and knowledge-engineering Copernican corrections, plural Bearers, non-transfer, and undertaking-relative contribution;
- **Knowledge:** the governed epistemic medium, question and reliance relations, and non-reification;
- **Knowledge Engineering:** the general framework, epistemic and cooperative conditions, three Views, recursive consequence return, and bounded jurisdiction;
- **Education for Cooperation:** Formation and continuing capability for responsible plural cooperation without making Cooperation the final objective.

The papers remain explanatory sources and do not acquire constitutional authority through publication.

### 12.5 Derived projects and Engineering

Derived projects would need to state:

- the governed knowledge undertaking and Domain purpose they instantiate;
- which participants are claimed as Intelligence Bearers and on what Evidence;
- whether they claim DIA, responsible-DIA, or SymK-compatible status and under which accepted conditions;
- which roles, Authority, Responsibility, affected-subject, challenge, and consequence relations apply;
- which concepts they inherit, specialize, represent, implement, or challenge;
- how operational evidence returns without allowing implementation to own meaning.

No project or implementation is changed by this proposal.

## 13. Jurisdiction and anti-expansion controls

The broader identity must not turn SymK into:

- a complete theory of Intelligence, Knowledge, mind, consciousness, rationality, or collective cognition;
- a universal scientific method, domain Ontology, Logic, ethics, law, economics, pedagogy, governance theory, or social philosophy;
- a mandatory architecture, workflow, agent framework, knowledge graph, ontology language, model stack, or platform;
- a claim that every knowledge activity is cooperation or every cooperative formation is a DIA;
- a license to override Domain, professional, legal, organizational, democratic, or affected-subject authority;
- a universal requirement for AI, Human review, automation, consensus, openness, or one form of representation;
- a guarantee that plurality, cooperation, amplification, learning, or system-level Intelligence is beneficial.

SymK governs only the universal epistemic, cooperative, responsibility, representation, value, consequence, and evolution conditions necessary to express and engineer its own identity and compatible realizations.
