## 4. Governing semantic effect

Acceptance establishes for continuation of 2.1 that:

1. Cooperation is the governed process in the Product Vision value chain, not its final objective or a sufficient moral, epistemic, or practical value.
2. Cooperation requires level-qualified material Contributions, mutual orientation, a bounded shared undertaking, and relational connection among distinguishable Participants.
3. Actual occurrence remains separate from responsible qualification and from answer, action, outcome, value, and consequence.
4. Harmful, coercively constrained, exploitative, or collusive Cooperation may be diagnosed without being endorsed; successful non-cooperative outcomes remain possible.
5. Participant, Contributor, actor, resource, source, beneficiary, affected party, Bearer, authority, and responsible party do not substitute for one another.
6. A cooperative unit is an analytical boundary rather than an automatic Participant, Intelligence Bearer, Learner, legal person, authority, or responsible party.
7. Formation is value-neutral and preserves intended/unintended, beneficial/harmful, integrative/assimilative, gain/loss, and present/historical distinctions.
8. Learning, Training, and Education are distinct, sometimes overlapping supporting concepts without universal co-entailment. Practice is broader than Training; persistence and architecture make none automatic.
9. Power capacity, exercise, mechanism, effect, qualification, authority, response, and record remain distinct; conflict and dissent may coexist with Cooperation.
10. Formal permission is not effective challengeability; the layered challenge path and affected-party horizon remain visible.
11. Episode, repetition, recurring pattern, continuing relation, persistent system, trajectory, current status, and record are different temporal objects.
12. Trace, storage, retrieval, procedural retention, institutional memory, distributed memory, and Learning remain distinct.
13. System Learning requires a stable level, integrated durable change, system-level mechanism, relevant variation, and alternative-explanation controls; predicates do not transfer automatically across component, member, institution, configured-system, distributed, or hybrid levels.
14. Human, Scientific, and Engineering Views must remain materially aligned with declared loss.
15. Claims, attributions, assessments, statuses, records, profiles, scores, workflows, and dashboards remain Representations or Projections rather than the represented occurrence, effect, achievement, memory, or capability.

The complete rules, prohibited entailments, 24-case treatment, and three-view obligations in accepted DR-013 govern over this summary.
