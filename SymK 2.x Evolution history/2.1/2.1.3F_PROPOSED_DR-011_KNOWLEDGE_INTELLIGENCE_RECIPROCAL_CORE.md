## 1. Decision question

> What minimum concepts of Knowledge and Intelligence must SymK govern, what can bear them, and how should their reciprocal relationship be expressed without making either concept a hidden definition, representation, performance result, authority status, or automatic implication of the other?

## 2. Proposed decision

> **SymK shall govern Knowledge and Intelligence as distinct foundational concepts. Knowledge is a scoped epistemic achievement attributable to a Bearer, in which success concerning what is the case, how to act, or a subject of familiarity is appropriately connected to truth, competence, or encounter rather than to mere luck, assertion, acceptance, or Representation. Intelligence is a scoped, substrate-realized capacity attributable to an organized Bearer to integrate available structure, signals, experience, and feedback in interpreting situations or producing effective responses across relevant variation, including cases not exhaustively fixed in advance.**
>
> **Knowledge and Intelligence do not definitionally presuppose one another, and an attribution of either does not automatically establish the other. Their reciprocal connections shall be governed as directional, typed, scoped, contingent, and challengeable relationships. Intelligence may enable the pursuit, achievement, interpretation, testing, connection, application, expression, and revision of Knowledge-related work. Knowledge may enable, constrain, scaffold, develop, specialize, and evidence Intelligence through realized Bearers, practices, and Representations. No single symmetric `reciprocal_with` or generic `related_to` relation preserves this meaning.**
>
> **Bearer remains the governed supporting attribution role established by DR-010. SymK shall distinguish an actual bearing relation, a fallible attribution claim, a governed attribution assessment, authority for a use, and an attribution record. Attribution is governed as a supporting relationship family; Knowledge Attribution and Intelligence Attribution use predicate-specific criteria; their records are Representations rather than the relations or predicates themselves.**
>
> **Propositional Knowledge requires truth and an appropriate non-accidental epistemic connection attributable to the Bearer. Practical Knowledge requires competence across a relevant task class. Familiarity Knowledge requires a direct or accumulated epistemically significant encounter. These modes may combine without being reduced to one test. Intelligence is a capacity rather than a performance event and must be attributed through an explicit Bearer, organized-system boundary, capability profile, relevant variation, Scope, Context, resources, dependencies, failures, alternatives, and temporal conditions. Minimal flexible response across relevant variation is part of the proposed Intelligence nucleus; online learning, agency, autonomy, rationality, consciousness, moral standing, authority, and responsibility are not universal entailments.**
>
> **Knowledge remains the governed epistemic medium in the SymK value chain, but operation and sharing occur through claims, grounds, methods, capabilities, histories, and other Representations. A document, graph, model, database, property assertion, score, status, approval, output, or repository does not become Knowledge or Intelligence merely by storage, structure, acceptance, computation, or successful use. Engineering can construct conditions and projections that enable epistemic achievement and capability development without manufacturing truth, Knowledge, or Intelligence by declaration.**
>
> **Every material Knowledge or Intelligence attribution shall preserve, at an appropriate level for its consequence, predicate identity and version; Bearer identity and stable boundary; Domain, Scope, Context, time, and criteria; evidence, failures, alternatives, resources, assistance, and dependencies; source, assessor, authority, intended use, status, uncertainty, challenge, and lineage. Derived projects own Domain-specific propositions, type schemes, competence standards, capability profiles, thresholds, tests, role allocations, and technical projections while preserving this universal minimum.**

## 4. Required distinctions

If accepted, later SymK work must preserve at least:

- Knowledge from knowledge claim, truth alone, grounds alone, asset, Representation, data, information, status, consensus, reliance, and authority;
- propositional Knowledge, practical Knowledge, and familiarity without forcing one mode's criteria onto every other mode;
- Intelligence from substrate, organized System, capability generally, competence, skill, performance, profile, score, participant type, and attribution;
- flexible response from persistent adaptation, learning, development, design, and evolution;
- Intelligence from agency, autonomy, rationality, understanding, consciousness, wisdom, moral standing, authority, authorization, and responsibility;
- actual bearing from attribution claim, assessment, adoption for use, and stored record;
- Bearer from source, evidence producer, assessor, authority, actor, represented subject, owner, responsible party, and affected party;
- actual Scope from declared Scope, current Context, Domain, Applicability, and authority;
- epistemic enabling from constitutive dependency, evidence, development, operation, Representation, and authorization;
- and universal SymK meaning from Domain specialization and engineering Projection.

## 5. Attribution and boundary rules

1. **Explanatory level:** attribute at the lowest organized level that explains the predicate without omitting constitutive integration.
2. **Stable boundary:** keep the success boundary visible when analyzing failure, dependencies, cost, authority, responsibility, and consequence.
3. **Resource/component distinction:** disclose every material dependency; include it in the Bearer only when coupling is sufficiently stable, integrated, and identity-relevant.
4. **Temporal validity:** state which changes in participants, models, tools, data, procedures, or environment preserve, restrict, supersede, or replace the attribution.
5. **Multiple levels:** permit separately evidenced individual, component, collective, institutional, distributed, and hybrid attributions without copying one predicate automatically across levels.
6. **No origin privilege:** biological, artificial, social, or hybrid origin proves or disproves neither predicate by itself.
7. **No role transfer:** capability or Knowledge does not automatically grant authority, permission, responsibility, legal personality, or moral standing.

## 6. Reciprocal relation rules

### Intelligence toward Knowledge

Permitted universal relation types include `pursues`, `contributes_to_achievement_of`, `interprets`, `tests`, `challenges`, `connects`, `applies`, `expresses_or_projects`, and `revises_contribution_or_representation`, each qualified by capability, role, Domain, Scope, Context, evidence, and authority where relevant.

### Knowledge toward Intelligence

Permitted universal relation types include `enables`, `scaffolds`, `constrains`, `develops`, `specializes`, `evidences`, and `persists_as_reusable_support`, each mediated by an actual Bearer, process, resource, practice, or Representation.

### Prohibited universal implications

```text
Knowledge attribution    -/-> Intelligence attribution
Intelligence attribution -/-> Knowledge attribution
stored representation    -/-> Knowledge or Intelligence
successful performance   -/-> Knowledge or Intelligence
Knowledge or Intelligence -/-> authority, authorization,
                              responsibility, consciousness,
                              legal personality, or moral standing
```

The relation between practical Knowledge and Intelligence remains a preserved overlap question. Some cases may satisfy both predicates through competence across variation; no universal identity or implication is accepted by this proposal.

## 7. Alternatives considered

### A — Adopt both Foundation Paper v0.1 definitions unchanged

**Rejected:** each definition partially uses the other, responsible reliance risks becoming constitutive of Knowledge, and online adaptation and Knowledge risk becoming universal components of Intelligence.

### B — Define Knowledge through whatever an Intelligence possesses or produces

**Rejected:** circular, makes output and bearer labels epistemically authoritative, and cannot handle true claims accepted for defective reasons.

### C — Define Intelligence through accumulated Knowledge or successful performance

**Rejected:** confuses content with capacity, skill with acquisition/transfer, and one event with a stable profile.

### D — Treat both as project-local labels with no SymK minimum

**Rejected:** projects could redefine storage as Knowledge and product type or score as Intelligence while claiming compatibility.

### E — Select one scalar, formal system, ontology, or implementation model

**Rejected:** projection choices would acquire premature S2 authority and suppress plural modes, profiles, attribution evidence, and semantic loss.

### F — Independent anchors plus common attribution and typed reciprocity

**Proposed:** preserves the accepted Product Vision, survives the mandatory counterexamples, supports Domain specialization, and remains expressible in both semantic lanes without choosing a final representation.

## 9. Consequences if accepted

### Product Vision

The existing value chain remains intact. “Knowledge as medium” receives a non-container interpretation; Intelligences require evidence-based participant and role attribution; Amplified Intelligence later requires an explicit Bearer and capacity increase rather than additional content, access, output, or performance alone.

### Candidate Constitution

- A1 must distinguish Knowledge from attribution, asset, status, and reliance and avoid defining warrant solely through authorized reliance.
- A2 must distinguish actual capability from capability attribution and preserve stable boundaries and separate responsibility allocation.
- A4 must qualify epistemic activities by capability and role and explain that expression, preservation, sharing, and revision operate through Representations and governed processes.

No axiom is revised or Ratified by DR-011.

### Foundation Papers

The v0.2 Knowledge and Intelligence papers must use the independent anchors, shared attribution vocabulary, typed reciprocity, and preserved dissent. Knowledge Engineering must explain the mediated cycle without treating an artifact as either predicate. Education must later distinguish Knowledge-mediated formation from content delivery and capability development from conditioned output. Publication remains 2.1.10.

### Derived projects and engineering

Derived projects inherit the universal meanings and own Domain specializations. A legal `DocumentType` and a medical `DocumentType` may specialize one qualified classification relationship without sharing a value scheme. Neutral metadata stores preserve full assertions; SharePoint, AWS, filesystems, search indexes, MySQL JSON, APIs, and code types are declared Projections. No current semantic package, schema, or runtime assertion is promoted by acceptance.

## 11. Preserved dissent and open questions

1. Knowledge may be better understood as a standing state or relation than an “achievement” in some modes.
2. The anti-luck phrase “appropriately connected” remains underdetermined until mode- and Domain-specific criteria are supplied.
3. Belief, epistemic commitment, uptake, or another condition may be required for propositional Knowledge depending on Bearer class.
4. Every actual Intelligence may necessarily possess at least practical Knowledge even though cross-attribution is not licensed from current evidence.
5. Every actual Knowledge Bearer may require a minimal Intelligence even though Knowledge can be independently defined.
6. The threshold separating capability, competence, practical Knowledge, and Intelligence remains contestable.
7. “Integration,” “relevant variation,” and “not exhaustively fixed in advance” require Domain-operational criteria.
8. Institutions, collectives, distributed systems, and artificial systems may literally bear the predicates or may warrant only explanatory shorthand.
9. Understanding may be a Knowledge mode, Intelligence dimension, both, or a distinct concept.
10. “Knowledge as medium” may retain a misleading container implication unless v0.2 explanatory text is precise.
11. Actual Scope can exceed or differ from declared Scope and may remain partly unknowable.
12. The lowest explanatory Bearer rule may require revision for multi-level emergence and distributed causation.

## 12. Confidence and reopening conditions

**Confidence:** High in the need for two distinct foundational concepts; High in the separation of bearing, claim, assessment, authority, and record; High in performance/Representation/status/origin non-substitution; High in directional typed reciprocity; Medium-High in the proposed anchor wording; Medium in practical-Knowledge overlap, thresholds, and collective/artificial literal bearing.

Reopen or revise if:

- 2.1.4 cannot preserve Knowledge, attribution, asset, status, and reliance without contradiction;
- practical Knowledge and Intelligence prove inseparable across the relevant counterexamples;
- every viable bearer account produces a universal cross-dependency excluded here;
- simple adaptive systems satisfy the Intelligence wording without the intended organized capacity;
- fixed capable systems are wrongly excluded by the variation condition;
- multi-level attribution cannot be expressed without contradictory Bearers;
- 2.1.6 requires agency or normative responsiveness for every SymK Intelligence participant;
- 2.1.7 cannot distinguish amplification from accumulated content, access, or performance;
- 2.5 cannot preserve the required relationships without semantic duplication or loss;
- or cross-domain evidence shows the universal minimum to be unusable or harmful.
