## 1. Purpose

This record preserves five material Human-review observations, their controlled dispositions and the corrective review objects produced in response. It does not represent the project steward's complete reading, a complete Human-review declaration, publication acceptance or checkpoint 2.1.10H.

The corrective objective is newcomer-first progressive disclosure:

- the main paper must state its purpose, audience, central question and practical meaning without requiring the evolution history;
- the main argument must remain understandable without a companion paper;
- deeper conceptual, historical, scientific and engineering detail remains available to readers who pursue it;
- recurring technical terms receive plain-language orientation before specialist use; and
- governance status remains visible without displacing the reader's entry into the subject.

## 2. Human findings

The project steward reported:

1. v0.2 is too close to a direct Markdown-to-PDF projection and is extremely difficult for a newcomer;
2. unexplained technical expressions and the absence of a glossary create a material misunderstanding risk;
3. an abstract should explain what SymK is before presenting project history;
4. the v0.1 PDFs were materially friendlier than the v0.2 projections; and
5. the papers lack a clear purpose-and-audience statement.

These findings are recorded as SYMK-2X-HF-001 through SYMK-2X-HF-005 in the controlled Stream-H response CSV. Each receives the required REVISE_V0.2 disposition. Their implementation status is IMPLEMENTED_PENDING_HUMAN_REREAD, not accepted or closed.

## 3. Corrective architecture

Each v0.2 paper now contains, before the specialist body:

- a concise review-status cue;
- an explicit purpose, audience, central question and authority boundary;
- a plain-language answer to “What is SymK?”;
- a short reading path and a full reading path;
- one coordinated bridge-inspection example, interpreted from the paper's own center;
- essential local terms in plain language;
- a rewritten abstract that begins with SymK and the paper's problem rather than project history; and
- a detailed source-control block placed after the reader has been oriented.

The numbered technical body, citations, distinctions, counterexamples, dissent, live questions, references and governance note remain present for deeper reading.

## 4. Shared companion

SymK_Reader_Orientation_and_Shared_Glossary_v0.1.md and its PDF provide:

- a plain-language map of the four-paper conversation;
- the coordinated bridge-inspection example;
- the branching and recursive lifecycle in ordinary language;
- the Human, Scientific and Engineering Views;
- a shared current-use glossary; and
- focused safeguards for affected-subject standing/challenge, Evidence and urgency.

The companion is explicitly non-canonical. It does not replace any paper, accepted decision, future vocabulary, contract, ontology, workflow or implementation specification. If a paper's main argument requires the companion, the paper remains deficient.
