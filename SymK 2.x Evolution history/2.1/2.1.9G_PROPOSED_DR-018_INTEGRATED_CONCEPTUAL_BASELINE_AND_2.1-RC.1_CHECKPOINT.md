## 2. Proposed decision

Stage 2.1 is proposed to adopt the following integrated conceptual baseline.

### 2.1 Authority and preservation

> **The exact Stage-Accepted texts of DR-009 through DR-017 remain controlling. DR-018 integrates, indexes and constrains their combined interpretation; it does not silently rewrite, merge, narrow, expand or supersede them. Where a summary in this proposal conflicts with an exact accepted object, the exact accepted object governs unless the project steward explicitly invokes and decides an applicable reopening path.**

> **The integrated baseline contains seventeen Foundational anchors and no eighteenth anchor. It preserves the complete supporting, compound, imported, derived, Engineering, excluded and deferred dispositions of DR-009–017. Semantic admission, governance status and artifact form remain separate. The final `FC-*` package anatomy, identifiers, formal semantics and implementation remain owned by later stages.**

### 2.2 Integrated SymK identity

> **SymK retains a layered identity: its historical Human–AI symbiotic-cooperation protocol remains lineage; its Stage-Accepted DIA orientation remains durable historical authority and principal realization; and its broader identity is the engineering discipline and general framework for responsible Knowledge Engineering through governed cooperation among heterogeneous forms and Bearers of Intelligence. DIA is principal but non-exhaustive. SymK does not thereby claim all Knowledge Engineering, Intelligence, inquiry, science, ethics, law, organization, education or social cooperation.**

> **The governed knowledge undertaking remains a semantically eliminable, non-reifying analytical organizing center. It is expressed through accepted Questions, epistemic conditions, Bearers, Contributions, Cooperation, Responsibility, Authority, Representations, Answers, Value, consequences, challenge, Correction, Learning and lineage. It is not admitted as a Foundational concept, System class, legal object, project, workflow, actor, sovereign authority or synonym for DIA.**

### 2.3 Structural and epistemic system

> **Identity, Context, Scope, Domain and Representation govern the structural minimum. Relationship, Applicability, Bearer and Projection remain governed supporting distinctions; System remains imported support; Entity remains an Engineering/meta-model candidate rather than a universal semantic parent. No identifier, record, schema, graph, formalism or implementation substitutes for the subject it represents.**

> **Knowledge and Intelligence remain distinct Foundational concepts with typed reciprocal relations. Knowledge, claim, Ground, Evidence, Provenance, assessment, status, reliance, asset, record, Document, Representation and Projection remain non-substitutable. Intelligence is attributed only to an adequate organized Bearer for a scoped multidimensional capacity under Evidence and relevant variation; capability, performance, profile, score, Agency, Authority, Responsibility, origin and moral or legal status do not entail one another.**

> **Knowledge Engineering remains the disciplined engineering of epistemic conditions, not the manufacture of truth or Knowledge. The lifecycle remains recursive and challengeable. Human, Scientific and Engineering Views are the adequate current universal minimum; they preserve lived meaning and affected standing, construct and evidential validity, and inspectable realizability respectively. Transformation among Views declares source, target, method, version, omission, uncertainty, authority, reversibility and material loss. No View is sovereign.**

### 2.4 Cooperation, formation, responsibility and authority

> **Cooperation remains a thin actual relational process and not the final objective, moral sufficiency, consent, legitimacy, Value or favorable outcome. Formation remains a distinct developmental concept; Learning, Training and Education remain non-identical and do not universally entail one another. Power, participation, contribution, challenge, memory, persistence, correction and system-level claims retain their accepted level and Evidence controls.**

> **Responsibility remains a sourced, scoped, phased and challengeable relationship family. Agency, causal Contribution, control, role, Authority, Authorization, decision, delegation, supervision, answerability, accountability, blame, liability, standing, consent, right, duty and remedy remain distinct. Eleven typed authority kinds remain analytically distinguished across bounded governance loci. Affected-subject standing/challenge remains an unresolved locus and is not declared a settled twelfth authority kind.**

> **Evidence may challenge claims, premises, Grounds, assumptions, applicability, consequences, decisions and exercises of authority. Evidence does not automatically amend an authority source or governed artifact; change follows competent procedure. Material risk or consequence triggers urgent consideration by competent operational or external authority and does not create an emergency duty, containment authority, decision right or remedy by analytical implication.**

### 2.5 Amplification, DIA and professional continuity

> **Amplification remains a scoped, temporal and evidenced comparative relationship concerning an identified Bearer and materially comparable baseline. Amplified Intelligence remains a governed compound attribution. DIA remains the accepted seven-condition specific System class. Assistance, access, automation, substitution, replacement, performance, productivity, one better Answer, provider designation, certificate, AI presence or marketing label does not establish Amplification or DIA status.**

> **Integration, dependency, lifecycle, professional transformation, retained capability, deskilling and capability continuity remain level-qualified and non-scalar. Responsible or SymK-compatible operation is an additional governed qualification, never entailed by descriptive DIA membership or project success.**

### 2.6 Questions, reasoning, answers, value and recursive return

> **Question, Reasoning, Answer and Value remain Foundational. The Product Vision chain is branching, defeasible, recursive and non-automatic: Domain situations may be framed as Questions; inquiry, Evidence acquisition and Reasoning may contribute to Answers; Answers may be assessed for multiple qualities, time, total cost and plural Value; recommendations may inform authorized decisions and actions; outcomes and consequences may be observed and challenged; and feedback, response, Correction, Learning or Formation may return to any earlier object. Every transition is typed, scoped, temporal, independently evidenced and challengeable. No downstream state retroactively manufactures an upstream predicate.**

> **Prompt, task, query, output, explanation, trace, recommendation, decision, action, quality score, latency, price, benefit, outcome, feedback, update or record does not substitute for Question, Reasoning, Answer, Value, Evidence, Correction or Learning. Better, faster and lower total cost remain independent comparisons within a plural constrained-value profile, not one universal scalar objective.**

### 2.7 Jurisdiction and realization

> **SymK owns the universal semantic minimum and minimum claim-preservation obligations stated in the accepted decisions. Constitutional stages own normative force and conflict procedure. Domains, sciences, professions, legal and political systems, ethical authorities, organizations, communities, projects, providers and assessors retain competent jurisdiction over their methods, facts, roles, thresholds, policies, duties, rights, remedies and actual decisions. Affected-subject standing/challenge remains separately visible without a settled universal authority classification. Representations and implementations remain fallible realization artifacts and do not own universal meaning.**

No clause in this artifact has Stage-Accepted authority until explicit project-steward approval.
