## 1. Decision question

> What minimum conceptual system must SymK govern for Questions, Reasoning, Answers, Answer Quality, time, total cost, Value, outcomes, consequences, feedback, correction, and Learning so that a prompt, output, fluent explanation, fast response, low price, favorable outcome, score change, or implementation record cannot automatically substitute for a better and responsible Answer to a complex Domain Question?

## 2. Proposed decision

> **SymK shall govern Question as a Foundational concept. A Question is a scoped and challengeable inquiry object relating identified subject matter to what is sought under a material Domain, Scope, Context, purpose, assumptions, stakeholders and affected horizon, uncertainty, and answer obligation. Question Identity may persist across wording, language, interface, equivalent decomposition, and faithful Representation, but material change to subject, assumptions, scope, purpose, answer obligation, affected horizon, or required answer form creates a new version or Question whose relation to the prior object shall be recorded. Prompt, request, task instruction, search query, test item, ticket, workflow, decision problem, formulation, or record may represent or operationalize a Question but does not own its Identity or meaning.**
>
> **SymK shall govern Complex Question as a governed supporting relational qualification of Question rather than an independently Foundational concept. A Question is complex where material interaction, uncertainty, contest, distribution, change, cross-level or cross-jurisdictional dependence, consequence, reflexivity, or non-decomposability makes framing, inquiry, decomposition, recomposition, answer, use, or revision materially non-trivial. Length, difficulty, expense, duration, AI use, DIA status, organizational importance, or provider designation does not establish complexity. Domain-specific criteria and thresholds remain with competent Domain and scientific authority.**
>
> **Domain situation, Domain problem, problem claim, frame, framing act, Question, formulation, prompt, request, task, query, test, decision problem, inquiry, operation, assessment, status, and record shall remain distinct. Decomposition is valid only relative to the governed Question and shall preserve material interactions, dependencies, assumptions, affected subjects, uncertainty, and recomposition obligations. Reframing, narrowing, translation, or changed answer form may be legitimate but shall not be reported as answering, accelerating, improving, or lowering the cost of the prior Question without declared lineage and comparison consequences.**
>
> **SymK shall govern Reasoning as a Foundational concept. Reasoning is a scoped, temporally situated, consideration-sensitive process in which an identified Human, artificial, collective, institutional, distributed, hybrid, or other organized arrangement relates material considerations in pursuit of a conclusion, judgment, Answer, decision support, action orientation, Question revision, or qualified no-conclusion. A material Reasoning claim preserves the Question version; reasoner or arrangement, level, Domain, Scope, Context, episode, time, and purpose; premises, assumptions, Grounds, Evidence, counterevidence, rules, models, alternatives, conclusions, defeaters, uncertainty, and applicability; operations and contributions; process Evidence, assessment, challenge, status, and lineage.**
>
> **Reasoning is not identical to inference, derivation, proof, computation, calculation, search, retrieval, association, simulation, generation, deliberation, judgment, explanation, justification, rationale, presentation, trace, or output. These may participate in or represent Reasoning under separately evidenced relations. Formal validity does not establish premise truth, soundness, real-world truth, relevance, warrant, robustness, Applicability, or responsible use. Correct content does not prove the claimed process; an incorrect conclusion does not prove that no Reasoning occurred.**
>
> **An actual Reasoning episode, operation, product, trace or Representation, claim or assessment, and governance status are distinct. A fluent explanation, displayed chain, generated rationale, proof object, log, citation list, model architecture, provider statement, Human review, or system label is not automatic Evidence of the actual causal or epistemic process. SymK shall require proportionate process Evidence and protected inspectability appropriate to consequence and jurisdiction; it shall not require universal verbatim disclosure of internal chains where those chains are unavailable, misleading, unsafe, privacy-sensitive, security-sensitive, legally protected, or not causally faithful.**
>
> **SymK shall govern Answer as a Foundational relational concept. An Answer is scoped and challengeable content presented or available as satisfying, partially satisfying, conditionally satisfying, negatively satisfying, or warrantedly declining an identified Question version and answer obligation. Answer content, occurrence, Representation, claim, assessment, status, adoption or use, and record or lineage remain distinct. Answer Identity preserves its Question correspondence, material content, coverage, conditions, qualifications, uncertainty, sources and contributions, and version relations across faithful Representations.**
>
> **Response, reply, acknowledgment, output, interpretation, explanation, recommendation, proposal, decision support, plan, strategy, instruction, commitment, selection, adoption, approval, Ratification, decision, execution, action, omission, outcome, and consequence are non-substitutable. A true or fluent output may fail to answer the governed Question. A partial, plural, conditional, negative, abstaining, or qualified no-answer may be the responsible Answer form. An Answer may inform a recommendation or decision but grants no Authority, Authorization, adoption, execution, Responsibility, favorable outcome, or value automatically.**
>
> **SymK shall govern Answer Quality as a governed multidimensional compound assessment-profile family rather than one scalar Foundational property. A quality profile relates an identified Answer version to an identified Question version, answer obligation, intended use, Context, affected horizon, applicable dimensions, Evidence, method, assessors, uncertainty, disagreement, distribution, challenge, and lineage. Correctness, accuracy, truth, relevance, responsiveness, completeness, coverage, precision, support, warrant, reasoning adequacy, explanation, comprehensibility, calibration, uncertainty, robustness, transfer, timeliness, actionability, usability, accessibility, safety, security, privacy, fairness, legality, compliance, Authority, Value, and outcome remain distinct dimensions, constraints, or adjacent assessments whose inclusion and meaning are declared.**
>
> **Unknown, not assessed, not applicable, disputed, failed, distributed, and non-compensable states shall remain visible. No average, score, benchmark, ranking, threshold, weight, certification, approval, assessor status, fluent presentation, confidence, adoption, compliance, or favorable outcome may erase a material failed or unknown dimension. Thresholds, weights, aggregation rules, benchmarks, and Reliance Authorization require competent purpose-specific authority and do not create truth, quality, Value, or responsible use. A responsible-answer profile is a governed compound of applicable quality, Evidence, uncertainty, review, affected-subject, Authority, use, correction, and consequence conditions, not a universal status or fixed workflow.**
>
> **SymK shall govern speed and time as scoped supporting temporal comparisons. Immediate response latency, generation time, inquiry duration, Evidence acquisition, verification, coordination, decision, action, correction, recovery, and lifecycle time are distinct intervals or event relations. Time to responsible Answer is the elapsed and structured time required for an identified Answer version to reach a declared reliance-ready condition for a declared use under applicable quality, Evidence, review, Authority, affected-subject, and correction requirements. “Faster” shall identify comparable start and end events, subjects, included work, waiting, concurrency, Context, quality obligations, uncertainty, and horizon. Faster does not entail better, lower total cost, greater Value, responsible use, or favorable outcome.**
>
> **SymK shall govern cost as a scoped supporting attribution relation and total cost as a governed compound profile. A cost is a burden, resource use, sacrifice, exposure, or foregone alternative attributed under a declared method to an identified cost object and Bearer over a declared boundary, counterfactual, and time. A total-cost profile is a bounded material-completeness claim covering applicable direct, indirect, fixed, variable, contingent, integration, verification, supervision, transition, opportunity, dependency, capability, correction, harm, remedy, externalized, and residual cost families; payers, funders, providers, beneficiaries, affected subjects, and cost Bearers; transfer, allocation, distribution, uncertainty, and exclusions. “Total” does not claim omniscience. Price, payment, budget, compute, labor, or provider expense alone does not establish total cost.**
>
> **SymK shall govern Value as a Foundational relational concept. Value is a scoped and challengeable evaluative relation connecting an identified object, state, change, or alternative to identified claimants, beneficiaries, affected subjects, purposes, commitments, criteria, authorities, counterfactuals, time horizons, uncertainty, and distributions. Utility, usefulness, benefit, preference satisfaction, goal attainment, epistemic, professional, economic, public, moral, and market value are non-substitutable families. Value may be positive, negative, mixed, conditional, disputed, incomparable, unknown, or not applicable. No one value function, stakeholder set, currency, weighting, aggregation, outcome, market, institution, or implementation has universal SymK authority.**
>
> **Benefit, harm, avoided harm, preserved capability, compatibility, target, objective, expected outcome, actual outcome, consequence, side effect, residual effect, causal Contribution, attribution, and Responsibility remain distinct. An outcome is a realized Domain-relevant state or change assessed against an identified target, expectation, baseline, or counterfactual. A consequence is an effect or changed condition associated causally or practically with an Answer, decision, action, omission, system, or outcome across identified subjects, boundaries, and time. Favorable outcome does not validate the Question, Reasoning, Answer, quality, Value, Authorization, or Responsibility chain automatically; adverse outcome does not falsify it automatically.**
>
> **“Better,” “faster,” and “lower total cost” are independently evidenced comparison families within a plural constrained-value profile, not one scalar objective. A trade-off is a scoped comparative claim that alternatives differ across two or more material dimensions, subjects, horizons, or constraints such that a gain, preservation, or reduction in one accompanies or requires a loss, burden, risk, or forgone gain in another under stated assumptions. A material trade-off claim shall preserve alternatives, affected subjects, gains, losses, baselines, counterfactuals, constraints, avoidability, causal basis, Authority, non-compensable conditions, uncertainty, distribution, reversibility, correction, repair, residual effects, and lineage. Correlation, provider inconvenience, current design, budget convention, transferred burden, aggregate score, or optimization result does not establish necessity or legitimacy.**
>
> **SymK shall retain Feedback, Uptake, Correction, and Learning as governed supporting concepts under DR-013 and Formation as Foundational under DR-013; DR-016 does not reclassify them. Feedback is a scoped information-or-consequence return relation in which an identified signal, observation, assessment, response, or effect concerning an identified object becomes available to an identified potential receiver or governing process relative to a target, expectation, prior action, result, standard, policy, or state. Feedback may be absent, blocked, delayed, distorted, selective, manipulated, harmful, or irrelevant. Occurrence, observation, signal, measurement, Evidence, counterevidence, feedback, transmission, receipt, interpretation, uptake, response, correction, change, Learning, Formation, and improvement remain distinct.**
>
> **A complaint, challenge, anomaly, alert, rating, reward, dashboard, outcome, or consequence may become feedback but does not become valid Evidence without an identified Ground, claim relation, integrity assessment, relevance, uncertainty, and competent evaluation. Transmission does not entail receipt; receipt does not entail interpretation; interpretation does not entail uptake; uptake does not entail agreement, correction, capability change, Learning, or improvement. Rejection, deferral, non-use, and contest may be warranted.**
>
> **Correction is a scoped response-and-change family directed at an identified error, defect, deviation, harmful state, claim, record, process, decision, system, or consequence. Defect claim, assessment, correction proposal, Authority, decision, corrective action, actual changed state, verification, communication, restriction, withdrawal, rollback, repair, remedy, compensation, recurrence prevention, residual effect, and historical record remain distinct. A wording edit, apology, rollback, changed output, closed ticket, or “fixed” status does not establish verified correction. Correction shall preserve the prior state, uses, consequences, reasons, authority, challenge, and lineage rather than overwrite history.**
>
> **Update, adaptation, behavior change, capability change, Learning, Formation, and improvement remain distinct. Learning retains DR-013's requirement for an identified stable Learner boundary and level; comparatively durable experience-, inquiry-, practice-, feedback-, interaction-, or adaptation-linked change in an identified capacity, disposition, practice, judgment, relationship, or organized pattern; manifestation beyond the immediate support condition under relevant variation; and Evidence, uncertainty, competing-explanation, version, challenge, and lineage controls. Selection, replacement, redesign, retrieval, current Context, external intervention, component change, or score change may explain output differences without Learning. Member Learning does not establish System Learning. Learning may be harmful or value-neutral and does not entail improvement.**
>
> **Consequences may return recursively to validation, Responsibility, policy, concept, and constitutional review, but no return path grants automatic promotion or authority. Occurrence does not create observation; observation does not create measurement or Evidence; Evidence does not create conclusion; consequence does not allocate Responsibility; complaint does not establish defect; response does not establish correction; update does not establish Learning; repeated project practice does not create universal SymK policy or meaning; and candidate propositions do not become constitutional commitments. Every return preserves competent jurisdiction, challenge, uncertainty, decision, versioning, migration, and historical lineage.**
>
> **Human, Scientific, and Engineering Views shall preserve materially aligned meaning without substitution. The Human View preserves lived meaning, participation, affected subjects, Power, trust, burden, challenge, refusal, response, and repair. The Scientific View preserves construct validity, hypotheses, causal and epistemic Evidence, sampling, measurement, uncertainty, defeaters, reproducibility, and generalization. The Engineering View preserves identities, versions, schemas, events, states, dependencies, provenance, protected inspectability, monitoring, correction, revalidation, and interoperability. Every transformation or Projection declares source and target View, object, version, method, aggregation, omission, uncertainty, authority, reversibility, and material loss.**
>
> **SymK owns these universal conceptual distinctions and minimum claim obligations. Constitutional stages own normative force and conflict rules. Domains, sciences, professions, legal systems, ethical and political authorities, organizations, projects, providers, assessors, and affected authorities own applicable Question types, reasoning methods, quality dimensions, cost recognition, valuation criteria, outcome models, metrics, thresholds, weights, policies, duties, rights, remedies, and actual trade-offs within jurisdiction. Engineering artifacts may represent or project the system but do not own its meaning.**

No clause has authority until the project steward explicitly Stage-Accepts this proposal.

## 4. Consolidated semantic spine

The proposed decision governs a branching and recursive relation system, not a guaranteed pipeline:

```text
Domain situation or problem
    -> frame / affected horizon / governed Question
    -> inquiry, Evidence acquisition, Reasoning, and adjacent operations
    -> Answer content-to-Question relation
    -> multidimensional Answer-Quality and responsible-answer assessment
    -> temporal, total-cost, plural-Value, benefit/harm, and trade-off assessment
    -> recommendation / decision support
    -> authorized decision / Action / omission
    -> actual outcome / causal Contribution / distributed consequences
    -> observation / signal / measurement / feedback / challenge
    -> interpretation / uptake / response / Correction / repair / remedy / update
    -> capability change / Learning / Formation / improvement assessment
    -> possible revision of Question, Knowledge, Reasoning, Answer, policy,
       Responsibility, concept, or constitutional commitment
```

Every node can be absent, plural, distributed, contested, unknown, failed, superseded, or not applicable. Every arrow is typed, scoped, temporal, challengeable, and independently evidenced. No downstream state retroactively manufactures an upstream predicate.

## 5. Nine analytical planes

1. **Question and inquiry:** Domain problem, frame, Question, obligation, assumptions, decomposition, affected horizon, and inquiry.
2. **Reasoning and epistemic process:** episode, operations, inference regimes, considerations, product, process Evidence, trace, assessment, and status.
3. **Answer and governance:** content, occurrence, Representation, correspondence, recommendation, decision, Action, status, use, and lineage.
4. **Quality and responsible reliance:** dimensions, applicability, Evidence, assessor, profile, uncertainty, distribution, threshold Authority, challenge, and use.
5. **Time, cost, and constrained Value:** temporal phases, cost objects/families/Bearers, Value claims/kinds, benefit/harm, alternatives, and trade-offs.
6. **Decision, causality, and consequence:** targets, expected and actual outcomes, Action, causal Contribution, side/residual effects, and affected subjects.
7. **Feedback and response:** observation, signal, measurement, Evidence role, feedback, challenge, reception, interpretation, uptake, response, and correction.
8. **Formation and Learning:** update, adaptation, behavior, capability, durability, manifestation, Learner, Formation, Learning, and competing explanations.
9. **Governance, Views, and lineage:** Authority, Authorization, Responsibility, jurisdiction, three Views, record, version, correction, revalidation, and supersession.

The planes overlap through governed relations but do not collapse into one workflow, taxonomy, score, optimization problem, schema, or database object.

## 7. Core Question, Reasoning, and Answer rules

1. Question Identity follows material inquiry relations rather than string identity.
2. Equivalent wording or translation can preserve one Question; a small material assumption change can create another.
3. A frame is challengeable and does not become correct through Authority or adoption.
4. Decomposition does not preserve a Complex Question unless material interactions and recomposition obligations survive.
5. Inquiry may end in an Answer, qualified no-answer, reframing, suspension, or unresolved state.
6. Reasoning attribution selects the lowest adequate organized level supported by process Evidence.
7. Inference, computation, retrieval, generation, simulation, judgment, and explanation are individually classified rather than universally promoted to Reasoning.
8. Validity, soundness, truth, warrant, relevance, robustness, Applicability, and responsible use remain non-scalar and non-substitutable.
9. A trace is a fallible Representation whose relation to the actual process shall be evidenced.
10. Protected inspectability balances challenge with privacy, security, confidentiality, safety, and legal constraints without erasing evidentiary obligations.
11. Answer Identity requires a Question relation and material content continuity.
12. Partial, plural, incompatible, conditional, negative, and abstaining Answers preserve their exact coverage and uncertainty.
13. Answer sources and contributions remain distinct from authorship, approval, Authority, use, and Responsibility.
14. Recommendation, decision support, decision, Action, outcome, and consequence require their own objects, roles, authority, Evidence, and lineage.
15. Correction, expiry, revalidation, restriction, withdrawal, supersession, and historical retention are distinct lifecycle relations.

## 8. Quality, time, cost, Value, and consequence rules

1. Answer Quality is assessed for an identified Answer/Question/use relation, never for text in the abstract.
2. Dimension inventories are purpose- and Domain-sensitive while the non-substitution grammar is universal.
3. Unknown, unassessed, disputed, failed, and non-applicable dimensions remain visible.
4. Non-compensable conditions may block use regardless of aggregate profile under competent authority.
5. Assessor competence, assessment validity, threshold Authority, and reliance decision remain distinct.
6. “Faster” identifies the relevant clock and stable comparison conditions.
7. Immediate latency cannot stand for time to responsible Answer or lifecycle time without Evidence.
8. “Lower total cost” identifies cost objects, families, Bearers, boundary, counterfactual, horizon, allocation, uncertainty, transfer, externalization, distribution, and residuals.
9. Cost attribution does not allocate Responsibility automatically.
10. Value claims identify claimant, beneficiaries, affected subjects, purpose, criteria, authority, alternatives, counterfactual, horizon, uncertainty, and distribution.
11. No value family or currency universally subsumes all others.
12. Aggregate benefit cannot erase non-consensual, non-remediable, or otherwise non-compensable loss.
13. Target, expected outcome, actual outcome, consequence, causal Contribution, side effect, residual, and later revision remain distinct.
14. Favorable consequence can update a claim only through governed Evidence and causal assessment.
15. Trade-off necessity shall be distinguished from current design, provider boundary, budget convention, Power, and avoidable burden transfer.

## 9. Feedback, correction, Learning, and return rules

1. Observation records selected aspects under an identified mode; it is not complete access or truth.
2. Signal, measurement, Evidence, counterevidence, and feedback are distinct relations.
3. A feedback source, object, content, reference, potential receiver, timing, and transformation shall be identifiable where material.
4. Feedback can be missing, blocked, delayed, distorted, manipulated, selective, harmful, or irrelevant.
5. Challenge and complaint retain governance importance even before their factual claims are accepted.
6. Transmission, receipt, interpretation, uptake, rejection, response, and correction are separately evidenced transitions.
7. Correction preserves defect claim, Evidence, proposal, Authority, action, changed state, verification, repair/remedy, recurrence, residual, and history.
8. Correction never authorizes historical erasure.
9. Update, adaptation, behavior change, capability change, Learning, Formation, and improvement remain distinct.
10. System Learning requires evidence at the stable System Learner level, not component or member change copied upward.
11. Consequence-to-validation return can trigger revalidation, restriction, expiry, suspension, or supersession under competent authority.
12. Consequence-to-Responsibility return can trigger inquiry but does not assign Responsibility, blame, liability, duty, or sanction.
13. Consequence-to-policy return requires policy authority, scope, Evidence, alternatives, affected distribution, transition, enforcement, and appeal.
14. Consequence-to-concept return requires governed analysis and decision, not repeated use or project convention.

## 14. Additional negative controls

1. Monitoring excludes an affected group: absence of record is not absence of harm.
2. A mistaken complaint reveals an inaccessible correction channel: defect status and governance failure remain separate.
3. A manipulated rating produces a valid anomaly signal: source defect does not make every derived inquiry useless.
4. A late reliable outcome defeats an earlier assessment: temporal and version lineage preserves revision.
5. A wording change leaves the operative rule intact: Representation update is not object correction.
6. Rollback removes one defect but restores a vulnerability: correction is mixed and creates a new challenge.
7. A user adopts feedback under coercion: uptake is not agreement, consent, Learning, or improvement.
8. A team learns while a member loses capability: levels and Bearers preserve both claims.
9. Members learn but the organization cannot retain capability after turnover: member Learning is not System Learning.
10. A replacement System performs better immediately: improvement may occur without Learning by either System.
11. A score rises through metric gaming: Projection performance changes without established construct improvement.
12. Correction is verified locally but harms a downstream dependency: local closure is not lifecycle correction.
13. Policy changes after one dramatic incident: consequence motivates review but does not prove general adequacy.
14. Repeated project success conflicts with a Foundation Paper: project Evidence can challenge but not silently revise it.
15. Human explanation and Scientific causal model disagree while Engineering output is stable: operation does not resolve semantic or causal conflict.
16. Feedback cannot be disclosed because of protected information: bounded inspectability does not erase Evidence and challenge obligations.
17. A no-answer prevents immediate harm but creates later delay cost: quality and Value remain temporally and distributively mixed.
18. A high-Value Answer cannot be acted upon legally: Value, actionability, Authority, legality, and outcome remain distinct.

## 15. Alternatives considered

### A — Question as a compound of Scope, Context, and intent

Rejected because what is sought, its subject matter, answer obligation, assumptions, affected horizon, Identity, and revision remain irreducible and structurally necessary.

### B — Complex Question as independently Foundational

Rejected because complexity is a governed relational qualification of a Question rather than a second universal inquiry substance.

### C — Reasoning as any input-to-output transformation

Rejected because it collapses computation, retrieval, generation, causal process, and epistemic attribution.

### D — Reasoning as inference only

Rejected because consideration-sensitive processes include but are not exhausted by inference.

### E — Universal verbatim internal-chain requirement

Rejected because traces may be unavailable, post hoc, incomplete, misleading, unsafe to disclose, protected, or not causally faithful.

### F — Answer as any response or output

Rejected because occurrence does not establish content correspondence to the governed Question and answer obligation.

### G — Answer as a governed compound only

Rejected because Answer Identity remains independently stable before quality, use, decision, and consequence assessments.

### H — Correctness as complete Answer Quality

Rejected because relevance, completeness, support, uncertainty, robustness, safety, and other dimensions remain independent.

### I — One universal Answer-Quality scalar

Rejected because aggregation erases non-applicability, disagreement, distribution, critical failure, non-compensable conditions, and authority.

### J — Response latency as speed

Rejected because inquiry, verification, coordination, correction, recovery, and lifecycle clocks can reverse or qualify the comparison.

### K — Price or direct resource use as total cost

Rejected because Bearers, indirect cost, transfer, externalization, opportunity, dependency, harm, remedy, and residuals remain material.

### L — Value as only a compound with no universal anchor

Rejected because plural evaluative claims share an irreducible scoped relation despite distinct theories, claimants, and scales.

### M — One universal utility function

Rejected because claimants, affected subjects, authority, horizon, distribution, incommensurability, and non-compensable conditions vary.

### N — Favorable outcome validates the whole value chain

Rejected because luck, intervention, changed Context, confounding, use, and affected distribution break automatic validation.

### O — Every returned signal is feedback and Evidence

Rejected because source, object, return, transmission, integrity, relevance, Ground, assessment, and status remain separate.

### P — Any update or improved score is Learning

Rejected because stable Learner, durable experience link, capacity or pattern change, manifestation, and competing explanations are required.

### Q — Correction overwrites the prior state

Rejected because challenge, use, effects, Responsibility, repair, recurrence, and accountability require historical lineage.

### R — Pure Domain pluralism

Rejected because universal non-substitution, Identity, Evidence, Authority, distribution, correction, View, and lifecycle boundaries are necessary for SymK coherence and interoperability.

The proposed decision adopts Foundational Question, Reasoning, Answer, and Value; supporting Complex Question; multidimensional governed profiles; retained DR-013 feedback/correction/Learning dispositions; and recursive governance return as the leanest complete alternative.

Preserve Identity, version, schema, event, dependency, state, transition, Provenance, access, protected inspectability, monitoring, correction, rollback, revalidation, and interoperability. Machine fields, scores, events, or workflow states shall not become complete Human or Scientific meanings.

### Alignment rule

Every material mapping identifies source and target View; object, Identity, version, Scope, and Context; Representation or Projection; transformation, aggregation, abstraction, omission, and uncertainty; semantic, evidentiary, temporal, distributive, and authority loss; reversibility; validation; competent review; conflict and challenge; and correction or supersession lineage.

Agreement is not universally required. Material disagreement remains representable and is routed to competent authority.
