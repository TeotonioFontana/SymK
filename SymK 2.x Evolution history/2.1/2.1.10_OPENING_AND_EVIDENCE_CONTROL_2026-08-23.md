## 5. Review object and required layers

Each paper and the coordinated set must pass four distinct layers:

1. **Conceptual review:** exact comparison with Product Vision v0.3 and the Stage-Accepted DR-009–018/`2.1-rc.1` baseline.
2. **Cross-paper review:** inconsistent terminology, duplicated or conflicting claims, missing dependencies, authority cues and explanatory gaps across the set.
3. **Human-communication review:** complete-paper reading for intended audience, comprehension, explanatory sequence, assumptions, ambiguity, inquiry preservation and misleading authority.
4. **Publication review:** Markdown/PDF correspondence, citations, typography, diagrams, page flow, legibility, accessibility and absence of clipping or rendering defects.

Automated comparison, extraction, terminology analysis and page rendering may support every layer but cannot satisfy human-communication review by themselves.

## 9. Allowed finding dispositions

For every material finding, record exactly one:

1. revise into v0.2;
2. retain an identified v0.1 passage with explicit justification;
3. issue a governed erratum or addendum; or
4. defer a named issue to 2.1.11 with owner and consequence.

Silent deletion, unrecorded harmonization, authority laundering and historical overwrite are prohibited.

## 11. Human review requirements

Human review must identify:

1. intended and actual reader assumptions;
2. comprehension failures and ambiguous transitions;
3. terminology conflicting with the integrated baseline or another paper;
4. language falsely appearing constitutional, final or automatically authoritative;
5. missing context, rationale, alternatives, dissent, limitations or cross-references;
6. citation, typography, diagram, page-flow, legibility and accessibility defects; and
7. the disposition and verification of every material finding.

The project steward or an explicitly designated Human reviewer must complete the reading. AI assistance cannot impersonate, infer or certify that Human review.
