## 2. Result

**Yes, provisionally.** The Stream A manifest and complete Intelligence review queue verify. All 157 `I-CLM-*` units were dispositioned before the v0.2 draft was constructed: 71 require reconstruction in v0.2 and 86 remain justified as historical, literature, risk or boundary claims. All twenty-six v0.1 bibliography entries, nineteen inline citation groups and twenty linked source entries were checked. The two research sources retained from the stronger H lineage were also checked, yielding twenty-eight references in v0.2. No externally attributed verbatim quotation is reproduced. The paper's central blockquotes are SymK decisions or SymK explanatory formulations and are identified by their program lineage.

No material bibliographic defect requires a v0.1 erratum. Several sources require scope qualification: psychometric results remain population/instrument sensitive; named multiple, emotional and social Intelligences are not accepted as independent natural kinds by citation alone; group-performance evidence does not automatically establish any particular collective Bearer; and the 2025 Ng manuscript remains a recent research input, not semantic authority.

The v0.2 draft retains the v0.1 paper's strongest contributions: the Copernican correction; non-equivalence; substrate independence without indifference; organized-system attribution; the literature's partial-projection structure; multidimensional assessment before scalar rank; non-substitution among Intelligence and adjacent concepts; collective-attribution caution; counterfactual evidence; and challengeable, temporally scoped attribution.

It reconstructs the material conflicts and ambiguities identified below. In particular, it:

- uses the exact Stage-Accepted DR-011 Intelligence definition;
- distinguishes actual Bearer, actual capability configuration, manifestation/performance, attribution claim, assessment, attributed profile, Projection, authorization, status and record;
- replaces informal “flavors” with dimensions, specializations, actual configurations, profiles and declared score/class Projections;
- removes Knowledge, online Learning, adaptation, objectives and action as universal definitional requirements while preserving their contingent relations;
- preserves Knowledge–Intelligence independence and directional reciprocity;
- replaces the `IntelligenceObject` with a non-container review envelope;
- converts I-01–I-12 from candidate constitutional commitments into explanatory obligations traceable to DR-009–018;
- applies the lowest-adequate-Bearer rule and system-level Evidence requirements to collective, distributed, institutional and hybrid cases;
- separates capability from Authority, Authorization, Responsibility, affected-subject standing, status and moral/legal consequence;
- presents Cooperation as a thin process that may but need not contribute to system-level Intelligence or Amplification;
- states the exact seven-condition DIA class without making the DIA System the automatic amplified Bearer;
- adds Human, Scientific and Engineering Views with declared Projection loss;
- separates Evidence challenge from competent artifact or authority-source amendment and uses only the accepted urgent-consideration boundary;
- maps all twelve v0.1 open questions to accepted minima, live dissent and downstream ownership; and
- replaces the linear “Cooperation is the objective” motto with the branching, defeasible and recursive value relation.

The v0.1 Markdown/PDF and all G/H artifacts remain unchanged. Knowledge v0.2 remains unchanged. No Intelligence v0.2 PDF is created. No Human review, cross-paper integration, publication acceptance, Stream D, 2.1.11 opening, constitutional selection, Ratification or commit follows.

## 10. Dissent, confidence and reopening

### Preserved dissent and uncertainty

- whether every actual Intelligence entails some practical Knowledge;
- which capability dimensions, if any beyond constitutive multidimensionality and integration, belong to a universal semantic inventory;
- when emotional, social, logical-mathematical or another named construct is an Intelligence specialization rather than a skill or capability bundle;
- how material variation and non-individual specification should be operationalized across Domains;
- when a collective, institution or hybrid is literally an Intelligence Bearer;
- how identity and succession survive model, member, tool or institutional change;
- whether understanding is a Knowledge mode, Intelligence dimension, both or another epistemic achievement;
- how capability profiles can be compared without importing one Domain's target values;
- which attribution-envelope elements are universal semantics and which belong to downstream standards; and
- publication readability, accessibility, cross-paper final consistency and Human comprehension remain untested.

These are preserved uncertainties, not new tensions or deferred questions created by Stream C.

### Confidence

- **High** that v0.1 and G/H are preserved and all 157 Stream A review units are covered.
- **High** that the exact DR-011 definition and actual/attributed distinctions are reconstructed.
- **High** that candidate-axiom, package, authority, collective-Bearer, value-chain and DIA substitutions are blocked.
- **High** that every v0.1 bibliography entry and inline citation group was checked and the two H-lineage additions are appropriate.
- **Moderate-high** that the draft is coherent enough for later cross-paper and Human review.
- **Low** in publication readability, accessibility, final cross-paper consistency and Human comprehension because Streams D–H have not occurred.

Reopen Stream C if a controlled source hash changes; an `I-CLM-*` row lacks a permitted disposition; a cited source materially fails verification; v0.2 differs from exact DR-011–018; the paper implies capacity/profile/performance/record identity, automatic collective bearing, capability transfer, a canonical package, constitutional force or a linear objective not supported by accepted decisions; Stream F finds an irreparable cross-paper inconsistency; or Human review finds material incomprehensibility.
