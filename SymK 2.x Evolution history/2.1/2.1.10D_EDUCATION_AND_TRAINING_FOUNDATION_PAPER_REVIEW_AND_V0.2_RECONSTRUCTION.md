## 2. Result

**Yes, provisionally.** The Stream C evidence manifest and complete Education and Training review queue verify. All 110 `E-CLM-*` units were dispositioned before the v0.2 draft was constructed: 58 require reconstruction in v0.2 and 52 remain justified as literature, inquiry, risk, review-question or boundary claims. All sixteen v0.1 bibliography entries, three inline citation groups and ten source links were checked. No externally attributed verbatim quotation is reproduced. The eleven central blockquotes are SymK v0.1 program statements, definitions, questions or Proposed reasoning rather than external-source quotations.

No material bibliographic defect requires a v0.1 erratum. Every source is retained with scope qualification. The literature supports historical, philosophical, empirical or pedagogical inquiry; it does not determine SymK semantics, establish a universal pedagogy or virtue list, decide an actual Formation attribution, or grant constitutional force. The Durlak meta-analysis remains limited to its school-based universal social-and-emotional-learning corpus; the OECD report is a survey/reporting authority rather than a universal capability ontology; Dewey, Freire, Mezirow, Kohlberg, Vygotsky, Ryle, Schön, Lave and Wenger illuminate traditions rather than settle substrate-neutral definitions.

The v0.2 draft retains the v0.1 paper's strongest contributions: Intelligence and Knowledge do not entail cooperative fitness; Learning, Instruction, Training, Education and Socialization must be distinguished; harmful Learning and Formation remain possible; integration is not assimilation; power and hidden curricula matter; formative attribution must be developmental and scoped; member and system predicates must not be transferred; reciprocal influence, decay, correctability, repair and frame challenge remain visible; and practical review must include subject, purpose, Evidence, power and consequence.

It reconstructs the material conflicts and ambiguities identified below. In particular, it:

- makes Formation the Foundational concept and treats Learning, Training and Education as distinct supporting concepts;
- uses the exact accepted definitions and separates purpose, condition, environment, exposure, activity, mechanism, effect, achievement, attribution, assessment, status and record;
- rejects universal Education–Training necessity or co-entailment while preserving contingent overlap and support;
- preserves practice as broader than Training and allows Education without separately directed repeated Training;
- retains the proposed Axiom of Cooperative Formation only as historical v0.1 reasoning and advances no candidate constitutional proposition;
- corrects “every architecture trains/educates” to a formative-condition hypothesis requiring subject-level Evidence of actual effect;
- separates Human, artificial, member, component, collective, institutional, distributed, hybrid and configured-system predicates through the lowest-adequate-Bearer rule;
- states the accepted system-Learning requirements and controls replacement, selection, redesign, Context, retrieval, supervision and environmental alternatives;
- treats the nine proposed cooperative habits as contextual paper-level proposals rather than universal virtues, schema or duties;
- converts `E-01`–`E-12` into explanatory obligations without axiom or Ratification authority;
- preserves eleven typed authority kinds while keeping affected-subject standing/challenge an unresolved locus distinct from them;
- states that Evidence challenges claims, premises, applicability, consequences and decisions under Authority while artifact or authority-source change follows competent procedure;
- limits urgent adverse Evidence to urgent consideration by competent operational or external authority and creates no emergency duty or power;
- preserves Cooperation as a thin relational process and replaces final-objective language with the branching, defeasible and recursive value relation;
- adds Human, Scientific and Engineering Views with declared Projection loss;
- maps all eight v0.1 questions to accepted minima and live downstream remainders without creating a new deferred question; and
- supplies eighteen counterexamples and a non-container review envelope rather than a certification gate or canonical package.

The v0.1 Markdown and PDF remain unchanged. Knowledge and Intelligence v0.2 remain unchanged. No Education and Training v0.2 PDF is created. No Human review, cross-paper integration, publication acceptance, Stream E, 2.1.11 opening, constitutional selection, Ratification or commit follows.
