# SymK 2.1.3A — Knowledge Independent-Anchor Analysis

**Status:** Analytical working proposal — no DR-011 authority
**Date:** 20 August 2026
**Working baseline:** `2.1-dev.9`
**Evidence record:** `SYMK-2X-EV-088`
**Governing method:** Stage-Accepted DR-009
**Structural constraints:** Stage-Accepted DR-010

## 1. Analytical question

> What minimum meaning of Knowledge does SymK require that remains useful across propositional, practical, and familiarity modes without defining Knowledge through Intelligence, representation, governance status, or authorized reliance?

This document completes the first analytical stream defined by the 2.1.3 opening control. It proposes an independent Knowledge anchor for later reciprocal testing. It does not complete Knowledge analysis, decide possible bearer classes, or create a DR-011 proposal.

## 2. Why an independent anchor is necessary

The accepted Product Vision requires governed domain Knowledge as the medium through which participants cooperate. If Knowledge means only stored content, approved content, useful information, or whatever an Intelligence possesses, SymK cannot distinguish:

- truth from institutional acceptance;
- Knowledge from a claim or Representation;
- practical competence from a procedure document;
- an epistemic achievement from evidence that it occurred;
- responsible reliance from the truth or epistemic standing of its object;
- and Knowledge from Intelligence itself.

The v0.1 Foundation Paper already rejects most of these substitutions, but its working definition names “an intelligence, or a cooperative system of intelligences” as the subject of Knowledge. The Intelligence paper then partly explains Intelligence through its relation to Knowledge. Unless each concept can first be stated independently, their reciprocity risks becoming a definitional loop.

## 4. Term normalization

For this analysis:

- **Knowledge** names the candidate universal SymK concept.
- **knowledge-that** names propositional Knowledge concerning what is the case.
- **knowledge-how** names practical Knowledge manifested through competence in a class of activity.
- **knowledge by acquaintance or familiarity** names an epistemically significant direct or accumulated relation to a subject.
- **Knowledge attribution** is a claim that an identified Bearer has Knowledge in a declared mode and Scope.
- **knowledge claim** is represented content presented as true, warranted, reliable, or fit for reliance; it may be false or defeated.
- **knowledge asset** is a governed durable package supporting preservation, evaluation, communication, and application of epistemic contributions.
- **epistemic status** is a governed assessment state attached to a claim, asset, capability, or attribution; it is not truth and is not Knowledge itself.

Capitalization cannot carry the distinctions by itself. Final terminology and multilingual policy belong to later package construction.

## 5. Candidate approaches

### Candidate A — Propositional JTB-style foundation

> A Bearer knows a proposition when the proposition is true, the Bearer believes it, and the belief is justified.

**Strength:** supplies a clear truth condition and distinguishes Knowledge from a lucky assertion.

**Failure as the universal SymK anchor:** it is vulnerable to Gettier-type cases; it privileges propositional Knowledge; `belief` may presuppose one disputed theory of artificial or collective bearers; and it does not cover practical competence or familiarity without forcing them into propositions.

**Disposition:** preserve as one important test for propositional Knowledge, not the universal definition.

### Candidate B — Adopt the v0.1 definition unchanged

> Knowledge is an epistemic achievement in which an Intelligence or cooperative system of Intelligences stands in a sufficiently truth-connected, competence-bearing, or direct relation to what is known such that it may be responsibly relied upon within a stated Scope.

**Strength:** plural, consequence-aware, scoped, and explicitly non-representational.

**Failure:** it embeds Intelligence in the Knowledge definition before Intelligence has an independent anchor. It also risks excluding Knowledge that is never relied upon, or conflating Knowledge with a later decision that reliance is responsible under particular stakes and authority.

**Disposition:** revise rather than adopt unchanged.

### Candidate C — Operational or institutional acceptance

> Knowledge is content accepted for use after satisfying a governed validation process.

**Strength:** directly implementable and honest about institutional fallibility.

**Failure:** defines an `AcceptedClaim` or authorization for reliance, not Knowledge. A false claim can pass a process, and a true epistemic achievement can exist before institutional review.

**Disposition:** reject as the Knowledge definition; retain operational acceptance as a distinct 2.1.4 concept.

### Candidate D — Knowledge-first with engineering characterization only

> Knowledge is primitive; SymK governs only its attributions, evidence, assets, and consequences.

**Strength:** avoids pretending that a schema resolves Epistemology.

**Failure:** primitive does not mean boundaryless. Without a minimum semantic nucleus, projects could call any stored, approved, or useful artifact Knowledge and still claim compatibility.

**Disposition:** retain knowledge-first humility, but require a bounded characterization.

### Candidate E — Plural, bearer-neutral epistemic achievement

> **Knowledge is a scoped epistemic achievement attributable to a Bearer, in which success concerning what is the case, how to act, or a subject of familiarity is appropriately connected to truth, competence, or encounter rather than to mere luck, assertion, acceptance, or Representation.**

This is the current analytical recommendation.

It changes the v0.1 formulation in four material ways:

1. **Bearer replaces Intelligence.** The definition no longer decides in advance which kinds of subject can know and does not define Knowledge through the other member of the reciprocal pair.
2. **Mode-specific success remains plural.** Propositional, practical, and familiarity modes do not receive one false common test.
3. **Responsible reliance becomes a consequence, not a constitutive condition.** Knowledge can ground reliance, but reliance also depends on Context, stakes, authority, purpose, and alternatives.
4. **Scope is actual before it is declared.** Every Knowledge attribution has limits; SymK additionally requires those limits to be represented and declared for governed use.

“Appropriately connected” intentionally preserves the anti-luck problem without selecting reliabilism, virtue epistemology, safety, causal theory, or another complete theory. It is a bounded placeholder that later mode-specific criteria and domain evidence must make challengeable.

## 6. Mode-specific constitutive nucleus

The umbrella concept is coherent only if each mode has distinct minimum conditions.

| Mode | Candidate minimum nucleus | What does not suffice |
|---|---|---|
| Propositional Knowledge | A truth-apt proposition is true, is attributable to the Bearer through an epistemically significant state or commitment, and its truth is non-accidentally connected to the Bearer's grounds, process, or competence | Truth alone; belief alone; justification alone; assertion, approval, consensus, logical entailment from unverified premises, or a correct output by luck |
| Practical Knowledge | The Bearer has competence to perform, guide, or reliably navigate a relevant class of tasks under declared variation, with success connected to developed ability rather than one accidental result | A procedure document, verbal description, role title, one successful event, or access to a tool without integrated competence |
| Acquaintance or familiarity | The Bearer has a direct or accumulated epistemically significant relation to a subject that supports recognition, discrimination, orientation, or situated judgment | A finite list of detached claims, a copied profile, mere data possession, or one unintegrated encounter |

These modes may combine. A physician's practice can include true propositions, trained competence, and accumulated familiarity. Their combination does not prove they reduce to one mode.

## 8. Scope, Context, and Applicability

Three different statements must remain distinct:

1. **Truth/content variation:** the proposition itself contains contextual parameters or changes content with time, jurisdiction, population, or referent.
2. **Epistemic-attribution variation:** the same proposition may be known by one Bearer and not another because grounds, competence, access, or non-accidentality differ.
3. **Reliance variation:** the proposition and grounds may remain fixed while authorization to rely changes with purpose, stakes, alternatives, law, and affected parties.

Scope declares the reach claimed for an attribution or reliance decision. Context supplies conditions material to interpretation, evaluation, or application. Applicability judges whether the governed subject may be used in the present Context under declared Scope and criteria. None makes a false proposition true.

## 9. Bearer analysis boundary

The use of Bearer in the candidate definition does not mean every subject can know. It keeps the class question open while requiring every attribution to identify its subject.

A candidate Knowledge Bearer must at least support a mode-appropriate attribution at its own level:

- an identifiable continuity condition;
- a relation to the relevant proposition, task, or subject of familiarity;
- mode-specific epistemic integration rather than passive containment;
- grounds, process, competence, or encounter attributable to that Bearer;
- a non-accidental connection between the Bearer's epistemic success and those conditions;
- and a Scope and time boundary under which the attribution can be challenged.

For a collective, institution, artificial, distributed, or hybrid candidate, the analysis must additionally justify why the relevant organization, memory, feedback, control, and revision belong to one Bearer rather than to a convenient aggregation. These criteria are analytical constraints, not a decision that every such system literally knows.

A document, graph, database, model file, corpus, or repository is not a Knowledge Bearer merely because it contains representations. It may participate in a larger organized Bearer or support an attribution without itself satisfying one.

## 10. Dependency analysis

The current candidate dependencies are:

- Knowledge **presupposes** a Bearer role for attribution but does not presuppose a final taxonomy of Bearer kinds.
- Knowledge attribution **presupposes** Identity, Context, Scope, and typed Relationship from DR-010.
- Propositional Knowledge is **constituted partly by** truth and an appropriate non-accidental epistemic connection.
- Practical Knowledge is **constituted partly by** competence across a relevant class of situations.
- Familiarity Knowledge is **constituted partly by** direct or accumulated epistemically significant encounter.
- Knowledge **contrasts with** claim, Representation, asset, status, consensus, authority, and reliance.
- A Knowledge attribution or asset **represents** selected evidence about Knowledge and may be wrong.
- Domain Knowledge **specializes** the universal minimum with domain objects, methods, standards, consequences, and validation conditions.
- Knowledge does **not definitionally presuppose** Intelligence under this candidate. Whether every actual Knowledge Bearer must independently qualify as an Intelligence is a later reciprocal and bearer question, not a premise of the definition.

## 11. Counterexample tests

| Case | Result under Candidate E |
|---|---|
| True claim accepted for the wrong reason | Truth is present, but the Bearer's connection is accidental or defective; propositional Knowledge is not established |
| False claim responsibly relied upon under emergency procedure | Reliance may be authorized and reasonable; propositional Knowledge is absent because the claim is false |
| Richly linked graph with provenance | Strong Representation and possible asset; no Knowledge follows without a qualifying Bearer relation and epistemic achievement |
| Skilled physician unable to fully verbalize a diagnostic discrimination | Practical and familiarity Knowledge remain possible; incomplete propositional Representation does not eliminate them |
| Correct generated answer obtained through leakage | Performance and truth are present, but the relevant capability/grounds connection may be absent; neither Intelligence nor Knowledge follows automatically |
| Book containing a true proof | The book represents content and grounds; it is not thereby the Bearer that knows the theorem |
| Organization with distributed evidence, procedures, memory, and revision | Candidate system-level Knowledge remains possible if the integrated organization satisfies bearer criteria; mere member aggregation does not settle it |
| `symk.document.classified_as_type` stored and validated | A governed assertion and Representation exist; Knowledge depends on the Bearer, grounds, correctness, Scope, and attribution, not the JSON/MySQL/SharePoint field |

Candidate E survives these opening tests better than Candidates A–D while preserving their strongest insights.

## 13. Human-semantic nucleus

For an informed reader, the minimum explanation is:

> Knowledge is not the file, sentence, graph, score, approval, or database row through which something is presented. It is the epistemic success attributable to some Bearer: non-accidentally getting what is the case right, competently knowing how to do something, or possessing epistemically significant familiarity. SymK can preserve and govern claims, evidence, attributions, capabilities, and representations of that success, but cannot manufacture the success merely by naming or storing it.

## 14. Machine-semantic preservation obligations

Without selecting a formal language, later projections must be able to distinguish at least:

- Bearer from represented subject and source;
- Knowledge mode;
- proposition, task class, or subject of familiarity;
- Knowledge attribution from Knowledge itself;
- claim from truth assessment;
- grounds, process, competence, and encounter evidence;
- actual or claimed Scope from current Context and Applicability;
- epistemic status from truth and reliance authorization;
- knowledge asset from its individual representations;
- confidence and uncertainty from categorical Knowledge attribution;
- challenge, defeater, supersession, and temporal validity;
- and Domain specialization from the universal minimum.

No boolean `is_knowledge`, class membership, validation result, or repository location can by itself preserve the concept.

## 15. Consequences if retained

### Product Vision

“Governed domain Knowledge” continues to name the medium, but governance applies directly to claims, assets, attributions, grounds, representations, and reliance decisions. Governance supports Knowledge; it does not produce truth or epistemic achievement by authority.

### Candidate Constitution

- A1 becomes more precise by separating Knowledge from Knowledge attribution and epistemic status.
- A2 must not infer epistemic standing from participant type; bearer criteria remain evidence-based.
- A4 must say that engineering acts on epistemic conditions and representations rather than manufacturing Knowledge directly.

These are impact findings, not Ratification or axiom wording decisions.

### Foundation Paper revision ledger

The Knowledge v0.2 revision should:

- replace Intelligence inside the minimum Knowledge definition with Bearer;
- separate Knowledge from Knowledge attribution explicitly;
- move responsible reliance from the constitutive definition to a governed consequence and applicability decision;
- distinguish actual Scope limits from their declaration;
- retain the plural modes while giving each its own minimum conditions;
- and reconsider whether `KnowledgeObject` should be called `KnowledgeAsset`, `KnowledgeAttribution`, or a family of objects rather than one container-like object.

The Intelligence and Knowledge Engineering papers must later align with the same boundary.

### Derived projects

Projects own the domain-specific propositions, competence standards, familiarity evidence, reliance thresholds, and validation methods. They may not redefine a database, ontology class, document type, legal status, medical authorization, or successful output as universal Knowledge.

## 16. Preserved dissent and open questions

1. “Epistemic achievement” may imply an event where some Knowledge is better understood as a standing state, capacity, or relation.
2. “Success” may be too broad unless the mode-specific nuclei become part of the governed definition.
3. “Appropriately connected” preserves the anti-luck requirement but may be too indeterminate for later machine-semantic obligations.
4. Factivity is strongly supported for propositional Knowledge, but the correct truth/accuracy analogue for practical and familiarity modes remains contested.
5. Belief may be necessary for human propositional Knowledge but unsuitable as a universal bearer condition; `epistemic commitment`, `uptake`, or another role may be required.
6. It remains open whether artificial, collective, institutional, or hybrid systems literally know, or only distribute claims, capabilities, and evidence.
7. The relation between Knowledge and understanding remains unresolved.
8. Some may argue that responsible reliance is intrinsic to the SymK concept rather than a separate normative consequence.
9. Domain Knowledge may require additional relations to Domain practice, methods, and consequences beyond simple specialization.

## 17. Confidence and reopening conditions

**Confidence:** Medium-High that Knowledge requires an independent, bearer-neutral, plural epistemic anchor and foundational disposition; Medium in the exact wording of Candidate E; lower for universal bearer conditions.

Reopen or revise this analysis if:

- the independent Intelligence analysis shows that Bearer-neutral wording loses a constitutive condition;
- mode-specific testing reveals no coherent common nucleus;
- collective or artificial bearer analysis requires a narrower attribution vocabulary;
- 2.1.4 cannot distinguish Knowledge attribution, asset, status, and reliance without contradiction;
- cross-domain tests show that “appropriately connected” is unusably vague;
- or candidate-axiom analysis exposes an ungoverned normative premise.

## 18. Working conclusion and next dependency

Candidate E is the strongest current independent anchor and Knowledge passes the DR-009 eligibility gate as a **candidate foundational concept**. Neither conclusion has Stage-Accepted authority.

The next required action is the independent Intelligence anchor. Only after both anchors and the Bearer model exist may 2.1.3 decide whether their reciprocal relationship is constitutive, enabling, developmental, evidentiary, operational, or some typed combination.
