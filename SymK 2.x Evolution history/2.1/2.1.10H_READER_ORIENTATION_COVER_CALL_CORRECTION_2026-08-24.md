The project steward found that the improved Reader Orientation still allowed a newcomer to reach its contents without first receiving a clear, memorable account of what SymK is in Knowledge Engineering terms. The requested correction was one meaningful paragraph on the first page, preferably as the first substantive paragraph.

`SYMK-2X-HF-006` is dispositioned `REVISE_V0.2`. The implementation state is `IMPLEMENTED_PENDING_HUMAN_REREAD`; it is not an acceptance or closure finding.

## 2. Implemented paragraph

> SymK is a general framework and engineering discipline for responsible Knowledge Engineering through governed cooperation among different forms and Bearers of Intelligence. It calls us to engineer the conditions in which difficult questions can produce scoped, evidence-grounded, challengeable and revisable knowledge work - while never confusing information with Knowledge, performance with Intelligence, capability with Authority, cooperation with legitimacy, or a successful outcome with proof.

This paragraph appears:

1. as the first substantive paragraph in the governed Reader Orientation Markdown; and
2. as a dedicated call on the PDF cover, before author and date.

The first sentence preserves the accepted layered SymK identity. The second states the practical Knowledge Engineering orientation and the anti-conflation mindset already supported by DR-009 through DR-018 and the four Foundation Papers. It adds no concept, Authority, duty, status or constitutional claim.
