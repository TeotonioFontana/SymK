## Question

> What qualifies as cooperation among Intelligences, and what formation is required for that cooperation to remain productive, responsible, and challengeable over time?

## Relational distinctions

Examine cooperation, coordination, communication, aggregation, compliance, consensus, competition, coercion, and collusion. Determine when difference, conflict, and asymmetry remain compatible with cooperation.

## Formative distinctions

Examine learning, instruction, Training, Education, socialization, habit formation, reflective challenge, correction, repair, and developmental decay.

Required tests include:

- Education and Training as distinct but reciprocal;
- repetition as necessary in some formation but insufficient for Education;
- integration versus assimilation;
- formation of human, artificial, collective, institutional, and hybrid bearers;
- whether persistent DIAs necessarily educate or train participants;
- hidden curricula, coercion, power, and the right to challenge the frame;
- whether the cooperative system itself may become a learner.

## Deliverables

- cooperation definition and failure boundaries;
- formation concept system and bearer analysis;
- treatment of DQ-003 and DQ-004;
- conceptual evidence for DQ-019;
- Education Foundation Paper revision-ledger entries;
- consequences for candidate A3 and derived projects.

## Out of scope

Complete pedagogy, universal social policy, institutional curriculum design, labor policy, or constitutional selection of A3.
