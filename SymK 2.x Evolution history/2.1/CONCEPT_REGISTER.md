## Admission warning

Appearance in this register does not make a term foundational. Historical `FC-*` identifiers, file locations, engineering usefulness, and Foundation Paper prominence are evidence to examine, not authority for admission.

## Required future fields

For each accepted disposition, later revisions of this register must record:

- working definition and scope;
- primary owner and authority;
- dependencies and reciprocal relationships;
- prohibited conflations;
- counterexamples;
- applicable decision identifier;
- human-semantic and machine-semantic obligations;
- canonical and derived-project consequences;
- dissent, confidence, and status.
