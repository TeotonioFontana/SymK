## Stage-Accepted 2.1.3 dispositions — DR-011

| Candidate | Working disposition | Analytical result | Status |

| Intelligence specialization | Governed supporting specialization distinction; named instances require a Derived-project specialization or another expressly governed semantic authority | Constrains the universal Intelligence concept to a Domain or governed situation family; inherits the nucleus and declares authority, dimensions, variation, evidence, limitations, assessment conditions, version, and lineage; may overlap or nest | Stage-Accepted pattern through DR-011; no named specialization admitted |
| Actual capability configuration | Governed supporting distinction, not a separate artifact class | Multidimensional capacity actually borne by a particular organized system, including interacting strengths, limits, and organization | Stage-Accepted through DR-011 |
| Attributed capability profile | Engineering/governed Representation | Fallible, scoped, contextual, temporal, evidence-bearing, uncertain, challengeable, and revisable account of an actual configuration | Stage-Accepted classification through DR-011 |
| Score, class, ranking, or general factor | Engineering/assessment Projection | Purpose-specific reduction of evidence or profile dimensions with source, transformation, omission, and semantic loss declared | Stage-Accepted classification through DR-011 |
| Knowledge–Intelligence reciprocal relations | Governed supporting typed relationship family | Enabling, constraining, developmental, evidentiary, operational, and representational relations are directional, scoped, and contingent; neither attribution entails the other automatically | Stage-Accepted through DR-011 |

An attribution record and attributed profile are Representations, not the bearing relation, epistemic achievement, actual capability configuration, or Intelligence itself. Emotional, social, logical-mathematical, and other named constructs remain candidate specializations rather than admitted SymK concepts. Practical Knowledge and Intelligence may overlap through competence across variation, but universal cross-entailment remains unestablished. Every dissent item and reopening condition in the accepted DR-011 remains active.
