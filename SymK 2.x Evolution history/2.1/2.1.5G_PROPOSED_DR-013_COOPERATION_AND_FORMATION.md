## 1. Decision question

> What minimum concepts of Cooperation and Formation must SymK govern; how must participants, contributions, adjacent relations, Learning, Training, Education, power, challenge, persistence, memory, and system Learning be distinguished; and how can persistent human, artificial, collective, institutional, distributed, and hybrid arrangements be assessed without substituting coordination, compliance, consensus, output, architecture, record, duration, or responsibility for the predicates actually at issue?

## 2. Proposed decision

> **SymK shall govern Cooperation and Formation as distinct foundational concepts. Cooperation is an actual, scoped relational process in which two or more distinguishable Participants make materially relevant Contributions that are mutually oriented toward a bounded shared undertaking and sufficiently connected for the Contributions to form part of that undertaking rather than merely coincide or aggregate. Formation is the temporally extended shaping or reorganization of an identified subject's comparatively durable capacities, dispositions, practices, judgments, relationships, or organized patterns through experience, intervention, participation, environment, or self-directed activity.**
>
> **Cooperation identifies a thin relational occurrence. It is distinct from responsible or governed qualification and from answer, action, outcome, value, and consequence. Cooperation does not by itself establish legitimacy, freedom, equality, consent, truth, Knowledge, Intelligence, authority, responsibility, affected-party protection, persistence, Education, or success. Harmful, coercively constrained, exploitative, or collusive Cooperation may therefore be diagnosed without being endorsed. Conversely, a successful answer or compatible output does not establish Cooperation.**
>
> **A Cooperation Participant is a subject occupying a scoped relational role in an actual cooperative occurrence when a materially relevant Contribution and mutual orientation to the shared undertaking are supportable at that subject's own explanatory level. Participant, Contributor, actor, resource, source, beneficiary, affected party, Bearer, authority, and responsible party remain distinct roles. A cooperative unit is the analytical configuration under review; it is not automatically a Participant, Intelligence Bearer, Learner, legal person, authority, or responsible party. Individual, artificial, collective, institutional, distributed, hybrid, component, and whole-system predicates require their own stable boundaries and evidence.**
>
> **Formation is value-neutral and may be intended or unintended, beneficial or harmful, integrative or deformative. Formative purpose, condition, environment, exposure, activity, process, mechanism, effect, achievement, attribution, assessment, status, and record remain distinct. Architecture, curriculum, interaction, repetition, feedback, model updating, organizational continuity, or stored traces may create formative conditions or evidence, but none automatically establishes a formative effect, Learning, Training success, Education, improvement, or responsible development.**
>
> **Learning, Training, and Education shall be governed as distinct supporting concepts within the Formation and epistemic-lifecycle system. Learning is comparatively durable change at an identified Learner level linked to experience, inquiry, practice, feedback, interaction, or adaptation. Training is directed and structured formative activity toward declared performance or disposition conditions; a Training activity may fail, and trained achievement requires separate evidence. Education is broad, developmental, integrated, participatory, and challengeable formation connecting Knowledge, judgment, conduct, relationship, and capacity to examine and revise the frame. The three may overlap contingently, but none universally entails either of the others. Practice is broader than Training. Persistence and architecture make none of them automatic.**
>
> **Power, constraint, influence, dependence, vulnerability, asymmetry, coercion, domination, manipulation, suppression, agenda control, exploitation, conflict, dissent, refusal, and challenge shall be governed as supporting relational or qualification distinctions where material. Power capacity, exercise, mechanism, effect, qualification, authority, governance response, and record remain distinct. Conflict, disagreement, dissent, bounded competition, and unequal roles may coexist with Cooperation while the relational nucleus persists. Formal permission to challenge is not effective challengeability: notice, access, practical capacity, protection, standing or representation, reception, evaluation, reasoned response, possible corrective consequence, repair or remedy interface, appeal, recurrence, and Learning must remain distinguishable. Detailed authority, consent, duty, responsibility, accountability, remedy, liability, and affected-party standing remain for 2.1.6 and later constitutional work.**
>
> **A cooperative episode, repeated interaction, recurring pattern, continuing cooperative relation, and persistent system shall remain different temporal objects. Persistence is qualified continuity through change under declared Identity conditions; it is not established by duration, repetition, stable name, organizational status, storage, or unchanged membership. Reciprocal formation may occur across persistent relations without symmetry, benefit, Learning at every level, or Education. Stored trace, retrieval, current Context, procedural retention, institutional memory, distributed memory, developmental history, and actual Learning remain distinct.**
>
> **System Learning requires an identified stable Learner boundary, system-level experience and mechanism, integrated comparatively durable change, manifestation under relevant variation, and control of competing explanations such as selection, replacement, redesign, current Context, retrieval, or environmental change. Component Learning does not automatically become system Learning; system Learning does not automatically become member Learning, Intelligence, amplification, Education, or value. A fixed component may remain unchanged while a wider configured or hybrid system learns through changed organization, roles, practices, and feedback.**
>
> **Every material Cooperation or Formation claim shall preserve, proportionately to consequence: predicate identity and version; subject, Participant, Learner, and system boundaries; Domain, Scope, Context, time, and temporal object; undertaking, Contribution, orientation, dependencies, alternatives, and affected parties; formative purpose, condition, activity, mechanism, effect, durability, transfer, and competing explanations where applicable; power, conflict, challenge, loss, and consequence; source, Evidence, assessment, uncertainty, authority, status, reliance, dissent, revision, and lineage; and Human, Scientific, and Engineering View alignment with declared material loss. Claims, attributions, assessments, statuses, records, profiles, scores, workflows, and dashboards are Representations or Projections rather than the occurrence, relation, capacity, effect, achievement, memory, or predicate itself.**
>
> **SymK owns only this universal semantic minimum. Derived projects and legitimate Domain or external authorities own specialized cooperative purposes, participant-admission procedures, curricula, pedagogies, training methods, professional standards, named habits and capabilities, conflict and consent procedures, evidence thresholds, identity and memory practices, operational statuses, metrics, schemas, and workflows. Local specialization may add within jurisdiction but may not silently redefine universal Cooperation or Formation.**

No clause has authority until the project steward explicitly Stage-Accepts this proposal.

## 6. Cooperation rules

1. **Actual occurrence:** Cooperation is a relation that occurs; an intention, invitation, membership, status, policy, architecture, or output does not create it.
2. **Plural distinguishability:** at least two distinguishable Participants are required at the declared explanatory level; internal components do not become Participants automatically.
3. **Material Contribution:** each Participant must make a materially relevant Contribution at its own level; mere presence, use, causal background, or resource status is insufficient.
4. **Mutual orientation:** Contributions must be sufficiently reciprocally related to the bounded shared undertaking; identical mental states, purposes, benefits, or Representations are unnecessary.
5. **Relational connection:** Contributions must form part of the same undertaking rather than be post hoc aggregation or coincidence.
6. **Occurrence/qualification separation:** actual Cooperation may be coercive, exploitative, collusive, harmful, or irresponsible; those qualifications must remain visible and cannot be sanitized by denying the occurrence.
7. **Occurrence/outcome separation:** Cooperation may fail epistemically or practically; a successful non-cooperative process remains possible.
8. **Scope separation:** Cooperation may occur within one relation while wider conduct is competitive, harmful, or non-cooperative, and vice versa.
9. **Difference and conflict:** disagreement, dissent, opposition, unequal roles, and material conflict may remain internal Contributions while the nucleus persists.
10. **No permanent status:** every current attribution is scoped and temporally qualified; historical Cooperation does not establish present Cooperation.

## 7. Participant, Contribution, and boundary rules

1. Identify the lowest organized subject level that explains the claimed Contribution and orientation without omitting constitutive integration.
2. Keep the successful boundary fixed when examining failure, dependency, power, cost, harm, responsibility, and consequence.
3. Permit separately evidenced individual, artificial, collective, institutional, distributed, hybrid, component, and whole-system Participation without copying predicates across levels.
4. Distinguish a Participant from an instrument, resource, infrastructure dependency, source, passive beneficiary, represented subject, and affected nonparticipant.
5. Treat forced conduct as a layered case: coercion may defeat own Contribution or orientation and therefore Participant status, or may coexist with thin Participation while defeating responsible qualification.
6. Do not infer Participation from Intelligence, agency, autonomy, membership, output, contractual status, legal personality, interface inclusion, or model invocation.
7. Do not infer Intelligence, agency, authority, responsibility, consent, authorship, ownership, or moral standing from Participation.
8. Record material dependencies and alternative boundary interpretations, especially when a configured, distributed, or hybrid subject is proposed.
9. Reassess after participant entry, exit, replacement, model change, organizational redesign, fork, merge, or succession.
10. Preserve affected parties even when they do not meet the Participant conditions.

## 8. Formation and developmental-layer rules

1. Name the identified subject and explanatory level before attributing a formative condition, effect, achievement, or Learning.
2. Separate purpose, condition, exposure, activity, mechanism, process, effect, achievement, attribution, assessment, status, and record.
3. Formation may be intended or unintended, beneficial or harmful, integrative or assimilative, reversible or persistent.
4. A formative condition or environment establishes possibility or influence, not effect or achievement.
5. A recorded output difference establishes neither comparative durability nor an experience-linked change.
6. A favorable effect establishes neither Education nor responsibility; a harmful effect may still be actual Formation or Learning.
7. State duration, transfer, relevant variation, competing explanations, dependencies, and Context changes proportionately to consequence.
8. Preserve developmental gain and loss together, including dependency, drift, decay, deskilling, exclusion, and affected-party effects.
9. Treat historical achievement and current status separately; conditions and habits may decay or become harmful after Context changes.
10. Engineering may alter formative conditions and records but cannot manufacture developmental predicates by declaration.

## 9. Learning, Training, and Education rules

1. **Learning:** requires comparatively durable change at an identified Learner level linked to experience, inquiry, practice, feedback, interaction, or adaptation and manifested beyond the immediate support condition.
2. **Training:** identifies directed structured formative activity toward declared conditions; distinguish activity, completion, immediate performance, transfer, trained effect, and current capability.
3. **Education:** requires breadth and integration across Knowledge, judgment, conduct, relationship, and the practical capacity to examine and revise the frame; mere information, compliance, certification, or alignment is insufficient.
4. Training may occur without Education, Education may occur without a separately directed repeated Training program, and practice is broader than Training.
5. Repetition may stabilize harmful habits; successful compliance may contradict Education when challenge capacity is destroyed.
6. Reflective development may be real yet fragile under pressure; absence of robust habit limits current Scope without proving that no Education occurred.
7. Machine training or fine-tuning may change parameters or outputs without establishing Education, transfer, stable capability, or system Learning.
8. Artificial, collective, institutional, distributed, and hybrid attributions are not excluded by substrate, but must satisfy the same predicate-specific and level-stability rules without anthropomorphic transfer.
9. No learner-level effect may be copied to members, components, institution, configured system, hybrid, or whole solely through composition.
10. Persistence, architecture, curriculum, feedback, record, memory, or stable Cooperation does not make Education constitutive or actual automatically.

## 10. Power, conflict, and challenge rules

1. Record Power basis, capacity, exercise, practical alternatives, mechanism, effect, qualification, response, and Representation separately.
2. Distinguish constraint, influence, authority, dependence, vulnerability, asymmetry, coercion, domination, manipulation, suppression, agenda control, and exploitation.
3. Do not infer legitimacy, responsibility, or truth from capacity or recognized authority.
4. Conflict, competition, disagreement, dissent, opposition, and refusal may coexist with Cooperation when the shared undertaking, material Contributions, mutual orientation, and relational connection persist.
5. Consensus, silence, compliance, retention, and successful output do not establish free agreement or effective challenge.
6. Preserve formal permission, notice, access, practical capacity, protection, standing or representation, reception, evaluation, reasoned response, corrective possibility, repair/remedy interface, appeal, recurrence, and Learning as distinguishable challenge layers.
7. An affected nonparticipant may require challenge or representation even though it does not occupy the Participant role.
8. Hidden curricula include interfaces, defaults, incentives, metrics, examples, sanctions, tolerated violations, and operational dependencies.
9. A Cooperation occurrence may survive coercion or domination while responsible qualification fails; complete compulsion may instead defeat Participant status when own Contribution and orientation disappear.
10. Detailed consent, legitimate authority, duty, responsibility, accountability, remedy, liability, standing, and role allocation remain for 2.1.6 and later constitutional selection.

## 11. Persistence, memory, reciprocal-formation, and system-Learning rules

1. Distinguish encounter or episode, repeated interaction, recurring pattern, continuing relation, persistent system, developmental trajectory, historical achievement, current status, and record.
2. Persistence requires declared Identity conditions and qualified continuity through relevant change; duration, repetition, name, archive, legal continuity, or stable status is insufficient.
3. State what participant, component, model, role, procedure, data, practice, authority, or environmental changes preserve, restrict, supersede, fork, merge, reconstitute, succeed, or terminate the subject.
4. Distinguish trace, retention, retrieval, current-context support, procedural retention, institutional memory, distributed memory, and developmental history.
5. Memory attribution requires proportionate evidence of preservation, availability, retrieval or enactment, interpretation, use, and challenge at the claimed system level.
6. Reciprocal formation is directional and may be unequal, harmful, asynchronous, or confined to one level; it entails neither symmetry nor mutual Learning.
7. System Learning requires a stable Learner boundary, system-level experience and mechanism, integrated comparatively durable change, manifestation under relevant variation, and controlled competing explanations.
8. Selection, replacement, redesign, current Context, retrieval, external operator change, or environmental change may explain output differences without system Learning.
9. A fixed model may remain unchanged while humans, configured systems, institutions, or hybrid arrangements learn through changed roles, practices, organization, or feedback.
10. Preserve developmental profiles rather than one favorable score: gain, loss, dependency, deskilling, power, cost, drift, decay, correction, repair, recovery, resilience, and affected-party consequence remain visible.

## 12. Attribution, assessment, status, and record rules

Every material Cooperation, Formation, Learning, Training, Education, Persistence, memory, or system-Learning attribution shall identify, proportionately to consequence:

1. the exact predicate, definition or profile version, and claimed relationship to supporting distinctions;
2. the proposed subject, Participant, Contributor, Learner, cooperative-unit, component, system, and affected-party boundaries;
3. Domain, Scope, Context, temporal object, time, comparison baseline, and validity conditions;
4. the shared undertaking, material Contributions, mutual orientation, and relational connection for Cooperation claims;
5. purpose, conditions, activities, mechanisms, effects, durability, transfer, relevant variation, and alternative explanations for Formation or Learning claims;
6. power, practical alternatives, hidden curriculum, challenge layers, harm, benefit, loss, dependency, and consequence;
7. Grounds, Evidence, counterevidence, Provenance, method, assessment criteria, uncertainty, failures, and dissent;
8. assessor, semantic authority, decision authority, intended use, governance status, Reliance Authorization, challenge route, revision conditions, and lineage;
9. Human, Scientific, and Engineering View statements, material mismatches, transformations, omissions, and declared loss; and
10. which changes preserve, limit, suspend, supersede, terminate, or require reassessment of the attribution.

Actual occurrence, predicate bearing, attribution claim, assessment, governance status, Reliance Authorization, responsible qualification, and record remain distinct. A representation can preserve a claim without making it true; a status can govern use without creating the predicate.

## 13. Three-view and declared-loss obligations

### Human View

The Human View shall make purpose, undertaking, participant meaning, experience, identity, difference, conflict, formative significance, power, refusal, challenge, harm, repair, continuity, consequence, and dissent understandable without treating narrative or declared intention as sufficient evidence.

### Scientific View

The Scientific View shall make hypotheses, methods, boundary choices, observations, comparisons, counterfactuals, mechanisms, uncertainty, transfer, longitudinal change, alternative explanations, failure cases, and defeat conditions inspectable without reducing the concepts to one instrument or metric.

### Engineering View

The Engineering View shall distinguish identities, versions, components, roles, Contributions, relations, conditions, events, attributions, assessments, statuses, records, dependencies, challenges, effects, changes, succession, and losses without making a schema, workflow, database, or runtime state the semantic authority.

### Triangular alignment

| Material mismatch | Required treatment |
|---|---|
| Human account reports Cooperation while Engineering evidence shows post hoc aggregation | Cooperation attribution remains challenged |
| Scientific evidence reports output gain while Human evidence shows coercion | Gain cannot establish responsible qualification |
| Human curriculum declares Education while Engineering conditions punish challenge | Education attribution is defeated or materially qualified |
| Engineering version changes while Scientific evidence shows no durable system change | System Learning is not established |
| Human narrative claims institutional memory while Engineering evidence shows archive only | Memory capability remains unestablished |
| Scientific analysis uses one system boundary while harm or cost analysis uses another | Overall attribution is invalid until boundaries align or the transformation is declared |

No view is permanently supreme. A formally governed clause controls its declared formal Scope, not every wider meaning. Material omission, reduction, translation, or view substitution requires explicit declared loss.

## 15. Alternatives considered

### A — Cooperation as a morally thick success concept

**Rejected:** it hides collusion, coercion, domination, exploitation, false answers, harmful outcomes, and the process/qualification/value boundary.

### B — Cooperation as coordination, communication, aggregation, compliance, consensus, exchange, or compatible output

**Rejected:** each can occur without the Participant, Contribution, mutual-orientation, shared-undertaking, and relational-connection conditions.

### C — Cooperation as SymK's final objective

**Rejected:** the Stage-Accepted Product Vision makes Cooperation the process, Amplified Intelligence the target capability, and better answers under accountability, responsibility, Learning, and consequence constraints the principal value.

### D — Only autonomous, consenting, responsible, or morally qualified agents can participate

**Rejected:** it pre-decides 2.1.6, hides coercively constrained occurrences, and excludes artificial, institutional, distributed, and hybrid cases by metaphysical stipulation rather than evidence.

### E — Every interacting component is a Participant or every cooperative unit is a collective Bearer

**Rejected:** it collapses component, resource, actor, contributor, Participant, Learner, Intelligence Bearer, authority, and responsible party and permits boundary shopping.

### F — Formation as improvement, Education, Training, or Learning

**Rejected:** SymK would become unable to represent harmful, unintended, coercive, assimilative, decaying, or non-learning developmental shaping.

### G — Education and Training as universal reciprocal necessities

**Rejected:** Education can occur without a separately directed repeated Training program, Training can occur without Education, and practice is broader than Training.

### H — Every persistent architecture educates or trains

**Rejected:** architecture and persistence create possible conditions and influence; they do not establish learner-level effect, integrated achievement, or challengeability.

### I — Conflict and dissent as failures; consensus and compliance as success

**Rejected:** conflict and dissent may contribute materially within Cooperation while suppression, agenda control, and coercion can manufacture consensus or compliance.

### J — Duration, name, version, archive, or organization as Persistence and memory

**Rejected:** qualified Identity continuity, preservation, retrieval or enactment, use, change, and competing explanations remain necessary.

### K — Any component improvement or output gain as system Learning

**Rejected:** stable Learner boundary, integrated system-level mechanism, durability, relevant variation, and alternative-explanation controls are missing.

### L — One comprehensive foundational concept or an independent foundational package for every supporting term

**Rejected:** one concept would erase material distinctions, while indiscriminate foundational proliferation would duplicate structural and Domain ownership. The proposed two-anchor system preserves independent semantic necessity with governed supporting families.

## 17. Consequences if Stage-Accepted

### Product Vision

- Cooperation remains the governed process, not the objective or sufficient value.
- Knowledge remains the governed epistemic medium without becoming a shared substance or requiring every Contribution to be Knowledge.
- Amplified Intelligence remains the target capability; neither Cooperation, system Learning, participant count, nor output gain establishes it.
- Better answers under epistemic accountability, responsibility, Learning, and consequence constraints remain the principal value.
- Disagreement, dissent, Provenance, challenge, uncertainty, and material view mismatch survive canonicalization.

The Product Vision itself would not be revised by DR-013.

### Candidate constitutional artifacts

**A0 — Cooperative Purpose:** material revision is required before constitutional review. “Cooperation is the objective” conflicts with the Product Vision and must become a process/value-chain statement. Domination, deception, collusion, coercion, and harm should defeat responsible qualification rather than be defined out of the occurrence.

**A1 — Epistemic Humility:** compatible in direction. Dissent, challenge, counterevidence, uncertainty, authority/warrant separation, and revisability align, but A1 does not by itself supply Participant, Power, standing, repair, or responsibility semantics.

**A2 — Non-Privilege and Governed Role Allocation:** compatible in direction and dependent on 2.1.6. Origin grants neither Participation, cooperative fitness, Learner status, authority, nor responsibility.

**A3 — Cooperative Formation:** cannot proceed unchanged. Stable Cooperation does not universally require Education; Education does not universally become effective through repeated directed Training; practice is broader than Training; persistence and architecture create formative conditions rather than inevitable Education or Training; reciprocal formation need not be symmetric; and system Learning requires its own evidence. A narrower constitutional duty to govern material formative conditions and effects may remain viable after 2.1.6–2.1.9 and DQ-019 integration.

**A4 — Engineering of Epistemic Conditions:** compatible with correction. Architectures may engineer epistemic and formative conditions, feedback, memory, challenge, correction, and records but do not manufacture Knowledge, Cooperation, Learning, Training success, Education, responsible qualification, or value.

**J1 — Philosophical and Engineering Jurisdiction:** compatible in direction. SymK governs the universal minimum; pedagogy, psychology, social theory, ethics, labor governance, law, curricula, professional standards, operational thresholds, and remedies remain with proper Domain or external authorities.

No constitutional candidate is accepted, revised, rejected, superseded, or Ratified by DR-013.
