# SymK 2.1.11.1 — Cross-Document Consistency Audit

**Status:** Planned  
**Gate effect:** Blocks final 2.1 acceptance until complete

## Audit scope

The audit will verify:

- terminology and definitions across all 2.1 work packages and registers;
- consistency with the Stage-Accepted 2.0 Product Vision and decision lineage;
- concept dispositions, dependencies, bearers, scope, and prohibited conflations;
- candidate-Constitution and axiom interpretability without premature selection;
- Foundation Paper v0.2 source consistency and v0.1 lineage;
- Markdown/PDF correspondence and visual-verification evidence;
- authority, status, identifier, path, and version accuracy;
- deferred-question routing and tension preservation;
- derived-project impact and formalization/lowering boundaries;
- Markdown links and repository integrity for affected artifacts.

## Required outcome

A finding-by-finding record that distinguishes objective defects, substantive unresolved conflicts, acceptable deferrals, and blockers to final Stage-Acceptance.
