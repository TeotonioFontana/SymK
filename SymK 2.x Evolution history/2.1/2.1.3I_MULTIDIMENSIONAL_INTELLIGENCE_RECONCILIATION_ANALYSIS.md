## 1. Reconciliation question

> Do the Bearer and multidimensional-specialization corrections developed during review of the Intelligence Foundation Paper require revision of Proposed `SYMK-2X-DR-011`, and do the resulting changes survive the applicable 2.1.3 counterexamples, predecessor decisions, reciprocal Knowledge boundary, Product Vision, jurisdiction, and two-lane tests?

The answer is **yes, revision is required and the revised candidate passes the applicable analytical gates**. The current proposal in `2.1.3F` correctly establishes the independent Knowledge and Intelligence anchors, attribution layers, typed reciprocity, and non-substitution rules. It does not, however, state strongly enough that actual Intelligence is multidimensional, distinguish an actual capability configuration from its attributed profile, or govern Intelligence specialization separately from a type label or score.

This record preserves `2.1.3F` unchanged as the first proposal. It does not silently import a Foundation Paper into semantic authority. It extracts the challenged propositions from the non-canonical H draft, classifies them, tests them under DR-009 and DR-010, and authorizes a separate revised proposal only because the tests below pass.

## 4. Revised Intelligence candidate

The reconciled candidate definition is:

> **Intelligence is a substrate-realized, multidimensional capacity of an organized system to integrate relevant resources in producing context-sensitive interpretations or effective responses across materially varying situations, including cases not individually specified in advance.**

This wording changes the first proposal in four controlled ways:

1. **Multidimensionality becomes constitutive.** It belongs to the actual capacity and not merely to an assessment instrument.
2. **The organized system is the ontological subject in the definition.** Bearer remains the DR-010 attribution role occupied by that system when it actually bears the capacity.
3. **Attribution is removed from the definition.** A capacity can exist before it is recognized; an attribution fallibly identifies a proposed Bearer and claims the bearing relation.
4. **The variation language is sharpened.** `Materially varying situations` and `not individually specified in advance` exclude exact replay without requiring online learning, stochasticity, self-modification, or Human-like generality.

The revised wording does not change the established independent Knowledge anchor and does not insert Knowledge, learning, agency, autonomy, rationality, consciousness, authority, responsibility, or moral standing into the universal Intelligence definition.

## 5. Bearer reconciliation

The H challenge exposes three statements that must remain distinct:

1. the universal concept of Intelligence does not require a particular Bearer to be named;
2. every actual Intelligence is borne by an organized system;
3. an Intelligence attribution identifies a proposed Bearer and fallibly claims that the actual bearing relation obtains.

This is compatible with DR-010. `System` remains imported support and `Bearer` remains a governed supporting attribution role. No new foundational object kind is created. The distinction also remains compatible with 2.1.3C:

```text
organized system with actual multidimensional capacity
    → occupies the Bearer role for Intelligence

attribution claim
    → identifies that system as the proposed Bearer
    → claims the actual bearing relation
    → may be true, false, incomplete, obsolete, or contested

assessment and authority for use
    → evaluate or license bounded reliance on the claim
    → do not create the capacity

record
    → represents the claim, assessment, and lineage
    → is not the bearing relation or the capacity
```

The correction therefore **clarifies DR-010 without reopening it**. The wider questions of whether a particular artificial, collective, distributed, institutional, or hybrid organization literally bears Intelligence remain evidence-dependent.

## 6. Multidimensional architecture

The reconciliation requires the following levels:

| Level | Minimum interpretation | Disposition |
|---|---|---|
| Intelligence | Universal foundational candidate and minimum multidimensional nucleus | Proposed foundational concept under revised DR-011 |
| Capability dimension | A distinguishable aspect along which the actual capacity may vary | Proposed governed supporting distinction; no universal closed inventory |
| Intelligence specialization | The universal Intelligence concept constrained to a declared Domain or governed family of situations | Proposed governed supporting specialization distinction; each named instance requires Derived-project specialization or another expressly governed semantic authority |
| Actual capability configuration | The capacity actually borne by a particular organized system, including its interacting strengths, limits, and organization | Proposed governed supporting distinction; an ontological condition rather than a stored artifact or independent foundational kind |
| Attributed capability profile | A governed and fallible Representation of an actual capability configuration | Representation; must preserve Scope, evidence, uncertainty, time, challenge, and lineage |
| Score, class, ranking, or general factor | A purpose-specific reduction from evidence or profile dimensions | Projection; may be useful but cannot substitute for Intelligence or the actual configuration |

Multidimensionality does not assert that dimensions are mutually independent, equally weighted, universally enumerable, or identical across substrates and Domains. Dimensions may overlap, correlate, nest, share mechanisms, or form supported hierarchies. A claimed general factor remains a statistical or assessment Projection unless stronger evidence establishes a wider ontological role.

## 7. Specialization rule

The candidate specialization rule is:

> **An Intelligence specialization constrains the universal Intelligence concept to a declared Domain or governed family of situations and identifies the capability dimensions, materially relevant variation, evidence, limitations, and assessment conditions appropriate to that specialization.**

A specialization must:

1. inherit the universal Intelligence nucleus;
2. identify its Domain or governed situation family and semantic authority;
3. identify distinguishing dimensions without claiming a universal closed list;
4. state relevant variation, Context, Scope, resources, dependencies, failures, and evidence;
5. distinguish the specialization from one skill, competence, trait, role, status, behavior, or outcome;
6. preserve overlap, nesting, covariance, and non-exclusion where supported;
7. state assessment, uncertainty, challenge, revision, version, and lineage; and
8. remain separate from a Bearer-specific attribution and from scores or classifications projected for a particular use.

Emotional, social, and logical-mathematical Intelligence remain examples of claims that could enter this admission process. None is admitted as a governed SymK specialization by this analysis or by revised DR-011.

### 8.2 DR-010 compatibility

- `Identity` governs continuity of the organized system and its versions.
- `Context`, `Scope`, and `Domain` qualify profiles, specializations, evidence, and use.
- `Representation` classifies attributed profiles and assessment records.
- `Relationship` supports bearing, attribution, specialization, evidence, and reciprocal relation types.
- `Bearer` remains a role and does not become a new foundational superclass.
- `Projection` classifies scores, rankings, general factors, storage forms, and implementation reductions.
- `System` remains imported support whose organization and boundary must be justified.

No accepted structural disposition requires reopening.

### 8.3 Knowledge and reciprocity compatibility

The Knowledge definition and its propositional, practical, and familiarity modes remain unchanged. Multidimensionality does not make Knowledge a constitutive dimension of every Intelligence. Knowledge may still enable, constrain, scaffold, develop, specialize, evidence, and support Intelligence through typed, directional, scoped, contingent relations. Intelligence may still pursue, contribute to, interpret, test, challenge, connect, apply, express, and revise Knowledge-related work.

Practical Knowledge may overlap one or more capability dimensions. That possibility does not establish identity or automatic cross-attribution and remains preserved dissent.

## 12. Two-lane preservation test

### Human-semantic lane

An informed reader can distinguish the universal Intelligence concept, a capability dimension, a specialization, an actual Bearer-specific configuration, an attributed profile, and a score or class. The account explains why each level has different evidence and authority.

### Machine-semantic lane

Later projections must be capable of preserving:

- universal concept and version;
- specialization identity, version, semantic authority, lineage, Domain or situation family, and inherited conditions;
- dimension identity and relationships without requiring a universal closed enumeration;
- proposed Bearer, stable boundary, Identity, substrate, organization, and actual-bearing claim;
- attributed profile, Domain, Scope, Context, resources, dependencies, evidence, failures, alternatives, uncertainty, time, challenge, and lineage;
- overlap and nesting among specializations;
- and the separate identity, purpose, reduction rule, and declared loss of each score, class, ranking, or general-factor Projection.

### Mismatch test

The following forms are insufficient for consequential use unless backed by the governed semantics and richer record:

```text
is_intelligent = true
intelligence_type = social
emotional_intelligence = 0.82
general_intelligence_score = 127
```

**Result:** Pass. The obligations remain representation-independent and select no schema, ontology, logic, database, API, or programming language.

## 13. Jurisdiction and non-effects

The reconciliation does not:

- admit emotional, social, logical-mathematical, or another named specialization;
- define a universal inventory, cardinality, independence relation, or weighting for dimensions;
- select a general-factor theory or psychometric instrument;
- decide the Intelligence threshold for every Domain;
- decide literal Intelligence bearing for all artificial, collective, institutional, distributed, or hybrid systems;
- complete cooperation, formation, responsibility, Amplified Intelligence, answer value, or feedback;
- revise DR-009 or DR-010;
- accept DR-011;
- revise the canonical Foundation Paper v0.1 or publish v0.2;
- create final `FC-*`, property, relationship, or semantic-package artifacts;
- accept the `02_CORE_MODEL` candidate scaffold; or
- modify a derived project or implementation.

## 14. Preserved dissent and reopening conditions

The following questions remain open:

1. whether `multidimensional` names a universal ontological condition or the minimum unavoidable structure of any useful capacity account;
2. which dimensions, if any, belong to a universal semantic inventory;
3. how dimensions should be individuated across substrates, Domains, and assessment traditions;
4. when covariance or a higher-order factor reflects actual organization rather than a population- and instrument-bound Projection;
5. when a named capability bundle warrants admission as an Intelligence specialization;
6. how overlapping specializations should inherit, constrain, or conflict without duplicating meaning;
7. how profiles can be compared without importing one Domain's priorities or one population's instrument;
8. the threshold between broad competence, narrow Intelligence, and general Intelligence;
9. literal versus explanatory Intelligence attribution to collective, institutional, distributed, and hybrid systems;
10. practical-Knowledge overlap and whether every actual Intelligence bears some practical Knowledge;
11. which capability changes count as amplification when dimensions trade off; and
12. how cultural and institutional dependence affects evidence for emotional and social candidate specializations.

Reopen the reconciliation if later work shows that multidimensionality cannot be distinguished from an assessment artifact, that the specialization pattern cannot preserve the universal nucleus without Domain leakage, that actual configuration cannot be represented without conflation, or that 2.1.5–2.1.8 cannot use the downstream constraints coherently.

No new tension or deferred-question identifier is required. These issues refine the existing Intelligence-attribution, universal/domain, formal-loss, cooperation, amplification, and authority lenses and remain carried by DQ-002 and DQ-012 plus their named downstream packages.

## 15. Gate result and next action

The reconciliation passes:

- the DR-009 admission and challengeability gate;
- the DR-010 structural compatibility gate;
- the independent Knowledge and typed-reciprocity gate;
- all twelve original mandatory counterexamples;
- ten additional multidimensional and specialization counterexamples;
- the Product Vision and downstream-jurisdiction gate; and
- both semantic-lane preservation tests.

The changes are material enough that `2.1.3F` should not be accepted unchanged. They are bounded enough that 2.1.3A–E need not be rewritten and DR-009 or DR-010 need not be reopened.

The next authorized artifact is therefore a separate **revised Proposed `SYMK-2X-DR-011`**, preserving `2.1.3F` as proposal lineage and presenting the reconciled Knowledge, Intelligence, Bearer, multidimensional, specialization, attribution, reciprocal, and non-effect rules for project-steward review.
