## 1. Question and analytical result

> Can every material accepted concept, supporting family, relation kind, Capability claim, governed-undertaking expansion, Product Vision branch, role, claim, assessment, status, and recursive return be expressed coherently across Human, Scientific, and Engineering Views without making format determine View, hiding transformation or semantic loss, requiring a fourth universal View, selecting a formal language, or allowing one Representation to own meaning?

Stream D supports the following working answer:

> **Yes, provisionally. All forty-seven Stream B analytical nodes and all nineteen working relation kinds admit material Human, Scientific, and Engineering expressions under the exact DR-012 rules. The three Views remain sufficient as the current universal minimum when View identity is assigned by epistemic and representational function rather than format, institution, actor, or artifact type. No material accepted meaning requires a fourth universal View.**

> **Governance, constitutional, legal, ethical, professional, organizational, economic, educational, political, affected-subject, and operational concerns do not become additional Views by importance. They are jurisdictions, authorities, objects, methods, consequences, or cross-view dimensions that may be represented in all three Views. The three program lanes, specialization layers, maturity states, concept roles, stages, and execution environments remain orthogonal.**

> **The Stream C governed knowledge undertaking result survives the Three-View audit. In the Human View it functions as an intelligible organizing label; in the Scientific View it decomposes into declared units of analysis, hypotheses, evidence relations, causal models, affected populations, and limitations; in the Engineering View it may be projected as a versioned relation bundle or navigational aggregate, but never as an automatically real System, actor, Bearer, workflow, legal object, semantic parent, or responsibility sink. The fuller accepted relation bundle remains authoritative over every reduced Projection.**

> **The nine Capability claim families also survive without promotion. Human descriptions state whose capability for what matters and why; Scientific expressions operationalize a specified construct under methods, variation, uncertainty, and competing explanations; Engineering expressions record claims, measurements, configurations, dependencies, assessments, and statuses without making a field, score, benchmark, component property, or runtime event the actual capability. No common `Capability` class or property is required by alignment or future formalizability.**

> **The principal material losses are affected-subject erasure, purpose/frame narrowing, Bearer or level collapse, epistemic-status flattening, uncertainty and dissent loss, authority/Responsibility laundering, Value/distribution aggregation, temporal and corrective-lineage loss, and non-entailment omission. Each can be detected and governed through the DR-012 projection contract. No present mismatch blocks continuation, but the canonical Product Vision's vertical chain, the final name of the Scientific View, proportional materialization duties, tacit/practical/familiarity Knowledge, and formal expression of plural Value and affected standing remain active later-Stream issues.**

Stream D therefore passes provisionally to separately instructed Stream E. It creates no canonical View artifact, formal profile, Ontology, Logic, schema, alignment contract, source revision, concept disposition, DR-018 content, or `2.1-rc.1` baseline.

## 2. Authority and interpretation controls

Exact revised DR-012 governs the Three-View model:

- the **Human View** preserves purpose, meaning, distinctions, explanation, Context, Scope, examples, alternatives, uncertainty, dissent, affected parties, consequences, and limits;
- the **Scientific View** preserves questions or hypotheses, studied subjects, methods, observations, measurements, Evidence, counterevidence, models, competing accounts, uncertainty, robustness, reproducibility, adequacy, limitations, and defeat conditions; and
- the **Engineering View** preserves semantic sources, requirements, constraints, interfaces, models, formal profiles, algorithms, architectures, validation, runtime assertions, deployment, observability, failure handling, migration, security, authorization, and operational consequences.

Human readability, formal expression, and machine inspectability are cross-view capabilities. Natural language, diagrams, mathematics, logic, structured data, schemas, code, and executable artifacts may express any View or a declared combination. Format does not select the View.

Semantic authority remains with the governed subject record and decision lineage. Evidence or defects exposed through a View may challenge the governed subject but cannot amend it automatically.

The local `B-F*`, `B-S*`, `C-CAP-*`, interface, gate, loss, and route identifiers in this analysis are noncanonical. They do not create package anatomy, universal classes, formal predicates, conformance requirements, or runtime identifiers.

## 3. View identity and orthogonality model

| Question | Human View | Scientific View | Engineering View | Orthogonal structure that must not substitute |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| principal function | make governed meaning, interpretation, purpose, standing, disagreement, consequence, and limits intelligible | investigate and assess constructs, claims, mechanisms, evidence, uncertainty, and defeat | specify, realize, validate, operate, observe, migrate, and constrain representations and systems | authority, layer, package, stage, program lane, project, or implementation |
| typical question | What does this mean, for whom, why, under what conditions, with what contest and consequence? | What is the claim or construct, how is it investigated, what supports it, and what would defeat it? | What is represented or implemented, under which source, contract, constraint, state, and failure mode? | actor type, document form, institution, or tool |
| adequacy lens | intelligibility, semantic fidelity, practical interpretability, affected-horizon and dissent visibility | construct validity, epistemic or empirical adequacy, method fitness, uncertainty, robustness, reproducibility, generalization | specification fidelity, traceability, validation, inspectability, interoperability, observability, security, operational fitness | truth, legal validity, moral legitimacy, market success, or constitutional force by form |
| authority limit | clarity, professional language, lived experience, or consensus does not create truth or final authority | scientific prestige, measurement, or model adequacy does not create semantic, legal, moral, or constitutional authority | formal precision, schema validity, runtime success, or adoption does not create semantic truth or universal policy | competent Domain/external authority remains separate |
| typical failure | ambiguous meaning, hidden assumptions, erased dissent, inaccessible explanation, missing affected subjects | construct substitution, biased sample, proxy capture, weak causal inference, hidden uncertainty, inapplicable generalization | wrong semantic source, lossy schema, incorrect level, unobservable failure, authority leakage, irreversible migration | failure in one View is not repaired by success in another |

The following do **not** become fourth Views:

1. governance or constitutional authority;
2. legal, ethical, professional, political, economic, educational, or organizational reasoning;
3. affected-subject experience and standing;
4. corpus integrity and publication;
5. grounding, verification, or operational evidence;
6. formal semantics or machine processing;
7. project or Domain specialization; or
8. implementation and runtime operation.

Each may require distinct methods and authorities. Their material claims still need Human intelligibility, Scientific/evidential adequacy appropriate to the Domain, and Engineering realization or record treatment where implemented.

## 10. Directional transformation routes

Cross-view alignment is directional. A validated mapping in one direction never establishes the reverse mapping.

| Route | Typical transformation | Material content easily lost | Minimum return control |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1. Human → Scientific | meaning and concern become constructs, questions, hypotheses, populations, methods and measures | lived meaning, dissent, tacit/practical Knowledge, affected standing, institutional history and non-quantified consequence | link every construct and operationalization to source terms, affected accounts, exclusions and challenges |
| 2. Scientific → Human | results and uncertainty become explanation, interpretation and practical implication | method conditions, sampling limits, effect distribution, competing explanation, uncertainty and defeat | expose method, scope, strength, uncertainty, alternatives and what the findings do not authorize |
| 3. Human → Engineering | purpose and meaning become requirements, models, interfaces, policies and records | ambiguity that is legitimate, value plurality, contested authority, tacit context, exceptions and burden distribution | source-linked requirements with assumptions, unresolved alternatives, affected subjects and non-entailments |
| 4. Engineering → Human | structures and runtime records become explanation, status and consequence | implementation defaults, hidden dependencies, data exclusions, automated inferences and uncertainty | explain source, configuration, rule, inference, failure, omission, appeal and operational consequence |
| 5. Scientific → Engineering | constructs and evidence models become variables, schemas, algorithms, tests and monitoring | construct boundaries, method limitations, counterevidence, causal uncertainty and population validity | versioned operationalization, construct-validity tests, uncertainty representation and model cards/limits |
| 6. Engineering → Scientific | telemetry, traces, data and implementations become observations, measures and experiment conditions | missingness, instrumentation effect, configuration drift, selection, data-generating process and semantic mismatch | provenance, instrumentation/configuration version, sampling frame, missingness and independent validity review |
| 7. triangular integration | Human, Scientific and Engineering expressions are compared and jointly constrained without fusion | minority/dissent account, incompatible assumptions, unresolved mismatch, authority differences and irreversible loss | three source routes, explicit disagreement object, per-View adequacy evidence and governed challenge/correction |

The seventh route is not a fourth View or a synthesis artifact that owns meaning. It is a governed comparison among non-identical expressions. Pairwise agreement does not validate the absent View, and three-way agreement does not create truth, Authority, Responsibility, Value, or constitutional force.

## 11. Semantic and evidential loss register

| Loss | What becomes unrecoverable or misleading in the target alone | Detection and treatment |
|---:|---|---|
| 1. governed-subject loss | target no longer identifies the accepted subject or version | stable identity/version link; block unqualified use |
| 2. purpose/frame loss | intended question, use or reason for representation disappears | require purpose/audience/use and alternative frames |
| 3. Domain loss | field-specific meaning or competent authority is flattened | record Domain and external/domain source |
| 4. Context loss | conditions material to interpretation or assessment disappear | explicit Context fields and changed-context checks |
| 5. Scope/Applicability loss | target appears universal beyond valid boundary | encode inclusions, exclusions, criteria and defeaters |
| 6. Bearer loss | predicate is detached from whose/what capability or Knowledge it concerns | typed Bearer and actual/claim/assessment separation |
| 7. level loss | member/component predicates appear to belong to whole or conversely | adequate-level field and aggregation/non-transfer tests |
| 8. relation/modality loss | typed, contingent or prohibited relation becomes generic edge | relation kind, direction and modality required |
| 9. status-regime loss | proposed, assessed, accepted, authorized and actual states collapse | typed status, issuer, regime, interval and source |
| 10. authority/jurisdiction loss | technical or epistemic source appears to authorize use | distinguish semantic, epistemic, governance, Domain and external authority |
| 11. Responsibility loss | answerability, object, source, phase or response route disappears | object-first responsibility and challenge fields |
| 12. affected-subject/standing loss | people or other affected subjects outside participant/user model vanish | affected horizon, distribution, standing and remedy checks |
| 13. Power/consent-integrity loss | apparently cooperative or agreed relation hides coercion or practical refusal limits | Power, notice, access, voluntariness and external-authority record |
| 14. uncertainty loss | estimate, interpretation or mapping appears certain | typed epistemic, measurement, model and mapping uncertainty |
| 15. dissent/alternative loss | minority account, countermodel or unresolved disagreement disappears | preserved dissent object and competing expressions |
| 16. evidence/counterevidence loss | conclusion remains while Grounds, limitations or defeaters disappear | evidence graph, counterevidence and defeat conditions |
| 17. provenance/transformation loss | origin, additions, defaults or derivation cannot be reconstructed | full lineage and transformation log |
| 18. temporal/lifecycle loss | current state erases predecessor, persistence, succession or revalidation | version/event/interval and correction lineage |
| 19. distribution loss | aggregate measure hides who gains, bears cost or remains excluded | per-group/subject distribution and non-compensability |
| 20. negative-state loss | no-answer, absence, suspension, refusal, failure or unresolved state has no representation | explicit negative and unknown states; no null-as-absence shortcut |
| 21. non-entailment loss | target invites prohibited property or authority transfer | machine- and human-visible negative controls |
| 22. tacit/practical/familiarity loss | propositional or schema projection omits embodied, relational or encounter-based Knowledge | declare inexpressibility, retain testimony/practice source and avoid equivalence |
| 23. implementation-dependency loss | target hides provider, resource, integration, security or failure dependencies | configuration, dependency, failure and removal records |
| 24. correction/challenge loss | target looks final because contest, response, supersession or reopening route is absent | challenge access, response obligation, correction/supersession and immutable history |

Loss may be acceptable for a bounded purpose, but it must be declared and proportionate. A target with material undeclared loss cannot claim semantic equivalence, complete alignment, safe downstream reuse, or authority to replace the fuller source.

## 12. Reversibility, identity, and lineage model

| Class | Condition | Permitted claim | Required control |
|---|---|---|---|
| `D-RV1` fully reversible | target preserves every in-scope distinction and deterministically reconstructs the source expression/version | reversible for the declared fields and version only | bidirectional tests and immutable source/target versions |
| `D-RV2` traceably lossy | target omits declared material that remains available through an authoritative source route | fit for the stated bounded use, not equivalent | loss declaration, source link, use constraint and revalidation trigger |
| `D-RV3` partially reconstructible | some source dimensions can be inferred only with external context, human judgment or uncertain matching | partial reconstruction with stated confidence | reconstruction method, uncertainty, reviewer and unresolved fields |
| `D-RV4` irreversible/opaque | source meaning, identity, history or evidence cannot be recovered reliably | no source-replacement or equivalence claim | quarantine or restrict use; preserve original where possible; route correction |
| `D-RV5` invalid equivalence | target asserts sameness or authoritative substitution despite material difference/loss | no aligned status | fail validation, withdraw reliance, notify affected users and repair lineage |

Reversibility is field- and purpose-relative. Byte recovery, regeneration, round-trip syntax, or stable identifiers do not establish semantic reversibility. A later target version cannot overwrite the source, dissent, failed mapping, or correction history.

## 15. Triangular disagreement and governance

Cross-view alignment uses the following governed disagreement pattern:

1. identify the governed subject, versions, Views, artifacts, authors or systems, and applicable authorities;
2. locate whether disagreement concerns meaning, construct/measurement, evidence, implementation, status, jurisdiction, Value, or consequence;
3. preserve each account and its supporting Grounds without normalizing it into a premature common statement;
4. test translation and loss in every relevant direction, including the absent or least developed View;
5. distinguish defects in the governed subject from defects in a Representation, Projection, method, data, implementation, or use;
6. route the issue to competent semantic, epistemic, Domain, governance or external authority without letting one jurisdiction silently decide another;
7. record outcome, residual dissent, affected consequences, correction, supersession and reopening conditions; and
8. revalidate dependent artifacts and uses rather than editing one representation and assuming propagation.

No View has a standing tie-break. Human consensus, Scientific confidence, and Engineering precision are each defeasible and jurisdiction-limited. Where authorities conflict or no competent forum recognizes affected standing, the mismatch remains explicit; technical deployment or publication cannot resolve it by default.
