## 1. Stream question

> Can the complete Knowledge Engineering Foundation Paper be reconstructed as a coherent v0.2 Human-facing explanation of Stage-Accepted DR-012 and the integrated DR-009–018 baseline while preserving v0.1, its historical discipline and strongest epistemic-condition conclusion, and while correcting its identity, object, Representation/Projection, lifecycle, Bearer, Authority, standing, Evidence, Education, Cooperation, candidate-force, gate, and conclusion boundaries?

## 2. Result

**Yes, provisionally.** The Stream D evidence manifest and complete Knowledge Engineering review queue verify. All 137 `KE-CLM-*` units were dispositioned before drafting: 90 require reconstruction in v0.2 and 47 remain justified as history, literature, inquiry, distinction, risk, requirement, or boundary. All fourteen bibliography entries, six inline citation groups, twelve links, nine central callouts, the prominent table, the lifecycle diagram, generated contents, and the preserved 19-page PDF projection were reviewed.

The paper's central result survives: Knowledge Engineering does not engineer truth or Knowledge directly; it engineers epistemic conditions. Its historical account of expert systems, the acquisition bottleneck, model-based engineering, ontologies, Knowledge graphs, machine learning, and generative systems remains useful when presented as bounded literature rather than semantic authority. Acquisition remains construction rather than extraction; Representation remains selective; formal validity remains local; generative output remains candidate contribution; plural assessment and consequence return remain essential.

The v0.2 review draft reconstructs the material conflicts and ambiguities. It:

- uses the exact Stage-Accepted Knowledge Engineering definition and Epistemic Condition boundary;
- exposes the layered SymK identity and all seventeen Foundational anchors;
- keeps the governed knowledge undertaking non-reifying and semantically eliminable;
- replaces the linear five-level object stack and eleven-step workflow with a branching, recursive object-and-transition model;
- prevents Grounds, Evidence, Provenance, claims, Representations, assessments, status, Reliance Authorization, decisions, actions, consequences, Knowledge, and Learning from collapsing;
- applies exact Representation/Projection and Human/Scientific/Engineering View controls without selecting a universal formalism;
- uses the lowest-adequate-Bearer rule and prohibits member/component/whole transfer;
- retains eleven typed Authority kinds while treating affected-subject standing/challenge as a distinct unresolved locus;
- states that Evidence challenges claims, premises, applicability, consequences, assessments, and decisions under Authority while artifact and authority-source change follows competent procedure;
- limits urgent adverse Evidence to urgent consideration by competent operational or external Authority and creates no emergency duty or power;
- removes universal Education/Training dependency and architecture-effect language;
- keeps Cooperation thin, non-final, and non-guaranteeing;
- converts `KE-01`–`KE-12` from candidate foundational commitments into explanatory obligations;
- replaces the certification-like gate with a non-container Three-View review envelope;
- maps v0.1 open questions to accepted minima and live remainders without creating a new tension or deferred question; and
- supplies eighteen counterexamples.

The citation audit found one bibliographic defect: v0.1 attributes Polanyi's 1966 first edition of *The Tacit Dimension* to the University of Chicago Press, but the first-edition record is Doubleday; the University of Chicago Press edition is a later reissue. v0.2 corrects the citation. v0.1 remains unchanged as historical evidence, and this review records the erratum explicitly. No other material author, title, year, venue, volume, issue, page, DOI, ISBN, or standards-status defect was found.

No externally attributed verbatim quotation is reproduced. The nine central blockquotes are v0.1 SymK program claims, definitions, or synthesis rather than external-source quotations. The inherited-discipline summary is retained as synthesis, not falsely attributed as a quotation from one source.

The v0.1 Markdown and PDF remain unchanged. Knowledge, Intelligence, and Education and Training v0.2 remain unchanged. No Knowledge Engineering v0.2 PDF is created. No cross-paper integration, publication layout, Human review, publication acceptance, Stream F, 2.1.11 opening, final Stage 2.1 decision, Ratification, or commit follows.
