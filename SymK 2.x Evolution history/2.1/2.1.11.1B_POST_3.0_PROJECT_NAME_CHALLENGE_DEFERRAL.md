## Decision boundary

The project steward proposes and instructs the project to record that the name challenge occurs only after the complete 3.0 release has been accepted and closed through 3.0E.

This record supplies the controlled deferral permitted by `2.1.11.1A_PROJECT_NAME_AND_IDENTITY_FITNESS_REVIEW.md`. It does not decide the name.

## Required interpretation through 3.0

> **“SymK” identifies the governed project/framework by historical continuity. Its meaning comes from exact accepted identity and jurisdiction decisions, not from automatic expansion as “Symbiotic Knowledge.”**

Documents may describe the historical expansion when clearly marked as history. They must not use it to reinstate a Human–AI dyad, automatic symbiosis/mutual benefit, Cooperation as final objective, or Knowledge as a directly engineered object.
