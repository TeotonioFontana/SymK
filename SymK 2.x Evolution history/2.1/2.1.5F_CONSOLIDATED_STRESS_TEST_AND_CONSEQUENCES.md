## 3. Consolidated semantic spine

The combined result can be stated as one typed sequence:

```text
identified subjects and levels
    may make material contributions
        mutually oriented and relationally connected
            toward a bounded shared undertaking
                producing a possible cooperative occurrence
                    separately attributed, assessed, qualified, and recorded
                        under power, conflict, challenge, affected-party, and consequence conditions
                            across episodes, continuing relations, and persistent systems
                                that may create formative conditions and reciprocal influence
                                    that may produce separately evidenced effects and Learning at identified levels
                                        while gain, loss, dependency, drift, decay, correction, and repair remain visible
```

No arrow is automatic. No later state retroactively creates an earlier occurrence. No favorable value at one layer settles the others.

### 3.1 Cooperation nucleus

> **Cooperation is an actual, scoped relational process in which two or more distinguishable participants make materially relevant contributions that are mutually oriented toward a bounded shared undertaking and sufficiently connected for the contributions to form part of that undertaking rather than merely coincide or aggregate.**

This nucleus identifies occurrence. It does not establish legitimacy, freedom, equality, responsibility, affected-party protection, truth, Knowledge, successful outcome, Education, persistence, or authorization.

### 3.2 Participant and cooperative-unit boundary

> **A Cooperation Participant is a subject occupying a scoped relational role in an actual cooperative occurrence when a materially relevant contribution and mutual orientation to the shared undertaking are supportable at that subject's own explanatory level.**

A cooperative unit is the analytical configuration under review, not automatically a participant, learner, Intelligence, responsible party, authority, or legal person. Member, component, team, institution, distributed, hybrid, and whole-system predicates require separate evidence.

### 3.3 Formation nucleus

> **Formation is the temporally extended shaping or reorganization of an identified subject's comparatively durable capacities, dispositions, practices, judgments, relationships, or organized patterns through experience, intervention, participation, environment, or self-directed activity.**

Formation is value-neutral and may be intended or unintended, beneficial or harmful, integrative or deformative. It does not entail Education, Training, Learning, Knowledge, Intelligence, cooperative disposition, responsibility, or improvement.

### 3.4 Learning, Training, and Education

- **Learning** is comparatively durable experience-, inquiry-, practice-, feedback-, interaction-, or adaptation-linked change at an identified learner level.
- **Training** is directed and structured formative activity toward declared performance or disposition conditions; a Training activity may fail, and trained achievement requires separate evidence.
- **Education** is broad, developmental, integrated, participatory, and challengeable formation connecting Knowledge, judgment, conduct, relationship, and capacity to examine and revise the frame.

Training can occur without Education. Education can occur without a separately directed repeated Training program. Practice is broader than Training. Neither persistence nor architecture makes either occur automatically.

### 3.5 Power and challenge

Power is a scoped relational capacity to shape options, agenda, participation, interpretation, conduct, distribution, consequences, or revision. Capacity, exercise, effect, qualification, authority, governance response, and record remain distinct.

Challengeability is layered: notice, permission, access, practical capacity, protection, standing or representation, reception, evaluation, reasoned response, possible corrective consequence, repair/remedy interface, appeal, recurrence, and learning. Formal permission alone is insufficient; effective challenge does not guarantee agreement or revision.

### 3.6 Persistence and system Learning

Persistence is qualified continuity through change under declared identity conditions. A continuing cooperative relation is distinct from one episode or repeated interaction. Stored records are distinct from retrieval, institutional memory, and Learning.

System Learning requires a stable learner boundary, system-level experience and mechanism, integrated durable change, manifestation under relevant variation, and control of competing explanations such as selection, replacement, redesign, current context, retrieval, or changed environment.

## 7. Boundary-invariance audit

The combined model was retested using five invariance rules:

1. **Same explanatory level:** participant, learner, power holder, contributor, and system boundaries may not expand for success and shrink for failure.
2. **Same temporal object:** episode, continuing relation, persistent system, historical achievement, and current status may not substitute for one another.
3. **Same predicate:** contribution, Cooperation, Learning, Education, Intelligence, authority, responsibility, and value require their own evidence.
4. **Affected-party preservation:** nonparticipants do not disappear from consequence and challenge analysis.
5. **Three-view preservation:** a favorable narrative, metric, or implementation state cannot override a material contradiction in another view.

### 7.1 Results

- The Human–AI boundary remained stable when output improved, the model failed, humans supplied correction, or professionals deskilled.
- The institutional boundary remained stable through member change, record retention, policy updates, and coercive internal practice.
- The collusion and domination cases retained the immediate cooperative participant set while preserving excluded third parties.
- The Training and Education cases retained the same learner boundary across compliance, transfer failure, pressure, habit, and decay.
- The persistence cases retained declared identity conditions through replacement, succession, and Context change.
- The system-Learning cases did not count component replacement or temporary support as experience-linked system change.

**Result:** Pass. No counterexample required boundary shopping.

### 15.4 A3 — Cooperative Formation

**Cannot proceed unchanged.**

The current normative statement contains unsupported universal necessity claims:

1. stable Cooperation does not conceptually require Education in every case;
2. Education does not universally become effective through repeated directed Training;
3. practice and feedback may matter without every practice being Training;
4. a persistent architecture creates formative conditions and possible influence, not inevitable Training or Education;
5. reciprocal formation does not mean symmetric Learning or benefit;
6. the cooperative system may learn only under stable system-level evidence;
7. named habits are candidates, not a universal accepted catalogue; and
8. formative accountability, power, challenge, decay, and affected-party consequence are stronger universal concerns than an automatic Education attribution.

A future constitutional candidate may state a bounded duty to govern material formative conditions and effects in persistent systems, but that normative selection belongs to 2.2 after DR-013, 2.1.6–2.1.9, and DQ-019 integration. A3 remains Proposed and unchanged.
