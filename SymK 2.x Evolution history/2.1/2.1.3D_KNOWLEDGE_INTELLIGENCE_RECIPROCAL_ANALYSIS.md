# SymK 2.1.3D — Knowledge–Intelligence Reciprocal Analysis

**Status:** Analytical working proposal — no DR-011 authority
**Date:** 20 August 2026
**Working baseline:** `2.1-dev.12`
**Evidence record:** `SYMK-2X-EV-091`
**Depends on:** `2.1.3A_KNOWLEDGE_INDEPENDENT_ANCHOR_ANALYSIS.md`, `2.1.3B_INTELLIGENCE_INDEPENDENT_ANCHOR_ANALYSIS.md`, and `2.1.3C_BEARER_AND_ATTRIBUTION_MODEL.md`
**Structural authority:** Stage-Accepted DR-009 and DR-010

## 1. Analytical question

> Once Knowledge and Intelligence can each be stated independently and attributed through a common Bearer model, which typed relations legitimately connect them without turning either into a hidden definition, universal implication, stored artifact, or authority claim?

The accepted Product Vision calls Knowledge the governed medium and Intelligences the participants whose cooperation produces Amplified Intelligence. The two concepts must therefore be connected strongly enough to explain the SymK value chain and kept distinct strongly enough to diagnose false claims about storage, output, capability, truth, and authority.

## 2. Independent anchors under test

### Knowledge candidate

> **Knowledge is a scoped epistemic achievement attributable to a Bearer, in which success concerning what is the case, how to act, or a subject of familiarity is appropriately connected to truth, competence, or encounter rather than to mere luck, assertion, acceptance, or Representation.**

### Intelligence candidate

> **Intelligence is a scoped, substrate-realized capacity attributable to an organized Bearer to integrate available structure, signals, experience, and feedback in interpreting situations or producing effective responses across relevant variation, including cases not exhaustively fixed in advance.**

### Common attribution constraint

Actual bearing, an attribution claim, an attribution assessment, and an attribution record are distinct. Evidence for one predicate does not create the other, and a convenient system boundary cannot expand for success and contract for failure, responsibility, or cost.

## 3. Strongest reciprocal statement

The current analytical recommendation is:

> **Knowledge and Intelligence are distinct candidate foundational concepts connected by contingent, typed, and developmental relations. Intelligence can enable the pursuit, achievement, interpretation, testing, connection, application, and revision of Knowledge. Knowledge can enable, constrain, scaffold, and develop Intelligence by supplying truth-connected achievements, competences, familiarity, structures, methods, examples, and preserved epistemic history. Neither stored Knowledge-related artifacts nor successful Intelligence performance establishes the other predicate automatically.**

“Can” is essential. These relations depend on the Bearer, capability profile, Knowledge mode, Domain, Scope, Context, role, resources, organization, and evidence. No claim is made that every Intelligence performs every epistemic role or that every instance of Knowledge develops Intelligence.

## 4. Directional relationship analysis

### 4.1 Intelligence toward Knowledge

| Typed relation | Legitimate minimum | What must not be inferred |
|---|---|---|
| `pursues` | An Intelligence with inquiry capability can frame questions, seek evidence, compare alternatives, and investigate epistemic gaps | Inquiry activity already is Knowledge |
| `contributes_to_achievement_of` | Intelligent interpretation, experimentation, reasoning, discrimination, or competent action can causally contribute to a Knowledge achievement | The Intelligence creates truth, or every output becomes Knowledge |
| `interprets` | A capable Bearer can relate a claim or Representation to Domain, Context, purpose, and alternatives | One interpretation is uniquely correct or sufficiently warranted |
| `tests_or_challenges` | An Intelligence can produce counterexamples, check grounds, expose defeaters, or contest Scope and attribution | Challenge alone defeats a claim; agreement validates it |
| `connects` | An Intelligence can identify relevant relations among claims, evidence, capabilities, encounters, and representations | Structural connection proves truth or epistemic significance |
| `applies` | An Intelligence can use Knowledge, a claim, or an asset in reasoning or action under an authorized role | Knowledge automatically authorizes use, or use proves Knowledge |
| `revises_contribution_or_representation` | An Intelligence can correct, restrict, supersede, or enrich claims, models, methods, and attribution records | A Bearer revises truth itself or directly edits another Bearer's Knowledge state |
| `expresses_or_projects` | An Intelligence can express aspects of an epistemic achievement through testimony, demonstration, procedure, example, model, or other Representation | Expression transfers the complete achievement without loss |

These are capability- and role-qualified relations. A narrow classifier may interpret and propose without being able to investigate, challenge, or revise. An institution may perform those roles through distributed processes. An Intelligence need not possess unitary agency, self-originated goals, action authority, or responsibility merely because one relation applies.

### 4.2 Knowledge toward Intelligence

| Typed relation | Legitimate minimum | What must not be inferred |
|---|---|---|
| `enables` | Relevant Knowledge can make an otherwise unavailable interpretation, inference, action, or learning path possible | Knowledge alone supplies the capacity, resources, authority, or application fit |
| `scaffolds` | Preserved claims, grounds, examples, methods, models, and histories can reduce rediscovery and organize later intelligent work | The artifact is Knowledge or Intelligence; retrieval is understanding |
| `constrains` | Knowledge can rule out options, bound expectations, expose consequences, or require revision of a response | Epistemic constraint is equivalent to legal, moral, or operational authority |
| `develops` | Through learning, Training, Education, practice, encounter, feedback, and institutional memory, Knowledge can participate in the formation or reorganization of capability | Every Intelligence learns online; possession of content increases Intelligence |
| `specializes` | Domain Knowledge can shape Domain Intelligence by supplying objects, distinctions, practices, methods, standards, and relevant variation | Universal Intelligence reduces to accumulated domain facts |
| `evidences` | Practical Knowledge, familiarity, or the competent use of propositional Knowledge can support an Intelligence attribution within Scope | A Knowledge attribution proves broad or general Intelligence |
| `persists_as_reusable_support` | Knowledge-related achievements can leave claims, methods, demonstrations, and other durable Representations usable after a contributing Bearer changes or disappears | The surviving asset remains the original Bearer's Knowledge or becomes a knowing subject |

Knowledge affects capability only through a realized process and organized Bearer. An unread book, inaccessible database, unused classification, or disconnected model does not scaffold a particular Intelligence merely by existing.

## 5. Relationship classes

### 5.1 Constitutive relations

No universal cross-constitution is established.

- Knowledge does not enter the minimum Intelligence definition. An Intelligence may operate under uncertainty and without propositional Knowledge of every relevant fact.
- Intelligence does not enter the minimum Knowledge definition. A Knowledge attribution must satisfy its own mode-specific conditions rather than borrow the label of its Bearer.
- Some specialized cases may have narrower constitutive relations. Practical Knowledge is partly constituted by competence, and some Intelligence profiles are partly constituted by integrated competence across variation. This overlap does not establish concept identity or a universal implication.

### 5.2 Enabling and constraining relations

Bidirectional enabling is strongly supported. Intelligence enables epistemic work; Knowledge enables informed and cumulative capability. The relation is nevertheless non-sufficient: enabling conditions may be unused, misapplied, defeated, inaccessible, or outweighed by other constraints.

Knowledge may constrain intelligent responses by making some interpretations or actions epistemically unavailable. This does not make Knowledge an agent, command, permission, or responsibility Bearer.

### 5.3 Developmental relations

Reciprocal development is supported across time:

```text
Intelligent inquiry, practice, encounter, and challenge
    → possible epistemic achievement
    → claims, evidence, methods, examples, and other Representations
    → governed preservation, access, practice, Training, and Education
    → possible capability development or reorganization
    → new intelligent inquiry and application
```

Every arrow is contingent and governed. The sequence can produce error, dogma, deskilling, dependency, exclusion, or capability decay. Representations mediate the cycle but do not become identical to Knowledge or Intelligence.

### 5.4 Evidentiary relations

- Intelligent performance can produce evidence relevant to a Knowledge attribution, particularly practical Knowledge, but correctness and non-accidentality remain independently required.
- Knowledge attribution can evidence competence, method use, integration, or familiarity relevant to an Intelligence profile, but breadth, variation, organization, and alternative explanations remain independently required.
- Performance, claims, assets, scores, approvals, credentials, and system labels remain evidence or Representations rather than either predicate itself.

### 5.5 Operational relations

In a Domain Intelligence Amplifier, participants operate through claims, evidence, assets, models, and other Representations related to Knowledge. “Knowledge is the shared medium” identifies the governed epistemic role of the medium; it does not mean that Knowledge is a file-like substance transferred losslessly between containers.

Operational sharing can make a claim, ground, method, example, or attribution record available. Whether another Bearer thereby comes to know, becomes more Intelligent, or may act depends on uptake, competence, integration, truth, evidence, Scope, Context, Applicability, authority, and consequence.

### 5.6 Representational and projection relations

- Knowledge claims, assets, evidence, capability profiles, and attribution records can represent selected aspects relevant to Knowledge and Intelligence.
- An Intelligence profile is a projection of evidence about capacity, not the capacity itself.
- A knowledge asset is a governed support package, not a container whose presence proves a Knowledge Bearer.
- Every technical projection must declare purpose, omissions, additions, transformations, and semantic loss.

## 6. Non-entailment and overlap tests

### 6.1 Knowledge attribution does not establish Intelligence

A narrow propositional Knowledge attribution may show a truth-connected epistemic relation without establishing the integrated capacity, organization, relevant variation, or threshold required for Intelligence. Practical Knowledge may provide stronger evidence, but one competence still does not prove general or broad Intelligence.

Therefore:

```text
Knowledge attribution -/-> Intelligence attribution
```

This is an attribution-level non-entailment. It does not claim that actual Knowledge can never depend on cognitive or intelligent capacities.

### 6.2 Intelligence attribution does not establish Knowledge

An Intelligence attribution establishes a scoped capacity under evidence. It does not identify a true proposition, mode-specific competence standard, subject of familiarity, non-accidental epistemic relation, or actual achievement in a particular case.

Therefore:

```text
Intelligence attribution -/-> Knowledge attribution
```

The strongest tension is practical Knowledge. A sufficiently reliable integrated capacity across a task class may satisfy both an Intelligence profile and knowledge-how criteria. The present evidence supports overlap, not a universal entailment, because:

- Intelligence may be attributed to a capacity profile broader or differently organized than one competence;
- practical Knowledge requires a mode-specific standard and non-accidental developed ability;
- Intelligence evidence may remain insufficient for that standard or may concern interpretation rather than performance;
- and the minimum threshold separating capability, competence, practical Knowledge, and Intelligence remains contested.

If later analysis proves that every actual Intelligence necessarily bears some practical Knowledge, DR-011 must state that narrower implication explicitly without collapsing the concepts.

### 6.3 Knowledge without established Intelligence

A Bearer may have a narrowly scoped, non-accidental truth-connected epistemic achievement while available evidence does not establish the integration and variation threshold for Intelligence. SymK need not deny the Bearer's possible Intelligence; it must refuse to infer it from the Knowledge attribution alone.

### 6.4 Intelligence without established Knowledge

A Bearer may display organized flexible interpretation or response while no specific propositional, practical, or familiarity Knowledge attribution has been adequately identified or assessed. SymK may attribute the capacity while keeping the epistemic achievement unresolved. This is especially important when outputs are novel, plausible, or effective but their truth, grounds, or competence conditions remain uncertain.

## 7. Failure cases

| Case | Correct reciprocal reading | Failure rejected |
|---|---|---|
| Language model gives a true answer through hidden leakage | True output is evidence; neither Intelligence nor Knowledge follows without Bearer, capacity, grounds, alternatives, and Scope | Correctness creates both predicates |
| Fixed solver handles novel cases but does not learn online | Intelligence may be supported; accumulated Knowledge may scaffold it; online learning is not required | No learning means no Intelligence |
| Expert recalls a true fact through reliable practice | Propositional Knowledge may be present; one fact does not establish broad Intelligence | Knowledge score is an Intelligence score |
| Skilled physician cannot fully verbalize a diagnosis | Practical/familiarity Knowledge and domain Intelligence may overlap; incomplete Representation defeats neither automatically | What cannot be serialized is absent |
| Repository contains validated classifications | Governed claims and records can scaffold later inquiry; repository storage bears neither epistemic predicate by itself | The database knows and is Intelligent |
| Team combines distributed evidence into a novel conclusion | Collective Knowledge or Intelligence requires separate organization and attribution tests at the team level | Aggregation automatically creates a collective Bearer |
| Institution preserves methods after member turnover | Representations and processes may scaffold institutional capability; persistence of identifiers or records alone proves neither predicate | Durable storage equals institutional Knowledge |
| Autonomous system applies a false but authorized rule | Agency and authorization may be present while Knowledge is absent; Intelligence depends on separate capacity evidence | Authority or autonomy proves epistemic standing |
| Intelligence applies true Knowledge outside its Scope | Truth may remain, but applicability, competence, and authorized reliance can fail | Knowledge licenses every use |
| Knowledge asset survives its author | Reusable support persists; the artifact is not thereby the original Knowledge Bearer | Knowledge transfers as an unchanged substance |

## 8. Applied document-discovery case

Consider a provider submitting a set of documents under an asserted business Domain, with mixing across incompatible Domains prohibited.

1. The provider's Domain declaration is a governed claim and processing constraint, not proof that every file belongs to that Domain.
2. The configured discoverer or classifier may bear an Intelligence capability for interpreting document features across relevant variation.
3. A classification result such as `symk.document.classified_as_type` is an attribution claim about the Document under a Domain classification scheme.
4. Correctness, evidence, provenance, Domain fit, Scope, alternatives, and review may support a Knowledge attribution to an identified human, artificial, institutional, or hybrid Bearer.
5. Storing the classification in a neutral metadata service, MySQL JSON, or SharePoint column creates technical Representations and Projections. It creates neither the document property nor Knowledge by storage.
6. The stored history can scaffold later search, review, retraining, challenge, and classification, thereby enabling or developing later Intelligence without being that Intelligence.
7. Deliberate Domain mixing is a provenance, integrity, and responsibility breach. It can corrupt evidence and capability assessment even when a classifier happens to return a plausible type.

This case demonstrates the complete reciprocal cycle while preserving semantic authority in SymK, Domain specialization in the derived project, and storage-specific projections in engineering environments.

## 9. Product Vision consistency

The reciprocal account preserves the accepted value-chain distinctions:

| Product Vision role | 2.1.3 interpretation |
|---|---|
| Governed domain Knowledge — medium | The epistemic achievements, and governed access to claims, grounds, methods, competences, familiarity evidence, and histories through which inquiry becomes cumulative |
| Cooperation among Intelligences — process | Typed participation by distinct Bearers through inquiry, contribution, challenge, interpretation, application, revision, and formation |
| Amplified Intelligence — target capability | A later system-level capability attribution requiring an explicit Bearer and evidence of amplification, not merely more stored content or one better answer |
| Domain reasoning — exercised capability | Manifestation that may use Knowledge and evidence Intelligence but is identical to neither |
| Answer — output | A Representation that may express a claim; quality and Knowledge status require further assessment |
| Feedback and learning | Possible evidence and developmental mechanism, not guaranteed correction or a universal Intelligence condition |

The account also sharpens the statement that SymK engineers conditions through which Intelligences pursue, express, preserve, share, challenge, apply, and revise Knowledge. Expression, preservation, and sharing operate through Representations; pursuit and challenge do not guarantee achievement; application requires separate applicability and authority; revision changes claims, models, attributions, or capability—not truth by decree.

## 10. DR-009 disposition tests

### Knowledge

The reciprocal analysis does not expose a need to define Knowledge through Intelligence. Its independent candidate foundational disposition remains supported.

### Intelligence

The reciprocal analysis does not expose a need to define Intelligence through Knowledge. Its independent candidate foundational disposition remains supported.

### Reciprocal relationship family

| Test | Result | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Necessary distinction | Pass | SymK cannot explain its medium, participants, capability development, and value chain without typed cross-relations |
| Independent foundational subject | Not established | The reciprocal relations depend on Knowledge, Intelligence, Bearer, Scope, Context, Domain, and role rather than supplying a third foundational kind |
| Material distinctiveness | Pass | Collapsing enablement, constitution, evidence, development, application, and Representation causes false entailments |
| Best disposition | Governed supporting typed relationships | Universal minimum at S2; domain specializations and concrete criteria at P2; projections at E2 |

**Working recommendation:** retain Knowledge and Intelligence as distinct candidate foundational concepts and govern their reciprocal connections as a typed relationship family. No single universal `related_to` or symmetric `reciprocal_with` relation is semantically sufficient.

## 11. Human-semantic nucleus

> Intelligence makes epistemic work possible, but capable activity does not guarantee Knowledge. Knowledge makes intelligent work cumulative and constrained by what has been learned, but stored content does not itself become Intelligence. They develop and support one another through Bearers, practices, and Representations while remaining different kinds of achievement and capacity.

## 12. Machine-semantic preservation obligations

Without selecting a formal language, later projections must be able to preserve:

- the distinct identities and versions of the Knowledge and Intelligence concepts;
- the Bearer and attribution level on each side of a claim;
- direction and type of relation: pursues, contributes to achievement, interprets, tests, challenges, connects, applies, expresses, projects, enables, scaffolds, constrains, develops, specializes, or evidences;
- Knowledge mode and Intelligence capability/profile dimension;
- actual and declared Scope, Context, Domain, time, and applicability;
- whether a relation is constitutive, enabling, developmental, evidentiary, operational, representational, or merely hypothesized;
- source, assessor, evidence, alternatives, uncertainty, authority, intended use, challenge, expiry, and lineage;
- resources and representations mediating the relation;
- component, individual, collective, institutional, distributed, and hybrid Bearer boundaries;
- and explicit non-entailment where a projection might otherwise infer one predicate from the other.

No symmetric edge, shared label, participant type, document property, score, or boolean can preserve this reciprocal model by itself.

## 13. Consequences if retained

### Candidate Constitution

- A1 should distinguish Knowledge, Knowledge attribution, evidence, and representations while treating both Knowledge and Intelligence attributions as fallible.
- A2 should allocate roles from qualified capability and epistemic evidence without deriving authority, responsibility, or standing from Intelligence or Knowledge alone.
- A4 should govern conditions and projections that support the reciprocal cycle without claiming that engineering stores Knowledge or manufactures Intelligence directly.
- A later DIA axiom or definition must identify the amplified Bearer and distinguish increased capacity from increased content, output, or access.

These are impact findings, not constitutional decisions.

### Foundation Papers

The Knowledge and Intelligence v0.2 papers should share the reciprocal statement and typed relation vocabulary. The Knowledge paper should no longer define the knowing subject as an Intelligence. The Intelligence paper should no longer place Knowledge inside the independent minimum definition. Both may then explain enabling, constraining, developmental, evidentiary, operational, and representational relations explicitly.

The Knowledge Engineering paper should explain that engineered representations mediate the reciprocal cycle without becoming either predicate. The Education paper should later distinguish Knowledge-mediated formation from mere content delivery and capability development from output conditioning.

### Derived projects

Projects own Domain-specific Knowledge objects, propositions, competences, tasks, capability profiles, thresholds, tests, and relationship specializations. They must not redefine universal Knowledge as stored metadata or universal Intelligence as successful classification. Concrete systems must declare the Bearer and evidence for every material attribution.

## 15. Preserved dissent and open questions

1. Some theories may hold that every actual Intelligence necessarily possesses at least practical Knowledge; current evidence establishes overlap but not universal implication.
2. Some theories may hold that every Knowledge Bearer must possess a minimal Intelligence; attribution-level non-entailment does not settle that metaphysical claim.
3. Practical Knowledge and Intelligence both invoke competence across variation; their threshold and organization boundary requires further testing.
4. “Knowledge as medium” can sound container-like even when operational sharing occurs through claims, assets, and Representations.
5. “Knowledge develops Intelligence” may anthropomorphize Knowledge unless the mediating learning, practice, institutional, or engineering process is explicit.
6. “Intelligence produces Knowledge” can obscure truth, non-accidentality, distributed grounds, and the contribution of external participants.
7. Institutional and collective cases may support literal system-level predicates or only explanatory shorthand; separate evidence remains necessary.
8. An Intelligence may interpret or act effectively without propositional Knowledge, but whether its competence thereby constitutes practical Knowledge remains open.
9. Understanding may be a Knowledge mode, Intelligence capability, both, or a distinct epistemic achievement.
10. The value chain may require stronger vocabulary for epistemic uptake, teaching, testimony, trust, and distributed dependence.

## 16. Confidence and reopening conditions

**Confidence:** High that Knowledge and Intelligence must remain distinct; High that their universal relations are typed and contingent rather than one symmetric dependency; High in the enablement, development, evidence, operational, and Representation distinctions; Medium in the practical-Knowledge overlap and cross-bearer implications.

Reopen or revise if:

- practical Knowledge and Intelligence cannot be separated under counterexample testing;
- every viable Intelligence anchor proves a universal Knowledge dependency;
- every viable Knowledge bearer condition proves a universal Intelligence dependency;
- 2.1.4 cannot express the medium role without making an asset identical to Knowledge;
- 2.1.5 shows that formation requires a stronger reciprocal relation;
- 2.1.7 cannot distinguish amplification from accumulated Knowledge or improved performance;
- or derived-project translation cannot preserve the typed relations without semantic contradiction.

## 17. Working conclusion and next dependency

Knowledge and Intelligence remain distinct candidate foundational concepts. Their reciprocity is best represented by a governed family of directional, typed, scoped, and challengeable relationships—not by circular definitions, automatic cross-attribution, or one symmetric edge.

The independent anchors, common Bearer/attribution model, and reciprocal account are now available. The next required 2.1.3 action is combined counterexample, cross-domain, candidate-axiom, and impact testing. Only if that consolidated stress test survives should `SYMK-2X-DR-011` be drafted as Proposed for project-steward review.
