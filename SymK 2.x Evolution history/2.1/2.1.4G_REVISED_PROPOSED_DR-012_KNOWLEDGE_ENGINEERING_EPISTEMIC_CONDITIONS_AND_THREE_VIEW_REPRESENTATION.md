## 1. Decision question

> What does SymK engineer when it cannot engineer truth or Knowledge directly, which epistemic distinctions and lifecycle obligations must it govern, and what must Human, Scientific, and Engineering views preserve across Representation and Projection?

## 2. Revised proposed decision

Stage-Accept the following as provisional program semantics:

1. **Knowledge Engineering is a foundational SymK concept.** It designs, constructs, governs, evaluates, and evolves epistemic conditions. It may improve conditions for Knowledge without manufacturing truth or Knowledge as an artifact.
2. **Epistemic Condition is a governed supporting distinction.** It identifies a condition materially affecting pursuit, possible achievement, attribution, expression, preservation, assessment, application, challenge, or revision of Knowledge in declared Context and Scope.
3. **The epistemic lifecycle is recursive.** Framing, inquiry/acquisition, representation/claim, grounding/evaluation, integration, reliance/application, consequence, challenge/revision, and preservation/supersession can repeat, branch, and reopen one another.
4. **The epistemic and artifact distinctions in Section 3 remain separately expressible.** No claim, artifact, status, Representation, authority, or successful consequence substitutes automatically for truth or Knowledge.
5. **`KnowledgeObject` is not admitted as a foundational concept.** A narrowly qualified engineering umbrella remains deferred to 2.5 only if it avoids conflating achievement, asset, record, Document, resource, and runtime instance.
6. **Representation remains foundational and Projection remains a governed supporting relationship under DR-010.** Their 2.1.4 obligations include provenance, transformation, uncertainty, dissent, alignment, and declared semantic loss.
7. **SymK requires three coordinated semantic views: Human, Scientific, and Engineering.** They are roles in preserving and testing meaning, not formats, organizations, maturity levels, or exclusive artifact classes.
8. **Human readability, formal expressibility, and machine inspectability are cross-view capabilities.** They are not an exhaustive substitute for the three views.
9. **Alignment is triangular.** Human↔Scientific, Scientific↔Engineering, and Human↔Engineering mappings must be inspectable, with collective closure across complete transformation paths.
10. **DR-009’s human/machine rule remains a minimum preservation obligation.** This decision supersedes only its interpretation as exactly two exhaustive semantic views.
11. **Program-work lanes remain distinct from semantic views.** Semantic/constitutional development, corpus integrity, and grounding/verification may proceed as separate lanes while using all three views.
12. **SymK governs a minimum Ontology and Logic boundary for its own meanings and prohibited conflations.** It does not select a universal Domain Ontology, one Logic, formal language, final package anatomy, or runtime architecture.

## 4. Lifecycle and artifact rules

The lifecycle is a relationship model, not one mandatory workflow. A material lifecycle Representation must preserve, where applicable: subject and identity; question and purpose; Context, Scope, Domain, and Applicability; Bearer or candidate Bearer; activities, actors, sources, methods, and authorities; claims, Grounds, Evidence, counterevidence, and inferential bridges; assessment scheme and statuses; uncertainty, disagreement, limitations, and alternatives; intended reliance and authorization; consequences and affected parties; challenge, correction, revision, preservation, and supersession; version, lineage, Projection, and semantic loss.

No activity automatically entails the next. Acquisition is not Knowledge; Representation is not truth; Evidence is not sufficient warrant in every Context; assessment is not actual bearing; authorization is not correctness; successful application is not proof; and preservation is not current authority.

The DR-011 plural Knowledge minimum remains controlling. Propositional, practical, experiential or acquaintance-like, and candidate collective/distributed modes can require different conditions and evidence. One artifact anatomy cannot be assumed to capture all modes.

Knowledge semantics describe the concept. Epistemic artifacts express claims, Grounds, assessments, provenance, uncertainty, disagreement, and reliance decisions. Runtime records track operational state. Documents are Representations/resources. Search indexes, embeddings, graphs, vectors, summaries, rankings, and interfaces are Projections or engineering artifacts. None becomes actual Knowledge by storage or naming.

## 5. Three-view model

### Human view

Makes meaning available for comprehension, interpretation, judgment, explanation, dissent, and responsible challenge. It preserves purpose, Context, Scope, examples, counterexamples, assumptions, uncertainty, stakes, limitations, and reasons.

### Scientific view

Supports disciplined inquiry and criticism. It preserves questions, claims, methods, Grounds, Evidence, counterevidence, models, inferential bridges, uncertainty, disagreement, replication or reproducibility, defeat, and revision. “Scientific” includes appropriately disciplined empirical, formal, interpretive, and practice-based inquiry; the label alone grants no authority.

### Engineering view

Supports construction, operation, validation, interoperability, traceability, automation, and controlled transformation. It preserves identities, typed relationships, constraints, lifecycle, provenance, status, version, validation, declared transformation, and loss without allowing schema or implementation to own meaning.

Human readability, formal expressibility, and machine inspectability may occur in every view. Formal expression can clarify or suppress; machine inspection can validate structure but not manufacture epistemic sufficiency; readability can expose purpose and dissent but does not guarantee precision.

## 6. Alignment and Ontology/Logic minimum

Every material Projection or cross-view mapping identifies source and target subjects/views; purpose, authority, Context, Scope, and consumer; selected dimensions and relations; additions, omissions, transformations, assumptions, and defaults; treatment of Evidence, uncertainty, disagreement, and status; validation and known non-correspondence; version and lineage; and declared semantic loss and consequences.

Triangular alignment tests Human↔Scientific, Scientific↔Engineering, and Human↔Engineering. Complete paths must also be tested collectively because pairwise mappings can hide compounded loss.

SymK must identify and relate its governed subjects, concepts, distinctions, Contexts, Scopes, Domains, Bearers, authorities, statuses, dependencies, Representations, Projections, claims, Grounds, Evidence roles, assessments, uncertainty, disagreement, reliance decisions, lifecycle events, versions, and lineage. It must express constraints sufficient to detect identity substitution, scope leakage, authority substitution, status conflation, circular definition, invalid dependency, undisclosed transformation, and loss.

This is a semantic requirement, not a language selection. Packages belong to 2.5; formal profiles, conformance, lowering, and implementation readiness belong to 2.9; Domain Ontology and Logic remain project-owned under SymK constraints.

## 7. Alternatives considered

- **Engineer Knowledge or truth directly:** rejected as reification of achievement.
- **Reduce Knowledge Engineering to information, data, or software engineering:** rejected as incomplete.
- **Admit one foundational `KnowledgeObject`:** rejected because it conflates achievement, claim, asset, record, Document, resource, and instance.
- **Use exactly two exhaustive human and machine lanes:** rejected after project-steward challenge because it suppresses the Scientific view and conflates views, capabilities, and program lanes.
- **Give formal semantics or one Logic exclusive authority:** rejected because a formal system is a scoped Representation, not epistemic sufficiency.
- **Delegate all epistemic semantics to projects:** rejected because SymK needs a universal minimum.
- **Reconciled lifecycle system with three coordinated views:** proposed.
