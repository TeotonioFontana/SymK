## 2. Authority and interpretation controls

Authority is typed and scoped. No artifact or actor has all of the following merely because it is canonical, expert-authored, formally precise, implemented, successful, legally binding in one jurisdiction, or accepted for program continuation:

| Authority kind | Governing object | Typical competent source | Does not automatically establish |
|---|---|---|---|
| semantic | universal or Domain meaning, distinctions and non-entailments | Stage-Accepted SymK decision or competent Domain semantic authority | truth, constitutional force, legal validity, actual predicate or implementation adequacy |
| constitutional | universal compatibility commitment and jurisdiction | expressly Ratified Constitution, axiom or constitutional article | epistemic truth, Domain expertise, local authorization or implementation fact |
| epistemic/evidential | support, warrant, method, uncertainty and defeat | competent inquiry, Evidence, assessment and scientific/Domain methods | governance status, legal permission, moral legitimacy or constitutional amendment |
| Domain/professional | Domain concepts, methods, competence and professional standards | competent profession, institution or Domain authority | universal SymK meaning or authority outside the Domain |
| legal/regulatory | rights, duties, prohibitions, legal status and permitted action | applicable law, court, regulator or lawful authority | truth, moral completeness or universal SymK semantics |
| ethical/social/community | moral reasons, community norms, affected standing and social legitimacy | competent ethical, democratic, community and affected-subject processes | one universal legal rule, scientific result or technical design |
| program governance | stage status, decision lineage, acceptance and repository change | project steward and governed SymK process | constitutional Ratification, truth or external authorization |
| project governance | derived-project identity, project axioms and compatibility claim | declared P1 project authority | universal SymK amendment or organizational adoption |
| engineering/design | durable engineering obligations, specifications and architectures | E1/E2 owner within inherited constraints | semantic, constitutional, Domain or operational authority by design choice |
| implementation | concrete code, model, schema, pipeline and runtime behavior | I1 owner and observed system state | governing meaning, conformance, responsible use or authorization |
| organizational/operational | adoption, staffing, access, configuration, risk acceptance, suspension and use | competent organization or deployment authority | project/SymK compatibility, truth or universal policy |
| affected-subject/challenge | evidence of consequence, burden, exclusion, interest and grounds for challenge | affected persons, communities, representatives and competent forums | automatic factual correctness, universal veto or silent amendment |

Affected-subject standing is not merely another expertise or capability rank. It may arise from exposure, burden, rights, dependency, exclusion, or consequence and therefore cannot be conditioned on Intelligence, professional performance, participation, contribution, technical access, or agreement.

Evidence may challenge every authority, but it changes an owned artifact only through that owner's governed procedure. External authority may decisively constrain action without owning SymK meaning. Constitutional or semantic compatibility never authorizes an otherwise prohibited deployment. Legal or organizational authorization never manufactures truth, Knowledge, Value, or responsible use.

## 3. Jurisdiction and precedence model

The Stage-Accepted two-axis model remains adequate:

| Locus | Primary reach and authority | Required Stream E boundary |
|---|---|---|
| S0 inquiry/evidence | Evidence, criticism, alternatives and candidate conclusions | no normative force by publication, prestige, consensus or recency |
| S1 SymK Constitution | universal Ratified compatibility commitments | cannot define complete concepts, Domains, architecture, implementation or local use |
| S2 SymK semantic/program corpus | universal semantic and methodological authority by status | cannot acquire constitutional force or external jurisdiction silently |
| P1 project Constitution | Domain-project identity and durable project commitments | cannot redefine inherited S1/S2 meaning; may challenge or declare incompatibility |
| P2 Domain semantic corpus | Domain Ontology, Logic, concepts, methods and thresholds | cannot universalize Domain categories or professional authority |
| E1 engineering governance | durable project/system engineering obligations | cannot create philosophical or Domain meaning as design preference |
| E2 specification/architecture | contextual requirements, contracts, projections and architectures | alternative conforming realization remains possible unless expressly constrained |
| I1 implementation | concrete realization and runtime fact | supplies evidence but not governing meaning or authorization |
| O1 organization/deployment | local policy, authorization, configuration and operation | may restrict or stop use without rewriting higher artifacts |
| external authority | law, regulation, science, ethics, profession, community and technical standards | enters laterally at applicable Scope and retains its own owner and jurisdiction |

Precedence therefore requires five questions: Are the statements applicable to the same subject, Scope, Context, Domain, time and version? What authority kind does each possess? Which artifact does each owner control? Can both be honored through specialization, strengthening, Projection, local restriction or separate status? If not, which competent authority may change or suspend which object?

No universal total order among authority kinds is admitted. A legal prohibition may control deployment while leaving semantic meaning unchanged. A semantic correction may require project migration while leaving an external rule intact. A severe affected-subject consequence may require immediate operational containment while epistemic and constitutional review remain open.

## 4. Tension audit method

Each tension is tested for:

1. preservation of both legitimate poles;
2. exact accepted concepts and distinctions that make it expressible;
3. continued validity of its 2.0 problem and relationship classes;
4. minimum universal or foundational SymK obligation;
5. project, organizational, empirical or external-authority remainder;
6. Three-View expression and declared-loss risk;
7. affected-subject, dissent, challenge and correction route;
8. constitutional consequence without selection;
9. blocker, reopening or later-owner condition; and
10. non-resolution by integration, implementation, consensus or status.

“Preserved” means governable without erasure, not balanced, optimized, settled or closed.

## 10. Analytical constitutional-readiness tests

The following ten tests are inputs to DQ-021 and 2.2 only. They are not an accepted axiom method.

| Test | Passing question | Failure signal |
|---:|---|---|
| 1. constitutional necessity | Would omission permit a system incompatible with SymK's durable universal identity or governance? | desirable principle or implementation preference presented as constitutional necessity |
| 2. irreducibility/compression | Does the statement express one commitment not fully derivable as interpretation or consequence of another? | exposition, policy, process or several commitments compressed into one axiom |
| 3. universality | Does it survive every legitimate Domain, intelligence configuration, non-DIA use and affected horizon within SymK reach? | historical dyad, one workflow, one institution, one pedagogy or DIA-only assumption made universal |
| 4. semantic maturity | Are every governed term, relation, non-entailment and status supported by Stage-Accepted semantics? | obsolete `FC-*` reference, homonym, hidden primitive or unaccepted concept does normative work |
| 5. jurisdiction | Does the commitment remain within S1 and route Domain, legal, ethical, organizational, project and operational choices correctly? | constitutional imperialism, external-authority erasure or local rule universalization |
| 6. independence/coherence | Can it coexist with every other candidate and accepted DR without duplication, contradiction or circular derivation? | purposive parent both derives and is derived from children; conflicting necessary claims |
| 7. counterexample/non-substitution | Does it reject tempting role, capability, artifact, status, outcome and authority substitutions? | conformance possible through ceremonial status or proxy optimization |
| 8. Three-View expressibility | Can its meaning, evidential test and engineering implications align with declared loss and disagreement? | readable slogan, scientific construct or schema becomes owner of commitment |
| 9. tension/DQ preservation | Does it preserve both poles, accepted components, downstream remainders, dissent and reopening? | axiom silently closes a tension, DQ or external-policy question |
| 10. governability/migration | Are owner, ratifier, challenge, amendment, dependent artifacts, transition and failure consequences identifiable? | immutable declaration, undefined authority or automatic downstream rewrite |

Passing means ready for constitutional deliberation, not Candidate status or Ratification. Candidate promotion additionally requires the adopted axiom-document standard, exact source selection, independent review, designated authority and governed decision.

## 13. Principal conflict and reconstruction findings

### 13.1 A0 purpose/process/Value conflict

The preserved statement “Knowledge is their shared medium; cooperation is their objective” cannot govern after DR-016/017 without revision:

1. Cooperation is a Foundational relation and constitutive process where it actually occurs, not the final good or universal terminal state.
2. DR-017 places the broader organizing center on a governed knowledge undertaking and the general discipline/framework of responsible Knowledge Engineering, with DIA as principal but non-exhaustive realization.
3. DR-016 makes Answer, Value, consequence, affected distribution, correction and Learning independently governed; none is reducible to more Cooperation.
4. Harmful, coercive, collusive or exclusionary Cooperation can satisfy the thin occurrence nucleus while failing responsible qualification and Value.
5. Some bounded SymK-governed uses may involve one Intelligence Bearer or no continuing cooperative relation; exact general-realization status remains open.

This is a direct mismatch between a Proposed axiom and accepted semantics. It does not reopen the accepted semantics. A0 must be reconstructed in 2.2 so purpose, medium, process, principal realization, plural Value, affected horizon and bounded jurisdiction remain distinct.

### 13.2 A2 allocation and standing conflict

A2 correctly prohibits origin-only epistemic privilege but over-compresses five different relation families. A mature statement must distinguish:

- capability and epistemic role claims based on predicate-specific Evidence;
- Authority and Authorization arising from competent governance or external jurisdiction;
- Responsibility relations sourced by object, phase, standard, causation, role and response;
- affected-subject standing arising from exposure, burden, rights or consequence rather than performance; and
- moral, legal, democratic or community standing not owned by universal SymK.

Non-privilege does not imply identical criteria, equal capability, equal Authority, equal Responsibility, interchangeable roles, or SymK power to reallocate external standing. The current exposition says much of this, but a Ratified normative statement cannot depend on exposition to repair an overbroad grammatical grouping.

### 13.3 A3 Formation/Education/Training conflict

DR-013 Stage-Accepts Formation as Foundational and keeps Learning, Training and Education distinct. Neither Education nor Training universally entails the other; neither is universally necessary or sufficient for persistent DIA status. DR-015 adds a narrower capability-continuity obligation satisfied by plural renewal, replenishment, substitution, challenge, recovery or succession paths—or an explicit gap—not one universal Education/Training mechanism.

A3 may retain a constitutional question about formative effects, challenge capacity and responsible continuity. It cannot retain the current universal necessary chain `stable Cooperation → Education → repeated Training → effective habits`. Any later statement must specify Bearer, level, persistence, mechanism, Power, affected consequence, alternatives, uncertainty and external educational authority without constitutionalizing one pedagogy.

### 13.4 J1 identity and bounded-jurisdiction mismatch

J1's article class and anti-imperialism are coherent, but “requirements necessary to create Domain Intelligence Amplifiers” now under-describes SymK's accepted identity. A revision must:

- state the broader responsible Knowledge Engineering discipline/framework without claiming generic Knowledge Engineering as a whole;
- retain DIA as principal, non-exhaustive realization;
- include Question, Reasoning, Answer, Value, consequence, correction, affected horizon and Three-View boundaries;
- keep the undertaking non-reifying;
- distinguish SymK semantic/program authority from later constitutional authority; and
- preserve Domain, project, organization, deployment, legal, professional, ethical, democratic, community and affected-subject jurisdictions.

The current text remains Proposed and may continue as historical/candidate evidence. It cannot be treated as the final DR-017-compatible jurisdiction article.
