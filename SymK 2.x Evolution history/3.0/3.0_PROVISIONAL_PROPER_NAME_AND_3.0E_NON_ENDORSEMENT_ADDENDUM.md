# SymK 3.0 — Provisional Proper Name and 3.0E Non-Endorsement Addendum

**Status:** Scaffold addendum — 3.0 remains closed
**Evidence:** `SYMK-2X-EV-172`

## Provisional release convention

The first consolidated release may use “SymK” as its controlled historical proper name. “Symbiotic Knowledge” remains historical lineage and is not presumed to define the current framework.

## 3.0B boundary

The integrated audit verifies consistent use of the provisional proper name, exact DR-017 identity semantics, lineage and non-entailments. It does not decide permanent name fitness.

## 3.0E required non-effect

Any successful 3.0E decision must state:

> **Acceptance and publication of SymK 3.0 use “SymK” as the controlled historical proper name of this release. The decision does not Ratify, permanently endorse or close challenge to the project/framework name or its historical expansion. The substantive challenge remains assigned to separately opened post-3.0 Stage 3.1.**

No 3.0 acceptance language may imply otherwise.
