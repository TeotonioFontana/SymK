## Required evidence discipline

Every conclusion must identify source authority, exact object, alternatives, counterexamples, limitations, dissent, affected artifacts/projects, decision competence and reopening conditions. Publication, placement, implementation, success or repetition cannot manufacture semantic or normative authority.

## Stop conditions

Stop and route the issue if work would bypass a predecessor gate, silently reopen an accepted decision, erase a tension or deferred remainder, manufacture Authority/standing/right/duty/remedy, migrate without accepted disposition, allow a projection to own meaning, or imply successor-stage approval.

An immutable, reproducible and governed first consolidated release with preserved dissent, limitations, history and continuation roadmap.
