# SymK 2.8 — Framework-Name Migration Deferral Addendum

**Status:** Scaffold addendum — 2.8 remains closed
**Evidence:** `SYMK-2X-EV-172`

2.8 may normalize corrupted filenames, repair paths/links and preserve aliases under accepted dispositions. It may inventory the effects of a possible future framework rename and establish migration capability.

It must not decide or execute a framework-wide “SymK” rename. That semantic challenge and any conditional migration belong to 3.1 after accepted 3.0E. Any broader implication in the 2.8 scaffold is constrained by this addendum.
