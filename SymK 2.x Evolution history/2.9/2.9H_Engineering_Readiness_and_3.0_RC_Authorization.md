## Purpose

Audit readiness and authorize or refuse an exact 3.0 release candidate.

## Entry conditions

- Formal 2.9 opening is effective.
- Required predecessor packages have passed.
- Inputs and authority are exact, current and frozen.
- No unresolved issue invalidates the inherited baseline.

## Required inputs

Passed 2.9G and complete readiness dossier.

## Required analysis

- State questions, alternatives, claims and exclusions explicitly.

- Record affected artifacts, projects, identifiers and downstream obligations.
- Separate evidence from decision and semantic disposition from physical migration.
- Require competent procedure for every governed change.

## Deliverables

## Non-effects
