# SymK 2.x Evolution Overview

## Program objective

The SymK 2.x line will consolidate SymK through a sequence of intellectually meaningful stages. Version numbers indicate maturation of the governed corpus, not elapsed time, document count, or implementation activity.

The current baseline is developmental. It is not a final Constitution, and consolidation does not mean philosophical closure. SymK must remain capable of improvement while making every improvement explicit, traceable, challengeable, and compatible with its lineage.

## Evolution sequence

- **2.0 — Identity and Jurisdiction:** Product Vision, purpose, jurisdiction, layer boundaries, conflict direction, and governing tensions; former A5 reclassified as Proposed Jurisdiction Article J1.
- **2.1 — Foundational Conceptual System:** governed meanings, distinctions, dependencies, and boundaries for the concepts required by the SymK identity, value chain, and candidate Constitution; followed by Foundation Papers v0.2 for human communication.
- **2.2 — Constitutional Axiom Set:** necessity, independence, wording, relationships, tests, and preserved dissent.
- **2.3 — Constitutional Governance:** statuses, authority, ratification, challenges, exceptions, amendments, and supersession.
- **2.4 — Legacy Disposition:** explicit treatment of existing axioms, principles, and incompatible terminology.
- **2.5 — Foundational Concepts and Principles:** reconstruction of `FC-*`, `FP-*`, and `SYMK-P-*` beneath the Constitution.
- **2.6 — Derived-Project Governance:** project constitutions, Engineering Charters, inheritance, specialization, and compatibility.
- **2.7 — Grounding Validation:** apply the framework to LexBrain and sshConnectivity and return conflicts to SymK.
- **2.8 — Corpus Consolidation:** Knowledge Families, review queue, lineage, canonical registry, and repository structure.
- **2.9 — Engineering Readiness:** stable conceptual interfaces, specifications, validation gates, and readiness for implementation.
- **3.0 — First consolidated SymK release:** constitutionally coherent, governed, traceable, and ready for sustained engineering, but still revisable.

## Current stage

The active stage is **SymK 2.1 — Foundational Conceptual System**. SymK 2.0 — Identity and Jurisdiction was Stage-Accepted on 18 August 2026 through `SYMK-2X-DR-008`.

The 2.1 central question is:

> **What minimum coherent conceptual system must SymK govern so that its identity, value chain, and candidate Constitution can be understood without allowing projects, representations, or implementations to redefine their meaning accidentally?**

## Control philosophy

Every stage must preserve:

- the questions asked;
- the historical formulations considered;
- the sources and evidence used;
- alternatives adopted, rejected, or deferred;
- decisions and their authority;
- dissent and uncertainty;
- effects on other artifacts and projects;
- and the conditions for later revision.
