# SymK 2.x Versioning Policy

## Version meaning

Each minor version marks completion of one consolidation stage. It does not force every source paper or dependent artifact to adopt the same version.

- `2.0-dev.n` — working iteration; incomplete and unstable.
- `2.0-rc.n` — complete proposed stage baseline under final review.
- `2.0` — Identity and Jurisdiction baseline accepted for continuation of the 2.x program.
- `2.1` through `2.9` — later accepted consolidation stages.
- `3.0` — first fully consolidated governed corpus.

Patch versions after acceptance correct or clarify a stage without changing its accepted semantic commitment. A semantic change reopens the appropriate stage or advances the governed version according to its impact.

## Independent artifact versions

Foundation Papers, axioms, concept specifications, project constitutions, and Engineering Charters retain independent versions. Their metadata must state which SymK baseline they inform, implement, or inherit.

## Stage completion

A stage version is accepted only when its gate is satisfied, its summary is complete, its decisions are traceable, and the affected canonical corpus has passed consistency review.
