# PLC_META — 1.6.1 Decision Framework (Template)

## Purpose

Define the **allowed decisions** at the end of PLC_1 for any product instance and the **preconditions** to make that decision.

PLC_1.6 does **not** authorize a product launch.  
It decides whether the idea deserves to enter the **architecture & engineering lab** (PLC_2+).

## Decision Types at PLC_1.6

At the end of PLC_1, each product instance must choose **exactly one** of:

1. **Advance to Architecture — Product Track**
   - The instance is allowed to enter PLC_2+ as a **candidate product**.
   - It will be treated as something that might become a market-facing offer, if the architecture/sizing phases confirm feasibility and value.

2. **Advance to Architecture — Internal Tool Track**
   - The instance is allowed to enter PLC_2+ as a **candidate internal tool**:
     - To support internal operations,
     - To power consulting / professional services,
     - Or to remain as a strategic enabler rather than a direct product.
   - It is **not** positioned as a market-facing product at this stage.

3. **No-Go / Park**
   - The instance will **not** proceed to architecture and engineering now.
   - Knowledge is retained; **re-entry conditions** must be defined (what must change to revisit it).

A **clean No-Go or Park** with well-documented reasoning is a **successful outcome** of PLC_1: it means Discovery did its job of avoiding bad paths.

## Preconditions (Baseline Discovery Outcomes)

Before a product instance is allowed to make a PLC_1.6 decision, these PLC_1 artifacts must exist in a minimally solid form:

- **1.1 Product Vision**
  - Target segments / personas
  - Problem and promise
  - North Star narrative / “why now”

- **1.2 Market Research**
  - Defined segments and initial prioritization
  - Competitors and alternatives, with pricing patterns
  - Evidence the problem exists beyond imagination

- **1.3 Stakeholder Map**
  - Buying center and power dynamics for main segments
  - Key roles, goals, fears, triggers, objections

- **1.4 Discovery Methodologies (PLC-side)**
  - Methods used (interviews, JTBD, competitor teardown, etc.)
  - Explicit “done criteria” for PLC_1

If these are missing or clearly weak, the product’s 1.6.1 file should state:

> Discovery not complete — return to strengthen 1.x before deciding Advance / No-Go.

No pretending.

## Product-specific file requirements

Each product-specific `1.6.1_*.md` file must:

1. Confirm that the preconditions (1.1–1.4) are met at a “good enough” level.
2. Declare the chosen decision type (Advance to Architecture — Product, Advance to Architecture — Internal Tool, or No-Go / Park).
3. Justify the decision with a few clear reasons.
4. Reference the other 1.6.x files (business size fit, consulting leverage, career leverage, success metrics & risks).

This keeps 1.6.1 as the **gatekeeper summary**, while the detailed reasoning lives in 1.6.2–1.6.5.
