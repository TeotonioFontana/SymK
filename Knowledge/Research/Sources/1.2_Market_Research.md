# 1.2 Market Research

_Phase 1 — Discovery_

## 1. Purpose / Objective
Understand the market landscape, opportunity size, competition and external forces that influence the product strategy.

## 2. Description
Collects and structures information about customer segments, competitors, benchmarks, and regulatory context. The output informs positioning, scope decisions, and helps avoid building something that is already commoditized or strategically weak.
