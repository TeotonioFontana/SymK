# SymK boundary mapping for Legal Semantic Components

**Identifier:** SYMK-LEGAL-CSJ-BOUNDARY-0.1  
**Status:** Non-normative mapping evidence  
**Date:** 2026-09-20  
**Domain authority:** LexBrain legal domain  
**Vocabulary custody:** VocabularyRegistry package `legal.csj` 0.1.0

## Purpose

Record how the candidate Legal-domain concept **Componente Semântico Jurídico (CSJ)**
preserves applicable SymK distinctions without admitting CSJ into SymK Core.

## Mapping

| Legal/LexBrain entity | SymK-aligned treatment | Prohibited inference |
|---|---|---|
| Source document | Document, resource or Representation according to context | A document is not Knowledge by containment |
| Source fragment | Located Representation and possible Evidence | Extraction does not create semantic truth |
| CSJ candidate | Proposed domain interpretation or assertion | A model confidence score is not approval |
| CSJ | Legal-domain engineering entity with governed semantic identity | CSJ is not a SymK Core primitive |
| CSJ textual Representation | Concrete Representation of a domain entity | Wording is not the semantic identity |
| CSJ property assertion | Assertion with value, origin, provenance and review state | Stored value is not automatically true |
| CSJ evidence link | Provenance/Evidence relation under declared scope | Provenance alone does not establish truth |
| CSJ governance status | Review and reliance-control state | Approval is not universal applicability |
| CSJ search projection | Rebuildable engineering Projection | Index content is not authored semantic authority |
| Composition manifest | Epistemic/engineering record of selection and transformation | Composition does not grant legal authority |
| CSJ collection or package | Possible Knowledge asset role under explicit governance | CSJ does not automatically contain Knowledge |

## Authority boundary

- LexBrain legal-domain authority decides CSJ meaning and legal consequences.
- VocabularyRegistry provides governed custody, integrity and release discovery.
- Customer organizations govern their instances, values, content and reliance.
- SymK provides general distinctions only and acquires no authority over legal meaning.

## Core exclusion

Thesis, argument, request, legal basis, precedent, petition and Legal Semantic
Component remain domain concepts. This record does not modify SymK Core, its concept
register, canonical documents or standards.

## Change rule

If future SymK decisions alter the applicable distinctions, LexBrain and
VocabularyRegistry must reconcile through an explicit reviewed mapping version. No
silent equivalence or retroactive promotion is allowed.
