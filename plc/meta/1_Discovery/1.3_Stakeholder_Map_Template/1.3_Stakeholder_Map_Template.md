# PLC_META — 1.3 Stakeholder Map Template

## Purpose

Describe the **buying center** and key stakeholders around the product.

## Required views

1. **Roles, not job titles**
   - Strategic buyer, technical influencer, risk/compliance, finance, operations, partners.

2. **Goals and fears**
   - What each role wants and what they are afraid of.

3. **Triggers and objections**
   - Typical events that start the buying motion.
   - Common objections per role and how we might respond.

4. **Power dynamics**
   - Who can say “yes” and who can say “no”.
   - Informal influencers (e.g. trusted engineers, external advisors).

## Output shape

- Short prose sections per segment or a combined view if segments are similar.
- A simple table or diagram is encouraged but not mandatory.
