# PLC_META — 1.6.2 Business Size Fit Template

## Purpose

Provide a **standard way** to answer:

> “For what size of business is this product idea suitable?”

for any product instance.

## Size Buckets

Use these buckets as a baseline:

- **Very small / solo**  
  Freelancers, solo consultants, micro-startups (≈1–5 people).

- **Small**  
  5–50 people, few systems, little or no formal compliance.

- **Mid-market**  
  50–500 people, multiple systems/accounts, some formal audits or strong customer demands.

- **Enterprise**  
  500+, multi-team, multi-account / multi-region, heavy governance.

- **Service providers**  
  MSPs, consulting firms, platform providers, partners (operate across many client organizations).

## Scoring

For each bucket, assign a **fit score**:

- **0** — basically irrelevant / wrong target
- **1** — edge case / occasional fit
- **2** — workable but not ideal
- **3** — natural home / primary fit

Each product-specific `1.6.2_*.md` file must:

1. Provide a small table: bucket, description, score.
2. Explain the reasoning in a few bullets per bucket.
3. End with a **sentence-level answer**, e.g.:

> “This product is primarily suited for mid-market and service providers, and secondarily for lower-enterprise; it is overkill for very small setups and only selectively relevant for small companies under strong compliance pressure.”

The goal is to kill the illusion that “everyone is the target.”
