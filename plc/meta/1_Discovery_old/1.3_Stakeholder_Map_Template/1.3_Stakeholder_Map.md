# 1.3 Stakeholder Map

_Phase 1 — Discovery_

## 1. Purpose / Objective
Make explicit who can influence or is affected by the product, how much power they have, and what they care about.

## 2. Description
Builds a structured view of internal and external stakeholders: roles, interests, expectations, influence, and potential conflicts. This leaf underpins realistic decisions about scope, risks and communications.
