# PLC Product Vision Method

This directory preserves generic Product Life Cycle specifications and templates for creating product visions. These documents define a method applicable to products; they are not the SymK Product Vision.

`PLC_Chapter_10_Product_Vision_Features.md` is preserved as the more developed variant. The earlier `PLC_Chapter_10_Product_Vision.md` remains for comparison until a separate methodology review explicitly supersedes or merges it.
