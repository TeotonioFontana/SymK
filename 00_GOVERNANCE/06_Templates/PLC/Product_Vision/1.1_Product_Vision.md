# 1.1 Product Vision

_Phase 1 — Discovery_

## 1. Purpose / Objective
Establish a concise, shared understanding of why the product exists, who it serves, and what long-term change it aims to create.

## 2. Description
Captures the core narrative of the product: mission, target users, key problems, value proposition, and success criteria. The vision acts as the north star for all subsequent PLC decisions and should be stable enough to survive multiple delivery waves.
