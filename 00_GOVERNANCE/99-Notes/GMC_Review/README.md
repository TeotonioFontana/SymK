# SymK GMC Review Package

**Status:** Non-authoritative upstream-learning review package
**Version:** 0.1
**Date:** 4 September 2026
**Authority:** Project-steward instruction to return to and evaluate the GMCs
**Normative effect:** None

## Purpose

Preserve and evaluate fourteen generalizable method conclusions extracted from a
derived-project experience without importing protected material, transferring
project authority or treating local learning as a universal SymK rule.

## Package contents

1. `SYMK_GMC_DISPOSITION_REGISTER_v0.1.md` — one-row-per-conclusion classification,
   compatibility assessment, proposed route and remaining work.
2. `SYMK_GMC_COOPERATING_GOVERNANCE_PACKAGES_EVALUATION_v0.1.md` — integrated
   evaluation of the four cooperating governance packages and their interaction
   with `SYMK-GP-001`.

## Current result

The evaluation is complete enough to organize later review. No GMC is accepted,
rejected or made normative by this package. `SYMK-GP-001` remains the independently
adopted negative-control policy; its adoption does not dispose of any GMC.

The GMCs remain a non-exhaustive improvement set. Other SymK improvements may exist
outside this package and remain discoverable through ordinary governance and
challenge processes.

## Prohibited inferences — what this package does not mean

- evaluation does not establish acceptance;
- compatibility does not establish redundancy or completeness;
- a proposed route does not open its destination stage;
- grouping conclusions does not erase their independent status;
- one derived-project experience does not establish universal validity; and
- the fourteen GMCs are not claimed to be the only possible SymK improvements.
