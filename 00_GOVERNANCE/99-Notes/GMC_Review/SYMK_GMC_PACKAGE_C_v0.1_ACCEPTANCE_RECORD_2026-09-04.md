## 1. Decision

The project steward accepts the exact Package C v0.1 object identified above as a
provisional SymK proportional-assurance and governance-cost profile.

GMC-009 and GMC-010 are accepted only through the profile's exact
consequence-proportionality, bidirectional-calibration, governance total-cost,
governance-caused-risk, escalation/de-escalation, non-compensation, authority and
semantic-limit formulations. They do not acquire a universal risk scale, fixed
assurance ladder, Domain risk tolerance, independent policy or standard status
through this decision.

## 2. Acceptance basis

The accepted object:

1. makes assurance a contextual relation to an actual consequence and function
   configuration rather than an intrinsic project property;
2. preserves sensitivity, uncertainty, reversibility, distribution, external
   effect and non-compensable conditions without mandatory scalar aggregation;
3. tests assurance deficiency and assurance excess or misfit independently;
4. includes governance burden, delay, opportunity cost, fatigue, displacement,
   exposure, exclusion, lock-in and lost learning within total-cost and consequence
   assessment;
5. supports evidence-bearing escalation and de-escalation without inventing
   authority or erasing applicable duties;
6. prefers the least burdensome configuration credibly supported as adequate while
   keeping adequacy, alternatives and residual uncertainty challengeable;
7. composes with accepted Packages A and B without selecting a method, assigning a
   role, accepting risk or issuing a Trial or Delivery Release; and
8. passes initial SPServices, multi-Domain, escalation/de-escalation,
   under-governance, over-governance and negative-control tests.

## 3. Positive effects

This decision:

1. changes Package C v0.1 from proposal to accepted provisional
   proportional-assurance and governance-cost profile;
2. accepts GMC-009–010 only as bounded members of that exact profile;
3. makes the profile prospectively applicable to material new or materially revised
   SymK project assurance-calibration and governance total-cost/risk claims within
   project-steward jurisdiction;
4. permits the profile to constrain Package A method/release configurations and
   Package B function/role/handoff configurations when Package C is applicable;
5. makes later operational-template work eligible only under a separately
   applicable Execution Release;
6. makes later 2.4 classification and 2.6 specialization eligible when those stages
   are competently open; and
7. preserves Sections 29, 32 and 33 as continuing tests, dissent/open questions and
   reopening conditions.
