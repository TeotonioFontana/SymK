## 1. Purpose

Provide the smallest practical Human-readable records needed to apply the accepted
Acting Roadmap without collapsing its first three states:

```text
event record
  -> candidate call
  -> mediation constitution and engagement-admission decision
```

The suite operationalizes the question **“What is this application of SymK being
called to mediate?”** while preventing a request, alert, preferred solution or form
field from manufacturing an engagement, route or authority.

## 2. Proposed suite

1. `SYMK_ACTING_ROADMAP_EVENT_RECORD_TEMPLATE_v0.1.md` — records what was observed
   or requested and whether a candidate call should be considered.
2. `SYMK_ACTING_ROADMAP_CANDIDATE_CALL_TEMPLATE_v0.1.md` — challenges the caller's
   framing and decides whether there is enough basis to prepare a mediation
   constitution.
3. `SYMK_ACTING_ROADMAP_MEDIATION_CONSTITUTION_TEMPLATE_v0.1.md` — defines the
   possible undertaking, participants, affected parties, actual mediator,
   Representations, authority, feasibility and engagement-admission disposition.

Each template is a separate record because combining them would allow an event to
inherit engagement or route status merely by occupying one completed form.

## 3. Minimum-use rule

Use only the smallest record required by materiality and uncertainty:

- A low-consequence event handled under current standing authority may stop with a
  brief event record and reference the standing process.
- A materially new, disputed or reopened SymK application proceeds through the
  three separate records.
- Unknown information remains `unknown`; it is not completed by inference merely to
  satisfy the form.
- A field may be `not material` only with a short reason when omission could affect
  the decision.
- Existing trustworthy artifacts may be cited rather than copied.
- A record may be written in concise prose; tables and identifiers are optional
  project Representations, not semantic requirements.

## 4. Separation invariants

| Record | May establish | Must not establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| event | attributable observation or request and next-decision need | complete meaning, mediation warrant, route or Authority |
| candidate call | provisional mediation-object alternatives and whether constitution is worth preparing | admitted engagement, Problem, objective or action |
| mediation constitution | bounded engagement-admission decision | selected route, consequential-action release or successful outcome |

No later record rewrites the earlier source. Corrections create a new version with
declared lineage.

## 5. Human, Scientific and Engineering coverage

The templates preserve the current Three-View minimum without demanding three
different documents:

- **Human:** participant meaning, affected parties, Power, burden, refusal,
  disagreement and challenge;
- **Scientific:** claims, Evidence, counterevidence, uncertainty, alternative
  interpretations and disconfirmation; and
- **Engineering:** identity, version, event, state, dependencies, provenance,
  access, feasibility, transition, expiry and correction.

The strength of each coverage area remains proportional to consequence and the
claim being made.

## 6. Authority controls

Every template ends with an explicit authority statement. The required default is:

```text
authority created by this record: none
```

Only the mediation constitution contains the Acting Roadmap engagement-admission
dispositions. Even `admit` authorizes route selection within the constituted
boundary; it does not authorize consequential route action.

An urgent signal may be routed for consideration by competent authority. It does
not create emergency duty, access, containment or execution power.

## 7. Technology and context-portability constraint

The templates require an honest account of:

- context actually available to each participant;
- handoff and representation losses;
- unavailable group, memory, identity or access technology;
- Human coordination burden; and
- a feasible sufficient route under present capability.

A template cannot convert a future or unaffordable capability into a present
project assumption.

## 8. Relationship to downstream profiles

The suite stops before route selection. It does not invoke:

- the possible-Problem branch or challenged problem-first material;
- Problem-Investment complexity or investment assessment;
- Package A objective, method, trial or delivery controls;
- Package B role activation beyond constitution evidence;
- Package C assurance configuration; or
- Package D lifecycle action or upstream acceptance.

Those profiles become applicable only through later separately authorized work.

## 9. Acceptance conditions

Accept this suite only if review finds that it:

1. preserves event, candidate-call and constitution separation;
2. begins with what is being mediated;
3. challenges caller framing without inventing a Problem;
4. identifies actual mediator, participants, affected parties and authority;
5. supports refusal, deferral, redirect and no-mediation outcomes;
6. represents feasibility and context portability honestly;
7. remains short enough for practical use;
8. avoids mandatory document inflation for low-consequence work;
9. creates no route or action authority; and
10. passes the separate dry-run validation report.

## 10. Known limitations

- Real use may reveal overlap between candidate-call and constitution fields.
- Free-form records improve usability but reduce automatic validation.
- Compact forms can still conceal power, omitted participants or biased framing.
- No operational pilot has tested completion time, comprehension or decision
  quality.
- The suite does not yet provide route-selection, action-release or disposition
  templates.
