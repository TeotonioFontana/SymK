# SymK GMC Cooperating Governance Packages Evaluation v0.1

**Status:** Integrated analytical evaluation — no package accepted or opened
**Date:** 4 September 2026
**Depends on:** `SYMK_GMC_DISPOSITION_REGISTER_v0.1.md` and provisional active
`SYMK-GP-001`
**Normative effect:** None

## 1. Governing question

How can the fourteen GMCs cooperate to improve SymK governance without becoming a
single universal workflow, bypassing competent stages or implying that they exhaust
the available improvements?

## 2. Integrated answer

The GMCs form a recursive governance arrangement with four cooperating packages:

1. **objective and method fitness** identifies the external intended outcome and
   treats the chosen method as a challengeable route to it;
2. **epistemic role cooperation** organizes discovery, interpretation, evidence and
   control without transferring properties or authority among participants;
3. **proportional assurance and governance cost** calibrates the burden of control
   and includes the costs and risks created by governance itself; and
4. **observation, pause, pivot and upstream learning** detects divergence, enables a
   bounded lifecycle response and returns derived-project evidence to SymK.

`SYMK-GP-001` acts across all four packages by requiring material prohibited
inferences to remain visible. It does not determine the objective, choose the method,
allocate a Domain role, set risk tolerance, trigger a pivot or accept upstream
learning.

The arrangement is a feedback loop, not a guaranteed linear pipeline. Activities may
iterate, overlap or be configured differently under competent Domain and project
authority.

## 3. Package A — Objective and method fitness

**Members:** GMC-001 and GMC-002.

**Function:** Keep the externally observable intended outcome distinct from the
internal plan and treat method selection as an evidence-bearing, revisable choice.

**Contribution to governance:**

- makes business and affected-party justification legible;
- prevents implementation milestones from substituting for outcome;
- exposes assumptions and alternatives behind a selected method; and
- supplies an explicit basis for later method assessment.

**Evaluation:** Coherent and high-value. GMC-001 mainly operationalizes accepted
target/outcome/Value distinctions. GMC-002 adds a method-fitness and reassessment
relation that needs candidate development.

**Recommended analytical state:** **READY FOR A LATER METHOD-GOVERNANCE PROPOSAL.**
This does not open 2.4 or accept a universal objective form.

**Subsequent state:** Package A v0.1 exposed an execution-release gap under the
SPServices-like start-control test and remains preserved without acceptance. The
corrected current proposal is
`SYMK_GMC_PACKAGE_A_OBJECTIVE_AND_METHOD_FITNESS_PROPOSAL_v0.2.md`; it adds separate
Objective Acceptance, Method Selection and Execution Release gates and proportional
bounded autonomy. Correction does not constitute acceptance.

## 4. Package B — Epistemic role cooperation

**Members:** GMC-003 through GMC-008.

**Function:** Separate semantic discovery from evidence/execution control and define
an optional cooperation pattern among standards, AI, competent Humans and
deterministic systems.

**Contribution to governance:**

- standards can accelerate inquiry without becoming automatic local authority;
- AI can expand and challenge candidate space without becoming Knowledge or decision;
- Humans can adjudicate Domain meaning without ceremonial-review inflation;
- deterministic systems can verify form and effects without claiming semantic
  adequacy; and
- interfaces can preserve provenance, uncertainty, alternatives and declared loss.

**Evaluation:** GMC-005–008 strongly confirm accepted SymK boundaries. GMC-003 is a
useful operational distinction. GMC-004 is promising only as an optional,
architecture-neutral method profile. Making its four roles universally necessary
would conflict with substrate independence and legitimate project variation.

**Recommended analytical state:** **READY FOR OPTIONAL PROFILE DESIGN AND
COUNTEREXAMPLE TESTING.** No role allocation or Domain authority is accepted.

## 5. Package C — Proportional assurance and governance cost

**Members:** GMC-009 and GMC-010.

**Function:** Match governance effort to material sensitivity, reversibility,
uncertainty, external effect and consequence while recognizing the opportunity cost
and risks of governance activity.

**Contribution to governance:**

- protects high-consequence work without forcing maximum ceremony everywhere;
- makes under-governance and over-governance independently challengeable;
- includes delayed learning, reviewer fatigue, displaced Domain work and ceremonial
  compliance in total-cost analysis; and
- prevents document count or gate count from becoming an assurance metric.

**Evaluation:** Directionally compatible with accepted proportionality and DR-016
cost/Value distinctions. The addition is an operational assurance profile, not a new
universal risk scale. It requires cases across materially different Domains and
protection against strategic understatement of risk.

**Recommended analytical state:** **READY FOR MULTI-DOMAIN STRESS TESTING BEFORE A
POLICY OR STANDARD PROPOSAL.**

## 6. Package D — Observation, pause, pivot and upstream learning

**Members:** GMC-011 through GMC-014.

**Function:** Observe outcome/learning progress separately from control/readiness
progress; use material divergence as a method-review signal; support a distinct pause
state; and return qualified project evidence upstream.

**Contribution to governance:**

- exposes technically successful motion that is not producing intended learning;
- avoids collapsing pause into failure, rejection, rollback or abandonment;
- preserves accepted work while method fitness is reconsidered;
- connects project experience to SymK correction without silent amendment; and
- creates a recursive learning return rather than one-way inheritance.

**Evaluation:** This package is the most additive and the most dependent on later
governance architecture. GMC-011, GMC-012 and GMC-014 need method-lifecycle and
observability realization. GMC-013 is compatible with planned 2.6 upstream-proposal
work but cannot activate it.

**Recommended analytical state:** **HOLD AS AN INTEGRATED CANDIDATE FOR FUTURE
METHOD-LIFECYCLE, 2.6 AND OBSERVABILITY WORK.**

## 7. Cooperation contract among the packages

| From | To | Required relation | Failure prevented |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Package A | Package B | the intended outcome and selected method constrain role design | tools or roles becoming the objective |
| Package B | Package C | actual role/effect allocation informs required assurance | risk burden detached from the work performed |
| Package C | Package B | assurance constraints shape permissible operation without owning meaning | controls silently becoming semantic authority |
| Packages A–C | Package D | independent outcome and control observations remain available | completion reported from only one progress family |
| Package D | Package A | material divergence returns the selected method for reassessment | path dependence and continuation by inertia |
| Package D | SymK | qualified derived-project evidence enters an upstream review route | local learning lost or universalized silently |
| `SYMK-GP-001` | all transitions | material prohibited inferences remain explicit | property, status, authority and outcome transfer |

## 8. System-level invariants

1. Objective, method, implementation and outcome remain distinct.
2. Discovery, validation, adjudication and effect control may cooperate without
   automatic identity or property transfer.
3. No participant type is universally necessary or sufficient merely because it
   appears in the candidate four-role pattern.
4. Outcome/learning progress and control/readiness progress remain independently
   inspectable.
5. Assurance is consequence-sensitive without one universal scalar or threshold.
6. Governance burden and governance-caused risk remain assessable.
7. Divergence triggers review, not automatic pivot or rejection.
8. Pause preserves lineage and obligations without implying failure.
9. Derived-project evidence can challenge SymK but cannot amend it by transmission.
10. Negative controls constrain invalid inference but do not decide substantive
    questions.

## 9. Principal tensions and counterexamples still required

Before any package-level acceptance, later work must test at least:

- legitimate objectives that cannot be observed within the project horizon;
- regulated methods that cannot pivot freely;
- high-performing arrangements without AI, formal standards or a single Human
  adjudicator;
- emergencies where abbreviated governance is proportionate;
- high-risk work whose uncertainty prevents confident proportional calibration;
- early exploration where outcome progress is intentionally delayed;
- apparent divergence caused by measurement lag or bad indicators;
- pauses that allow evidence to expire or obligations to be neglected;
- project evidence that conflicts across Domains; and
- metrics that induce gaming or suppress unmeasured learning.

## 10. Proposed future sequence

When separately authorized and when owning stages are competent:

1. develop Package A as a method-fitness proposal;
2. design Package B as an optional role-cooperation profile and test alternatives;
3. stress-test Package C across multiple Domains before proposing an assurance policy
   or standard;
4. design Package D jointly with derived-project and observability governance;
5. re-run the package interaction tests and `SYMK-GP-001` prohibited-inference review;
6. decide each GMC independently before any integrated adoption claim; and
7. preserve an open route for improvements not represented by the GMC set.

This order is a proposed dependency sequence, not execution authority.

## 11. Prohibited inferences — what this evaluation does not mean

1. Cooperation among the packages does not establish one mandatory workflow.
2. Package coherence does not establish truth, completeness or acceptance.
3. A recommended analytical state does not open a stage or authorize a proposal.
4. The four-role pattern does not require AI, standards, Humans or deterministic
   systems in every case.
5. Proportionality does not authorize weaker control through convenience.
6. A divergence signal does not automatically require pivot or pause.
7. A pause does not suspend legal, ethical, retention or safety obligations.
8. Upstream evidence does not transfer derived-project authority to SymK.
9. `SYMK-GP-001` adoption does not accept any package or GMC.
10. This evaluation does not establish that the GMCs are SymK's only or most
    important remaining improvements.
