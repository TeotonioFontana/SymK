# SymK GMC Disposition Register v0.1

**Status:** Analytical intake register — no GMC accepted or rejected
**Date:** 4 September 2026
**Authority:** Project-steward instruction “do it” in direct response to the proposed
GMC disposition-register and four-package evaluation
**Scope:** Fourteen value-free generalizable method conclusions
**Normative effect:** None

## 1. Purpose

Classify what each GMC contributes to SymK, distinguish confirmation from extension,
identify the competent future route, and prevent a coherent package from being
mistaken for an accepted governance change.

## 2. Analytical disposition vocabulary

| Disposition | Meaning here |
| --- | --- |
| `CONFIRM_EXISTING` | Strongly reinforces an accepted SymK distinction; no new rule presently identified |
| `CONFIRM_AND_OPERATIONALIZE` | Reinforces accepted semantics and suggests a new project-process or method realization |
| `DEVELOP_CANDIDATE` | Adds a potentially valuable relation, profile or lifecycle mechanism requiring proposal and tests |
| `HOLD_FOR_ROUTE` | Useful but dependent on a closed or later competent work package |

These are analytical dispositions, not governance statuses. They do not accept,
reject, supersede or implement a GMC.

## 5. Cross-GMC dependencies

- GMC-001 supplies the outcome against which GMC-002 method fitness is assessed.
- GMC-003 defines the functional boundary within which GMC-004 allocates roles.
- GMC-005–008 constrain the sources and role claims inside GMC-004.
- GMC-009–010 constrain the burden and cost of every proposed method.
- GMC-014 supplies independent progress observations used by GMC-011.
- GMC-011 may trigger the lifecycle response modeled by GMC-012.
- GMC-013 returns derived-project evidence from the complete loop to SymK.
- `SYMK-GP-001` protects every dependency from property, status and authority
  transfer; it does not decide the dependency.

## 6. Conflicts and tensions preserved

1. outcome legibility versus legitimate long-horizon or indirect purpose;
2. method adaptability versus stability, compliance and comparability;
3. role specialization versus integrated or emergent performance;
4. Human semantic authority versus ceremonial or unavailable review;
5. standards efficiency versus lock-in, mismatch and inherited error;
6. deterministic assurance versus false confidence in semantic adequacy;
7. proportional control versus inconsistent or strategically understated risk;
8. governance discipline versus delay, fatigue and displaced Domain work;
9. pivot responsiveness versus premature abandonment;
10. project learning versus unjustified universalization; and
11. independent progress profiles versus dashboard complexity and metric gaming.

These tensions are not defects to remove automatically. They define required future
tests and jurisdiction-sensitive choices.

## 7. Prohibited inferences — what this register does not mean

1. `CONFIRM_EXISTING` does not establish that current SymK wording or realization is
   sufficient.
2. `CONFIRM_AND_OPERATIONALIZE` does not authorize a new policy or implementation.
3. `DEVELOP_CANDIDATE` does not establish likely acceptance.
4. `HOLD_FOR_ROUTE` does not open or reserve authority in the destination stage.
5. A count or disposition label does not express importance, confidence or priority.
6. Interdependence does not make the GMCs one indivisible proposal.
7. Absence from this register does not establish that another improvement is
   irrelevant.
8. The register does not dispose of the source project's method or roadmap.
