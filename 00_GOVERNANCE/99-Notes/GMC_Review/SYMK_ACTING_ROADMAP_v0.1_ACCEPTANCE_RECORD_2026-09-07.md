## 3. Accepted governing effect

Within SymK project-steward jurisdiction, the Acting Roadmap v0.1 is accepted as a
provisional project-process profile for material new or materially reopened SymK
applications.

It establishes that:

1. an observable event or direct request creates at most a candidate call;
2. every candidate call begins by asking **“What is this application of SymK being
   called to mediate?”**;
3. mediation constitution identifies the possible governed undertaking, mediation
   object, participants and affected parties, Representations, actual mediator,
   Context, Scope, standing, Authority, Responsibility, feasibility and uncertainty;
4. the caller's wording does not automatically own the mediation object;
5. engagement admission is separate from event registration and route selection;
6. clarification/translation, Representation reconciliation, possible-Problem
   investigation, Cooperation coordination, decision support, disagreement
   preservation and refusal/deferral/redirection are selectable and composable
   route families;
7. no route is universal, and routes may conclude, pause, compose, reroute, reopen,
   escalate or remain unresolved with preserved lineage;
8. route selection creates no authority for consequential action;
9. any consequential action requires a separate proportionate release identifying
   the act, actor, Authority, affected boundary, constraints, observation, stopping,
   expiry and retained Responsibility;
10. actual observations, outputs, effects, assessments, Correction, Learning and
    upstream evidence remain distinct; and
11. Problem-Investment and Package A remain downstream specializations rather than
    the universal SymK acting root.

The complete exact accepted object governs over this summary.

## 4. Non-reification and authority boundary

Acceptance does not classify SymK as an Intelligence Bearer, Agent, person,
institution, actual mediator, decision-maker or sovereign authority. “SymK acts” is
shorthand for governed application by identified Humans, artificial systems,
collectives, institutions, hybrids or configured arrangements.

Actual capability, Agency, Authority, Authorization, Responsibility, consent,
standing and affected-subject challenge remain separately evidenced and governed.
An event, candidate call, admitted engagement or selected route creates none of
those predicates automatically.
