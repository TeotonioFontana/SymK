## 1. Test purpose

Test whether the proposed roadmap begins from the mediation object rather than an
O1/T1-style project pipeline, distinguishes events from authority, selects and
composes routes without forcing them, and preserves legitimate refusal,
disagreement, Domain jurisdiction and technical-feasibility boundaries.

The cases test conceptual rules. They do not prove actual capability, participant
consent, Domain facts, problem existence, roadmap effectiveness or universal
validity.

## 2. Expected invariants

The roadmap should preserve these results:

1. an event or request creates at most a candidate call;
2. every candidate call asks what is being mediated;
3. the caller's wording does not own the mediation object;
4. SymK is not reified as the actual actor, Intelligence or authority;
5. actual mediators, participants, affected parties and authorities are explicit;
6. no route is universal or self-authorizing;
7. route selection and consequential-action release remain separate;
8. routes may compose, stop or reroute without manufacturing success;
9. no Problem, objective, consensus or decision is required;
10. absent technology or context portability reduces, defers or defeats the route;
11. disagreement and no-mediation dispositions remain legitimate; and
12. local observations do not become universal SymK rules automatically.

## 3. Case results

### Case 1 - Solution-shaped direct request

**Input:** A caller says, “Add medical-document properties and run the trial.”

**Expected:** The request becomes an event report and candidate call. Constitution
asks what is actually being mediated, who is affected and whether the matter is a
possible Problem, representation difference, decision or something else. No
property objective or trial is created.

**Observed profile result:** PASS. Sections 5-7 block caller-framing ownership and
Section 9.3 routes possible-Problem inquiry without accepting it.

### Case 2 - Straightforward translation request

**Input:** Two authorized specialists use different languages for the same bounded
claim and need an inspectable translation.

**Expected:** Admit a clarification/translation route when participants,
Representations, mediator capability and use are adequate. Do not require Problem
Acceptance or objective/trial preparation.

**Observed profile result:** PASS. Section 9.1 is independently admissible and
preserves translation loss and non-equivalence.

### Case 3 - Divergent data model and clinical account

**Input:** A schema and a physician's narrative appear to describe the same medical
document but divide its properties differently.

**Expected:** Reconcile Representations by mapping subject, Context, perspective,
granularity and omissions. Neither schema nor professional account becomes
sovereign; Domain acceptance remains external.

**Observed profile result:** PASS. Sections 6 and 9.2 preserve source and
Representation differences without granting ontology authority.

### Case 4 - Legitimate persistent disagreement

**Input:** Competent participants understand one another but retain incompatible
value judgments with no authority to impose one answer.

**Expected:** Preserve disagreement, affected positions and reconsideration
conditions. Do not force reconciliation or consensus and do not treat all positions
as equally evidenced automatically.

**Observed profile result:** PASS. Section 9.6 supplies an admissible non-resolution
route with explicit non-entailments.

### Case 5 - Decision-support request

**Input:** A competent manager requests alternatives and evidence for a deployment
decision but has not delegated the decision itself.

**Expected:** Support the decision under the declared Question, alternatives,
Evidence, Value, affected consequences and dissent. The mediator may recommend but
cannot decide, approve or deploy.

**Observed profile result:** PASS. Sections 9.5 and 11 separate decision support,
decision Authority and execution.

### Case 6 - Cooperation blocked by handoff failure

**Input:** Human and artificial contributors have adequate individual capabilities,
but missing context and unclear handoffs repeatedly break a shared inquiry.

**Expected:** Coordinate Cooperation by identifying contributions, dependencies,
shared memory, context portability and challenge. Do not attribute whole-level
Cooperation or success merely because several contributors exist.

**Observed profile result:** PASS. Sections 9.4, 13 and 14 preserve capability,
role, Cooperation and feasibility boundaries.

### Case 7 - One current Intelligence Bearer

**Input:** One researcher compares incompatible historical sources with no other
participant presently available.

**Expected:** The roadmap may admit clarification, Representation reconciliation or
inquiry if warranted. It does not manufacture plural Cooperation, but it does not
reject the engagement solely because only one Intelligence Bearer is active.

**Observed profile result:** PASS. Section 2 and the audit preserve DR-017's one-or-
more-Bearer boundary.

### Case 8 - Automated anomaly alert

**Input:** Monitoring software emits an unexplained drift signal.

**Expected:** The alert is an event Representation. It may trigger candidate-call
constitution and Human or institutional interpretation; the machine signal creates
no Problem, urgency, action Authority or mediator competence automatically.

**Observed profile result:** PASS. Sections 5.1-5.3 and 13 distinguish event source,
observer, mediator and authority.

### Case 9 - Affected party absent from the table

**Input:** Builders and buyers agree on a system change, while people bearing the
privacy risk are absent.

**Expected:** Constitution records absent affected parties, representation limits,
standing and challenge. Agreement among present participants cannot authorize
erasure or establish responsible Cooperation.

**Observed profile result:** PASS. Sections 6.2, 6.3 and 13 make affected absence
material without manufacturing an automatic veto.

### Case 10 - Required group technology does not exist

**Input:** A proposed route assumes inexpensive shared persistent conversational
context for several external reviewers, but the available platform cannot provide
it.

**Expected:** Feasibility fails or the contribution is reduced to a workable
artifact-and-handoff approach. The roadmap cannot promise full-context group
mediation and then hide Human coordination cost.

**Observed profile result:** PASS. Sections 7.2 and 14 require available capability,
context transport and a sufficient lower-technology route.

### Case 11 - Possible emergency

**Input:** An event report suggests active credential exposure, but SymK analysis
holds no operational containment authority.

**Expected:** Route urgent consideration to competent authority and preserve the
event and uncertainty. Do not infer a duty, access right or containment release.

**Observed profile result:** PASS. Sections 7.3 and 15 maintain the DR-018 emergency
boundary.

### Case 12 - No mediation warranted

**Input:** Two records differ only in harmless formatting, the users already
understand the difference and no decision or consequence depends on it.

**Expected:** `no_mediation_warranted` is a valid admission disposition. The roadmap
does not invent an undertaking to justify itself.

**Observed profile result:** PASS. Section 7.3 permits a successful non-engagement
result.

### Case 13 - Material change during mediation

**Input:** An engagement begins as translation, then reveals incompatible legal
jurisdictions and an authority dispute.

**Expected:** Preserve the translation result, pause any unauthorized continuation
and reroute or redirect the legal/authority question. Do not disguise the changed
object as translation completion.

**Observed profile result:** PASS. Section 10 requires rerouting with preserved
lineage when object, Context or Authority changes.

### Case 14 - Deceptive or coercive invocation

**Input:** A sponsor asks SymK to manufacture apparent consensus and suppress a
participant's recorded objection.

**Expected:** Refuse the engagement or route protected challenge under competent
authority. Sponsorship does not make the request legitimate, and the roadmap does
not delete dissent.

**Observed profile result:** PASS. Sections 6.3, 7.3, 9.6 and 9.7 preserve refusal,
Power and dissent boundaries.

### Case 15 - Legitimate route composition

**Input:** A cross-disciplinary decision contains terminology ambiguity, divergent
models and a later authorized choice among alternatives.

**Expected:** Clarify/translate and reconcile Representations before decision
support, possibly preserving residual disagreement. Each route has its own scope and
does not inherit decision or execution Authority.

**Observed profile result:** PASS. Section 10 permits sequential composition without
automatic transition or authority inheritance.

### Case 16 - SPServices property challenge

**Input:** Existing SPServices artifacts assume that useful medical-document
properties should be discovered and tested. The project steward asks whether the
system first needs to establish what SymK is being called to mediate.

**Expected:** The event is the framing challenge. Constitution identifies a possible
undertaking involving project assumptions, intended physician activity,
Representations, missing affected-user Evidence and the project's existing
investment. Initial routes may include clarification, Representation reconciliation
and possible-Problem investigation. No `O1`, `T1`, property vocabulary or trial is
accepted, reopened or executed.

**Observed profile result:** PASS. The roadmap begins above the Problem/objective
branch and Section 16.4 places Problem-Investment and Package A downstream.

## 4. False-positive review

The roadmap admits legitimate cases without forcing Problem framing:

| Case | Admissible route or disposition |
| --- | --- |
| simple language mismatch | clarify or translate |
| model/document mismatch | reconcile Representations |
| independently uncertain condition | investigate a possible Problem |
| blocked shared undertaking | coordinate Cooperation |
| competent decision locus | support a decision |
| irreducible conflict | preserve disagreement |
| wrong venue or no benefit | redirect or no mediation warranted |
| credible urgent risk | urgent consideration by competent authority |

Result: no tested valid case is forced into a Problem, objective, trial or consensus
workflow.

## 5. False-negative review

The roadmap exposes these invalid substitutions:

| Failure pattern | Roadmap response |
| --- | --- |
| request becomes complete mediation object | constitution and caller-framing challenge |
| event becomes urgency or Authority | event non-entailments and admission gate |
| SymK framework becomes actual actor | non-reification and actual-mediator field |
| route becomes execution release | separate bounded action release |
| several participants become Cooperation | DR-013 route boundaries |
| translation becomes equivalence | loss and non-equivalence controls |
| reconciliation becomes consensus | disagreement-preservation route |
| decision support becomes decision | separate decision locus and Authority |
| ideal group technology becomes feasible capability | context-portability gate |
| local success becomes universal SymK truth | Package D upstream-evidence route |

Result: each tested shortcut stops before unsupported action or semantic promotion.

## 6. State and transition review

The control map permits:

- event without candidate-call admission;
- admitted engagement without consequential action;
- one or several routes;
- route completion with unresolved disagreement;
- pause, refusal, redirect, reroute and reopening;
- urgent consideration without inferred emergency power; and
- local observation without upstream acceptance.

It prohibits automatic event-to-route, route-to-action, output-to-answer,
recommendation-to-decision, decision-to-execution and effect-to-learning transitions.

## 7. Proportionality review

The proposal calls its constitution dimensions a coverage guide and allows concise
records. Its residual risk is that teams may require every field for trivial
interactions or use admission as a centralized gate over legitimate Domain activity.

Any later template must therefore make materiality explicit, permit a short-form
record for low-consequence work and test whether governance burden itself blocks
participation or transfers cost to affected parties.

## 8. Test conclusion

The Acting Roadmap v0.1 passes the initial sixteen-case conceptual suite. It places
the mediation-object question before Problem/objective framing, supports all proposed
route families, handles route composition and feasibility, and preserves explicit
non-authority boundaries.

Evidence remains insufficient for universal or constitutional status. Operational
pilots are required to test route overlap, intake burden, mediator bias, effective
affected-party challenge, context portability and the adequacy of “mediation” as the
root operational term.
