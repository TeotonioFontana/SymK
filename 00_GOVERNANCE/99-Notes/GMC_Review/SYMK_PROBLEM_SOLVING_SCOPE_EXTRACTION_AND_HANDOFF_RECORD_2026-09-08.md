## 1. Decision represented

General problem identification, complexity assessment, solution planning,
investment-worth control and implementation management are externalized from
SymK's prospective core responsibility into an independent `ProblemSolving`
project.

SymK remains centered on mediation among different Intelligences,
Representations, interests and authorities. Its first operational inquiry remains:

> What is this application of SymK being called to mediate?

This disposition implements a project boundary. It does not accept the new
project's charter, research foundation, reconstructed method or handoff-interface
wording.

## 3. Current SymK-owned boundary

SymK retains responsibility for:

- event recognition and candidate mediation-call intake;
- asking what is to be mediated without treating the caller's frame as authority;
- mediation constitution, admission, deferral, refusal and redirection;
- participants, affected parties, standing, authority and responsibility;
- reconciliation or preservation of plural Representations and disagreement;
- mediation-route selection and composition;
- separating mediated contribution, recommendation, decision and authorization;
- receiving external analytical results where a mediated decision needs them; and
- traceability and qualified upstream learning.

The accepted Acting Roadmap and minimum intake template suite remain in SymK.

## 4. Externalized operational boundary

The independent project may develop methods for:

- problem finding, discovery, formulation and reframing;
- solution-contamination and fixation control;
- problem-type, complexity and uncertainty characterization;
- solution, inquiry, monitoring and inaction alternative generation;
- method/search strategy comparison;
- value-of-learning, total-burden and investment-worth assessment;
- capability and context feasibility;
- bounded probes, interventions and staged-program recommendations; and
- stop, reconsideration and lifecycle logic for problem-solving work.

Domain projects and competent authorities continue to own execution and domain
acceptance.

## 5. Scope test

> If an activity can be performed by a single Intelligence without mediating among
> materially different Representations, interests or authorities, it is not part of
> SymK's core merely because SymK can route to it.

The test is a boundary heuristic, not a Foundational definition or exclusion of a
legitimate single-participant mediation involving absent parties or plural sources.

## 7. Prospective composition rule

For material new or reopened SymK applications:

```text
event or request
  -> candidate mediation call
  -> mediation constitution and admission
  -> SymK mediation route
       -> optional bounded handoff to an external problem-solving method
       -> returned evidence, alternatives and recommendation
  -> mediated decision support and separate competent authorization
```

SymK may route to the external project, another method or no method. Routing does
not validate the method or authorize its recommendation.

## 8. Required safeguards

1. No acceptance status transfers from SymK to the new project.
2. The imported method must be reconstructed against a research foundation rather
   than cosmetically renamed.
3. External analysis must return material disagreements and authority conflicts to
   mediation rather than resolve them by technical scoring.
4. A domain project must separately authorize implementation.
5. Papers, cases and copied artifacts are evidence, not automatic rules.
6. The new project must remain usable without SymK where mediation is unnecessary.
