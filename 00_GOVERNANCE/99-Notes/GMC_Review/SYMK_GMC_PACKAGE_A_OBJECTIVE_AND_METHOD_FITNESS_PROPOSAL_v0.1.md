# SymK GMC Package A — Objective and Method Fitness Proposal v0.1

**Status:** Superseded by v0.2 after start-control stress testing — preserved
proposal lineage; never accepted
**Date:** 4 September 2026
**Preparation authority:** Project-steward instruction “Go ahead” following the GMC
package evaluation and its recommendation to develop Package A
**Members:** GMC-001 and GMC-002
**Depends on:** Stage-Accepted DR-016 distinctions, the GMC disposition register,
the cooperating-packages evaluation and provisional active `SYMK-GP-001`
**Normative effect:** None unless separately accepted through competent procedure

## 1. Decision requested

Accept, correct or reject a project-method governance profile that:

1. requires plans to express their objective as an externally meaningful,
   assessable intended outcome rather than as a recursive description of their own
   stages or artifacts; and
2. governs the selected method as a separate, evidence-bearing and challengeable
   hypothesis about how work is expected to contribute to that objective.

Acceptance would authorize later operationalization and competent 2.4
classification. It would not make the profile constitutional, select a method for a
real project, open a closed stage, or accept GMC-003–014.

## 2. Problem addressed

A plan can be internally complete and technically successful while failing to make
the intended external condition more likely. This occurs when:

- the objective is written as “complete the plan,” “implement the architecture” or
  “produce the governed artifacts”;
- method outputs are reported as business or Domain outcomes;
- extensive assurance demonstrates controlled execution without demonstrating
  useful progress;
- the chosen route becomes difficult to challenge because sunk work is treated as
  evidence of continuing fitness; or
- a sponsor cannot explain what is bought without learning the plan's internal
  vocabulary.

The opposite failure also matters. A slogan-like external aspiration may be clear to
a sponsor but too vague to govern decisions, observation, affected subjects, time or
accountability. Package A therefore requires both an outcome-facing objective and a
separate operational method-fitness record.

## 3. Compatibility boundary

This proposal operationalizes, without reopening, the accepted distinctions among:

- target, objective, expectation, intended outcome and actual outcome;
- proposal, plan, strategy, decision, Authorization, execution and action;
- output, quality, cost, Value, consequence, feedback and Learning; and
- Domain/project methods versus universal SymK concepts.

Package A proposes no new Foundational concept. Its working terms are method and
project-governance relations that remain subject to later 2.4 classification.

## 4. Working terms

| Term | Working meaning for Package A | Must remain distinct from |
| --- | --- | --- |
| outcome-facing objective | governed statement of an intended externally meaningful condition or capability, including affected parties, horizon and assessment basis | plan, deliverable, method, metric, actual outcome or Value |
| internal deliverable | artifact, capability, state or checkpoint the plan intends to produce | the external reason the deliverable matters |
| method | organized approach, procedure or pattern selected or considered for contributing to an objective | objective, plan instance, implementation, result or universal rule |
| method hypothesis | scoped claim that an identified method is expected to contribute to an identified objective under declared assumptions and conditions | truth, guarantee, decision, causal proof or implementation authority |
| method fitness | multidimensional assessment relation between a method version, objective version, Context, alternatives, constraints, evidence and affected horizon | method popularity, compliance, completion, performance score or Value |
| indicator or proxy | observable representation used as evidence about a target, objective, process or outcome | the represented construct or actual outcome |
| method assessment | evidence-bearing evaluation of one or more fitness dimensions by an identified assessor | method decision, acceptance, pause, pivot or success |
| method decision | authorized selection, continuation, restriction, replacement or cessation decision concerning a method | assessment, recommendation, implementation or outcome |
| review trigger | governed condition requiring reassessment or escalation | automatic judgment that the method is unfit or must change |

These definitions are proposal-local and do not amend accepted SymK vocabulary.

## 5. Objective rule

A material plan must contain an outcome-facing objective that can answer:

> **What externally meaningful condition, capability or decision quality should be
> different for identified beneficiaries or affected subjects if this work
> succeeds?**

“External” means outside the internal anatomy of the plan. It does not require a
commercial customer, immediate public effect or direct numerical measurement. The
relevant boundary may be a Domain practice, a user's capability, a sponsor's
decision, a governed knowledge state, an organization's resilience or another
declared beneficiary-facing boundary.

The objective may legitimately concern:

- a long-horizon outcome not yet observable, if the horizon, theory of contribution
  and nearer evidence are explicit;
- research or discovery, where the intended result may be reduced uncertainty,
  exposed alternatives, a justified no-answer or improved decision capability;
- infrastructure, where the result may be a dependable capability visible at the
  service or user boundary;
- governance, where the result may be materially better decision conditions,
  challengeability, accountability or prevention of specified invalid effects; or
- harm prevention, where the non-occurrence claim requires a baseline,
  counterfactual and limitations rather than absence-of-incident reasoning.

An objective fails the Package A legibility test when it can only be explained by
restating internal stages, document names, implementation components or compliance
activities and cannot identify why their completion matters outside that structure.

## 6. Minimum objective envelope

The level of detail is consequence-proportional, but a material objective should
identify:

1. objective Identity and version;
2. sponsoring or proposing subject;
3. beneficiaries and materially affected subjects;
4. Domain, Scope and Context;
5. the intended external condition, capability or decision-quality change;
6. current reference condition, baseline or acknowledged absence of one;
7. intended horizon and relevant intermediate horizons;
8. observable outcome criteria and important unknowns;
9. indicators or proxies, with their transformation and validity limits;
10. material constraints and non-compensable conditions;
11. assumptions, dependencies, uncertainty and alternative interpretations;
12. applicable authority and challenge path;
13. internal deliverables and their explicitly claimed contribution, without
    substitution; and
14. revision, expiry and reopening conditions.

Not every field requires a separate section. The information must remain
recoverable and inspectable.

## 7. Black-box objective test

Before approving a material plan, a reviewer should attempt to describe its intended
success while treating the implementation as a black box.

| Question | Passing evidence | Failure exposed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What is different outside the plan? | named condition, capability or decision-quality change | internal completion used as objective |
| For whom or for what affected boundary? | beneficiaries and affected subjects | provider-only or artifact-only framing |
| Why does the difference matter? | declared purpose and Value claim, separately governed | deliverable assumed valuable |
| When could the claim be assessed? | horizon and observation conditions | timeless aspiration |
| What would count against success? | failure, uncertainty and counterevidence conditions | unfalsifiable objective |
| Can it be explained without plan-specific component names? | sponsor-readable statement | recursive technical justification |

A plan may retain detailed internal acceptance criteria after passing this test.
Black-box legibility does not replace Engineering detail.

## 8. Method-hypothesis rule

Every material method selection should state:

> **For objective version O, in Context C, method version M is expected to contribute
> through mechanism or rationale R, relative to alternatives A, under assumptions S,
> with evidence E, costs and risks K, and review conditions T.**

The claim may have low confidence or unresolved alternatives. Honest uncertainty is
preferable to treating the current method as self-justifying.

A mandated method remains governable. The record should distinguish:

- whether the mandate exists and which authority issued it;
- which parts are non-discretionary;
- whether the method is expected to achieve the objective or is required for another
  reason such as law, compatibility or safety;
- which complementary or alternative methods remain possible; and
- how evidence of poor fitness may be escalated when local replacement is not
  authorized.

## 9. Minimum method-selection record

A material selection should preserve:

1. method Identity, version and owner;
2. linked objective Identity and version;
3. selecting and reviewing authorities;
4. intended contribution and causal or practical rationale;
5. Domain, Scope, Context, horizon and affected subjects;
6. prerequisites, assumptions and dependencies;
7. alternatives considered, including continuation without change and qualified
   no-method where material;
8. evidence for and against comparative fitness;
9. material fitness dimensions and non-compensable conditions;
10. direct, indirect, transition, verification, opportunity and residual costs;
11. sensitivity, reversibility, external effects and failure consequences;
12. uncertainty, dissent and missing evidence;
13. observation plan and review triggers;
14. recommendation, assessment, decision and Authorization as distinct records; and
15. correction, reassessment, supersession and cessation paths.

## 10. Method-fitness profile

Method fitness is plural and objective-relative. Applicable dimensions may include:

- relevance to the intended outcome;
- Domain and stakeholder adequacy;
- epistemic yield and uncertainty reduction;
- feasibility and capability availability;
- time to useful or reliance-ready result;
- direct and total cost;
- safety, privacy, legality and ethical compatibility;
- reversibility, recoverability and correction cost;
- interoperability, dependency and lock-in;
- evidence quality and observability;
- adaptability and Learning capacity;
- burden distribution and affected-subject consequences; and
- compatibility with mandatory constraints.

This is an open candidate library. No dimension is universally mandatory or
sufficient. No aggregate score may erase a material failed, disputed, unknown or
non-compensable condition. Criteria, measures, thresholds and weights remain within
competent Domain/project jurisdiction.

## 11. Assessment and decision separation

The following roles may coincide in one subject only when authority and evidence
permit, but their relations remain distinct:

| Role | Contribution | Does not establish automatically |
| --- | --- | --- |
| objective sponsor or claimant | proposes intended outcome and justification | Domain adequacy, Value or authority for all affected subjects |
| Domain authority or reviewer | evaluates substantive meaning and applicability | project funding, implementation or universal truth |
| method proposer | describes method hypothesis and alternatives | selection or comparative fitness |
| method assessor | evaluates declared fitness dimensions | decision or Authorization |
| method decision authority | selects, continues, restricts or changes method | correct assessment, execution or success |
| implementation authority | authorizes or performs work | method authorship, semantic authority or favorable outcome |
| affected subject or representative | supplies experience, standing, challenge or consent relations where applicable | sole ownership of every decision or automatic veto absent governing authority |

A method assessment must not be rewritten as a method decision. A method decision
must not be reported as evidence that the assessment was correct.

## 12. Observation and reassessment interface

Package A requires every selected material method to declare review triggers, but it
does not define the later pause/pivot lifecycle owned principally by GMC-011–012 and
GMC-014.

Candidate triggers include:

- the objective, beneficiaries, constraints or external Context materially change;
- critical assumptions or dependencies fail;
- expected outcome/learning progress and control/readiness progress materially
  diverge;
- important new evidence, counterevidence, alternatives or standards appear;
- total cost, delay, harm, lock-in or governance burden materially exceeds the
  selected boundary;
- a mandated method conflicts with a higher or competent external authority;
- an indicator ceases to represent the intended outcome adequately; or
- the selected review horizon arrives.

A trigger requires reassessment or escalation. It does not automatically establish
pause, pivot, replacement, failure or abandonment.

## 13. Counterexamples and stress tests

| Case | Required Package A result |
| --- | --- |
| plan completes every deliverable but users cannot make the intended decision | report internal completion and external objective failure separately |
| intended public benefit may take ten years to appear | preserve long horizon, contribution theory, interim evidence and uncertainty; do not require immediate outcome |
| research responsibly concludes that available evidence cannot answer the question | allow justified uncertainty reduction or no-answer as an outcome when that was governed explicitly |
| regulator mandates a method that appears inefficient | preserve mandate and compliance while recording fitness evidence and escalation; no unauthorized substitution |
| a team reaches the desired outcome through luck while the method was poor | record outcome without retrospectively validating method fitness |
| method is sound but the objective becomes obsolete | reassess objective and method separately; sunk correctness does not justify continuation |
| a proxy improves while the real-world condition worsens | reject proxy substitution and preserve construct-validity failure |
| no baseline exists | record the absence, use bounded alternatives where possible and reduce claim strength |
| several methods contribute jointly | allow plural contribution without forcing exclusive attribution |
| no known method is presently adequate | permit qualified no-selection, experiment or staged learning rather than false certainty |
| infrastructure has no external customer | use the service, capability, dependent process or affected boundary as the black-box boundary |
| governance prevents a feared event but no incident occurs | require threat/counterfactual reasoning and limits; absence alone is not proof |
| method changes for political or budget reasons | record actual decision basis separately from technical fitness assessment |
| success criteria are gamed | preserve affected outcomes and competing evidence; metric attainment is not objective attainment |

## 14. Acceptance tests for this proposal

Before Package A itself could be accepted, review should establish that it:

1. preserves exact DR-016 objective/outcome/Value and plan/decision/action boundaries;
2. introduces no new Foundational concept or universal Domain objective;
3. makes objectives sponsor-readable without suppressing Engineering detail;
4. accommodates research, infrastructure, governance, prevention and long-horizon
   objectives;
5. keeps indicators and proxies distinct from represented outcomes;
6. governs mandated and discretionary methods without overriding competent authority;
7. preserves alternatives, counterevidence, dissent and uncertainty;
8. treats fitness as multidimensional without one scalar or universal dimension set;
9. preserves assessment, decision, Authorization, implementation and result as
   distinct;
10. interfaces with proportional assurance and later observability without deciding
    GMC-009–014;
11. passes all Section 13 cases under Human, Scientific and Engineering Views;
12. applies `SYMK-GP-001` proportionately; and
13. remains usable without forcing maximal documentation on low-consequence work.

## 15. Proposed operational record shape

A later template may use this compact structure:

1. **Outcome-facing objective** — intended external condition, beneficiaries,
   affected subjects and horizon.
2. **Why it matters** — scoped purpose and Value claim.
3. **How it will be assessed** — baseline, evidence, indicators, uncertainty and
   failure conditions.
4. **Internal deliverables** — outputs and their claimed contribution, without
   substitution.
5. **Selected method hypothesis** — method, rationale, assumptions and dependencies.
6. **Alternatives** — compared, deferred and excluded routes.
7. **Fitness profile** — applicable dimensions, evidence, costs, risks, dissent and
   unknowns.
8. **Authority** — proposers, assessors, decision-makers, implementers and affected
   parties.
9. **Observation and review** — evidence cadence, triggers and escalation.
10. **Prohibited inferences** — what the record does not establish.

This shape is illustrative and creates no schema or mandatory file format.

## 16. Prohibited inferences — what this proposal does not mean

1. An externally legible objective is not automatically correct, valuable,
   authorized or measurable.
2. “Black box” does not mean that implementation, provenance, safety or cost may be
   hidden from competent reviewers.
3. An internal deliverable may be valuable; it simply does not establish the
   external outcome by itself.
4. A method hypothesis does not establish causal contribution, fitness, selection or
   success.
5. Method adaptability does not authorize deviation from law, standards, contracts
   or competent mandates.
6. A review trigger does not automatically establish that a method should pause or
   change.
7. A favorable outcome does not automatically validate the selected method; an
   adverse outcome does not automatically falsify it.
8. More indicators, dimensions or documentation do not establish a better objective
   or method assessment.
9. Acceptance of Package A would not accept Package B, C or D or any individual
   GMC outside GMC-001–002.
10. Preparation or placement of this proposal does not open 2.4, create a policy,
    amend DR-016 or authorize implementation.
11. Package A is not claimed to be the only or highest-priority SymK improvement.

## 17. Dissent and open questions

1. How externally observable must an objective be when outcomes are generational,
   probabilistic or counterfactual?
2. Can reduced uncertainty always be treated as an outcome, or only when an inquiry
   and decision use are identified?
3. When does sponsor-readable simplification conceal a material affected-subject or
   Engineering condition?
4. What minimum alternatives must be considered before method selection is
   responsible?
5. How should method fitness be compared when alternatives have incomparable
   benefits, harms or evidence maturity?
6. Which authority may require reassessment when the method decision authority
   refuses it?
7. When may a legal or contractual mandate be described as method-unfit but still
   binding?
8. How should contributions be represented when many methods, actors and external
   conditions jointly affect an outcome?
9. What is the minimum viable record for low-consequence work?
10. Which Package A requirements belong in policy, methodology, project template,
    standard or later package?

## 18. Reopening conditions

Reassess this proposal if:

- it encourages outcome claims beyond available evidence;
- “external” systematically excludes legitimate internal capabilities or knowledge
  outcomes;
- projects use method-hypothesis language to avoid decisions or accountability;
- required comparison makes small or mandatory work impractical;
- the fitness library becomes a universal checklist or scalar;
- Package D later establishes incompatible lifecycle or trigger semantics;
- multi-Domain tests expose authority, Value or affected-subject conflicts;
- implementation experience shows material burden without improved decision quality;
  or
- accepted SymK conceptual or constitutional changes alter its dependencies.

## 19. Authorities and non-effects

This proposal does not:

- accept GMC-001 or GMC-002;
- decide a real objective, method, assessment or trade-off;
- create a metric, threshold, score, schema, template requirement or automation;
- open, modify or bypass SymK 2.4 or another evolution stage;
- amend Stage-Accepted DR-009–018;
- revise a Product Vision, Foundation Paper, Constitution, axiom or standard;
- alter `SYMK-GP-001`;
- accept or reject GMC-003–014; or
- establish that the GMC set exhausts SymK improvement.

## 20. Proposal result

**PACKAGE A IS PREPARED FOR PROJECT-STEWARD REVIEW AS A NON-NORMATIVE
OBJECTIVE-AND-METHOD-FITNESS PROPOSAL. IT REQUIRES AN OUTCOME-FACING OBJECTIVE AND A
SEPARATE, EVIDENCE-BEARING METHOD HYPOTHESIS; DEFINES MINIMUM OBJECTIVE AND METHOD
ENVELOPES, A BLACK-BOX LEGIBILITY TEST, MULTIDIMENSIONAL FITNESS, AUTHORITY
SEPARATION, REVIEW-TRIGGER INTERFACE, FOURTEEN COUNTEREXAMPLES AND THIRTEEN
ACCEPTANCE TESTS; AND PRESERVES TEN OPEN QUESTIONS. NO GMC, POLICY, METHOD, STAGE OR
IMPLEMENTATION IS ACCEPTED OR OPENED.**
