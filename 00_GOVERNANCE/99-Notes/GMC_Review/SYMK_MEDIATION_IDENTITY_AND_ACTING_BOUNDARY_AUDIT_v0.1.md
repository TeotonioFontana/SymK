and stated that SymK's main role is to mediate among different Intelligences, so its

## 3. Findings from accepted authority

### 3.1 Mediation and cooperation are central, but not a final objective

The Product Vision states that Knowledge is the governed medium and Cooperation is
the process. It places heterogeneous Human, artificial, collective, institutional
and hybrid Intelligences inside shared inquiries, decisions and systems whose
Representations require governance.

DR-013 accepts Cooperation as a thin, actual, scoped relational process. It requires
distinguishable Participants, material Contributions, mutual orientation and a
bounded shared undertaking while keeping occurrence separate from legitimacy,
responsibility, Value and outcome.

The project steward's mediation formulation is therefore compatible with accepted
direction when mediation denotes governed support for the relations, questions,
representations and cooperative conditions of an undertaking. It must not make
Cooperation itself the final objective.

### 3.2 The governed undertaking, not a project pipeline, is the center

DR-017 establishes the governed knowledge undertaking as SymK's accepted
center-of-gravity rule. One or more Intelligence Bearers, supporting participants,
Representations, institutions and engineered systems contribute within declared
relations, scopes, roles, authorities, responsibilities, Evidence, consequences and
challenge conditions.

DR-018 preserves that undertaking as a non-reifying analytical organizing center.
It is not a new Foundational concept, System class, actor, workflow, project, legal
object or sovereign authority.

An acting roadmap may therefore organize application around the undertaking, but it
must not treat SymK itself as an autonomous person, Intelligence Bearer or decision
authority. Every actual mediator, actor, decision-maker and authority remains
identified separately.

### 3.3 A call, request or event does not establish the object to be mediated

DR-016 distinguishes Domain situation, Domain problem, problem claim, Question,
prompt, request, task, workflow, decision problem and record. A request or prompt
may represent or operationalize a Question but does not automatically establish its
Identity, scope, completeness or meaning.

The same anti-substitution rule applies to a mediation call: the event or caller's
wording is Evidence about a possible undertaking, not automatic authority over its
frame. “Fix the properties” cannot manufacture a property problem; “resolve the
disagreement” cannot manufacture a genuine conflict or require consensus.

### 3.4 The accepted lifecycle is branching and recursive

DR-018 rejects a deterministic Product Vision workflow. Domain situations may be
framed as Questions; inquiry and Reasoning may contribute to Answers; decisions and
actions require separate authority; consequences may return to any earlier object.
Every transition remains typed, scoped, temporal, independently evidenced and
challengeable.

This supports an event-to-intake-to-route roadmap with pause, refusal, rerouting and
reopening. It does not support a universal objective-to-trial pipeline.

### 3.5 Plural Intelligence is central but not an admission shortcut

The historical and general SymK direction concerns governed cooperation among
heterogeneous forms and Bearers of Intelligence. DR-017 nevertheless permits one or
more Intelligence Bearers in a governed undertaking. Mediation may involve several
current participants, representations across time, absent affected parties,
institutional positions or a single present participant confronting incompatible
sources.

The roadmap must not manufacture Cooperation merely from participant count, nor
exclude legitimate SymK inquiry merely because only one Intelligence Bearer is
currently active.

## 4. Exact acting-boundary conclusion

Accepted SymK authority supports the following governing orientation:

> When an event presents a candidate use of SymK, the first inquiry is: **What is
> this application being called to mediate?** The answer must identify the possible
> governed undertaking, relevant relations or objects, participants and affected
> parties, Representations, Context, Scope, standing, Authority, requested role and
> uncertainty before a problem, objective, method or action is presumed.

This is an operational derivation from the accepted identity, not a replacement
identity. The phrase “SymK acts” is shorthand for governed application by identified
participants or systems; it does not attribute autonomous Agency, Intelligence,
Authority or Responsibility to SymK.

## 5. Gap identified

The accepted corpus contains the necessary identity, concepts and anti-substitution
boundaries but no concise project-process profile for:

- recognizing an event without treating it as authority;
- constituting a candidate mediation engagement;
- identifying the actual mediator and affected boundary;
- selecting and composing mediation routes;
- issuing proportionate action authority separately from route selection;
- concluding, pausing, refusing, redirecting or rerouting; and
- returning observations into SymK learning without automatic universalization.

That is the legitimate scope of a proposed SymK Acting Roadmap.

## 6. Required roadmap constraints

Any Acting Roadmap proposal must:

1. begin with event recognition and the mediation-object question;
2. distinguish event, call, engagement, route, action, output, decision and effect;
3. prevent the caller's framing from owning the mediation object;
4. identify actual participants, affected parties, mediator, authority and
   responsibility rather than personifying SymK;
5. permit clarification, representation reconciliation, possible-problem inquiry,
   cooperation coordination, decision support, disagreement preservation, refusal,
   deferral and redirection;
6. permit route composition and rerouting;
7. preserve the possibility that no mediation is warranted;
8. preserve Domain and external authority;
9. remain feasible with current technology and context-portability constraints;
10. treat problem-investment and Package A as downstream specialized routes; and
11. create no action or execution authority merely through event detection or route
    selection.

## 7. Disposition

**Audit result:** Existing accepted identity is sufficient to prepare a provisional
Acting Roadmap proposal. No identity reopening is required at this point.

The roadmap itself remains unaccepted until separately reviewed. Operational
evidence may later show that accepted identity wording or concepts require
reopening, but this audit does not establish that result.
