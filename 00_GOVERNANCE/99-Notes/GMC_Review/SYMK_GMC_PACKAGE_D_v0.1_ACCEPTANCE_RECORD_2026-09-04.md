## 2. Acceptance basis

The accepted object:

1. keeps outcome/learning progress independently inspectable from control/readiness
   progress without one universal score;
2. preserves plural indicators, Evidence, uncertainty, disagreement, affected
   distribution, lags and non-compensable conditions;
3. separates observation, indicator, divergence signal, review trigger, assessment,
   lifecycle decision, Authorization, action and effect;
4. treats material divergence as a competent-review signal rather than causal proof
   or an automatic pivot;
5. defines pause as a bounded, attributable lifecycle state with preservation,
   expiry, resumption and continuing-duty controls;
6. prevents passage of time, renewed activity or preserved files from manufacturing
   valid resumption;
7. routes provenance-bearing derived-project evidence into upstream proposal and
   challenge without transferring local authority or silently amending SymK;
8. composes with accepted Packages A–C without selecting an objective or method,
   assigning a role, accepting risk or issuing a Trial or Delivery Release; and
9. passes initial SPServices, multi-Domain, false-divergence, pause/resumption,
   metric-gaming, cross-project and upstream-learning tests.
