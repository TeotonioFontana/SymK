## 3. Accepted governing effect

The suite is accepted provisionally as the minimum Human-readable operational
Representation for the first three Acting Roadmap states:

```text
event record
  -> candidate call
  -> mediation constitution and engagement-admission decision
```

Acceptance establishes that:

1. the event record represents what happened or was requested and admits no
   engagement;
2. the candidate-call record asks what SymK is being called to mediate, challenges
   caller framing and decides only whether constitution should be prepared;
3. the mediation constitution may record an engagement-admission disposition but
   selects no route and authorizes no consequential action;
4. `unknown` is preferable to invented completion;
5. existing trustworthy artifacts may be cited rather than copied;
6. low-consequence work under a current standing process may stop with a brief event
   record;
7. `not material` requires a short reason when omission could affect the decision;
8. actual mediator, affected parties, standing, Authority, Responsibility,
   feasibility and context portability remain explicit; and
9. every record defaults authority created to `none`.

The exact suite proposal and templates govern over this summary.

## 4. Relationship to Acting Roadmap v0.1

The suite operationalizes only event recognition, candidate-call assessment and
mediation constitution under the accepted Acting Roadmap. It does not amend that
roadmap or the accepted Product Vision, DR-013, DR-016, DR-017 or DR-018.

Route selection, route composition, action release, observation and final
disposition remain later steps without an accepted template in this suite.

Packages A-D and Problem-Investment v0.1 remain downstream cooperating profiles.
The challenged problem-first proposal remains unaccepted.

## 5. Proportionality and non-bureaucracy rule

Acceptance does not require three documents for every interaction. The suite is the
minimum separation model for material new or materially reopened SymK applications,
not a universal paperwork quota.

A project may represent the same required distinctions in concise prose or an
equivalent controlled artifact when identity, state, authority and lineage remain
inspectable. Template fields do not manufacture adequacy by being filled.
