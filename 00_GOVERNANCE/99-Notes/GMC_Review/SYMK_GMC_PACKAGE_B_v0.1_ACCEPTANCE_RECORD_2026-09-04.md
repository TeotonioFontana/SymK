The accepted object:

1. makes GMC-003 the boundary preventing semantic discovery from silently becoming

2. treats GMC-004 as an optional, architecture-neutral cooperation pattern rather
   than a mandatory four-part workflow;

   uncertainty, dissent, authority and declared loss;
6. composes with accepted Package A without changing Package A or issuing a Trial or
   Delivery Release; and
7. passes initial overlap, handoff-loss, cross-Domain and SPServices
   medical-property stress tests.
