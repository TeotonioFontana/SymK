## 3. Current composition rule

Acting Roadmap v0.1 governs the root project-process entry for material new or
materially reopened SymK applications:

```text
event or request
  -> candidate call
  -> mediation constitution and admission
  -> explicit route selection or composition
  -> separately authorized consequential action, when applicable
  -> observation, disposition and recursive return
```

Packages A-D and Problem-Investment v0.1 remain cooperating downstream profiles:

- Package B may support role, capability and handoff design for the admitted route;
- Package C scales assurance and governance burden;
- Package D governs observation, divergence, pause, pivot and upstream learning;
- Problem-Investment applies when the selected route concerns a possible Problem
  and material investment; and
- Package A applies when an admitted route legitimately reaches objective, method,
  trial, selection or delivery questions.

No downstream profile becomes universally applicable or inherits action Authority
from roadmap acceptance.

## 5. Proposal-time and decision-time wording

Accepted objects may retain “Proposal,” “candidate,” “not accepted” or review-
checkpoint language because their exact files are immutable evidence of what was
accepted. Separate acceptance records supply current authority without changing
accepted-object hashes.

Decision-time non-transfer clauses remain true for their original decisions. A
later separate acceptance changes current status without making the earlier
decision retroactively authorize the later object.

## 6. Reading and citation rule

substantive rule, cite the immutable accepted object. For historical reasoning,
cite the applicable audit, proposal, challenge, evaluation or test artifact.

acceptance decision. This note cannot perform that correction.
