## 1. Decision

The project steward accepts the exact Package A v0.3 object identified above as a
provisional SymK project-method governance profile.

GMC-001 and GMC-002 are accepted only through the exact objective, method-fitness,
comparative-validation, execution-release, lifecycle and prohibited-inference
formulations of that profile. They do not acquire broader wording, universal Domain
validity or Foundational status through this decision.

## 2. Acceptance basis

The accepted object incorporates two successive corrections exposed by the
SPServices case:

1. v0.1 could evaluate objectives and methods but did not require a distinct
   Execution Release;
2. v0.2 corrected uncontrolled start but did not make pre-selection empirical
   comparison sufficiently explicit; and
3. v0.3 preserves execution control while adding credible candidates, bounded
   comparative trials, explicit exceptions, distinct Trial and Delivery Releases,
   and continuing-fitness, stop-loss and pivot controls.

The acceptance recognizes that correct execution does not establish method fitness,
and that technical maturity, repeatability, sunk cost or artifact volume cannot
substitute for outcome-relative comparative evidence.

## 3. Positive effects

This decision:

1. changes Package A v0.3 from corrected proposal to accepted provisional
   project-method governance profile;
2. accepts GMC-001 and GMC-002 only as bounded members of that exact profile;
3. makes the profile prospectively applicable to material new or materially revised
   SymK project objectives, method choices, trials and delivery releases within
   project-steward jurisdiction;
4. authorizes later operational-template work only when covered by a separately
   applicable Execution Release;
5. makes later SymK 2.4 classification eligible when that stage is competently open;
   and
6. preserves Sections 24, 27 and 28 as continuing tests, dissent/open questions and
   reopening conditions.

## 4. Prohibited inferences — what this acceptance does not mean

1. Acceptance of Package A does not select, trial, reject or implement any real
   project method.
2. This record is not a Trial Release, Delivery Release or authorization for
   operational-template construction.
3. Acceptance of GMC-001 and GMC-002 through Package A does not accept GMC-003–014,
   Packages B–D or the complete GMC set.
4. Provisional project-process authority does not establish constitutional,
   Foundational, universal Domain or external legal authority.
5. Eligibility for later 2.4 classification does not open 2.4 or predetermine its
   classification result.
6. A required comparison does not presume that AI–Human, deterministic, Human-led,
   standards-led or hybrid methods will win in every Context.
7. Adoption of governance wording does not establish implementation, enforcement,
   compliance, substantive review or successful outcome.
8. This acceptance does not retroactively invalidate earlier work or establish that
   every earlier project decision violated the accepted profile.
9. Package A acceptance does not establish that Package A is complete, permanent or
   the only improvement SymK requires.
