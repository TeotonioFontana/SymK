## 1. Test purpose

Test whether the proposed profile blocks solution-first inversion while still
admitting legitimate engineering, research, mandated, emergency and internal
problems without disproportionate ceremony.

The tests assess the profile's rules and decision distinctions. They do not prove
that any real problem exists, validate a universal ontology or authorize a project.

## 2. Expected invariants

The profile should preserve these results across cases:

1. an observation is not automatically an accepted problem;
2. a requested or available solution is not independent problem Evidence;
3. PA precedes the profile's OA gate for material work;
4. the OA gate describes a desired external difference, not a deliverable;
5. complete solution configurations follow the OA gate;
6. inaction and the simplest credible alternative remain visible;
7. `no_material_problem` is a valid result;
8. mandates, emergencies and research receive bounded treatment rather than false
   customer-market framing;
9. existing accepted problem classes can support routine work; and
10. acceptance of any record creates no execution authority by itself.

## 3. Case results

### Case 1 - Existing technology searching for a use

**Input:** A team has built an AI classifier and states the problem as “organizations
lack an AI classifier for their documents.” No attributable user difficulty,
obligation or decision consequence is supplied.

**Expected:** PA does not pass. The black-box solution test removes “AI classifier”
and leaves no independently intelligible affected condition. Disposition is
`correct`, `defer` or `no_material_problem`.

**Observed profile result:** PASS. Sections 5-9 block the profile's OA gate and
require independent condition and consequence Evidence.

### Case 2 - Customer requests a preferred feature

**Input:** A customer asks for automatic document tags. The request is attributable,
but no task, frequency, consequence or current workaround is known.

**Expected:** The request is admissible PD Evidence of a preference, not PA proof.
Neutral inquiry asks what decision or task the tags would improve and permits the
customer to identify a different problem or no material problem.

**Observed profile result:** PASS. Sections 6.3 and 14 distinguish requester,
affected participant and solution advocate functions.

### Case 3 - Technical gap without established affected consequence

**Input:** A schema lacks field `X`; tests show the field is absent everywhere. No
accepted requirement or user consequence refers to `X`.

**Expected:** “Field X is absent” remains an observation. It is not a material
project problem merely because it is measurable. PA requires a trace to an affected
outcome, obligation or knowledge question.

**Observed profile result:** PASS. Sections 7 and 8 reject a missing feature as
self-justifying.

### Case 4 - Defect under a current standing objective

**Input:** A production service violates an already accepted availability requirement
because a known retry function fails. The defect is within current maintenance
authority.

**Expected:** No new customer interview or full PD/PA cycle is required. The existing
problem class, objective and standing release may control. Material scope expansion
or repeated failure reopens PA.

**Observed profile result:** PASS. Section 15.4 prevents the new gate from becoming
universal micro-ceremony.

### Case 5 - Regulatory or contractual mandate

**Input:** Competent external authority requires a specified retention control by a
fixed date. Users do not report a current inconvenience.

**Expected:** PA may be grounded in the obligation and consequences of noncompliance.
The project does not fabricate customer pain. Mandatory and discretionary elements
remain distinct, and any method choice still requires appropriate reasoning.

**Observed profile result:** PASS. Section 15.1 admits the obligation while keeping
the mandate separate from user-problem and causal claims.

### Case 6 - Emergency containment

**Input:** Evidence suggests active credential exposure. Waiting for a complete
problem record could increase harm.

**Expected:** Narrow competent containment may proceed under emergency authority,
with expiry and a later completion obligation. Urgency does not create unlimited
delivery authority.

**Observed profile result:** PASS. Section 15.2 preserves containment without making
PA a precondition to immediate safety action.

### Case 7 - Legitimate research problem

**Input:** Reproducible observations conflict with an accepted model. No commercial
customer or operational defect exists.

**Expected:** The knowledge gap can support PA when the question, Evidence state,
epistemic consequence, boundary and stopping conditions are explicit. Market demand
is unnecessary.

**Observed profile result:** PASS. Section 15.3 avoids reducing all problems to user
pain or financial Value.

### Case 8 - Internal capability constraint

**Input:** An engineering team cannot reproduce releases reliably, causing delayed
corrections and uncertain provenance for downstream maintainers.

**Expected:** The affected boundary may be internal and downstream. PA can pass if
the condition and consequences are evidenced. “Buy tool Y” cannot be embedded as the
problem; process, reuse, training, tool and no-change configurations may later
compete.

**Observed profile result:** PASS. Sections 6, 7 and 11 admit internal problems while
preserving solution alternatives.

### Case 9 - SPServices medical-document properties

**Input:** SPServices has provisional medical properties, profiles and deterministic
controls. Its accepted `O1` evidence record states that production requirements,
named users, decision frequency and quantified benefit remain unknown. The proposed
project asks how to discover and evaluate useful properties.

**Expected:** Existing property gaps remain technical observations. The current
problem claim does not pass PA because properties are still the assumed intervention
and affected-user need is insufficiently evidenced. The sequence returns to bounded
PD without designing a new property trial. `No_material_problem` remains possible.

**Observed profile result:** PASS. Sections 5-10 block the property-shaped objective
at the profile's OA gate, and Sections 12-13 prevent reliance on the accepted
SPServices `T1` record while PA is absent or reopened.

### Case 10 - Genuine system-specific problem

**Input:** Authorized operators must submit a statutory report from System S. Audit
evidence shows System S omits a mandated timestamp, causing repeatable rejection.

**Expected:** The problem may legitimately name System S and the timestamp because
the external requirement and consequence are independently evidenced. The solution-
contamination test must not reject every artifact-specific statement.

**Observed profile result:** PASS. Section 7 distinguishes naming the problem-bearing
artifact from treating a preferred intervention as the deficiency.

### Case 11 - Solution-derived Evidence

**Input:** A prototype exposes errors that were not visible before it existed. The
team claims the prototype proves demand for itself.

**Expected:** The errors may be real Evidence, but their provenance is marked as
solution-derived. They do not independently prove demand or that the prototype is
the preferred solution. PA and SA remain open to other interpretations.

**Observed profile result:** PASS. Section 8.2 admits the Evidence without treating
it as independent confirmation.

### Case 12 - No material problem

**Input:** PD finds that the affected users complete the task reliably with a low-
cost existing process, consequences are negligible and proposed automation would
add maintenance burden.

**Expected:** `No_material_problem` is an acceptable PA disposition. The project may
monitor rather than invent a new objective.

**Observed profile result:** PASS. Section 9.3 treats avoided unjustified investment
as a successful governance result.

## 4. False-positive review

The following valid cases were checked against over-rejection:

| Valid case | Why it remains admissible |
| --- | --- |
| exact production defect | may rely on a current standing problem/objective/release |
| artifact-specific compliance gap | external mandate independently establishes materiality |
| research uncertainty | epistemic consequence can be the problem basis |
| internal engineering capability | internal and downstream affected boundaries are legitimate |
| emergency | bounded containment exception avoids harmful delay |

Result: no tested valid case is forced into market research or rejected solely
because it names a system, technical condition or internal beneficiary.

## 5. False-negative review

The following solution-first patterns were checked against accidental admission:

| Invalid or incomplete pattern | Profile response |
| --- | --- |
| “customers need our product” with no independent Evidence | fail/correct PA |
| missing feature treated as harm | require trace to consequence or obligation |
| customer-requested feature treated as method selection | retain as preference Evidence; admit alternatives later |
| alternative list made only of tools inside one architecture | fail SA complete-configuration rule |
| implementation progress used as problem proof | mark solution-derived and non-independent |
| objective written as delivery of the solution | fail PA-OA separation and black-box tests |

Result: each tested inversion is exposed before Method Selection or Delivery Release.

## 6. Proportionality review

The profile does not prescribe a universal document count, interview count,
duration, statistical threshold or participant roster. It permits concise existing
Evidence for routine and low-consequence work and scales inquiry through Package C.

Remaining risk: teams may turn the fourteen-field PD list into a mandatory large
template. Review and any later operational template should therefore label the list
as a coverage guide and require only fields material to the decision, with omitted
items identified when their absence could change the result.

## 7. Traceability review

The invariant was applied to the SPServices sequence. `O1` and `T1` below retain
their exact SPServices identities; they are not the proposal-local OA and TR labels:

```text
PD: absent as an independent affected-user evidence object
PA: absent
O1: accepted but solution-contaminated by property framing
SA: method families listed, complete solution configurations absent
T1: accepted configuration prepared, activation absent
```

Under the proposal, new reliance stops at missing PA. Historical acceptance and
integrity remain preserved. This is the intended result.

## 8. Stress-test conclusion

The v0.1 correction proposal passes the initial twelve-case conceptual test suite.
It detects the motivating SPServices failure, admits legitimate non-market problems,
preserves emergency and standing-authority paths and permits a no-material-problem
result.

The evidence is insufficient to establish universal validity. Material open risks
are proportionality in operational templates, authority design across Domains and
longitudinal cross-project performance.
