## 1. Profile result

Package B v0.1 converts GMC-003–008 into an architecture-neutral provisional
governance profile. Its core boundary is:

> **A function that discovers or proposes meaning does not thereby validate,
> adjudicate, authorize or execute that meaning. A participant may perform several
> functions, but the functions, outputs, evidence, decisions, authorities and
> effects must remain distinguishable.**

The profile treats standards, AI, Humans and deterministic systems as possible
participants or resources, not universally required roles. It governs what
functions occur, how their claims travel and which invalid status or authority
transfers must be prevented.

## 2. Acceptance decision and effect

The project steward accepts this exact v0.1 as a provisional SymK
epistemic-role-cooperation profile. The accepted profile:

- separates semantic discovery from grounding, validation, adjudication,
  authorization, execution control and observation;
- distinguishes a participant's identity from the functions it performs;
- permits one participant to perform several functions when overlap is explicit,
  supportable and proportionately controlled;
- permits several participants to cooperate in one function without inventing
  collective authority or competence;
- preserves provenance, status, uncertainty, dissent and declared loss at every
  material handoff;
- treats standards as candidate grounds rather than automatic local truth;
- treats AI outputs as provenance-bearing candidates rather than Knowledge or
  decisions;
- requires Human adjudication to be competent and substantive rather than
  ceremonial;
- treats deterministic verification as evidence of declared repeatability or
  conformance rather than semantic adequacy; and
- composes with accepted Package A without selecting a method, assigning a real
  role or issuing execution authority.

Acceptance incorporates GMC-003–008 only through this profile's exact bounded
formulations, controls and non-entailments. It makes later operational-template
work eligible only under a separate Execution Release and makes later 2.4C
classification or 2.6 specialization eligible when those stages are competent. It
does not open either stage, prescribe one universal workflow or accept Packages
C–D or GMC-009–014.

## 3. Problem addressed

A project may select a comparatively fit method under Package A and still fail
because it conflates functions inside that method. Typical failures include:

- a retrieval or standard source is treated as the accepted local meaning;
- a generative output is treated as an evidenced conclusion;
- a consistency check is treated as semantic validation;
- a designated Human approval is treated as proof of competent review;
- a technical control owner becomes the de facto semantic authority;
- a prototype's implemented schema determines the Domain vocabulary;
- uncertainty or dissent disappears during a handoff;
- the same participant proposes, reviews and approves an object without exposing
  the overlap; or
- separation is implemented ceremonially through labels while the same hidden
  dependency controls every result.

Conversely, requiring different people or systems for every function can create
cost, delay, handoff loss and false independence. Package B therefore governs
functional distinguishability and material conflict, not maximal organizational
separation.

## 4. Compatibility boundary

This profile operationalizes without reopening accepted distinctions among:

- output, claim, Ground, Evidence, assessment, Answer and Knowledge;
- Representation, represented subject and Projection;
- recommendation, proposal, instruction, decision and Authorization;
- actor, Participant, Contribution, role, control and Authority;
- process-Evidence, conclusion-Evidence and actual outcome;
- semantic adequacy, formal correctness, repeatability and implementation fact;
- Human, Scientific and Engineering Views; and
- universal SymK semantics, project method and Domain allocation.

It proposes no new Foundational concept and does not define one universal epistemic
workflow. Its functional and handoff structures are project-governance profiles
subject to later classification.

## 5. Working terms

| Term | Working meaning in v0.1 | Must remain distinct from |
| --- | --- | --- |
| epistemic function | bounded kind of contribution made within inquiry, assessment or governed use | permanent participant identity, office, authority or software component |
| semantic discovery | generation, recognition, elicitation or interpretation of candidate meaning, distinctions, relations or questions | truth, Knowledge, validation, acceptance or execution |
| candidate meaning | proposed Domain interpretation, property, distinction, mapping or relation with declared source and status | accepted local semantics or universal concept |
| grounding | identification and assessment of possible Grounds, sources, observations, standards or testimony relevant to a claim | automatic warrant, acceptance or authority |
| validation | testing a declared claim, representation, method or result against stated questions, evidence and conditions | universal proof, adjudication or authorization |
| adjudication | competent assessment that accepts, rejects, qualifies or returns an object for a declared purpose and Scope | truth manufacture, universal semantic authority or execution |
| authorization | attributable permission for bounded action or effect | semantic adequacy, execution fact or success |
| execution control | constraint, monitoring or verification of permitted operations and effects | discovery, adjudication or complete assurance |
| observation and feedback | recording results, consequences, anomalies or challenges for later assessment | automatic Evidence, Learning, status change or pivot |
| participant | Human, artificial, collective, institutional or other subject/configuration contributing in a declared Context | role, competence, authority or successful contribution |
| role assignment | contextual allocation of one or more functions, duties, permissions or expectations | intrinsic participant property or universal architecture |
| functional overlap | one participant or tightly coupled configuration performs more than one function | automatic defect, independence or authority aggregation |
| handoff | transfer or presentation of an object from one function, participant, stage or system boundary to another | preservation of meaning, status or authority by default |
| declared loss | known omission, transformation, uncertainty or limitation introduced by representation or handoff | proof that all other meaning was preserved |

These are profile-local working terms and do not amend accepted SymK vocabulary.

## 6. GMC-003 core rule — functional non-substitution

Every material epistemic arrangement must keep the following questions separately
answerable:

1. Who or what generated, elicited or interpreted the candidate?
2. What exact candidate, claim or Representation was produced?
3. Which Grounds and Evidence support or challenge it?
4. Which validation questions and conditions were actually tested?
5. Who or what assessed or adjudicated it, with what competence and authority?
6. Which decision or Authorization occurred?
7. Which action or effect was executed?
8. Which observations or consequences followed?

The answers may name the same participant. Their identity does not collapse the
functions or allow one result to substitute for another.

The minimum prohibited transition is:

> **Discovery output does not by itself establish evidence, validation,
> adjudication, authorization, execution or successful outcome.**

The converse also holds where material: a control, approval, conformance result or
execution record does not by itself establish that adequate semantic discovery
occurred.

## 7. Function topology

Package B recognizes the following reusable functions without requiring all of them
in every case or fixing their order:

1. **question and candidate formation** — frame questions and generate candidate
   distinctions, meanings, properties, relations or alternatives;
2. **source and Ground acquisition** — retrieve standards, records, observations,
   testimony, prior decisions and other possible Grounds;
3. **interpretation and contextualization** — relate sources and candidates to the
   declared Domain, Context, Scope and affected subjects;
4. **critique and validation** — test coherence, applicability, counterexamples,
   uncertainty, consequences and formal conditions;
5. **adjudication** — accept, reject, qualify, defer or return an object for a
   declared reliance purpose;
6. **decision and authorization** — issue a governed decision or bounded permission
   under competent authority;
7. **execution and effect control** — implement, constrain, monitor and verify
   permitted actions and effects; and
8. **observation, challenge and feedback** — expose results, anomalies, dissent,
   consequences and new questions to competent review.

This is a topology, not a mandatory pipeline. Functions may iterate, branch, occur
in parallel, be unnecessary in a low-consequence case or return earlier work for
revision. Any omitted material function requires an explicit reason or applicable
standing profile.

## 8. Participant–function separation

A role record identifies:

1. participant or configured-system Identity and version where material;
2. assigned function or functions;
3. object and decision boundary;
4. applicable Domain, Context, Scope and time;
5. claimed competence or capability and its evidence;
6. authority source and limits, if any;
7. permitted tools, sources and data;
8. required independence or allowed overlap;
9. expected outputs and their initial status;
10. handoff recipients and obligations;
11. conflict, dissent, challenge and escalation routes; and
12. expiry, reassignment and review conditions.

A role label does not establish that the function was performed. Performance
evidence remains separate from assignment. A participant type does not establish
competence for every object, and competence does not establish authority.

## 9. Functional-overlap rule

Functional overlap is permitted when proportionate to consequence and explicitly
governed. Before relying on a materially overlapping arrangement, record:

- which functions overlap and why;
- whether the same output is being self-reviewed;
- participant capability and conflict-of-interest conditions;
- what independent Evidence, counterexample or challenge remains available;
- whether another participant can inspect the intermediate objects;
- how dissent or uncertainty survives the overlap;
- which decisions require separate authority despite the overlap; and
- which event requires independent review, reassignment or safe stop.

Overlap is not automatically unsafe. Separation is not automatically independent.
Two nominal reviewers using the same hidden source, model, prompt, assumption or
organizational incentive may be less independent than one transparent participant
with strong counterevidence and challenge.

Exact separation should be required only when law, policy, professional duty,
material conflict, consequence or the accepted assurance profile demands it.

## 10. Material handoff contract

A material handoff preserves proportionately:

1. object Identity, version and Representation;
2. source participant or configured system;
3. function performed and function explicitly not performed;
4. input sources, Grounds and material transformations;
5. claim, candidate or result status;
6. Domain, Context, Scope, affected horizon and intended use;
7. assumptions, uncertainty and unresolved questions;
8. supporting and adverse Evidence;
9. alternatives, counterexamples and dissent;
10. known omissions, compression, translation and declared loss;
11. applicable authority and explicit authority limitations;
12. permitted reliance and prohibited reliance;
13. validation already performed and validation still required;
14. recipient obligations, expiry and escalation conditions; and
15. provenance needed to reproduce or challenge the contribution where feasible.

The burden is consequence-proportional. A low-risk handoff may be compact; a
high-consequence semantic or execution boundary may require a structured record.
More fields do not by themselves establish better preservation.

## 11. GMC-004 — optional role-specialized cooperation

Package B permits an optional cooperation pattern in which:

| Possible participant/resource | Typical contribution | Must not be inferred automatically |
| --- | --- | --- |
| standards and governed sources | candidate vocabulary, constraints, definitions, mappings and comparison grounds | local applicability, completeness, truth or authority |
| AI or generative systems | candidate generation, clustering, comparison, transformation, critique and question discovery | Knowledge, correctness, accepted meaning or decision |
| competent Humans or Domain bodies | contextual interpretation, affected-subject evidence, adjudication, decision or challenge | infallibility, complete review, independence or universal authority |
| deterministic systems | retrieval, parsing, normalization, reproducibility, constraint checks, enforcement and effect verification | semantic adequacy, best-method status or responsible outcome |
| hybrid or collective configurations | coordinated contribution across several functions | collective Knowledge, unified competence, authority or Responsibility |

The table is not a required architecture. A project may use none, one or several of
these participant families. Functions may be allocated differently when the
accepted objective, selected method, Domain conditions and assurance profile support
the arrangement.

## 12. GMC-005 — standards as candidate grounds

A standard, vocabulary, regulation, ontology or prior governed source may contribute
candidate Grounds efficiently. Before relying on it for local semantics, preserve:

- exact source, edition, authority and jurisdiction;
- applicable Domain, Context, Scope and time;
- the local question or object it is claimed to address;
- evidence of applicability and material mismatch;
- conflicts among sources or standards;
- obsolete, absent or intentionally underspecified areas;
- local extension, specialization or exception;
- affected-subject and external-authority constraints; and
- the competent decision that accepts, qualifies or rejects local reliance.

Publication, market adoption, formal status or inclusion in a source list does not
by itself establish local truth, completeness or authority.

## 13. GMC-006 — AI output as provenance-bearing candidate

An AI-generated output enters the arrangement as a candidate contribution. A
material record preserves proportionately:

- producing model or configured-system identity where available;
- relevant prompt, retrieval, tools, source set and material configuration;
- Human or system transformations before and after generation;
- output Identity and version;
- intended function and declared status;
- cited or inferred Grounds and their availability;
- uncertainty, alternatives, known limitations and counterevidence;
- applicable data, privacy, confidentiality and use constraints; and
- required Human, scientific, formal or Domain review before reliance.

Unavailable internal traces do not prohibit every use, but the missing provenance
and resulting reliance limit must be explicit. Fluency, confidence, model scale,
agreement with the requester or Human editing does not establish truth, Knowledge,
authorship, competent adjudication or decision authority.

## 14. GMC-007 — competent, non-ceremonial Human adjudication

When Human adjudication is material, the record must support rather than merely name:

1. competence relevant to the exact object and Domain;
2. sufficient access to the candidate, Grounds, Evidence and material alternatives;
3. adequate time and practical conditions for review;
4. independence, conflicts and organizational pressures;
5. understanding of the decision and reliance consequences;
6. ability to question, qualify, dissent, defer or reject;
7. feasible escalation or refusal where required;
8. attribution of the actual judgment rather than only the click or signature; and
9. preserved uncertainty, dissent and affected-subject challenge.

Human review may be unnecessary for some low-consequence or fully bounded formal
checks. When claimed as semantic or Domain assurance, however, mere presence,
assignment, interaction, signature or approval does not establish competence,
substantive review, control, authorship or correctness.

## 15. GMC-008 — determinism as repeatability, not semantic adequacy

A deterministic system can establish that a declared operation produces the same
result under the same represented inputs, configuration and environment, subject to
the limits of the evidence. It may also test syntax, constraints, invariants,
coverage, transformations, access rules or execution effects.

Its record must identify:

- exact implementation, version, configuration and environment;
- represented inputs and excluded conditions;
- declared semantics and assumptions used by the check;
- property actually tested;
- expected and observed result;
- reproducibility and variance boundary;
- false-positive, false-negative and incompleteness risks;
- semantic questions the check cannot decide; and
- the authority and reliance decision, if any, that uses the result.

Deterministic reproducibility does not establish that inputs are meaningful,
complete, representative or correctly interpreted. Formal conformance is local to
the declared rule and semantics. A repeatable error remains repeatable.

## 16. Composition with accepted Package A

Package A governs the objective, comparative method choice and bounded authority to
trial or deliver. Package B governs the epistemic functions and handoffs inside the
candidate or selected method.

The composition requires:

1. the accepted objective and comparison questions constrain Package B's function
   design;
2. a Trial Release identifies which participants may perform which trial functions,
   using which sources, data and tools;
3. role arrangement is part of the method configuration being compared when it can
   materially change performance;
4. Method Selection preserves the role/function configuration actually supported by
   evidence rather than silently generalizing from another configuration;
5. a Delivery Release identifies permitted execution and control roles without
   conferring semantic authority on them;
6. material participant, model, source, adjudicator or control changes trigger
   Package A's change-control and continuing-fitness rules; and
7. Package B observations may challenge method fitness but do not automatically
   pivot, pause or revoke authority.

Package B must not become the objective. A sophisticated role architecture does not
establish that the external outcome is useful or that the selected method is fit.

## 17. Interfaces with Packages C and D

Package B supplies Package C with the actual function, participant, overlap,
handoff, data, authority and effect configuration for consequence-proportional
assurance. Package C may require stronger independence, monitoring or control, but
control requirements do not acquire semantic authority.

Package B supplies Package D with observable role and handoff facts, including:

- candidate volume and disposition;
- provenance and status loss;
- review depth and disagreement;
- validation defects;
- unresolved uncertainty;
- execution deviations; and
- downstream consequences.

Package D may reveal divergence or failure and return Package A or B for
reassessment. Observation does not automatically establish the cause, responsible
participant, correct replacement or required lifecycle response.

Packages C and D remain unaccepted. These interfaces are profile boundaries, not
authority transfer.

## 18. Minimum role-cooperation decision record

A material Package B configuration records proportionately:

1. exact accepted objective, candidate/selected method and applicable release;
2. epistemic and execution functions required for the case;
3. functions omitted and reasons;
4. participants, configured systems and sources;
5. role assignments, competence claims and authority limits;
6. permitted and prohibited functional overlaps;
7. independence requirements and shared dependencies;
8. input/output objects, statuses and handoff contracts;
9. provenance, uncertainty, dissent and declared loss;
10. validation, adjudication and reliance conditions;
11. execution/control boundaries;
12. privacy, confidentiality, security and affected-subject conditions;
13. evidence, monitoring and consequence observations;
14. failure, escalation, reassignment and safe-stop conditions;
15. expiry, versioning and change-control rules; and
16. explicit prohibited inferences.

This record may be compact or incorporated into another governed artifact. Package B
does not require one document, schema, tool or meeting.

## 19. SPServices medical-property stress test

The business objective is to identify a useful, defensible and adaptable set of
properties for medical documents. Under accepted Package A, deterministic,
AI–Human and hybrid candidates require proportionate comparison before a primary
method is selected. Package B then asks whether the functions inside each candidate
are validly distinguished.

A bounded hybrid trial could allocate functions as follows:

| Function | Possible contribution in the trial | Required boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| question/candidate formation | AI and medical experts generate candidate document properties and missing questions | candidates are not accepted properties |
| source/Ground acquisition | standards, sample documents, existing vocabularies and professional practice provide possible Grounds | source inclusion is not local applicability |
| interpretation | medical experts and affected workflow representatives explain Domain meaning and use | expertise is not infallibility or sole authority |
| critique/validation | AI, Humans and deterministic checks test coverage, ambiguity, redundancy, conflicts and counterexamples | one validation family does not establish complete adequacy |
| adjudication | competent Domain review accepts, rejects, combines or defers properties for the trial purpose | adjudication is attributable and non-ceremonial |
| execution/control | deterministic tooling records provenance, normalizes forms, checks constraints and reproduces transformations | repeatability and schema validity are not semantic quality |
| observation/feedback | trial evidence records usefulness, discovery speed, correction burden, disagreement and total cost | observations do not automatically select the method |

The earlier strategic failure can be stated precisely: deterministic scanning was
allowed to combine candidate discovery, validation and de facto method authority.
Its engineering progress and control evidence became proxies for semantic adequacy.
Package B prevents that substitution even if one configured system still performs
several functions.

### 19.1 Required trial evidence

The medical-property test should preserve:

- exact representative document sample and material exclusions;
- candidate properties with source and contributor provenance;
- standard-derived, AI-derived, Human-elicited and algorithm-derived origins;
- duplicates, conflicts, rejected candidates and missing-property discoveries;
- medical meaning, workflow relevance and affected-user evidence;
- adjudicator competence, review depth, disagreement and decision;
- deterministic validation rules and their semantic limits;
- time, cost and correction burden by function;
- handoff losses and configuration dependencies; and
- results under plausible overlap and independence arrangements.

### 19.2 Stress-test outcomes

Package B fails the case if:

- a property becomes accepted merely because a scanner extracted it repeatedly;
- a standard term becomes locally authoritative without applicability assessment;
- an AI suggestion loses its candidate status during transcription;
- a Human approval is counted without evidence of substantive review;
- a schema or validation rule silently becomes the medical definition;
- rejected properties or dissent disappear from the handoff;
- trial outputs enter product delivery without a Delivery Release; or
- no one can reconstruct which function produced, tested, adjudicated and executed
  a material property decision.

## 20. Functional-overlap test suite

| Case | Required result |
| --- | --- |
| one medical expert proposes and adjudicates a property | disclose self-review; require proportionate counterevidence or challenge; do not reject automatically |
| one AI configuration generates and critiques candidates | distinguish generation and critique prompts, inputs and outputs; do not claim independence automatically |
| two reviewers rely on the same AI summary | expose shared dependency; reviewer count does not establish independent review |
| one deterministic pipeline extracts and validates syntax | permit overlap; keep extraction success distinct from semantic adequacy |
| a small low-risk project has one qualified Human | allow compact overlap controls when consequence and external authority permit |
| law requires independent approval | enforce actual separation and authority; convenience cannot waive it |
| the Domain body also funds the chosen method | disclose institutional conflict and preserve challenge |
| a hybrid team jointly produces one result | preserve individual/configured contributions; do not invent collective Knowledge or authority |
| a participant changes role during the lifecycle | version the assignment and reassess material conflicts or release boundaries |
| nominally separate tools share one hidden model or source | record common dependency; do not report false diversity |

## 21. Handoff-loss test suite

| Loss family | Failure example | Required control |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Identity/version | reviewer assesses a different candidate revision | exact object identity and version |
| status | AI candidate is relabeled “approved property” | source and destination status with transition authority |
| provenance | property list loses its producing source/model/expert | contributor and source lineage |
| Scope/Context | result from one specialty is generalized to all medicine | retained Domain, Context and applicability limits |
| uncertainty | probabilistic suggestion becomes categorical | uncertainty representation and transformation note |
| dissent | minority clinical concern is removed from summary | dissent identity, rationale and later disposition |
| counterevidence | failed examples are dropped from the accepted set | adverse Evidence and exclusion rationale |
| semantic detail | property description is compressed into an ambiguous label | declared loss and access to fuller Representation |
| authority | technical approver is presented as Domain authority | exact authority kind, source and limit |
| configuration | model/tool result is detached from prompt, data or version | material configured-system provenance |
| affected horizon | downstream burden on clinicians is omitted | affected subjects and consequence record |
| execution effect | accepted vocabulary differs from deployed schema | decision-to-implementation mapping and verification |

No handoff representation can guarantee lossless transfer. Package B requires
material loss to be visible, challengeable and proportionately recoverable.

## 22. Cross-Domain adequacy cases

As a continuing condition of fitness, Package B should remain usable in materially
different cases:

1. a single researcher interprets a small public dataset;
2. a regulated clinical terminology decision;
3. a software build in which formal checks dominate and semantic judgment is modest;
4. community knowledge where lived experience and contested standing are material;
5. an emergency decision with limited review time;
6. a non-AI, non-standard craft or field practice;
7. a multi-agent artificial workflow with no Human in the inner loop;
8. a Human-only expert panel;
9. a mandated method whose fitness cannot change the lawful choice; and
10. a derived project with conflicting parent standards or authorities.

The cases test architecture neutrality. They do not establish that one role
configuration is adequate across Domains.

## 23. Additional counterexamples

1. A valid standard definition conflicts with local law. Standard validity does not
   establish local permission or priority.
2. An AI proposes a clinically valuable property without a valid citation. Value,
   Ground, provenance and acceptance remain separately assessed.
3. A Human expert rejects an AI candidate incorrectly. Human authority does not make
   the rejection epistemically correct; challenge remains available.
4. A deterministic rule catches every malformed property but misses every important
   omitted property. Formal coverage does not establish semantic coverage.
5. A stochastic process is not bitwise repeatable but repeatedly exposes relevant
   overlooked concepts. Non-determinism does not establish uselessness.
6. Four participant types are present, but all merely follow one architect's hidden
   assumptions. Participant diversity does not establish epistemic diversity.
7. One participant performs all functions transparently in a low-risk task and
   produces a good result. Functional overlap does not establish governance failure.
8. Roles are separated, but the handoff removes uncertainty and dissent. Separation
   does not establish preservation.
9. Review is thorough, but the reviewer lacks the necessary specialty. Effort does
   not establish competence.
10. A property is accepted semantically but deployed under a different identifier
    and constraint. Acceptance does not establish faithful implementation.
11. A generated candidate is independently verified. Verification does not erase AI
    contribution or transfer authorship automatically.
12. An execution controller blocks unsafe publication. Operational authority does
    not establish that its preferred semantics are correct.
13. No applicable standard exists. Absence of a standard does not prohibit
    discovery, evidence or competent local decision.
14. A standard is fully applicable. GMC-005 does not require needless reinvention or
    perpetual skepticism.
15. A Human review is not required by a low-risk formal task. GMC-007 does not make
    Human approval universally necessary.
16. A deterministic result is semantically correct. GMC-008 does not make
    determinism evidence against adequacy.

## 26. Prohibited inferences — what v0.1 does not mean

1. Functional distinction does not require a different participant for every
   function.
2. One participant performing several functions does not establish invalidity,
   independence, competence or aggregated authority.
3. Several participants performing one function does not establish Cooperation,
   diversity, collective Knowledge or stronger evidence.
4. A role assignment does not establish performance, competence or authority.
5. Discovery does not establish Ground, Evidence, validation, adjudication,
   authorization, execution or outcome.
6. Ground or Evidence does not automatically establish truth, acceptance or
   authority.
7. Validation does not establish universal proof, correct adjudication or
   authorization.
8. Adjudication does not manufacture truth or establish execution.
9. Authorization does not establish semantic adequacy, action, compliance or
   success.
10. Execution control does not establish correct meaning or responsible outcome.
11. A standard does not establish local applicability, completeness or authority.
12. AI output does not establish Knowledge, truth, accepted meaning, authorship or
    decision.
13. Human review does not establish competence, substance, independence,
    correctness or complete control.
14. Deterministic repeatability does not establish semantic adequacy, completeness
    or best-method status.
15. Handoff documentation does not guarantee lossless communication.
16. Provenance does not establish truth or excuse harmful use.
17. An optional four-family pattern does not require standards, AI, Humans or
    deterministic systems in every case.
18. Package B acceptance does not accept a real role arrangement, method,
    Execution Release, Package C/D or GMC-009–014.
19. Later classification eligibility does not open 2.4C or 2.6.
20. Package B is not the only or necessarily highest-priority remaining SymK
    improvement.
