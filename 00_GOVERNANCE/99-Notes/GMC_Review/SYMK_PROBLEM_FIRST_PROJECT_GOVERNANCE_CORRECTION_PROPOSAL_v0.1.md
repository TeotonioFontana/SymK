# SymK Problem-First Project Governance Correction Proposal v0.1

**Status:** PROPOSAL PREPARED FOR PROJECT-STEWARD REVIEW ON 2026-09-07 - NOT
ACCEPTED; NO CONSTITUTIONAL, DOMAIN, DERIVED-PROJECT, INVESTMENT OR EXECUTION
AUTHORITY

**Evidence basis:**
`SYMK_SPSERVICES_SOLUTION_FIRST_DRIFT_UPSTREAM_EVIDENCE_BUNDLE_v0.1.md`

**Requested effect if accepted:** Establish a provisional SymK project-process
governance profile that must precede Objective Acceptance for material new or
materially reopened project investment. Require later consolidation into Package A
and the Problem-Investment Control Method without silently editing either accepted
object.

## 1. Purpose

Prevent a preferred technology, artifact, method, product or deliverable from
defining the problem used to justify it.

The profile adds the missing bridge from observation to an accepted problem claim:

```text
PD Problem Discovery
  -> PA Problem Acceptance
  -> OA Objective Acceptance
  -> SA Solution-Alternative Admission
  -> TR Trial Release, when comparison needs execution
  -> MS Method Selection
  -> DLR Delivery Release
```

These short labels are local gate labels for this proposed profile. They are not
SymK `DR-*` Decision Record identifiers and do not rename the SPServices `O1` or
`T1` records.

Every arrow is contingent. Evidence or acceptance at one state does not manufacture
its successor.

## 2. Problem addressed

Current SymK project-governance profiles can reject objectives stated as internal
deliverables and can require comparative method validation. They do not reliably
prevent this earlier inversion:

1. a project owns or prefers a solution;
2. the solution defines the vocabulary of the supposed problem;
3. the objective asks how to improve, validate or deploy that solution;
4. alternatives vary the method but retain the same assumed intervention class; and
5. governance controls make the sequence precise without establishing that an
   affected party has a material problem the solution can improve.

This produces technically sound solutions for weak, unverified or nonexistent
problems. It can also hide legitimate research, obligation or internal-capability
problems by forcing them into artificial customer-market language.

## 3. Status and compatibility boundary

This is a provisional project-process governance proposal. It introduces no
foundational SymK concept, universal ontology, market doctrine or constitutional
rule.

It composes with:

- Package A - Objective and Method Fitness;
- Package B - Epistemic Role Cooperation;
- Package C - Proportional Assurance and Governance Cost;
- Package D - Observation, Pause, Pivot and Upstream Learning; and
- Problem-Investment Control Method v0.1.

It preserves their accepted distinctions and adds a required earlier decision type
for material work. Until accepted, none of those objects is amended or superseded.

## 4. Working terms

| Term | Working meaning | Must remain distinct from |
| --- | --- | --- |
| observed condition | attributable observation about a current or expected state, task, event, capability, obligation or uncertainty | accepted Problem, cause, objective or solution |
| problem discovery (`PD`) | bounded inquiry that gathers and challenges evidence about whether a material problem situation exists | proof, acceptance, product discovery dogma or solution design |
| problem claim | versioned proposition that a condition, opportunity, obligation or knowledge gap matters enough to consider an objective | permanent truth, complaint, objective or authorization |
| problem situation | problem claim under explicit Scope, Context, time horizon, affected boundary and Evidence | abstract universal Problem or preferred remedy |
| Problem Acceptance (`PA`) | authorized decision that an exact problem claim is adequate to govern objective consideration | proof of cause, Value, urgency, objective acceptance or investment authority |
| solution contamination | a material condition in which a preferred intervention defines, narrows or supplies the evidence for the problem claim without adequate independent grounds | any mention of the problem-bearing system or Domain |
| solution alternative | complete enough intervention or non-intervention configuration to compare against an accepted objective | isolated capability, tool, role or implementation component |
| alternative family | class of possible contributions such as Human-led, process, policy, standards, software, AI, data or no-action | a complete candidate configuration |
| no-material-problem disposition | attributable conclusion that present Evidence does not justify further problem-driven investment | denial of future evidence, permanent closure or failure |

These are proposal-local terms and do not amend accepted universal vocabulary.

## 5. Core problem-first rule

> No material Objective Acceptance, solution-comparison Trial Release, Method
> Selection or Delivery Release may rely on a problem that has not been separately
> represented, challenged and accepted at PA, except through the explicit bounded
> exceptions in Section 15.

Problem Acceptance concerns the adequacy of the decision basis, not metaphysical
truth. A PA claim may later be corrected, qualified, superseded or rejected when new
Evidence changes the situation.

PD may occur before PA because its purpose is to determine whether a problem claim
is warranted. PD execution still requires authority proportionate to its data,
participants, cost and effects.

## 6. PD - Problem Discovery

### 6.1 Governing question

> What observed condition, opportunity, obligation or knowledge gap affects whom,
> in which Context and horizon, with what Evidence and consequence, and what would
> show that no material problem presently warrants further investment?

### 6.2 Minimum discovery record

A proportionate PD record gathers:

1. the observed task, condition, event, obligation, uncertainty or opportunity;
2. its source and observation time;
3. affected and benefiting subjects, including absent or indirect parties;
4. Scope, Context, Domain and horizon;
5. current behavior, baseline, workaround or reference condition;
6. frequency, magnitude, distribution and trend when evidenced;
7. consequences of the condition, action, inaction and delay;
8. claimant, observer, assessor and their competence, authority and interests;
9. Evidence, counterevidence, missingness and alternative interpretations;
10. possible causes stated as hypotheses rather than embedded facts;
11. conditions that would weaken, defeat or make the claim immaterial;
12. existing mandates or constraints without converting them into causes;
13. known proposed solutions, prior investment and contamination risk; and
14. the next decision requested, with zero implied objective or solution authority.

The record may be brief. A defect report, direct observation, research question,
incident, statutory duty or one-page interview summary may be sufficient when the
consequence and uncertainty justify it.

### 6.3 Neutral inquiry rule

When discovery involves people, questions should begin from their tasks,
observations, consequences and workarounds. A preferred solution may be disclosed
when ethically or practically required, but it must not be presented as proof of the
problem or constrain answers to its feature space.

The participant must be able to say:

- no problem exists;
- the condition is acceptable;
- the problem is different from the claimant's framing;
- the proposed solution would not help;
- another intervention is preferable; or
- there is insufficient evidence.

### 6.4 PD proportionality

Problem discovery must not become a universal research program. The smallest
credible evidence source is preferred. Higher-cost, sensitive or broad inquiry
requires evidence that lighter observation is insufficient and a separate release
when execution effects require it.

## 7. Minimum problem statement

A PA candidate states, in solution-neutral form where possible:

> In Context C and horizon T, affected subject or boundary B experiences or may
> experience condition X relative to baseline R, according to Evidence E, producing
> or risking consequence K. Uncertainty U, alternative interpretations A and
> disconfirmation conditions D remain explicit.

The statement also identifies:

- exact identity and version;
- claimant and proposed acceptance authority;
- legitimacy and jurisdiction boundary;
- current workaround and its cost or adequacy;
- consequences of action, inaction and delay;
- whether the claim concerns harm, opportunity, obligation, capability or knowledge;
- known solution-contamination risk; and
- challenge, expiry and reopening paths.

A problem statement may name the system, artifact or process in which the condition
occurs. It fails solution neutrality only when the preferred intervention is treated
as the deficiency itself without an independently established need. “The scanner
does not extract property P” is a technical observation; it becomes a justified
project problem only if an accepted requirement or affected consequence makes that
absence material.

## 8. Solution-contamination test

Before PA, reviewers apply every applicable question:

| Question | Passing evidence | Failure signal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Can the condition be explained without naming the preferred intervention? | task, condition, obligation or consequence remains intelligible | “lack of our solution” is the whole problem |
| Would the condition still matter if the proposed solution disappeared? | affected consequence persists | problem vanishes with the product concept |
| Is there Evidence independent of solution construction? | attributable observation, mandate, incident, user evidence or research gap | implementation artifacts are the only evidence |
| Is a technical gap linked to an accepted external need? | explicit trace from gap to affected outcome or obligation | missing field/feature is assumed harmful |
| Could the affected party reject or reframe the claim? | refusal, correction and no-problem routes exist | leading inquiry or forced confirmation |
| Does the objective remain unstated at PA? | statement describes current condition and consequence | desired deliverable is embedded as current problem |
| Are causes hypotheses rather than conclusions? | alternatives and uncertainty remain | preferred mechanism is declared causal without evidence |
| Can non-solution, non-technical and inaction alternatives later compete? | intervention space remains open | all candidates preserve the favored architecture |

Failure does not prove that the proposed solution is bad. It means the problem claim
is inadequate to justify objective or method commitment in its current form.

### 8.1 Black-box solution test

Hide the proposed product, technology and project deliverables from a competent
reviewer. If the reviewer cannot identify the affected condition, consequence and
decision from the remaining statement, the claim is solution-contaminated or
insufficiently evidenced.

### 8.2 Provenance inversion test

Record whether the evidence existed before the proposed solution, arose independently
of it or was generated by attempts to build it. Later evidence remains admissible,
but solution-derived evidence must be marked and cannot serve as independent
confirmation of the need it was designed to expose.

## 9. PA - Problem Acceptance

### 9.1 Decision question

> Is this exact problem claim sufficiently legitimate, evidenced, bounded,
> challengeable and independent of a preferred solution to justify objective
> consideration now?

### 9.2 Gate requirements

PA records:

- exact problem identity/version and discovery evidence;
- affected boundary and competent claimant/assessor input;
- baseline, consequence and horizon;
- action, inaction and delay implications without selecting among them;
- solution-contamination test result;
- uncertainty, dissent, counterevidence and disconfirmation conditions;
- applicable authority and non-compensable constraints;
- expiry and reopening triggers; and
- explicit zero Objective Acceptance, Method Selection or Execution Release.

### 9.3 Permitted dispositions

- `accept` - adequate basis for OA preparation;
- `accept_provisionally` - bounded objective inquiry may proceed under explicit
  uncertainty, expiry and correction conditions;
- `correct` - return for reframing or evidence;
- `defer` - retain the claim and define reconsideration triggers;
- `reject` - the exact claim is unsupported, illegitimate or materially defective;
- `no_material_problem` - present Evidence does not justify further problem-driven
  investment; or
- `route_to_authority` - another authority owns the problem decision.

No disposition determines the solution. `No_material_problem` is a successful
governance result when it avoids unjustified investment.

## 10. Relationship between PA and OA

OA answers what externally meaningful condition should differ if the accepted
problem is addressed. It does not restate the problem or name the preferred
deliverable as the outcome.

Every material OA must cite exactly one controlling PA version or explicitly compose
several compatible accepted problem claims. It must show:

1. which accepted condition and affected boundary it addresses;
2. how the desired difference responds to that condition;
3. what part of PA it excludes or leaves unchanged;
4. how success and counterevidence can be observed without inspecting the proposed
   implementation; and
5. which PA reopening condition would invalidate or require correction of OA.

An accepted PA does not require an objective to be accepted. OA may still be
disproportionate, outside authority or less valuable than inaction.

## 11. SA - Solution-Alternative Admission

### 11.1 Placement

Complete solution alternatives are admitted only relative to an accepted OA. Early
ideas discovered during PD are retained as provenance and contamination risks, not
promoted automatically into the SA set.

### 11.2 Minimum credible set

Unless an explicit exception applies, the set includes:

1. inaction, monitoring or deliberate deferral;
2. the simplest credible process, policy, organizational or reuse alternative;
3. the proposed or incumbent solution;
4. at least one materially different intervention configuration when credible; and
5. a combination or staged probe only when its added coordination burden is visible.

The set need not contain a non-technical candidate when the problem is necessarily
technical, nor a technical candidate when none is plausible. Exclusions require
attributable reasons.

### 11.3 Complete-configuration rule

A candidate is complete enough for comparison when it identifies:

- the change or non-change proposed;
- intended contribution to OA;
- active participants and capabilities;
- prerequisites, data and Context;
- expected burden, exposure and time to evidence;
- important failure modes and non-compensable conditions;
- reversibility and correction route; and
- what observation could distinguish it from other candidates.

“AI-assisted,” “standards-first,” “expert-led,” “scanner,” “automation” and similar
labels are alternative families or components unless the record supplies the
configuration above. A list of components does not satisfy SA.

### 11.4 Admission is not selection

Admission establishes plausibility for comparison. It does not prove Value, fitness,
authority or entitlement to trial resources. Existing investment receives no
automatic preference.

## 12. Traceability invariant

Material project governance preserves this chain:

| Object | Must answer | Must cite | Must not establish |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `PD` | what is observed and uncertain? | sources and affected observations | Problem Acceptance |
| `PA` | what condition matters enough to frame? | PD Evidence | objective or solution |
| `OA` | what external difference is sought? | accepted PA | method or execution |
| `SA` | what complete alternatives might contribute? | accepted OA | selection |
| `TR` | what bounded execution can discriminate candidates? | accepted OA and SA | Method Selection or delivery |
| `MS` | which candidate is preferred under current Evidence? | accepted OA, SA and applicable TR evidence | execution |
| `DLR` | who may do what, where, when and with which effects? | accepted OA and MS | success or outcome |

A later object with a missing, mismatched, expired or reopened predecessor is
fail-closed for new reliance. Historical occurrence remains preserved.

## 13. Drift and reopening controls

At every material gate, ask:

1. Does the current work still address the accepted PA condition?
2. Has the problem been redefined as absence or imperfection of the selected method?
3. Has a deliverable replaced the external objective?
4. Are candidate “alternatives” only components of one architecture?
5. Has Evidence from affected subjects changed or remained absent while control and
   implementation work grew?
6. Would stopping now reveal only sunk cost, or create a new material consequence?

Reopen PA when:

- the affected condition, population, Context or horizon changes materially;
- primary Evidence is contradicted, expires or proves non-representative;
- the proposed solution becomes the principal source of the problem claim;
- an intended beneficiary reports that the framed problem is absent or different;
- outcome learning remains weak while solution/control investment grows;
- an obligation or mandate changes; or
- a credible no-material-problem interpretation emerges.

Reopening PA pauses new downstream reliance until competent disposition. It does not
erase historical acceptance, automatically terminate every existing operation or
dictate the final lifecycle response.

## 14. Roles and authority

| Function | Contribution | Does not establish automatically |
| --- | --- | --- |
| claimant | reports a condition, opportunity, obligation or gap | representativeness, cause or acceptance |
| affected participant/representative | supplies experience, consequence, correction or refusal | sole authority over every project decision |
| observer/researcher | gathers and characterizes Evidence | Problem Acceptance or solution fitness |
| problem assessor | tests framing, contamination, uncertainty and alternatives | PA decision unless separately authorized |
| PA authority | accepts or disposes the exact problem claim | OA, solution selection or execution |
| solution advocate/owner | supplies intervention knowledge and limitations | independent problem evidence |
| project steward | governs project-process decisions within jurisdiction | Domain truth or authority over all affected subjects |

One person may perform several functions when overlap, interests and resulting
limits are explicit. A claimant's or customer's requested solution is evidence of a
request, not proof of the underlying problem or method fitness.

## 15. Bounded exceptions

### 15.1 External mandate

When competent authority imposes an obligation or exact method, PA may state the
mandated condition and consequences of noncompliance. The record distinguishes the
mandate, its authority and non-discretionary elements from any claimed user problem.
Remaining choices still require objective and alternative reasoning where they
exist.

### 15.2 Emergency containment

Immediate containment may precede complete PD/PA when delay could cause material
harm and competent authority permits action. The release must be narrow, expiring
and non-precedential, and must create an obligation to complete or explicitly waive
the problem record after stabilization.

### 15.3 Research and discovery

A knowledge gap, disputed proposition or unexplained observation can be a valid
problem situation. It requires a bounded research question, epistemic consequence,
Evidence state and stopping condition, not a fabricated customer pain or promised
commercial outcome.

### 15.4 Routine correction under standing authority

A minor defect or maintenance task may rely on an already accepted problem class,
objective and standing release. Material change, repeated failure or expansion
reopens PA. The profile does not require a new interview for every bug.

## 16. Composition with accepted SymK profiles

### 16.1 Problem-Investment Control Method v0.1

This profile supplies the missing acceptance and contamination controls around the
method's versioned problem situation. If accepted, future material use records PD/PA
before applying complexity and investment-worth assessment to an objective or
intervention. A later v0.2 consolidation should integrate Sections 5-13 directly.

### 16.2 Package A v0.3

Package A remains authoritative for outcome-facing objectives, candidate methods,
trials, Method Selection and execution releases within its accepted scope. This
profile adds PA as a predecessor and SA complete-solution admission before empirical
method comparison. A later Package A v0.4 consolidation should incorporate these
predecessor and traceability rules.

### 16.3 Package B v0.1

Package B governs claimant, affected-participant, observer, assessor and decision
handoffs. Solution advocates cannot silently substitute for affected or independent
problem evidence. Progressive role activation applies: only competence needed for
the current PD/PA decision is required.

### 16.4 Package C v0.1

Problem-discovery and acceptance burden is proportional to consequence and
uncertainty. Governance cost and leading-question risk are included. Strong controls
for high-consequence or disputed claims do not justify heavy ceremony for a minor,
reversible task.

### 16.5 Package D v0.1

Problem evidence remains observable and revisable. Outcome-to-control divergence,
changed affected-party evidence and solution-contamination signals can reopen PA.
Qualified project occurrences may challenge this profile through the existing
upstream-learning route.

## 18. Acceptance conditions

Accept this profile only if review finds that it:

1. separates observation, problem, objective, alternatives, trial, selection and
   delivery;
2. detects solution-first inversion without prohibiting legitimate system-specific
   problem statements;
3. admits no-problem, obligation, research, emergency and internal-capability cases;
4. requires complete candidate configurations rather than lists of tools;
5. remains proportional for small and reversible work;
6. composes without silently amending Packages A-D or Problem-Investment v0.1;
7. preserves affected-party correction and refusal;
8. makes missing or reopened predecessors visible and fail-closed for new reliance;
9. passes the separately recorded counterexample suite; and
10. creates no derived-project or execution authority.

## 19. Known limitations

- Evidence adequacy remains Domain- and consequence-dependent.
- A solution-neutral statement can still hide biased observation or excluded
  affected parties.
- PA authority structures are not universalized here.
- Some engineering and mandated problems legitimately name exact artifacts.
- The profile has one motivating project occurrence and a conceptual stress-test
  suite, not longitudinal cross-project validation.
- Machine-readable linting can detect missing fields and links, not semantic honesty
  or actual independence.

## 20. Prohibited inferences and non-effects

This proposal does not:

- establish that every complaint or preference is a material Problem;
- require market validation, commercial users or interviews for every task;
- forbid a problem statement from naming its system or Domain;
- make affected-party testimony infallible or sufficient by itself;
- require exhaustive causal proof before bounded learning;
- require a new PD/PA cycle for work already covered by a current standing record;
- make solution-derived Evidence inadmissible;
- select an objective, alternative, method or investment;
- reopen, pause, correct or authorize SPServices or another derived project;
- amend Packages A-D or Problem-Investment v0.1 before separate acceptance;
- create constitutional force or canonical Methodology status; or
- authorize contact, data access, model use, system change or production reliance.
