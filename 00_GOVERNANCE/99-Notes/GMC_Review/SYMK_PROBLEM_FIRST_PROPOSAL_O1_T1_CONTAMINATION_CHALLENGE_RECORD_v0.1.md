## 1. Challenge

The project steward stated:

> You are refusing to see how deep the problem is, and [the proposal] is
> contaminated by O1/T1. The main role of SymK is to act as mediator between
> different intelligences, an evolution of the cooperation protocol. Then, every
> time it is called to the table the first question should be: I am being called to
> mediate what?

The challenge was accepted analytically. No project-steward acceptance of this
record or a replacement profile is inferred.

## 2. What the challenged proposal got right

The proposal correctly detected that:

- an owned or preferred solution can define the problem used to justify it;
- a problem claim must not be hidden inside an objective;
- no-material-problem must remain a valid result;
- complete alternatives matter more than lists of technologies or capabilities;
- emergency, mandate, research and routine-maintenance cases require proportionate
  treatment; and
- the SPServices property sequence supplies useful upstream evidence.

These results remain analytical evidence available to a later possible-problem
investigation route.

## 3. How O1/T1 contaminated the proposal

The proposal moved one step upstream but retained the SPServices and Package A
pipeline as its universal architecture:

```text
problem -> objective -> alternatives -> trial -> method -> delivery
```

This caused four material errors:

1. **Problem presupposition:** It assumed that a candidate SymK engagement begins
   with a problem rather than first determining what relation, Question,
   Representation, decision, cooperative activity or uncertainty is being mediated.
2. **Project-pipeline centrality:** It treated objective, trial and delivery as the
   general acting spine even though accepted DR-017/018 center the non-reifying
   governed knowledge undertaking and require branching, recursive transitions.
3. **Missing mediation constitution:** It did not first identify the caller,
   participants, affected parties, actual mediator, standing, Authority, Context,
   Representations or requested mediation role.
4. **Restricted route space:** Clarification, translation, representation
   reconciliation, cooperation coordination, decision support, legitimate
   disagreement preservation, refusal and redirection appeared only indirectly or
   not at all.

The proposal was therefore a valid branch correction incorrectly positioned as the
root profile.

## 4. Correct disposition

The challenged proposal shall be preserved unchanged as proposal lineage. It should
not be accepted, silently rewritten or deleted.

Its proper future treatment is:

- **root status:** rejected as the general SymK acting architecture unless later
  evidence defeats this challenge;
- **branch status:** potentially reusable as input to an `investigate a possible
  problem` route after an Acting Roadmap has constituted and admitted the mediation
  engagement;
- **SPServices status:** evidence only; no project reopening or redesign follows;
- **authority status:** no current normative, project, investment or execution
  effect.

## 5. Replacement requirement

A replacement proposal must begin with:

> **What is this application of SymK being called to mediate?**

It must then establish the candidate governed undertaking and choose among multiple
mediation routes before invoking any problem, objective, trial or delivery
specialization.

## 6. Reopening and dissent

This challenge may be corrected or defeated if accepted SymK authority is shown to
require the objective/trial pipeline as the universal acting root, or if tests show
that mediation intake creates worse semantic, authority or governance failures.

The phrase “SymK acts as mediator” also remains subject to the non-reification rule:
actual Agency, Intelligence, Authority and Responsibility must be attributed to
identified Bearers and participants rather than to the framework by shorthand.
