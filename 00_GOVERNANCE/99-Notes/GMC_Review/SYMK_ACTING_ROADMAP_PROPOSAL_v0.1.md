# SymK Acting Roadmap Proposal v0.1

**Status:** PROPOSAL PREPARED FOR PROJECT-STEWARD REVIEW ON 2026-09-07 - NOT
ACCEPTED; NO MEDIATION, DOMAIN, PROJECT, DECISION, ACTION OR EXECUTION AUTHORITY

**Identity basis:**
`SYMK_MEDIATION_IDENTITY_AND_ACTING_BOUNDARY_AUDIT_v0.1.md`

**Challenge basis:**
`SYMK_PROBLEM_FIRST_PROPOSAL_O1_T1_CONTAMINATION_CHALLENGE_RECORD_v0.1.md`

**Requested effect if accepted:** Establish a provisional SymK project-process
profile governing how a candidate application of SymK moves from an observable
event to mediation constitution, route selection, bounded action, disposition and
upstream learning. Specialized problem-investment, objective, trial and delivery
profiles remain downstream and separately governed.

## 1. Purpose

Make SymK operationally answer its first acting question:

> **What is this application of SymK being called to mediate?**

The roadmap prevents a caller, prompt, requested solution, workflow, project plan or
available technology from silently defining the governed undertaking. It permits
SymK-guided participants and systems to recognize an event, determine whether a
mediation engagement is warranted, select and compose appropriate mediation routes,
act only under separate authority and return observations into correction and
learning.

## 2. Identity and non-reification boundary

This roadmap operationalizes the Stage-Accepted SymK identity as a general framework
for responsible Knowledge Engineering through governed cooperation among
heterogeneous forms and Bearers of Intelligence.

“SymK acts,” “SymK is called,” and “SymK mediates” are convenient shorthand. SymK is
not thereby classified as an Intelligence Bearer, Agent, person, institution,
decision-maker or sovereign authority. Every actual observer, mediator, participant,
actor, authority and responsible party must be identified at the adequate level.

The governed knowledge undertaking remains the non-reifying analytical organizing
center accepted through DR-017/018. This roadmap is not that undertaking, a new
Foundational concept or a universal workflow.

## 3. Core distinctions

| Object | Working meaning | Does not establish automatically |
| --- | --- | --- |
| observable event | attributable occurrence or changed condition that may warrant attention | complete meaning, mediation need or Authority |
| event report | Representation of an event by an identified source | event truth or completeness |
| candidate call | request or governance signal asking whether SymK should be applied | admitted engagement, caller ownership of framing or action |
| mediation object | relation, Question, Representation, claim, decision, activity, condition or uncertainty proposed for mediation | Problem, objective or solution |
| mediation contribution | bounded intervention intended to improve interpretation, inquiry, coordination, decision support, challenge or responsible non-action within the undertaking | neutrality, truth, consensus, decision Authority or success |
| mediation constitution | bounded account of what may be mediated, among whom, in which Context, through which Representations and under what standing | successful Cooperation, agreement or action release |
| mediation engagement | admitted, scoped application of SymK under declared role, authority and stopping conditions | authority beyond its release |
| mediation route | governed mode of contribution selected for the engagement | fixed workflow, answer, decision or success |
| route composition | ordered, parallel or conditional use of more than one route | mutual consistency or unlimited scope |
| bounded action | authorized act within an engagement | truth, acceptance, adoption or favorable outcome |
| disposition | attributable state such as conclude, pause, reroute, defer, refuse or redirect | success, failure, closure forever or upstream generalization |
| observed effect | represented consequence or change after action or non-action | causality, Value, correction or Learning |

Every distinction inherits accepted SymK Identity, Context, Scope, Representation,
Evidence, Authority, Responsibility, Question, Cooperation and recursive-return
boundaries.

The diagram is a control map, not a mandatory linear workflow. Transitions may loop,
branch, compose or stop. A later state never manufactures its predecessor.

## 5. Event recognition

### 5.1 Candidate event families

An event may include:

1. a participant requests mediation;
2. two or more Representations materially diverge;
3. a Question, ambiguity or unexplained observation arises;
4. Evidence conflicts, expires or changes;
5. Cooperation becomes blocked, distorted or contested;
6. a decision requires contributions from several Intelligences or authorities;
7. an affected party challenges a claim, frame, decision, practice or consequence;
8. risk, harm, opportunity, obligation or uncertainty becomes visible;
9. Context, Scope, participants, capability, technology, Authority or Responsibility
   changes materially;
10. an engagement reaches a stopping, expiry, correction or reopening condition; or
11. a machine or monitoring system emits a signal requiring Human or institutional
   interpretation.

The list is open and descriptive. Event type does not prescribe route.

### 5.2 Event registration minimum

Record proportionately:

- what was observed or requested;
- source, observer or caller;
- time and relevant Context;
- available Representation and original wording;
- immediate affected horizon or uncertainty;
- known urgency and possible consequence;
- what remains unknown; and
- the exact next decision requested: whether to constitute a mediation engagement.

### 5.3 Event non-entailments

No event, alert, request, complaint, disagreement, incident, metric or changed record
automatically establishes:

- a complete Question or problem;
- a need for SymK mediation;
- the caller's framing;
- Cooperation, conflict or consensus;
- urgency, harm, cause or Value;
- standing, Authority, Responsibility or consent;
- a route, method, action or execution release; or
- upstream SymK evidence.

## 6. Candidate call and mediation constitution

### 6.1 Universal entry question

Every candidate call begins with the Human-readable question:

> **I am being called to mediate what?**

The governed form is:

> What relation, Question, Representation, claim, decision, cooperative activity,
> condition or uncertainty is this application being asked to mediate; among which
> distinguishable participants, Intelligences and affected parties; in which Scope,
> Context and horizon; through which Representations; by which actual mediator; and
> under what standing, Authority, Responsibility and challenge conditions?

### 6.2 Constitution dimensions

A proportionate constitution makes material dimensions explicit:

1. **call identity:** versioned request or signal and its provenance;
2. **provisional mediation object:** what may require mediation, without accepting
   the caller's formulation as complete;
3. **undertaking boundary:** the relevant shared inquiry, relation, decision,
   activity or uncertainty;
4. **participants and Intelligences:** actual, proposed, represented, absent and
   excluded contributors at the adequate level;
5. **affected parties:** direct, indirect, future or nonparticipating subjects;
6. **Representations:** languages, documents, models, data, interfaces, tacit
   accounts and material transformation loss;
7. **positions and uncertainty:** what each source presently claims, seeks, rejects
   or cannot determine;
8. **Context, Scope, Domain and horizon:** including dependencies and relevant
   external jurisdictions;
9. **actual mediator:** Human, artificial, collective, institutional, hybrid or
   configured arrangement and its capability Evidence;
10. **standing and Authority:** who may request, participate, assess, decide,
    authorize action, challenge, refuse or redirect;
11. **Responsibility:** prospective duties, answerability and retained ownership;
12. **requested contribution:** clarification, translation, inquiry, coordination,
    decision support, dissent preservation or another bounded role;
13. **feasibility:** available technology, context portability, access, time, cost,
    skill and coordination capacity;
14. **risk and urgency:** potential consequences of action, inaction and delay;
15. **success and stopping conditions:** what sufficient contribution, impasse,
    expiry or changed Evidence would look like; and
16. **prohibited presumptions:** especially Problem, objective, preferred solution,
    consensus, Authority, truth or execution.

These dimensions are a coverage guide, not a universal document-size requirement.

### 6.3 Caller-framing challenge

The constitution must permit every participant or affected representative to state
that:

- the proposed mediation object is wrong or incomplete;
- the caller lacks standing or has omitted an authority;
- the matter is not a Problem;
- the apparent disagreement is terminological or representational;
- agreement is neither possible nor desirable;
- SymK is unnecessary or incompetent;
- available technology or participation cannot support responsible mediation; or
- refusal, deferral or redirection is preferable.

## 7. Engagement-admission gate

### 7.1 Decision question

> Is a bounded SymK mediation engagement sufficiently identified, legitimate,
> feasible, challengeable and authorized to select one or more routes now?

### 7.2 Minimum requirements

Admission requires proportionate Evidence that:

- the mediation object is intelligible enough to route;
- relevant participants, affected boundaries and exclusions are visible;
- an actual mediator or mediation arrangement is identified and capable enough for
  the proposed role;
- standing, Authority and Responsibility are sufficient for the current step;
- material Representation differences and access limits are known;
- feasibility does not depend on unavailable technology or unsupported context
  sharing;
- urgency and consequences are bounded;
- challenge, correction, refusal and exit paths exist; and
- admission grants no downstream decision or execution Authority.

### 7.3 Permitted dispositions

- `admit` — adequate basis for route selection within the declared boundary;
- `admit_provisionally` — limited routing under explicit uncertainty, expiry and
  correction conditions;
- `clarify_call` — insufficient identity, participant, authority or Context detail;
- `defer` — retain with explicit reconsideration trigger;
- `redirect` — another process, Domain or authority is the proper venue;
- `refuse` — mediation would be unauthorized, deceptive, infeasible, harmful or
  outside SymK jurisdiction;
- `no_mediation_warranted` — no material mediation contribution is presently
  justified; or
- `urgent_consideration` — notify competent operational or external authority;
  urgency creates no action power by itself.

## 8. Route selection principles

Route selection asks which contribution is appropriate now, not which predetermined
workflow should run.

Every route record states:

- why the route fits the admitted mediation object;
- inputs and source authority;
- participants and actual mediator;
- expected contribution and output;
- exclusions and prohibited inferences;
- needed action release, if any;
- observation and stopping conditions;
- adjacent or possible next routes; and
- rerouting triggers.

Selection of a route does not prove the route will work, compel participation,
create agreement or authorize its consequential actions.

## 9. Mediation routes

### 9.1 Clarify or translate

**Use when:** meaning, language, vocabulary, scope, Question Identity, assumptions or
Representation correspondence is unclear.

**Governing question:** What must become mutually interpretable without erasing
material difference or pretending identical internal Representation?

**Possible contribution:** versioned terms, faithful translations, ambiguity maps,
Question lineage, declared transformation loss and remaining non-equivalence.

**Must not establish automatically:** agreement, truth, compatibility, shared
ontology, preserved Question Identity or authority of the translator.

### 9.2 Reconcile Representations

**Use when:** documents, models, schemas, accounts, measurements or Views claim to
represent the same or related subjects but diverge materially.

**Governing question:** Which differences arise from source subject, Context,
perspective, granularity, transformation, omission, error or legitimate plurality?

**Possible contribution:** correspondence map, conflict inventory, source-priority
questions, loss declaration, compatible composition or explicit incompatibility.

**Must not establish automatically:** one sovereign Representation, factual truth,
semantic equivalence, merge authority or deletion of dissenting lineage.

### 9.3 Investigate a possible Problem

**Use when:** a condition, opportunity, obligation, capability gap or uncertainty may
justify further inquiry or investment.

**Governing question:** Is there an independently intelligible and evidenced
condition that matters, or has a preferred intervention defined the supposed
Problem?

**Possible contribution:** bounded Problem Discovery, solution-contamination test,
affected-party Evidence, disconfirmation conditions and a Problem Acceptance or
no-material-problem candidate.

**Must not establish automatically:** Problem, objective, solution, investment,
trial or execution. The challenged problem-first proposal may inform this branch but
does not govern it unless separately reconstructed and accepted.

### 9.4 Coordinate Cooperation

**Use when:** distinguishable participants contribute to a bounded shared undertaking
but their roles, dependencies, timing, handoffs, mutual orientation, challenge or
memory require governance.

**Governing question:** What cooperative relations and conditions allow material
Contributions without collapsing capability, role, Authority, Responsibility,
consent, benefit or affected standing?

**Possible contribution:** participant and contribution map, handoff protocol,
dependency and capability boundary, shared-memory arrangement, challenge path and
coordination checkpoint.

**Must not establish automatically:** Cooperation merely from interaction;
responsible or beneficial Cooperation; equal capability; consensus; compulsory
participation; or successful outcome.

### 9.5 Support a decision

**Use when:** a competent decision locus seeks governed contributions concerning an
identified decision Question and alternatives.

**Governing question:** What Evidence, Reasoning, alternatives, Value claims,
uncertainty, affected consequences and dissent should the competent decision-maker
consider?

**Possible contribution:** decision frame, alternatives, Evidence and uncertainty
profile, reasoning record, trade-off map, recommendation or qualified no-answer.

**Must not establish automatically:** decision Authority, adoption, approval,
execution, truth, consensus, favorable outcome or transferred Responsibility.

### 9.6 Preserve disagreement

**Use when:** positions remain materially incompatible, resolution is unavailable or
inappropriate, or forced consensus would erase Evidence, values, identities,
Authority boundaries or affected parties.

**Governing question:** What must remain separately represented, challengeable and
action-relevant while cooperation or coexistence continues?

**Possible contribution:** dissent record, incompatibility boundary, minority or
affected-party position, conditions for reconsideration, parallel paths and safe
non-resolution.

**Must not establish automatically:** equal evidential weight, permanent deadlock,
bad faith, conflict termination, veto, consensus or permission to ignore harm.

### 9.7 Refuse, defer or redirect

**Use when:** standing, Authority, legitimacy, competence, feasibility, safety,
participation or jurisdiction is inadequate, or another process owns the matter.

**Governing question:** Why should this engagement not proceed here and now, what
must be preserved, and which correction, trigger or destination remains available?

**Possible contribution:** refusal grounds, deferred conditions, competent venue,
handoff limits, evidence preservation and urgent notice where authorized.

**Must not establish automatically:** falsity of the underlying claim, permanent
closure, absence of harm, transfer acceptance or action by the receiving authority.

## 10. Route composition and rerouting

Routes may be:

- sequential, such as clarification before decision support;
- parallel, such as Representation reconciliation and disagreement preservation;
- conditional, such as possible-Problem inquiry only if translation shows a real
  affected condition;
- nested, such as decision support inside a wider cooperation-coordination
  engagement; or
- terminated independently when one route completes or loses authority.

Reroute when the mediation object, Question, participants, affected boundary,
Representation, Authority, capability, Context, urgency or Evidence changes
materially. Rerouting preserves prior lineage and does not disguise failure as a
successful completion.

No route may silently become the root. In particular:

- clarification does not force reconciliation;
- reconciliation does not force consensus;
- Problem investigation does not force an objective;
- Cooperation coordination does not force continued participation;
- decision support does not become decision-making; and
- refusal does not prevent later legitimate invocation.

## 11. Bounded mediation action

Route admission alone creates no authority to contact people, access data, transform
artifacts, execute code, change systems, spend resources, make decisions or deploy.

A consequential action requires a separate proportionate release identifying:

- exact act and actor;
- mediation engagement and route;
- Authority source and jurisdiction;
- affected systems, people, data and environments;
- prerequisites and feasibility;
- privacy, safety, security and non-compensable constraints;
- allowed effects and prohibited actions;
- observation, verification and stopping conditions;
- expiry, rollback, correction and escalation; and
- retained Responsibility.

Conversational analysis or preparation may proceed under the authority of the
specific request when its scope and effects are clear. Ambiguous “go ahead” language
does not authorize downstream routes or material external action beyond the exact
immediately presented step.

## 12. Observation, disposition and recursive return

During and after a route, distinguish:

- action performed;
- output or Representation produced;
- participant response;
- observed effect;
- causal or contribution claim;
- quality and Value assessment;
- decision or status;
- Correction or repair;
- Learning or Formation; and
- upstream SymK evidence.

Permitted engagement dispositions include:

- `conclude` — the bounded mediation obligation is satisfied;
- `partial_conclusion` — a declared portion is satisfied;
- `pause` — temporarily stop under stated conditions;
- `reroute` — select a different or additional route;
- `escalate` — send a bounded question to competent authority;
- `reopen` — prior constitution, route or result no longer supports reliance;
- `withdraw` — requester or competent authority ends the engagement;
- `refuse_continuation` — continued mediation lacks legitimacy, feasibility or
  authority; or
- `unresolved` — preserve a material open state without manufacturing closure.

Feedback becomes Evidence only through governed provenance and assessment. A local
occurrence becomes possible upstream SymK evidence only through Package D's bounded
transfer and authority controls.

## 13. Roles, participation and absent parties

| Function | Possible contribution | Does not establish automatically |
| --- | --- | --- |
| event source | supplies signal or observation | truth, completeness or standing |
| caller/requester | asks for SymK application | ownership of frame, participants or outcome |
| mediation sponsor | supplies bounded resources or authority | truth, Domain authority or result control |
| mediator/facilitator | performs the admitted mediation contribution | decision Authority or neutrality |
| participant/contributor | contributes to the undertaking | consent to every route or whole-level authority |
| affected party/representative | exposes consequences, correction or refusal | automatic veto or representativeness |
| Domain/scientific authority | supplies applicable substantive judgment | universal SymK authority |
| decision authority | decides within a declared locus | truth, execution or absence of Responsibility |
| actor/operator | performs authorized action | decision authorship or sole Responsibility |
| observer/assessor | records or evaluates occurrence | causality, value or acceptance authority |
| SymK project steward | governs this project-process profile | authority over every Domain or engagement |

One subject may hold several functions when overlaps, interests, capability limits
and retained Responsibility are explicit. Nominal participation is not effective
voice. Affected parties may require representation and challenge even when they are
not direct participants.

## 14. Feasibility and context portability

No roadmap route may depend silently on unavailable group context, shared-memory,
identity, permission, data-access, model, communication or coordination technology.

Before route action, record:

- what context each participant can actually access;
- what must be transported, summarized or revalidated;
- what information cannot be shared and why;
- how identity, version, provenance and authority survive handoff;
- what Human coordination is realistically available;
- what capability is required now rather than eventually desirable;
- what lower-technology route can provide a sufficient result; and
- what promise must be reduced, deferred or refused when enabling capability is
  absent.

The roadmap prefers a feasible sufficient mediation contribution over an idealized
architecture that cannot presently be operated responsibly.

## 15. Urgency and emergency boundary

An event indicating possible severe harm may shorten intake and route urgent
consideration to competent operational or external authority. It does not create an
emergency duty, decision right, containment power, access right or execution release
by semantic implication.

When independently authorized emergency action occurs, the roadmap preserves the
event, authority, action, effect, expiry, unresolved mediation objects and later
review obligation. Emergency treatment cannot become standing authority merely
through repetition.

## 16. Relationship to accepted project-governance profiles

### 16.1 Package B

Package B supplies role, capability, handoff, disagreement and context-portability
controls. The Acting Roadmap determines which roles are needed now and prevents a
fixed large team from being activated before the mediation route requires it.

### 16.2 Package C

Package C controls proportional assurance and governance cost. Event registration
and mediation constitution may be concise for low-consequence work; material
uncertainty, asymmetry, irreversibility or harm requires stronger controls.

### 16.3 Package D

Package D supplies observation, divergence, pause, pivot and upstream-learning
controls. Its events may trigger candidate calls, but no Package D trigger creates
engagement or action Authority automatically.

### 16.4 Problem-Investment and Package A

Problem-Investment and Package A are downstream specializations used only after an
admitted engagement selects a possible-Problem, objective/method or related route.
They do not define the universal acting root.

The challenged problem-first proposal remains evidence for later reconstruction of
the possible-Problem route. It receives no authority through this roadmap.

## 18. Acceptance conditions

Accept this roadmap only if review finds that it:

1. derives from accepted SymK identity without personifying the framework;
2. starts with what is being mediated rather than presuming Problem or objective;
3. distinguishes event, call, constitution, engagement, route, action and effect;
4. preserves caller-framing challenge, absent parties and Domain authority;
5. supports all seven mediation-route families without making any universal;
6. permits route composition, refusal, no-mediation, rerouting and unresolved states;
7. separates route selection from consequential-action authority;
8. respects current technical and context-portability feasibility;
9. places Problem-Investment and Package A downstream;
10. remains proportionate and does not become ceremonial intake bureaucracy;
11. passes the separately recorded stress-test cases; and
12. creates no real engagement, project change or execution authority.

## 19. Known limitations and dissent

- “Mediation” has no accepted independent Foundational-concept disposition; this
  roadmap uses it as an operational relation and contribution family expressible
  through existing accepted concepts.
- The route families may overlap or omit legitimate future forms.
- Real capability and authority thresholds remain Domain- and consequence-specific.
- Neutral mediation is not assumed; mediators shape framing through selection,
  translation and omission.
- A universal intake can itself become a power gate or bureaucracy.
- The roadmap has conceptual case evidence but no longitudinal operational
  validation.
- Some SymK uses may be better described as engineering, inquiry or governance than
  mediation; later evidence may require a broader root term.
- “Between different Intelligences” is central direction but not a requirement that
  every admitted engagement have several simultaneously active Intelligence
  Bearers.

## 20. Prohibited inferences and non-effects

This proposal does not:

- create a new Foundational concept, axiom, constitutional rule or final workflow;
- classify SymK itself as an Agent, Intelligence Bearer, mediator or authority;
- establish that every event deserves mediation;
- make the caller's framing, requested route or desired result authoritative;
- require consensus, agreement, Cooperation, a Problem, objective or solution;
- authorize participation, contact, data access, spending, system change or
  execution;
- decide any actual Question, Problem, method, value, policy or Domain fact;
- revise accepted DR-013, DR-016, DR-017, DR-018 or the Product Vision;
- amend Packages A-D or Problem-Investment v0.1;
- accept, reopen or modify the challenged problem-first proposal;
- reopen or change SPServices; or
- establish compatibility, conformance, success, Value, Learning or improvement.
