## 1. Decision

The project steward accepts the exact object identified above as a provisional
minimum-sufficient SymK problem-investment control method.

The method is accepted as presently usable for bounded SymK project-governance
assessment. Its declared incompleteness remains part of the accepted object. This
decision does not convert the method into a universal ontology, constitutional rule,
canonical Methodology-family artifact, derived-project mandate or execution release.

## 2. Acceptance basis

The accepted method:

1. assesses a versioned problem situation rather than assigning a permanent class to
   an abstract Problem;
2. preserves structural, epistemic, dynamic, agency/coordination and contextual
   complexity as independently visible claims;
3. keeps problem complexity separate from importance, consequence, value, solution
   complexity and investment authorization;
4. requires comparative incremental investment assessment, including inaction and a
   credible simpler alternative;
5. prefers the smallest bounded commitment capable of producing a useful outcome or
   decision-relevant evidence;
6. includes governance, coordination, handoff, context-transfer, maintenance,
   opportunity and remedy burdens in total investment;
7. detects complexity introduced by the proposed method rather than attributing all
   difficulty to the original problem;
8. treats essential technology and other enabling capabilities as hard feasibility
   boundaries that must be evidenced in the intended project environment;
9. prevents unavailable, speculative, inaccessible, operationally unverified or
   economically disproportionate capability from being assumed by an executable
   baseline;
10. requires context to be portable through presently viable means before Human
    participation becomes a method dependency;
11. permits progressive role activation rather than mandatory staffing of every
    possibly relevant function at every stage;
12. separates outcome/learning progress from control/readiness progress and requires
    reconsideration when they materially diverge; and
13. provides a minimum usable procedure while preserving full lifecycle semantics,
    cross-domain validation and canonical realization as later work.

## 4. Present-sufficiency boundary

Acceptance of present sufficiency means that the method may be used now within its
declared boundary even though later improvements remain open. It does not mean that
the method is eighty-percent correct, universally adequate or exempt from material
counterevidence.

A smaller presently executable method is preferred to a more complete-looking
method dependent on unavailable capability only when the smaller method preserves
mandatory constraints, exposes its limitations and can produce a useful result or
decision-relevant evidence.

## 5. Prohibited inferences and non-effects

This acceptance does not:

- define or classify a universal foundational Problem, Complexity, Value, Risk,
  Capability or Investment concept;
- create a scalar complexity, value, risk, maturity or method score;
- authorize investment from complexity alone;
- make any investment envelope a budget, target, approval or execution authority;
- select a real objective, method, role arrangement, technology, participant,
  assurance configuration or solution;
- determine that a particular technology or collaboration platform is available,
  adequate, secure or affordable;
- require ChatGPT, AI, shared conversation technology or any fixed architecture;
- permit future vendor capability or expected lower cost to count as present
  feasibility evidence;
- weaken an applicable legal, ethical, medical, privacy, safety, security,
  contractual or professional duty;
- amend or supersede Packages A–D, `SYMK-GP-001` or Stage-Accepted DR-009–018;
- open, close or bypass any SymK evolution stage;
- promote the object into `Knowledge/Methodology/Canonical/`;
- require adoption by SPServices or another derived project;
- redesign, activate, pause, reject or authorize SPServices T1;
- authorize data access, model calls, system change, deployment, publication or
  production reliance; or
- establish that the method is complete, permanent or the only valid method.

## 6. Review and reopening

Challenge, correction, supersession or retirement is required when material evidence
shows that the method:

- obscures rather than exposes a problem's context or uncertainty;
- permits one dimension or score to erase a material condition;
- treats complexity as importance or automatic investment justification;
- systematically underestimates total burden or distributed consequences;
- creates more governance complexity than the learning it enables;
- treats a summary or static snapshot as lossless shared context;
- accepts market existence as project capability;
- allows speculative capability into an executable baseline;
- suppresses necessary competence or authority in the name of simplification; or
- cannot guide a bounded real case without disproportionate ceremony.

The known incomplete lifecycle, cross-domain stress tests, canonical identifier and
machine-readable realization remain later work. Their absence does not silently
expand the accepted boundary.
