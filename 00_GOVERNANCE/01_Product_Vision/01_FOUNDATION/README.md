# SymK Foundation Papers

Foundation Papers develop the conceptual basis needed to interpret and challenge the SymK Product Vision and Constitution. They preserve argument, alternatives, evidence, candidate commitments, engineering consequences, and open questions.

They are constitutional sources, not constitutional norms by publication or directory placement. Their conclusions acquire normative force only when explicitly selected and ratified through governance.

## Current series

1. **Knowledge** — Knowledge as epistemic achievement and shared medium.
2. **Intelligence** — a minimal, non-anthropocentric participant concept.
3. **Education and Training** — formation of habits and judgment for cooperation.
4. **Knowledge Engineering** — engineering accountable epistemic conditions rather than Knowledge directly.

Each current paper is preserved as an editable Markdown source and a rendered PDF edition. Markdown is the governed source; PDF is a publication projection and must be regenerated after substantive source changes.
