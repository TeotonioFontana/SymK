## Governance roles

- **Product Vision:** states identity, purpose, desired future, beneficiaries, boundaries, and success conditions.
- **Foundation Papers:** preserve the arguments, alternatives, candidate concepts, and consequences supporting constitutional development.
- **Research:** records surveys and evidence used by the papers but does not acquire normative force by location.
- **Orientation:** explains SymK to newcomers without replacing authoritative governance documents.

The Product Vision motivates the Foundation Papers. Selected conclusions from those papers may become constitutional commitments only through explicit governance. The Constitution then derives principles and constraints for SymK and its projects.
