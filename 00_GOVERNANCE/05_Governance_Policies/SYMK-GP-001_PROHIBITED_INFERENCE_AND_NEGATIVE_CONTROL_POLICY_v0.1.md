# SYMK-GP-001 — Prohibited Inference and Negative-Control Policy v0.1

**Status:** Provisional active project-governance policy
**Effective date:** 4 September 2026
**Authority:** Explicit project-steward instruction to update SymK after review of
the proposed negative-control governance mechanism
**Jurisdiction:** SymK project governance and newly created or materially revised
governed artifacts
**Normative force:** Project-process policy; not a constitutional norm, Foundational
concept decision, Domain rule or implementation requirement
**Review route:** Joint review with the remaining generalizable-method conclusions
and later classification under the competent SymK principles, methods, standards and
derived-project governance stages

## 1. Purpose

Prevent a governed conclusion from acquiring unintended meaning, authority or
effect through plausible but invalid inference.

SymK has long used non-effects, prohibited entailments, counterexamples and negative
controls in analytical work. This policy makes the underlying practice explicit and
prospective across material governance artifacts, using the accessible heading:

> **Prohibited inferences — what this does not mean**

The policy does not claim that negative controls are the only improvement required
by SymK. It records one accepted process improvement while the wider
generalizable-method-conclusion package remains open for later review.

## 2. Policy rule

Every newly created or materially revised SymK decision, policy, method, standard,
roadmap decision, package gate, status transition or derived-project proposal must:

1. state its positive claim, decision or effect;
2. identify its material prohibited inferences under the heading **“Prohibited
   inferences — what this does not mean”**;
3. distinguish those inferences from operational non-effects and out-of-scope work;
4. include concrete counterexamples or tests when a boundary cannot be verified from
   the wording alone;
5. declare the Scope, Context, Domain, authority and status to which each material
   control applies; and
6. preserve challenge, correction, supersession and reopening paths.

If a responsible author or reviewer concludes that no negative control is material,
the artifact may say so with a brief rationale. Silence must not be treated as proof
that no invalid inference is possible.

## 3. What a negative control is

A negative control is a stated inference that a governed claim, fact, status,
artifact or event does **not** establish automatically.

It has the general form:

> **A does not by itself establish B.**

The control does not assert that A and B can never coexist. It requires separate
evidence or authority for the transition from A to B.

Examples include:

- an output does not by itself establish an Answer;
- evidence does not by itself establish truth or accepted status;
- a proposal does not by itself establish a decision;
- implementation does not by itself establish successful outcome; and
- local success does not by itself establish universal method validity.

## 4. Distinctions from adjacent controls

| Control | Governing question |
| --- | --- |
| positive claim or effect | What is established or changed? |
| prohibited inference / negative control | What tempting conclusion does the established fact not establish by itself? |
| non-effect | Which state change or action is not caused or authorized by this artifact? |
| exclusion / out of scope | Which subject is not being examined or governed here? |
| counterexample | Which concrete case demonstrates why an inference or rule fails? |
| reopening condition | Which later evidence or change requires review of the governed position? |
| exception | Under which governed conditions does a rule not apply or apply differently? |

An artifact may use several of these together. None substitutes for another.

## 5. Baseline cross-SymK controls

The following controls form a default cross-SymK review baseline where material.
They preserve accepted distinctions but do not require every artifact to repeat every
line.

1. Representation, output, record or repository placement does not by itself create
   truth, Knowledge, meaning, authority, status or acceptance.
2. Recommendation, proposal, plan, instruction or assessment does not by itself
   create a decision, adoption, Authorization or action.
3. Implementation, execution or procedural completion does not by itself establish
   objective achievement, quality, successful outcome or Value.
4. Evidence, provenance, reproducibility or assessment does not by itself establish
   the truth, completeness or accepted governance status of the supported claim.
5. Human presence, review assignment, interaction or approval does not by itself
   establish competence, understanding, substantive review, authorship, control or
   valid Authority.
6. A derived project's success, failure or repeated practice does not by itself
   establish universal validity, incompatibility or a SymK rule.

These are minimum review prompts, not exhaustive universal propositions detached
from Scope and Context.

## 6. Method and project control library

The following controls are available when material to a method, plan or derived
project. They are not mandatory claims about every case:

1. deterministic result does not by itself establish semantic adequacy;
2. inclusion in a standard does not by itself establish local applicability,
   sufficiency or authority;
3. AI-generated output does not by itself establish Knowledge, truth, accepted
   meaning or decision;
4. more gates or governance activity does not by itself establish proportionally
   lower risk;
5. absence of an observed incident does not by itself establish an adequate risk
   model;
6. faster generation does not by itself establish a faster responsible Answer or a
   better outcome;
7. lower direct expense does not by itself establish lower total cost; and
8. a pause does not by itself establish failure, rejection, rollback or abandonment.

Projects and Domains may add more specific controls under their competent authority.
Project-specific controls do not become cross-SymK controls through repetition,
placement or convenience.

## 7. Materiality and proportionality

Negative controls must be proportionate to the claim and its possible consequences.
The objective is inspectable semantic safety, not maximal document size.

A control is material when a plausible invalid inference could materially change:

- meaning or classification;
- evidentiary or governance status;
- Authority, Authorization, standing or Responsibility;
- a decision, action or reliance condition;
- affected-subject risk or access;
- implementation, publication, persistence or migration;
- reported quality, cost, Value, outcome or completion; or
- inheritance, specialization or universalization.

Low-consequence artifacts may use a compact set. High-consequence or high-ambiguity
artifacts may require a structured control table, counterexamples and independent
review. The number of controls is never an assurance metric by itself.

## 8. Quality requirements

A useful negative control is:

- **specific:** it names the source fact and prohibited conclusion;
- **plausible:** it blocks a misunderstanding that a reasonable reader or system
  could make;
- **scoped:** it preserves applicable Domain, Context, time and authority;
- **non-absolute unless warranted:** “does not by itself establish” is normally
  preferable to “can never”;
- **testable:** a reviewer can identify evidence that would be needed for the
  prohibited transition;
- **symmetric where material:** favorable and adverse outcomes should not
  automatically validate or invalidate an earlier process; and
- **maintainable:** it has a clear owner, review point or lineage when the artifact
  changes.

Tautological disclaimers, exhaustive boilerplate and controls unrelated to the
artifact do not satisfy this policy.

## 9. Review procedure

For a material artifact, the author and reviewer should ask:

1. What exactly is established?
2. Which neighboring concept, status, authority or outcome might be inferred
   incorrectly?
3. What independent evidence or decision would be required for that transition?
4. Does the wording preserve this boundary for Human, Scientific and Engineering
   Views?
5. Is a concrete counterexample needed?
6. Could the control conceal a real effect or become an excuse for avoiding a
   decision?
7. Which change would make the control obsolete or require revision?

Where a control is machine-testable, an implementation may verify it. Automated
verification proves only the declared machine-testable condition; it does not
establish semantic adequacy or complete policy compliance.

## 10. Lifecycle and exceptions

Negative controls travel with the governed artifact's lineage. Revision,
supersession, migration or projection must preserve them, explicitly revise them or
record why they no longer apply.

Urgency may justify a shorter initial record only under competent authority and with
an explicit completion obligation. Urgency does not erase a material inference
boundary, manufacture Authority or validate the resulting decision.

Conflicts between controls, or between a control and Domain or external authority,
must be exposed and routed through the applicable challenge procedure rather than
resolved silently.

## 11. Prospective application and existing corpus

This policy applies prospectively from its effective date. It does not require a
bulk rewrite of the existing corpus.

When an existing artifact undergoes material review or revision, its current
non-effects, prohibited entailments, counterexamples and negative controls should be
reconciled under this policy without erasing historical wording or authority.

Existing SymK records remain evidence that the method was already used. This policy
standardizes future use; it does not retroactively claim that every prior record was
complete or compliant.

## 12. Prohibited inferences — what this policy does not mean

1. Negative controls are not the only SymK improvement identified or required.
2. Adoption of this policy does not accept, reject or exhaust the remaining
   generalizable-method conclusions.
3. A longer negative-control list does not establish a better or safer artifact.
4. Compliance with this policy does not establish truth, Knowledge, semantic
   adequacy, responsible operation, valid Authority, successful outcome or Value.
5. The default control library is not an exhaustive universal ontology or mandatory
   checklist for every artifact.
6. A project-specific control does not become a SymK-wide rule automatically.
7. A negative control does not hide or reverse an actual effect that occurred.
8. A disclaimer does not cure an unauthorized action, invalid decision, harmful
   consequence or materially misleading positive claim.
9. This project-process policy does not amend Stage-Accepted DR-009–018, Ratify a
   constitutional source or open SymK 2.4, 2.6 or any other closed stage.
10. Policy placement does not make its content constitutionally Ratified or
    universally binding.

## 13. Review and reopening conditions

Review this policy with the remaining generalizable-method conclusions and whenever:

- authors apply it as boilerplate without boundary analysis;
- its burden materially suppresses useful low-risk work;
- a material inference failure is not captured;
- Human, Scientific and Engineering Views require incompatible expressions;
- derived-project experience challenges a cross-SymK control;
- automation mistakes syntactic presence for semantic compliance;
- a competent constitutional or later-stage decision changes its authority or
  placement; or
- the distinction among negative control, non-effect, exception and reopening
  condition proves operationally inadequate.

## 14. Adoption result

**SYMK-GP-001 IS PROVISIONALLY ACTIVE AS A PROSPECTIVE PROJECT-GOVERNANCE POLICY.
MATERIAL NEW OR REVISED GOVERNED ARTIFACTS MUST MAKE MATERIAL PROHIBITED INFERENCES
INSPECTABLE AND TESTABLE IN PROPORTION TO THEIR CLAIMS AND CONSEQUENCES. THIS IS ONE
BOUNDED IMPROVEMENT, NOT THE COMPLETE GMC DISPOSITION. NO CONSTITUTIONAL
RATIFICATION, STAGE OPENING, DOMAIN RULE OR IMPLEMENTATION AUTHORITY IS CREATED.**
