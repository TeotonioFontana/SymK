## Authority boundary

Governance policies and profiles regulate the SymK project process within their
declared scope. They do not amend Stage-Accepted decisions, Ratify constitutional
content, open a closed evolution stage, impose Domain rules or authorize
implementation merely by being active.

Policies and profiles remain challengeable. Revision, supersession and retirement
require an explicit record that preserves lineage and identifies the authority and
effect of the change.
