# LB-DRAFT-001
# Foundational Engineering Principles

**Status:** Working Draft
**Version:** 0.1
**Maturity:** Exploratory Baseline

---

# AXIOM-0 — Symbiotic Cooperation

## Statement

The purpose of SymK is to maximize the value created through sustained cooperation between different forms of intelligence.

Knowledge is the shared medium.

Cooperation is the objective.

## Rationale

SymK does not exist to replace human intelligence or constrain artificial intelligence.
It exists to engineer productive collaboration between them.

---

# P001 — Primacy of Knowledge

Knowledge SHALL be the primary modeling concern of SymK.

Representations are derived artifacts.

## Engineering Consequences

- Documents are not the conceptual center.
- Fragments are representations.
- Knowledge Objects become first-class citizens.

---

# P002 — Core Isolation

The SymK Core SHALL remain domain-independent.

Legal, medical, engineering and financial concepts belong to Domain Cores.

---

# P003 — Progressive Specialization

Architecture SHALL evolve through successive layers:

SymK Core → Domain Core → Organization Core.

Each layer extends the previous one without altering its semantics.

---

# P004 — Organization Isolation

Organization-specific knowledge SHALL remain exclusively within the Organization Core.

Intellectual property, strategies, playbooks and proprietary knowledge SHALL never migrate into the Core.

---

# P005 — Engineering Before Implementation

Specifications precede implementation.

Databases, APIs, programming languages, AI models and user interfaces SHALL implement the Core rather than define it.

---

# P006 — Primitive Minimalism

The Core SHALL contain the smallest possible set of Knowledge Primitives.

A new primitive SHALL only be introduced when it cannot be expressed as:

- a Property;
- a Relationship;
- a Specialization.

---

# P007 — Separation of Discovery and Specification

Ideas belong to NOTES.

Consensus belongs to DRAFTS.

Approved specifications belong to CORE STANDARDS.

This separation protects architectural stability while encouraging exploration.

---

# P008 — Specification-driven Evolution

SymK publishes specifications.

Projects adopt them explicitly and independently.

No project SHALL be forced to evolve merely because the SymK standards evolve.

---

# P009 — Stable Products, Evolving Standards

Products evolve according to their own lifecycle.

Standards evolve continuously.

Compatibility is preferred over disruption.

---

# Closing

These principles constitute the constitutional foundation of the SymK engineering discipline.

Every future concept, property, relationship, contract, vocabulary, database model or software component SHALL remain consistent with these principles.
