## Independence questions

- Could A1 be derived completely from A4, or does it constrain all SymK activity independently of engineering?
- Is A3 universal to every SymK interaction or only persistent cooperation?
- Is A6 constitutional content or a governance rule beneath the Constitution?
- Does A0 require affected parties and consequences in its statement, or are they contained sufficiently by “responsible”?
- Does J1 adequately distinguish SymK's necessary conceptual jurisdiction over Intelligence from an ambition to develop a complete philosophy or science of Intelligence?

## Missing-axiom test

Before adding another axiom, ask:

1. Which incompatible SymK system would the new axiom exclude that the existing candidate axioms do not?
2. Why is the candidate irreducible to a derived principle?
3. Does it survive every domain and technology?
4. What constitutional harm follows if it is violated?
