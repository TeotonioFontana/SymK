# SymK Constitutional Update Scaffold

**Package status:** Canonical constitutional review package under the Stage-Accepted SymK 2.0 orientation
**Version:** 0.3
**Date:** 18 August 2026
**Canonical effect:** Canonical location and structure for constitutional review; all substantive artifacts retain their declared Proposed status and require explicit Ratification for constitutional force

This package governs the candidate update of the SymK constitutional layer in light of the Foundation Papers and Stage-Accepted `2.0-rc.1` identity, purpose, jurisdiction, layer, and tension decisions.

It does **not** supersede the current effective SymK axiom artifacts. A0–A4 and A6 remain Proposed until reviewed and ratified through explicit SymK governance. Former A5 is now Proposed Constitutional Jurisdiction Article J1, not an axiom. The package may nevertheless serve as the canonical location and structure for that review.

## Objectives

1. Separate constitutional axioms from foundational principles, policies, architectures, and implementation constraints.
2. Replace fixed Human/AI constitutional roles with governed role allocation among Intelligences.
3. Make Epistemic Humility, Cooperative Formation, and Engineering of Epistemic Conditions explicit candidates.
4. Define SymK's philosophical and engineering jurisdiction without claiming exhaustive coverage of Epistemology or neighboring fields.
5. Establish constitutional inheritance for LexBrain, IaaC, sshConnectivity, and future derived projects.
6. Govern conflict between SymK axioms and project axioms.
7. Preserve lineage and prevent silent canonical mutation.

## Governance statuses

- **Observed:** recovered from existing documents or practice.
- **Proposed:** newly formulated and not yet constitutionally reviewed.
- **Candidate:** sufficiently developed for formal constitutional deliberation.
- **Ratified:** approved by the designated SymK constitutional authority.
- **Superseded:** previously authoritative and explicitly replaced with preserved lineage.
- **Retired:** no longer normative but retained historically.

## Review order

3. Review whether A0–A4 and A6 are necessary and independent, and whether J1 has adequate constitutional force as a jurisdiction article.
4. Review each axiom's statement, derivation, defined terms, and interpretation.

6. Review conflict and inheritance mechanics.
7. Decide the disposition of existing canonical documents.
8. Ratify or return individual artifacts for revision.
9. Migrate dependent documents only after ratification.

## Source and norm distinction

Foundation papers are **constitutional sources**, not constitutional norms. They preserve inquiry, alternatives, arguments, evidence, and open questions. The Constitution selects and ratifies only particular commitments from them. An axiom document must trace that selection without making the complete source paper binding.
