# SymK Normative Hierarchy

**Status:** Proposed

## Hierarchy

Foundation papers and research precede this hierarchy as **epistemic sources**. They inform deliberation but possess no normative precedence merely because they are published or designated foundational.

| Level | Artifact | Governing question | Change frequency |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | SymK Constitution | What must remain true for SymK compatibility? | Exceptional |
| 1 | Foundational concepts and principles | What follows across SymK engineering? | Low |
| 2 | Derived-project Constitution | What must remain true for project identity and domain coherence? | Exceptional within project |
| 3 | Project Engineering Charter | How must systems be designed to honor the constitutions? | Moderate |
| 4 | Policies, standards, and reference models | Which governed practices and realizations apply? | Moderate |
| 5 | ADRs and capability specifications | Which contextual design is selected and why? | Frequent |
| 6 | Implementation | How is the selected design realized now? | Continuous |

## Constitutional source layer

| Artifact | Function | Normative status |
|---|---|---|
| Foundation paper | Preserves inquiry, evidence, alternatives, and candidate commitments | Non-normative until selected |
| Constitutional-source map | Traces which source conclusions support which axioms | Governed provenance, not an axiom |
| Axiom exposition | Preserves adopted derivation and authoritative interpretation | Interpretively normative after ratification |
| Axiom statement | Provides the compressed binding commitment | Constitutionally normative after ratification |

## Precedence rule

A lower level may instantiate, specialize, strengthen, or realize a higher level. It may not silently contradict or redefine it.

## Jurisdiction rule

Precedence is qualified by jurisdiction:

- SymK does not dictate complete domain ontologies.
- Project constitutions do not redefine universal epistemic concepts silently.
- Engineering charters do not promote design preferences into ontology.
- Implementations do not create concepts merely because a schema or API requires them.

## Evidence return

Operational evidence travels upward. It may challenge any level, including the Constitution, but change occurs through the governance process of the challenged level.
