# Ratification and Migration Plan

**Status:** Proposed

## Phase 0 — Scaffold review

- Review this package structure.
- Confirm the meaning of constitutional axiom.
- Confirm the designated ratification authority.
- Review whether J1 has adequate constitutional force as a jurisdiction article and decide whether A6 belongs in the Constitution.
- Approve or revise the Axiom Document Standard before any axiom advances to Candidate.

## Phase 1 — Axiom maturation

For each proposed axiom A0–A4 and A6, and separately for jurisdiction article J1:

1. refine governed definitions;
2. identify philosophical and project sources;
3. test independence from other axioms;
4. generate counterexamples;
5. test against LexBrain and sshConnectivity;
6. identify derived principles;
7. record dissent and unresolved questions;
8. assign Candidate or return to Proposed.

No axiom advances to Candidate until its Constitutional Sources and Derivation section identifies the exact source versions, adopted conclusions, deferred alternatives, and unresolved questions.

## Phase 2 — Corpus impact analysis

- Preserve the registry count and classification verified during SymK 2.0 migration step 7; reverify it after every constitutional reclassification.
- Find every reference to current axioms and unsafe language.
- Classify dependent documents by normative level.
- Produce a reference migration map.
- Identify behavior or validation rules affected by reclassification.

## Phase 3 — Constitutional deliberation

- Review axioms individually rather than as one indivisible package.
- Record decisions: ratify, revise, merge, demote, reject, or defer.
- Ratify the Constitution version and effective date.
- Publish dissent and open questions.

## Phase 4 — Canonical migration

- Create canonical axiom files.
- Update the canonical index and registry.
- Mark superseded documents explicitly.
- Preserve legacy narratives in historical lineage.
- Reclassify Meta PLC and Pool Exclusivity.
- Update terminology in dependent standards and manuals.

## Phase 5 — Derived-project compatibility

- LexBrain declares inherited SymK version and creates its Constitution and Engineering Charter.
- sshConnectivity does the same.
- Each completes the compatibility and conflict matrices.
- Discovered conflicts return to SymK governance.

## Phase 6 — Operational validation

- Test with LibScan and Composer discovery.
- Test with sshConnectivity configurations and failure cases.
- Observe whether the constitutional distinctions improve decisions or create harmful ambiguity.
- Revise through explicit amendment rather than silent drift.

## Non-destructive rule

No existing canonical artifact is deleted or overwritten until:

- its successor is ratified;
- dependencies are mapped;
- migration guidance exists;
- historical lineage is preserved;
- and rollback or recovery is defined.
