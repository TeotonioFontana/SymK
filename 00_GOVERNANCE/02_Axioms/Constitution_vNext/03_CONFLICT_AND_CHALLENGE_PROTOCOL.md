# Constitutional Conflict and Challenge Protocol

**Status:** Proposed

## 1. Trigger

Use this protocol when a project axiom, engineering obligation, law, domain model, or operational finding appears incompatible with SymK.

## 2. Immediate response

- Do not conceal the conflict through ambiguous wording.
- Freeze canonical promotion of the conflicting statement.
- Project discovery and safe operations may continue where they do not depend on resolving the conflict.

## 3. Conflict record

Record:

- exact propositions;
- artifact versions;
- relevant definitions;
- jurisdictions and scopes;
- concrete cases;
- affected participants;
- consequences of each interpretation;
- and the authority responsible for resolution.

## 4. Classification

Classify the relationship as:

1. **Instantiation** — applies an inherited statement to a project object.
2. **Specialization** — adds domain conditions without changing meaning.
3. **Strengthening** — imposes a stricter domain constraint.
4. **Extension** — adds a domain commitment outside SymK's jurisdiction.
5. **Explicit exception** — temporarily departs under governed necessity.
6. **Contradiction** — propositions cannot coexist in overlapping scope.
7. **Vocabulary conflict** — the same term is used differently.
8. **Jurisdiction conflict** — a layer has exceeded its proper scope.

## 5. Resolution options

- clarify vocabulary;
- narrow scope;
- revise the project statement;
- demote one statement to a principle or policy;
- ratify a temporary exception;
- amend SymK;
- or declare incompatibility with the inherited constitutional version.

## 6. Decision requirements

The resolution preserves:

- evidence;
- dissent;
- rationale;
- consequences;
- dependent-document impact;
- migration duties;
- and future review conditions.

## 7. Anti-overreach principle

Frequent conflict may indicate either:

- the project is violating inherited epistemic commitments; or
- SymK has overreached into domain ontology, project logic, or technical design.

Neither interpretation is presumed without analysis.
