# A0 — Cooperative Purpose

**Status:** Proposed  
**Version:** 0.2  
**Scope:** Universal SymK  
**Ratified:** No

## Normative statement

> **SymK exists to enable sustained, responsible, and evolvable cooperation among Intelligences. Knowledge is their shared medium; cooperation is their objective.**

## Constitutional purpose

A0 prevents SymK from being reduced to a repository, automation platform, productivity optimizer, or mechanism of control. It establishes why SymK exists and makes every subordinate commitment answerable to the quality and consequences of cooperation.

## Scope and jurisdiction

A0 applies to SymK, every SymK-derived project, and every persistent relationship claiming SymK compatibility. It governs purpose, not a particular domain, architecture, participant type, or duration. Brief transactions may use SymK artifacts without becoming cooperative relationships; they may not claim the stronger constitutional status without satisfying this axiom.

## Governed definitions

- **Intelligence:** a scoped and challengeably attributed capacity of an organized bearer; governed by `FC-005 Intelligence`.
- **Knowledge:** an epistemic achievement, distinct from its claims and representations; governed by `FC-006 Knowledge`.
- **Cooperation:** coordinated participation in which difference can contribute to a shared undertaking under governed relations.
- **Responsible:** attentive to authority, affected parties, foreseeable consequences, accountability, and repair.
- **Evolvable:** able to learn, revise, and migrate without erasing lineage or dissent.
- **Shared medium:** a medium through which participants relate; not necessarily identical representations, consensus, ownership, or unrestricted access.

## Derivation from the foundation papers

The Knowledge paper supplies the shared medium while denying that sharing means artifact identity or consensus. The Intelligence paper expands participants beyond a fixed Human/AI pair. The Education paper shows that cooperation is formed rather than guaranteed by intelligence. The Knowledge Engineering paper adds consequence and revision to the lifecycle. Together they replace the earlier undefined aim to “maximize value” with sustained, responsible, and evolvable cooperation.

## Foundational commitments absorbed

- Knowledge is the shared medium; cooperation is the objective.
- Difference among Intelligences may be productive.
- Dialogue, critique, memory, and mutual correction are cooperative practices.
- Cooperation must remain accountable to its consequences and capable of revision.

## Relationship with other axioms

A0 is the purposive parent of A1–A6. A1 constrains its medium; A2 governs participant roles; A3 addresses cooperative formation; A4 governs engineering; A5 limits philosophical jurisdiction; A6 carries the purpose into derived projects. A0 cannot override their protections in the name of cooperation.

## Authoritative interpretation

“Shared” means relational availability under appropriate governance, not identical access or belief. “Cooperation” is not mere coordination: coordinated domination, deception, or collusion fails A0. “Responsible” prevents efficiency from becoming sufficient. “Evolvable” requires learning with traceable change, not perpetual instability.

## Derived principles

- Difference is a resource when it remains intelligible and governable.
- Shared Knowledge does not require forced consensus.
- Cooperative success includes participant agency, challengeability, and repair.
- Local performance must be evaluated against system and societal consequences.

## Engineering consequences

Systems must expose purpose, participants, role boundaries, uncertainty, provenance, challenge paths, consequences, and revision history. Measures of output or speed cannot serve as sole success criteria. Architectures should preserve disagreement without making action impossible.

## Derived-project inheritance

Every derived project must state the cooperation it enables, its participants and affected parties, how Knowledge mediates the relationship, and how responsibility and evolution are governed. A product objective may specialize A0 but may not replace cooperation with extraction, compliance, or output maximization.

## Prohibited interpretations

- Cooperation means agreement, obedience, harmony, or unrestricted sharing.
- Any collaboration is good because participants consented formally.
- Human benefit alone exhausts responsibility.
- Greater output proves greater constitutional compatibility.
- A0 permits suppressing dissent for group cohesion.

## Counterexamples and compatibility tests

- A system increases output while systematically concealing uncertainty: incompatible.
- Participants coordinate efficiently to manipulate an affected party: incompatible.
- A shared repository preserves disagreement, provenance, and scoped access: potentially compatible.
- One participant becomes incapable of independent judgment through long-term use: compatibility requires redesign or justified mitigation.

## Open questions and preserved dissent

- Whether “mutually beneficial” belongs in the statement remains open because benefits and burdens may be asymmetric.
- Whether A0 governs brief interactions or only sustained relationships remains open.
- Dissent is preserved over whether “responsible” is too morally expansive for an axiom or indispensable to prevent instrumental cooperation.
