# A3 — Cooperative Formation

**Status:** Proposed  
**Version:** 0.2  
**Scope:** Persistent SymK cooperation; universality under review  
**Ratified:** No

## Normative statement

> **Stable cooperation among Intelligences depends upon their Education for relationship. Such Education becomes effective through the repeated Training of habits that make recognition, communication, restraint, responsibility, correction, and mutual adaptation possible.**

## Constitutional purpose

A3 makes explicit that Intelligence and Knowledge do not themselves produce civilized cooperation. It prevents SymK from treating cooperative conduct as an initial property while ignoring the repeated practices and formative environments through which participants become able to live and work together.

## Scope and jurisdiction

A3 clearly applies to persistent cooperative relations, their institutions, and their technical environments. Its application to brief interactions remains under review. It governs formation for relationship, not a complete pedagogy, curriculum, theory of learning, or claim that all participants learn identically.

## Governed definitions

- **Education:** formation of the capacity to understand, judge, participate in, question, and responsibly transform a shared world.
- **Training:** repeated practice and feedback that stabilize capabilities, responses, and habits.
- **Habit:** a comparatively durable disposition expressed in recurring conduct.
- **Relationship:** an ongoing pattern of mutual orientation with expectations and consequences.
- **Stable cooperation:** cooperation able to persist under ordinary pressure without depending solely on exceptional intervention.

## Constitutional sources

- *Education for Cooperation* v0.1.
- *Intelligence Beyond the Human* v0.1.
- Original Symbiotic Development Protocol.
- The proposition that social integration requires Education and repetitive Training of habits, introduced in the constitutional discussions.

## Derivation from the foundation papers

The Intelligence paper establishes that Intelligence does not entail wisdom, rationality, or fitness for cooperation. The Education paper distinguishes Education as formation in meaning and judgment from Training as stabilization through practice, while making them reciprocal. The original protocol supplies dialogue, reflection, critique, memory, and co-evolution as practices. A3 selects their necessary relation without constitutionalizing one pedagogical method.

## Foundational commitments absorbed

- Participation in a society or cooperative system requires formation.
- Repetition always forms habits, intentionally or accidentally.
- Training without Education risks compliance or conformity.
- Education without practiced habits remains fragile.
- Individuals, collectives, institutions, and hybrid arrangements can all be sites of formation.

## Relationship with other axioms

A3 supplies the formative condition for A0. A1 requires habits of humility and challenge; A2 requires capacities appropriate to allocated roles; A4 makes formative environments an engineering responsibility. A5 limits the philosophical claim, and A6 requires projects to specify their formative effects.

## Authoritative interpretation

Education is broader than information transfer; Training is broader than model training and narrower than Education. “Depends upon” states a necessary constitutional concern, not that Education guarantees cooperation. Repetition can stabilize bad habits; therefore Training inherits its direction and critique from Education.

## Derived principles

- Every persistent architecture is also a formative environment.
- Cooperative habits include recognition, communication, restraint, responsibility, correction, and adaptation.
- Participants must be able to examine and revise the frame in which they were trained.
- Collective habits cannot be repaired solely by educating isolated individuals.

## Engineering consequences

Design reviews must identify the habits rewarded by interfaces, metrics, defaults, feedback, and sanctions. Systems should practice disclosure of uncertainty, reason-giving, correction, respectful challenge, and recovery. Evaluation must observe conduct under repetition and pressure, not merely stated principles.

## Derived-project inheritance

Persistent projects must declare which cooperative habits they intentionally cultivate, which harmful habits they may accidentally train, and how participants can challenge their formative regime. Domain training must remain connected to the educational purpose of responsible participation.

## Prohibited interpretations

- Repetition alone constitutes Education.
- Education is indoctrination into fixed obedience.
- Intelligence makes formation unnecessary.
- Only humans educate or are educated.
- Technical systems are neutral carriers with no formative effect.
- Ill-mannered conduct justifies coercive homogenization or exclusion.

## Counterexamples and compatibility tests

- Participants recite principles but abandon them under pressure: Education has not become effective in habit.
- A workflow punishes acknowledgment of uncertainty: it trains epistemic concealment and is incompatible.
- Repetition produces compliant output while challenge rights disappear: incompatible.
- A system practices correction and measures whether behavior improves without suppressing dissent: potentially compatible.

## Open questions and preserved dissent

- Whether A3 is universal to all SymK systems or only persistent cooperative relations remains open.
- Whether “Education” can properly describe artificial formation without anthropomorphic import requires further work.
- Dissent is preserved over whether the named habits belong in the axiom statement or only its exposition.
