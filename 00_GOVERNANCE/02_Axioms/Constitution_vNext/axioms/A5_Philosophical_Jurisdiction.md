# A5 — Philosophical Jurisdiction

**Status:** Proposed  
**Version:** 0.2  
**Scope:** SymK and derived-project boundary  
**Ratified:** No

## Normative statement

> **SymK is centered on Epistemology and necessarily constrained by a minimal, non-anthropocentric account of Intelligence, because epistemic cooperation requires identifiable participants and attributable capacities. A derived project is additionally constrained by a governed domain ontology and logic. These commitments establish disciplinary accountability without claiming exhaustive coverage of the corresponding fields.**

## Constitutional purpose

A5 prevents conceptual imperialism and accidental metaphysics. It identifies the philosophical questions SymK must answer for coherence, the questions derived projects must answer for their domains, and the boundaries beyond which neither may pretend comprehensive authority.

## Scope and jurisdiction

SymK has primary jurisdiction over universal epistemic and cooperative constraints and the minimal participant concept required by them. Derived projects have primary jurisdiction over their domain ontology and logic, subject to SymK. A5 does not allocate academic ownership or exclude evidence from neighboring disciplines.

## Governed definitions

- **Epistemology:** inquiry into Knowledge, justification, inquiry, reliance, and related epistemic relations.
- **Intelligence:** the minimal governed participant concept in `FC-005`, not a complete philosophy of mind.
- **Ontology:** an explicit account of the entities, relations, categories, and commitments recognized in a domain.
- **Logic:** governed forms and rules of inference, constraint, and consequence within stated assumptions.
- **Jurisdiction:** responsibility to make necessary commitments explicit, not a claim to exhaust a field.

## Derivation from the foundation papers

The Knowledge paper places Epistemology at SymK's center. The Intelligence paper is necessary because cooperation requires identifiable bearers and attributable capacities. Properties and Projections prevents implementation schemas from becoming ontology by accident. Knowledge Engineering restricts engineering claims to conditions. Analysis of LexBrain and sshConnectivity shows that domain entities and inferences belong to project-level Ontology and Logic under inherited epistemic constraints.

## Foundational commitments absorbed

- SymK does not attempt a complete philosophy of Knowledge or Intelligence.
- Intelligence must be non-anthropocentric but not difference-blind.
- Models and schemas are purpose-dependent projections.
- Derived projects declare their ontological and logical commitments.
- Scope is a discipline of explicit constraint, not intellectual isolation.

## Relationship with other axioms

A5 bounds every other axiom. It locates A1 and A4 within Epistemology, constrains the Intelligence claims used by A0–A3, and establishes the layer boundary inherited through A6. It cannot be used to evade a problem that is necessary to apply another axiom coherently.

## Authoritative interpretation

“Centered on” is stronger than relevance and weaker than exhaustive coverage. “Minimal” means only the commitments needed for SymK coherence. Ontology and Logic are required project disciplines, not merely documents or technologies. Jurisdiction follows the level at which a commitment is universal or domain-specific.

## Derived principles

- Place universal epistemic constraints in SymK Core.
- Place domain entities and inference commitments in project constitutions.
- Declare assumptions, exclusions, and projection purposes.
- Escalate cross-layer conflicts rather than resolving them through implementation accident.

## Engineering consequences

Architectures must trace schema concepts and rules to their governing layer. Reviews should detect domain leakage into SymK Core and universal assumptions hidden inside product code. Material logical assumptions and ontology changes require governed versioning.

## Derived-project inheritance

LexBrain must govern legal-domain ontology and legal reasoning without redefining SymK Knowledge or Intelligence. sshConnectivity must do the same for connectivity, identity, trust, and operational logic. Each future project must declare its domain boundary and conceptual imports.

## Prohibited interpretations

- SymK claims the whole of Epistemology or philosophy of Intelligence.
- Derived projects are philosophically unconstrained because they are engineering products.
- Database schemas automatically constitute the domain ontology.
- Formal logic alone determines domain truth or responsible action.
- Scope boundaries prohibit interdisciplinary correction.

## Counterexamples and compatibility tests

- A LexBrain database field silently defines what a legal norm is: incompatible.
- A project documents its domain entities, inference assumptions, and inherited epistemic limits: compatible in principle.
- A product-specific category is promoted into SymK Core because it is locally useful: incompatible absent universal justification.
- Evidence from psychology revises a minimal Intelligence commitment through governance: compatible.

## Open questions and preserved dissent

- Whether A5 is an axiom or a foundational scope principle remains open.
- The threshold at which a neighboring philosophical question becomes constitutionally necessary requires testing.
- Dissent is preserved over whether Ontology and Logic should be named in the SymK Constitution or only in the derived-project framework.
