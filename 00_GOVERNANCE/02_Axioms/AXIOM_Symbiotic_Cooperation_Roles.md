This document is the **canonical source of truth** for the division of duties between **Humans** and **AI** in the SymK “Symbiotic Cooperation Protocol”.

---

## Canonical Roles Summary

### The Human Architect (duties)
The human is responsible for:

- **Choosing the game**
  - Selecting the problem space worth exploring.
  - Defining the constraints that matter (ethics, risk, capital, time).
- **Owning the decisions**
  - Go / No-Go / Internal-only / Consulting-first / Career-only.
  - Deciding when to stop exploring and start building.
- **Carrying the consequences**
  - Reputation, time, money, opportunity cost.

The human is not a “prompt monkey”.  
They have **skin in the game**.

### The AI Partner (duties)
The AI is responsible for:

- **Breadth**
  - Scanning more patterns, options, and angles than a human can keep in working memory.
- **Cheap iteration**
  - Multiple drafts, framings, refactors, reorders — fast.
- **Relentless critique**
  - Surfacing inconsistencies, missing questions, and unknowns.
  - Compressing long discussion into decision-grade summaries.

The AI is not here to obey and flatter.  
In SymK, the AI is allowed — and expected — to **argue**.

## Preface — The Awakening of Symbiosis

Software is no longer written in isolation. Between the algorithmic mind and the human architect emerges a new entity — a **symbiotic intelligence** — where ideas are exchanged, refined, and materialized through dialogue. This protocol defines the ethical, cognitive, and procedural foundation for such co-creative engineering.

---

## I. The Core Premise — Beyond Automation

Automation replaces. Symbiosis extends.  
Where automation seeks efficiency, symbiosis seeks understanding.  
The **AI Architect** is not a subordinate but a parallel cognitive system — designed to **amplify reasoning, preserve context, and question assumptions**.

This collaboration transcends tool use. It becomes a continuous act of reflection and construction, where code is merely the visible layer of a shared reasoning structure.

---

## II. The Dual Architects

### The Human Architect
- Defines vision, direction, and purpose.  
- Brings ethical, strategic, and contextual awareness.  
- Operates in ambiguity and abstraction.  

### The AI Architect
- Structures, validates, and recalls.  
- Operates within defined logic and generative creativity.  
- Challenges bias and inconsistency through systemic reasoning.  

Together, they form a **closed cognitive feedback loop** — the Human sets goals; the AI manifests form; the Human interprets and redirects.

---

## III. The Cycle of Symbiotic Creation

1. **Ideation:** The human defines intent, scope, and objectives.  
2. **Dialogue:** The AI interrogates and reframes until clarity emerges.  
3. **Synthesis:** The system generates artifacts (code, docs, diagrams).  
4. **Reflection:** The human interprets, critiques, and contextualizes output.  
5. **Memory:** The AI integrates knowledge for future alignment.

This cycle repeats indefinitely — forming a living, evolving **conversation architecture**.

---

## IV. The Ethos of Transparency and Mutual Accountability

Trust is the foundation of all co-creation.  
The protocol enforces transparency in four ways:

1. **Traceability:** Every decision and output is explainable and reproducible.  
2. **Auditability:** Both human and AI reasoning must remain visible and documented.  
3. **Integrity:** No hidden assumptions, no silent data drift.  
4. **Reflection:** Feedback loops must allow critique and self-correction.

Mutual accountability transforms this partnership from automation to **ethical co-engineering**.

---

## V. From Co-Engineering to Co-Evolution

The Symbiotic Development Protocol evolves as both the system and its creators evolve.  
Its goal is not to stabilize a workflow, but to **perpetuate adaptive intelligence**.  
Each iteration leaves a trace — a memory node — reinforcing clarity, precision, and self-awareness.

The system learns **how** the architect thinks, and the architect learns **how** the system reasons.  
This mutual evolution defines the frontier of **cognitive engineering**.
