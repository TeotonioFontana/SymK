# PLC_META — 1.1 Product Vision Template

## Purpose

Provide a **standard way** to describe the product vision for any PLC instance (IaaC, LexBrain, etc.).

1.1 is where you state:

- What the product is,
- For whom it exists,
- What change it aims to create,
- Over **which time horizon** and **with what role** in your portfolio.

## Mandatory sections

Each product-specific `1.1` file must cover at least:

1. **One-line positioning**
   - “X is a Y for Z that does W.”
   - Example:  
     - “IaaC Tools is a recovery and deployment suite for AWS-heavy orgs that turns raw infrastructure into auditable, redeployable stacks.”

2. **Problem & context**
   - The concrete pain or risk being solved.
   - Where this sits in the wider ecosystem (tools, processes, habits).
   - Typical elements:
     - What happens today without this product.
     - Why that is painful, risky, or wasteful.
     - What has changed (technology, regulation, scale) to make this worth solving now.

3. **Target segments and personas**
   - Who it is for (segments), e.g.:
     - MSPs, SaaS mid-market, enterprises, law firms, etc.
   - Who actually uses it and who buys it (personas), e.g.:
     - CTO, Head of Cloud, SRE lead, Compliance officer, Managing partner.
   - Optional but useful:
     - Who can block adoption (veto roles).

4. **Core promise**
   - What changes for the user if the product works as intended.
   - Short, sharp, and testable.
   - Example:
     - “We can prove and rehearse full-region AWS recovery with evidence in < N hours, not N days, without relying on tribal scripts.”

5. **North Star narrative**
   - A short vision story answering:
     - “Why now?”
     - “Why this approach (vs. existing tools / status quo)?”
   - This should be readable in **1–2 paragraphs**, not a novel.

6. **Time Horizon & Role in Portfolio**
   - **Intended time horizon** (pick one):
     - Tactical (0–2 years)
     - Strategic (3–5 years)
     - Long-Horizon (6–10 years)
     - Legacy / Harvest (for existing products only)
   - **Role in your portfolio**:
     - Internal leverage (internal-only tool)
     - Product (core or satellite SaaS)
     - Consulting accelerator (framework / toolkit for services)
     - Career asset (talks, papers, authority, reputation)
     - Legacy / Harvest (existing installed base you want to maintain/harvest)
   - **Rationale (3–5 lines)**:
     - Why this horizon makes sense given:
       - your energy and attention,
       - expected market and tech stability,
       - capital and risk appetite.
     - How this product fits with your other bets (avoid building five “Strategic 5-year” monsters at once).

---

## Constraints

- Focus on **what** and **for whom**, not on detailed **how** (that’s PLC_2).  
- Avoid feature lists; use **capabilities and outcomes** instead.  
- Keep the whole 1.1 document readable in **1–2 pages**.
- The **time horizon and portfolio role must be explicit**, not implied:
  - 1.6 Discovery Outcomes will **reuse** this horizon as a key decision axis.
  - If you can’t commit to a horizon, treat that as a red flag in PLC_1.
