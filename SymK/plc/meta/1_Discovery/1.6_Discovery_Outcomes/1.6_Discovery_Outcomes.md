# PLC_META — 1.6 Discovery Outcomes

_Phase 1 — Discovery_

## 1. Purpose / Objective

1.6 exists to **close Discovery with a clear, bounded decision** about what happens next.

It should answer, explicitly:

- Does this idea **deserve architecture and engineering effort**?
- In what **mode**:
  - Go (full product exploration),
  - No-Go,
  - Internal-only,
  - Consulting-first,
  - Career-only.
- Under which **time horizon and portfolio role**:
  - Tactical (0–2 years),
  - Strategic (3–5 years),
  - Long-Horizon (6–10 years),
  - Legacy / Harvest (for existing assets).

The goal is not to “prove the idea is good”.  
The goal is to **stop weak ideas early** and only let the ones with coherent logic, feasible GTM, and realistic horizons proceed into the lab (PLC_2+).

---

## 2. Description

1.6 aggregates and compresses the rest of PLC_1:

- 1.1 Product Vision  
- 1.2 Market Research  
- 1.3 Stakeholder Map  
- 1.4 Discovery Methodologies  
- 1.5 Discovery Tools  

Then it forces a decision across **five lenses**:

1. **Decision Framework** – Is this a Go / No-Go / Internal / Consulting / Career idea?
2. **Business Size Fit** – For what **size of business** is this idea realistically suitable?
3. **Consulting Leverage** – Is this better as a consulting accelerator than as a standalone product?
4. **Career Leverage** – Does this make sense as a career / reputation asset, regardless of product revenue?
5. **Success Metrics & Risks** – What would “success” look like and what can kill it?

On top of that, 1.6 now makes **time horizon** explicit:

- How long is this idea supposed to:
  - stay relevant,
  - justify active investment,
  - be worth your personal attention?

This horizon is not decoration. It shapes whether the idea makes sense at all.

## 4. Core Questions 1.6 Must Answer

1. **Should this idea proceed beyond Discovery?**
   - Go
   - No-Go
   - Internal-only
   - Consulting-first
   - Career-only

2. **Under which time horizon?**
   - Tactical (0–2 years)
   - Strategic (3–5 years)
   - Long-Horizon (6–10 years)
   - Legacy / Harvest (for existing assets only)

3. **For which business size is this idea realistic and attractive?**
   - Solo / micro,
   - Small team / boutique,
   - Mid-sized firm,
   - Enterprise / large organization.

4. **What is the primary value mode?**
   - Product revenue,
   - Consulting leverage,
   - Internal capability,
   - Career / reputation asset.

5. **What kills it?**
   - Key risks and “deal-breaker” conditions that would justify a hard No-Go.

If 1.6 can’t answer these clearly, Discovery hasn’t done its job.

## 5. Time Horizon & Portfolio Role (Integrated)

This section is **mandatory** in every 1.6 outcome.

### 5.1 Horizon Categories

For simplicity, 1.6 uses four coarse buckets:

1. **Tactical (0–2 years)**
   - Purpose: unblock or optimize existing work quickly, create leverage fast.
   - Typical fit: internal tools, consulting accelerators, PoCs that may or may not grow up.

2. **Strategic (3–5 years)**
   - Purpose: become a core pillar of your offering or practice.
   - Typical fit: products you’re willing to maintain, evolve, and support seriously.

3. **Long-Horizon (6–10 years)**
   - Purpose: ride a structural trend (e.g., cloud DR, legal-tech memory, AI dev tooling).
   - Only sane if:
     - the domain will still matter later,
     - you’re willing to carry the long curve of adoption and evolution.

4. **Legacy / Harvest**
   - Purpose: extract value from an existing base with limited new investment.
   - Applies mostly to **old or existing products**, not new experiments.

### 5.2 Portfolio Role

In 1.6, you must also declare the **role** this idea should play in *your* portfolio:

- Internal leverage (internal-only tool),
- Product (core or satellite SaaS),
- Consulting accelerator (framework / toolkit for services),
- Career asset (talks, papers, authority, reputation),
- Legacy / Harvest (for things you already have in the wild).

### 5.3 Alignment Checks

1. **Human alignment**
   - Is this horizon realistic given:
     - your available attention and energy,
     - your time horizon as a practitioner,
     - your willingness to carry operational responsibility that long?

2. **Market & tech alignment**
   - Is the domain likely to still matter on this horizon?
   - Is there a clear risk of:
     - the opportunity shrinking early,
     - or the problem being solved by platform/commodity moves (e.g., AWS, big vendors)?

3. **Investment vs horizon**
   - Does the required investment in architecture, build, GTM, and support **match** the horizon?
   - Would a **smaller-scope, shorter-horizon** version of the idea make more sense?

If horizon + role look unrealistic, that’s a valid reason for:

- No-Go,
- Internal-only version,
- or a **re-scoped** idea with a different horizon.

## 6. Outcomes

Each 1.6 execution must produce:

1. **Decision statement**  
   - A short, explicit line, e.g.:

> “Go — Strategic (3–5y) product bet, with Internal-only first release and strong consulting component.”

2. **Horizon & role statement**  
   - Horizon bucket (Tactical / Strategic / Long-Horizon / Legacy).
   - Portfolio role (Internal / Product / Consulting / Career / Legacy).
   - 3–5 lines of rationale.

3. **Business size fit**  
   - Explicit statement of the primary **target business size**.
   - If multiple sizes are possible, which one is **first** and **why**.

4. **Mode-specific conclusions**
   - For each of:
     - Consulting leverage,
     - Career leverage,
     - Internal leverage:
   - A brief note:
     - “Strong fit / weak fit / irrelevant” and why.

5. **Key risks & kill switches**
   - The list of 3–7 conditions that would justify:
     - killing the idea,
     - or downgrading it (e.g., from Product to Internal-only).

Format suggestion (not mandatory):

- `1.6_Discovery_Outcomes/1.6_summary.md` — human-readable.
- Optional `1.6_Discovery_Outcomes/1.6_summary.json` — structured for tools.

## 7. Sub-Items (1.6.1–1.6.5)

To keep things sharp and reusable, 1.6 can be split into sub-files:

- `1.6.1_Decision_Framework.md`
- `1.6.2_Business_Size_Fit.md`
- `1.6.3_Consulting_Leverage.md`
- `1.6.4_Career_Leverage.md`
- `1.6.5_Success_Metrics_and_Risks.md`

The **time horizon & portfolio role** belong in the **main 1.6 summary**, but sub-items can reference it, e.g.:

- 1.6.2 can say: “Given a Strategic (3–5y) horizon, the realistic sweet spot is mid-sized firms…”.
- 1.6.3 can say: “On a Tactical (0–2y) horizon, the best use is as a consulting accelerator…”.

## 8. AI Support / Symbiotic Role

AI is useful here mainly as:

1. **Compressor**
   - Turn all of 1.1–1.5 into:
     - one “Best case / Worst case / Most likely” page,
     - one risk list,
     - one horizon & role proposal.

2. **Attacker**
   - Challenge:
     - whether the declared horizon is realistic,
     - whether the proposed business size fit makes sense,
     - whether No-Go should be on the table more strongly.

3. **Comparator**
   - Compare this 1.6 with past PLCs:
     - “This looks similar to [Project X] which was killed for reason Y.”
     - “This horizon doesn’t match your previous decisions in this domain.”

AI should **not** be used here to justify an idea you emotionally want.  
Its job at 1.6 is to make it harder to lie to yourself.
