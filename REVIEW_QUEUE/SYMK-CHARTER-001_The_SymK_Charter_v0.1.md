
# SYMK-CHARTER-001
# The SymK Charter
## Identity of an Engineering Discipline

**Status:** Foundational Charter (Draft)  
**Version:** 0.1

> *This document defines the identity of SymK. It intentionally precedes
> methods, standards, implementations and products.*

---

# 1. Purpose

SymK exists to establish an engineering discipline dedicated to the
systematic construction, evolution, governance and cooperative use of
knowledge systems.

Its purpose is not to create a single product.

Its purpose is to define principles that remain valid across products,
organizations, technologies and generations.

---

# 2. The Engineering Problem

Modern knowledge is fragmented across:

- documents;
- databases;
- software;
- organizations;
- individual experts;
- artificial intelligence.

The engineering problem is not merely storing information.

It is preserving **meaning**, enabling **cooperation**, and allowing
knowledge to evolve without losing its identity.

---

# 3. Mission

To provide an engineering foundation that allows multiple forms of
intelligence to cooperate through shared, durable and semantically stable
knowledge.

Knowledge is the medium.

Cooperation is the objective.

---

# 4. Vision

A future in which knowledge systems can evolve for decades while remaining:

- understandable;
- trustworthy;
- reusable;
- governed;
- interoperable;
- independent of specific technologies.

---

# 5. Scope

SymK concerns itself with:

- engineering principles;
- knowledge structures;
- governance;
- concept definition;
- semantic stability;
- knowledge evolution;
- cooperation between independent intelligences.

SymK does **not** prescribe a specific programming language, database,
LLM, cloud provider or application architecture.

---

# 6. What SymK Is Not

SymK is not:

- a legal framework;
- a medical ontology;
- a document management system;
- a knowledge graph implementation;
- a software platform;
- a SaaS product;
- a replacement for Ontology Engineering;
- a replacement for Domain-Driven Design;
- a replacement for Knowledge Management.

Instead, SymK integrates and orchestrates mature engineering disciplines
where they contribute to its mission.

---

# 7. Fundamental Principles

1. Reality precedes models.
2. Observation precedes abstraction.
3. Engineering precedes implementation.
4. Knowledge is independent of representation.
5. Concepts are refined through repeated evidence.
6. Standards emerge from validated experience.
7. Evolution is continuous and disciplined.
8. Simplicity is preferred over unnecessary sophistication.
9. Existing knowledge should be reused before new concepts are invented.
10. Humility is an engineering virtue.

---

# 8. Evidence-Based Evolution

Every concept follows the same maturation path:

Observation

↓

Hypothesis

↓

Evidence

↓

Engineering Decision

↓

Specification

↓

Implementation

↓

Operational Validation

↓

Refinement

No concept becomes part of the SymK Core solely because it appears
interesting or elegant.

---

# 9. The Three Layers of SymK

## I. Discipline

Defines the enduring principles.

## II. Body of Knowledge

Collects concepts, methods, patterns and standards.

## III. Reference Implementations

Applies SymK within real laboratories such as LexBrain,
SSHConnectivity and OsteoLab.

Each implementation validates and improves the discipline.

---

# 10. Relationship with Other Disciplines

SymK stands on the shoulders of existing engineering knowledge.

It actively learns from:

- Ontology Engineering
- Knowledge Engineering
- Terminology Science
- Domain-Driven Design
- Systems Engineering
- Knowledge Management
- Human–AI Cooperation

Its objective is integration rather than replacement.

---

# 11. Governance

A SymK artifact may be:

- Observation
- Note
- Research
- Working Draft
- Specification
- Standard
- Historical Record

Promotion between these states requires engineering justification and
traceable evidence.

---

# 12. Success Criterion

SymK succeeds if independent teams, domains and technologies can
cooperate over long periods while preserving semantic identity,
engineering clarity and organizational knowledge.

---

# Closing Statement

SymK is not defined by a software product.

It is defined by an engineering discipline.

Products will evolve.

Technologies will change.

Artificial intelligence will change.

The discipline should remain stable enough to guide them all while
continuing to learn from every new laboratory that adopts it.
