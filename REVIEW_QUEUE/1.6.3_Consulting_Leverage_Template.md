# PLC_META — 1.6.3 Consulting Leverage Template

## Purpose

Provide a standard way to answer:

> “Is this product good as a consulting / services weapon?”

regardless of whether it becomes a standalone product.

## Axes (0–3 each)

Evaluate the product instance along three axes:

1. **Sales hook**
   - Does this product help **win deals**?
   - Example: “We have our own engine to image your environments and simulate DR/migrations.”

2. **Delivery accelerator**
   - Does it make **project execution** faster, safer, or more repeatable?
   - Example: shared tooling instead of per-project scripts.

3. **Differentiation / margin**
   - Does it make your consulting **obviously better** than generic alternatives?
   - Example: unique reports, dashboards, methods, or artifacts.

For each axis, assign:

- **0** — negligible impact
- **1** — minor impact
- **2** — clear impact
- **3** — strong, obvious impact

Then sum (0–9) and map to a label:

- **0–3** → Weak
- **4–6** → Moderate
- **7–9** → Strong / Core pillar

## Product-specific file requirements

Each `1.6.3_*.md` file must:

1. Show a small table: axis, explanation, score.
2. Provide the total and label (Weak / Moderate / Strong / Core pillar).
3. Provide a **sentence-level answer**, e.g.:

> “Consulting leverage: Strong (8/9). This product can sit at the center of DR, migration, and resilience engagements, providing a tangible engine and reports that differentiate our work and reduce per-project scripting.”
